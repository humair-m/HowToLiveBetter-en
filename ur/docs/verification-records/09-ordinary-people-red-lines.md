### Public Security Administration Punishment Law (2025 revision)
- URL: <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>
- کھولا گیا، article by article تصدیق کیا گیا۔ Effective date: Article 144 "This Law shall be effective from January 1, 2026."
- Article 29 (اندراج 1): "Whoever commits any of the following acts shall be detained for 5 to 10 days and may also be fined up to 1,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively minor, detention of up to 5 days or a fine of up to 1,000 yuan: (1) intentionally spreading rumors, falsely reporting dangerous situations, epidemics, disasters, or police information, or disrupting public order by other means"
- Article 35 (اندراج 2): "Detention of 5 to 10 days or a fine of 1,000 to 3,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively serious, detention of 10 to 15 days and may also be fined up to 5,000 yuan: … (3) infringing on the name, portrait, reputation, or honor of a heroic martyr by insult, defamation, or other means, harming the social public interest; (4) desecrating or denying the deeds and spirit of heroic martyrs, or producing, disseminating, or distributing materials that propagandize or glorify wars of aggression or acts of aggression, disrupting public order"
- Article 43 (اندراج 6): "Detention of up to 5 days or a fine of up to 1,000 yuan; where the circumstances are serious, detention of 10 to 15 days and may also be fined up to 1,000 yuan: … (5) throwing objects from a building or other high place, endangering the safety of another person's life or public or private property or public safety"
- Article 46 (اندراج 8): "In violation of relevant laws and regulations on the management of flight airspace, flying a civil unmanned aerial vehicle … where the circumstances are relatively serious, detention of 5 to 10 days"
- Article 47 (اندراج 12): "Detention of 10 to 15 days and a fine of 1,000 to 2,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively minor, detention of 5 to 10 days and a fine of up to 1,000 yuan: … (3) unlawfully restricting another person's personal freedom, unlawfully entering another person's residence, or unlawfully searching another person's body"
- Article 50 (اندراج 9): "Detention of up to 5 days or a fine of up to 1,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively serious, detention of 5 to 10 days and may also be fined up to 1,000 yuan: … (6) peeping, secretly photographing, eavesdropping, or distributing another person's private information"
- Article 61 (اندراج 14): "Warning or a fine of up to 500 yuan; where the circumstances are serious, detention of 5 to 10 days and may also be fined up to 1,000 yuan: … (2) obstructing a state organ worker in the performance of duties in accordance with the law … Obstruction of a people's police officer in the performance of duties in accordance with the law shall be punished more severely"
- Article 80 (اندراج 3): "… Whoever disseminates obscene information via information networks, telephone, or other communication tools shall be detained for 10 to 15 days and may also be fined up to 5,000 yuan; where the circumstances are relatively minor, detention of up to 5 days or a fine of 1,000 to 3,000 yuan. Where the obscene materials or obscene information in the preceding paragraph involve minors, the punishment shall be more severe"
- Article 82 (اندراج 10): "Whoever, for profit, provides conditions for gambling, or participates in gambling with relatively large stakes, shall be detained for up to 5 days or fined up to 1,000 yuan; where the circumstances are serious, detained for 10 to 15 days and fined 1,000 to 5,000 yuan"
- بھی تصدیق شدہ: اس Law میں misuse of identity cards پر الگ provision نہیں ہے (وہ Resident Identity Card Law کے تحت ہیں)؛ fireworks اور firecrackers صرف Article 28 میں sports اور cultural venues پر ظاہر ہوتے ہیں، اس لیے "fireworks / open-fire" امیدوار شامل نہیں کیا گیا۔

- Article 280-1 (اندراج 13): "Whoever, in an activity where identity proof is required under state regulations, uses a forged, altered, or fraudulently obtained resident identity card, passport, social security card, driver's license, or other document that may be used to prove identity, where the circumstances are serious, shall be sentenced to criminal detention or control, and shall also or only be fined"

- Article 291-2 (اندراج 6): "Whoever throws objects from a building or other high place, where the circumstances are serious, shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to 1 year, criminal detention, or control, and shall also or only be fined"
- Article 299 (اندراج 2): "Whoever publicly insults the national flag or national emblem of the People's Republic of China by burning, destroying, defacing, staining, trampling, or other means shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to 3 years, criminal detention, control, or deprivation of political rights. Whoever publicly alters the lyrics or score of the national anthem, performs it in a distorted or degrading manner, or insults it by other means, where the circumstances are serious, shall be punished under the preceding paragraph"
- Article 299-1 (اندراج 2): "Whoever insults, defames, or infringes on the name, portrait, reputation, or honor of a heroic martyr by other means, harming the social public interest, where the circumstances are serious, shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to 3 years, criminal detention, control, or deprivation of political rights"
- Article 303 (اندراج 10): "Whoever, for profit, gathers persons for gambling or makes a livelihood from gambling shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to 3 years, criminal detention, or control, and a fine. Whoever operates a casino shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to 5 years, criminal detention, or control, and a fine; where the circumstances are serious, imprisonment of 5 to 10 years and a fine"
- Article 312 (اندراج 4): "Whoever, knowing that property is the proceeds of crime or the income derived therefrom, conceals, transfers, purchases, sells on commission, or disguises or conceals it by other means shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to 3 years, criminal detention, or control, and shall also or only be fined" (returned text یہیں ختم ہوا؛ "where the circumstances are serious, imprisonment of 3 to 7 years" return میں نہیں آیا؛ اندراج یہ نہیں لکھتا)
- Article 341 (اندراج 11): paragraph 1 "Imprisonment of up to 5 years or criminal detention, and a fine; where the circumstances are serious, imprisonment of 5 to 10 years, and a fine; where the circumstances are particularly serious, imprisonment of 10 years or more, and a fine or confiscation of property"; paragraph 2 "Imprisonment of up to 3 years, criminal detention, control, or a fine"; paragraph 3 "Whoever, for the purpose of consumption, unlawfully hunts, purchases, transports, or sells terrestrial wildlife growing and reproducing in the wild other than those specified in paragraph 1, where the circumstances are serious, shall be punished under the preceding paragraph"
- Article 363 (اندراج 3): "Whoever, for profit, produces, duplicates, publishes, sells, or disseminates obscene materials shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to 3 years, criminal detention, or control, and a fine; where the circumstances are serious, imprisonment of 3 to 10 years, and a fine; where the circumstances are particularly serious, imprisonment of 10 years or more or life imprisonment, and a fine or confiscation of property"
- Article 364 (اندراج 3): "Whoever disseminates obscene books, periodicals, films, audio-video materials, pictures, or other obscene materials, where the circumstances are serious, shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to 2 years, criminal detention, or control"

### اندراج 14 پولیس پر حملہ

- SPP 2021 briefing <https://www.spp.gov.cn/xwfbh/wsfbh/202105/t20210517_518308.shtml>: کھولا گیا، May 17, 2021۔ "Procuratorates nationwide approved the arrest of 405 suspects and prosecuted 101 persons for assault on police"; Jiangsu "among 42 assault-on-police cases, those triggered by drunken disturbances or disputes with others followed by violence against law-enforcement police accounted for 47.6%"; Hubei "among 16 assault-on-police cases, 12 involved suspects using minor-injury means such as punching, kicking, or biting to obstruct enforcement, accounting for 75%"۔ پیج میں کوئی individual cases نہیں ہیں۔

### اندراج 15 نابالغ لڑکیاں
- SPC official reply <https://www.court.gov.cn/hudong/xiangqing/13363.html>: کھولا گیا، February 9, 2015۔ "Where sexual intercourse with a young girl is achieved by force, coercion, or other coercive means, regardless of whether the actor knew the victim was a young girl, the actor shall be convicted and sentenced for rape in accordance with the law"; 12 سال سے کم "deemed to have known" یکساں طور پر؛ "12 or older but under 14" "based on physical development, speech and behavior, dress, etc., if it is possible that the victim is a young girl and the actor committed the sexual assault, 'knowing' shall be established"; "knowing" کی exceptions narrowly construe کی جاتی ہیں۔
- SPC 2003 Reply gazette page <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/7a8982b0b8e1394263eda222294e02.html> تین بار 502 واپس آیا، حوالہ نہیں دیا گیا۔

## شامل نہ کیے گئے امیدوار اور وجوہات
- الیکٹرک بائیسائیکلوں پر نشے میں سائیکل چلانا: electric bicycles اور electric motorcycles میں فرق اور متعلقہ case law صرف China Court Network پر ہے (کھولا نہیں جا سکا)؛ Guiding Case 271، جب کھولا گیا، تو وہ drunk-driving assisted-driving case تھا جو electric bicycles سے متعلق نہیں تھا؛ "صرف وہی لکھیں جو تصدیق ہو سکے" کی ضرورت کے مطابق شامل نہیں کیا گیا؛ سیکشن 8 اندراج 4 کا عنوان پہلے ہی electric vehicles کو cover کرتا ہے۔
