- Article body text Supreme People's Court gazette repost page <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/7f184078694d811fb3314f6af9accf.html> ("Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (continued)"، laws and regulations column) سے تصدیق شدہ: curl نے fetch اور مقامی parse کیا
- Quoted متن:
  - Article 1062 "The following property acquired by the spouses during the marriage is the joint property of the spouses and shall be jointly owned by both spouses: (1) wages, bonuses, and labor compensation; (2) income from production, operation, or investment; (3) income from intellectual property; (4) inherited or gifted property, except as provided in item 3, Article 1063 of this Law; (5) other property that should be jointly owned. Both spouses have equal right to dispose of joint property."
  - Article 1063 "The following property is the personal property of one spouse: (1) one party's pre-marital property; (2) compensation or compensation received for personal injury to one party; (3) property determined in a will or gift contract to belong only to one party; (4) articles for personal use of one party; (5) other property that should belong to one party."
  - Article 1065 "Both spouses may agree that the property acquired during the marriage and pre-marital property shall be owned separately, jointly, or partly separately and partly jointly. The agreement shall be in writing. In the absence of an agreement or where the agreement is unclear, the provisions of Articles 1062 and 1063 of this Law shall apply. The agreement of the spouses on the property acquired during the marriage and pre-marital property shall be legally binding on both parties."
  - Article 1076 "Where both spouses voluntarily divorce, they shall sign a written divorce agreement and personally go to the marriage registration organ to apply for divorce registration. The divorce agreement shall state the parties' mutual intent to divorce and their consensus on matters such as child custody, property, and debt handling."
  - Article 1077 "Within 30 days from the date the marriage registration organ receives the divorce registration application, either party may, if unwilling to divorce, withdraw the divorce registration application from the marriage registration organ. Within 30 days after the expiration of the period specified in the preceding paragraph, both parties shall personally go to the marriage registration organ to apply for the issuance of a divorce certificate; if no application is made, the divorce registration application shall be deemed withdrawn."
  - Article 1079 "Where one spouse requests a divorce, mediation may be conducted by the relevant organization, or the spouse may directly file a divorce lawsuit in the people's court. When trying a divorce case, the people's court shall conduct mediation; if the parties' relationship has truly broken down and mediation is ineffective, divorce shall be granted. Divorce shall be granted if mediation is ineffective under any of the following circumstances: (1) bigamy or cohabitation with another; (2) commission of domestic violence or abuse or abandonment of family members; (3) persistent gambling, drug use, or other vices despite repeated education; (4) separation due to incompatibility for two years; (5) other circumstances that cause the breakdown of the spousal relationship. … Where, after the people's court has ruled not to grant divorce, the parties separate for one more year and one party again files a divorce lawsuit, divorce shall be granted."
  - Article 1088 "Where one spouse has borne greater obligations such as child rearing, caring for elderly, or assisting the other spouse's work, that spouse shall have the right to request compensation from the other spouse at the time of divorce, and the other spouse shall pay compensation. The specific arrangement shall be agreed upon by both parties; if no agreement is reached, the people's court shall decide."
