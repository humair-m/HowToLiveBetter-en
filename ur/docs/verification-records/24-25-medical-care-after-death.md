## I. سیکشن 24 فی اندراج تصدیق شدہ اصل متن

ماخذ: General Office of the State Council "Several Measures on Accelerating the Construction of a Hierarchical Diagnosis and Treatment System" (April 2026) <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>۔

| اندراج | تصدیق شدہ اصل متن |
| --- | --- |
| Deductible continuous calculation | "For insured patients who are progressively referred by grassroots medical and health institutions within the coordinating region, the inpatient deductible at the higher-level hospital may be continuously calculated; for inpatients transferred from a higher-level hospital down to a grassroots medical and health institution, no additional inpatient deductible is set during the same disease cycle." |
| Reimbursement rate differential of 10 percentage points at each tier | "Appropriately widening the inpatient reimbursement levels of insured persons at medical and health institutions of different levels according to local conditions; in principle, within the coordinating region, the gap in inpatient reimbursement rates at medical and health institutions at each level should be around 10 percentage points." "Where conditions permit, the outpatient reimbursement levels of insured persons at medical and health institutions of different levels may be appropriately widened in line with the hierarchical-diagnosis-and-treatment orientation." |
| Reserved appointment slots and referral centers | "Lead hospitals shall reserve a certain proportion of appointment slots and beds for grassroots medical and health institutions and promptly attend to patients referred from the grassroots." "Medical institutions shall strengthen the unified management of referral services, establish referral centers or designate fixed departments to undertake patient referral services, and achieve full coverage by 2027." |
| Cross-province medical necessity assessment | "In principle, the necessity of cross-region and cross-province medical treatment for patients shall be assessed by deputy chief physicians or above at secondary or tertiary hospitals." "The reimbursement level of cross-province temporary outgoing patients shall maintain a reasonable differential with the reimbursement level of same-level medical institutions in the place of insurance." |
| Tertiary hospitals appropriately reduce general outpatient services | "Tertiary hospitals shall focus on acute, critical, severe, and difficult complex diseases... gradually reduce general outpatient services for common disease revisits and chronic diseases with clear diagnoses and stable conditions." "Within compact medical consortia, higher-level hospitals shall establish outpatient clinics for common and chronic diseases such as hypertension, diabetes, and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease at grassroots medical and health institutions... and extend expert-team general outpatient services to grassroots medical and health institutions." |

## IV. پیمانے اور فائدے کا درجہ

سیکشن 24 کے چھ اندراجوں میں سے، چار کا پیسہ میٹرک اور دو کا وقت میٹرک ہے۔ فائدے کا درجہ پیسے کی حدوفق کے مطابق مقرر کیا گیا: deductible اور reimbursement rate differences سینکڑوں سے ہزاروں یوآن کی حد میں ہیں، "درمیانہ" مقرر کیے گئے؛ کوئی بھی دس ہزار یوآن کی سطح تک نہیں پہنچتا، اس لیے اس سیکشن کا کوئی "بڑا" نہیں۔ سیکشن 25 کے پہلے تین اندراج سینکڑوں سے ہزاروں یوآن کے غیر مناسب چارجز سے بچنے کے بارے میں ہیں، "درمیانہ" مقرر کیے گئے؛ اندراج 4 provident fund balances اور social insurance benefits سے متعلق ہے جو دس ہزار یوآن کی سطح تک پہنچ سکتے ہیں، "بڑا" مقرر کیا گیا؛ اندراج 5 کا freedom میٹرک (personal information سمیت) ہے، اور سیکشن 14 کے دو اندراجوں کی طرح اس کیٹگری میں آتا ہے جس پر حدوفق لاگو نہیں ہوتے؛ فیصلے کے مطابق "درمیانہ" مقرر کیا گیا۔

دونوں سیکشنز کی لاگتیں تقریباً صفر ہیں، لیکن متعدد اندراج "willpower=کچھ" ریکارڈ کرتے ہیں: مشکل یہ نہیں کہ اصول جاننا بلکہ اس وقت خواہش کرنا، پہلے کمیونٹی کلینک جانا، اور جنازے کے انتظامات کے دوران ایک قیمت فہرست کو تنقیدی نگاہ سے دیکھنا۔
