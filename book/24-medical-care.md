# 24. Medical Care: How to Spend Less Money and Take Fewer Wrong Turns

Metric: Money and time. This section covers how to navigate medical care itself: which hospital to go to first, how to be referred upward, where money can be saved. It also covers how emergency rooms queue, and what assessments and certificates to obtain after treating a serious injury. For benefits you can claim, see Section 7. For long-term management of chronic disease, see Section 16. For what to do on the spot in an emergency, see Section 13.

This section is primarily based on the Several Measures on Accelerating the Construction of Hierarchical Diagnosis and Treatment, issued by the General Office of the State Council in April 2026. Hierarchical diagnosis and treatment means deciding which level of hospital to go to first based on severity. This document directly links "going to primary care" with "saving money." However, implementation progress varies by locality; the body of the text describes the national-level rules. For the specific pooling region (tongchou diqu) where you are, the local medical insurance and health authorities' statements prevail. The pooling region is your medical insurance participation location, usually a city.

### 1. For common illnesses, first seek care at the community level; through tiered upward referral from primary care, inpatient deductibles are calculated continuously
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. First make one trip to the community. The hard part is resisting the urge to go directly to a specialist number
- Plain-language summary: First go to the community, then have the community refer you to a large hospital for inpatient care, and the out-of-pocket portion is counted only once. If you go directly to a tertiary hospital for inpatient care, then transfer back to the community for recovery, that portion may be paid twice. For inpatients transferred from a higher-level hospital back to a community for continued stay, the deductible is not reset again within the same disease cycle
- Benefit: For insured patients referred upward through tiered referral from primary care, the inpatient deductible at the higher-level hospital can be calculated continuously. The deductible is the out-of-pocket portion paid each time you are hospitalized, after which medical insurance begins to reimburse. Continuous calculation means the two hospitals together count only once; you do not have to pay it anew. For inpatients transferred down from a higher-level hospital to primary care, no separate deductible is set within the same disease cycle
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2026). Several Measures on Accelerating the Construction of Hierarchical Diagnosis and Treatment. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: "For insured patients in the pooling region referred upward through tiered referral from primary care health institutions, the inpatient deductible at the higher-level hospital may be calculated continuously; for inpatients transferred down from higher-level hospitals to primary care health institutions, no separate inpatient deductible is set within the same disease cycle."
- Note: Going directly to a tertiary hospital for inpatient care, then transferring back to primary care for recovery, may mean paying the deductible twice; through referral it is paid once. Referral rules are set separately by each province, city, and medical consortium; ask at the community before going

### 2. Within the same pooling region (medical insurance participation location), the lower the level, the higher the reimbursement ratio, with a gap of about 10 percentage points
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is resisting the urge to run to the big hospital
- Plain-language summary: For the same inpatient cost, the lower the hospital level, the more is reimbursed. Localities are to widen the tiers, with about a 10-percentage-point gap per level upward. Going to a big hospital for what could be handled at the community means paying about 10% more out of pocket for the same cost. Newly added medical insurance funds also tilt toward primary care
- Benefit: The document requires localities to widen the inpatient reimbursement levels across different-tier medical institutions, in principle by about 10 percentage points per level. At this gap, for the same inpatient cost, every level upward you go means paying about 10% more out of pocket. Annual newly added medical insurance funds also tilt toward primary care
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2026). Several Measures on Accelerating the Construction of Hierarchical Diagnosis and Treatment. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: "Appropriately widen the inpatient reimbursement levels for insured persons across different-tier medical institutions in the pooling region, in principle a gap of about 10 percentage points per level for inpatient reimbursement ratios at medical and health institutions in the pooling region." "Localities with the conditions may, in line with the hierarchical diagnosis and treatment orientation, appropriately widen the outpatient reimbursement levels for insured persons across different-tier medical and health institutions."
- Note: For the same inpatient cost, the difference in out-of-pocket between primary care and a tertiary hospital may be in the thousands of yuan. For illnesses that can be handled at the primary level, do not go upward; this does not mean every illness should be handled at the community level

### 3. To go to a big hospital, go through primary care referral or the hospital's referral center; do not look for ticket scalpers
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. One more referral procedure
- Plain-language summary: Big hospitals must set aside a portion of appointment slots and beds specifically for those referred from primary care. Hospitals must also set up a referral center, or designate a fixed department to manage referrals, with full coverage by 2027. Not getting an appointment does not mean there is no path. Scalper-sold appointments, besides being expensive, may also send you to the wrong department
- Benefit: The lead hospital of a tight-form medical consortium must reserve a certain proportion of appointment slots and beds for primary care, and must promptly receive patients referred from primary care. A tight-form medical consortium is a group of several hospitals and community health institutions bound together for collaboration. So appointments and beds referred upward from the community are separate, and do not compete with those lining up in the lobby. Medical institutions must set up referral centers or designate a fixed department; full coverage by 2027
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2026). Several Measures on Accelerating the Construction of Hierarchical Diagnosis and Treatment. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: "The lead hospital must reserve a certain proportion of appointment slots and beds for primary care health institutions, and promptly receive patients referred upward." "Medical institutions must strengthen the unified management of referral services, establish referral centers or designate a fixed department to assume patient referral service work, with full coverage by 2027."
- Note: Reserved appointment slots are specifically for referred patients; if you cannot get an appointment, go through referral. Looking for scalpers, besides being expensive, may also land you in the wrong department. If you cannot find the referral center, call the hospital's service phone

### 4. Before seeking cross-province medical care, ask locally first; necessity must in principle be assessed by a deputy chief physician or above
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. One more question before departure
- Plain-language summary: Whether to seek care elsewhere is in principle assessed by a deputy chief physician or above at a secondary or tertiary hospital. The reimbursement level for temporary outside medical care will differ from that at the same-tier institution in your participation location — see local rules. Asking locally before departure is better than arriving outside and finding it cannot be reimbursed
- Benefit: The necessity of cross-pooling-region and cross-province medical care is in principle assessed by a deputy chief physician or above at a secondary or tertiary hospital. Cross-pooling-region means going outside your medical insurance participation location. Whether to seek care elsewhere is decided by a physician of sufficient rank. The reimbursement level for cross-province temporary outside medical care maintains a reasonable differential from the same-tier institution in the participation location. For the same illness treated outside, the money reimbursed is less than at home
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2026). Several Measures on Accelerating the Construction of Hierarchical Diagnosis and Treatment. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: "In principle, the necessity of cross-pooling-region and cross-province medical care for a patient is assessed by a deputy chief physician or above at a secondary or tertiary hospital." "Further strengthen the management service of cross-region direct settlement of medical care; the reimbursement level for cross-province temporary outside medical care maintains a reasonable differential from the reimbursement level at the same-tier medical institution in the participation location."
- Note: "Temporary outside medical care" and "long-term cross-region residence" are two different filing types, with different reimbursement levels. How to file, and which categories of disease can be directly settled, see Section 16. Do not go outside first and then think about reimbursement

### 5. Stop treating the tertiary hospital as a prescription refill point; stable-phase chronic disease returns to primary care
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is breaking the "trust only the big hospital" habit
- Plain-language summary: Tertiary hospitals will henceforth mainly handle acute critical illness and difficult cases. Common-disease return visits and stable-phase chronic-disease ordinary outpatient services will be gradually reduced. The same team of experts' ordinary outpatient services will be extended to the community. Continuing to treat the big hospital as a prescription refill point will make it harder and harder to get an appointment
- Benefit: Tertiary hospitals are to focus on acute critical illness and difficult complex disease, gradually reducing ordinary outpatient services for common-disease return visits and chronic diseases with clear diagnoses and stable conditions. The slots for such ordinary outpatient services will be reduced bit by bit. Higher-level hospitals will extend their expert teams' ordinary outpatient services to primary care, so the same experts' ordinary outpatient services will be moved to the community
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2026). Several Measures on Accelerating the Construction of Hierarchical Diagnosis and Treatment. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202604/content_7065031.htm>: "Tertiary hospitals are to focus on acute critical illness and difficult complex disease, strengthen referral consultation and inpatient services, and gradually reduce ordinary outpatient services such as common-disease return visits and chronic diseases with clear diagnoses and stable conditions." "Within tight-form medical consortiums, higher-level hospitals must open clinics for common and chronic diseases such as hypertension, diabetes, and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease at primary care health institutions, providing diagnosis, treatment, and health management services, and extending expert-team ordinary outpatient services to primary care health institutions."
- Note: This is a policy direction, not a recommendation. Big-hospital ordinary outpatient appointments will become increasingly hard to get. Stable-phase chronic disease at the primary level can yield a 12-week prescription per visit; see Section 16

### 6. After each visit, keep your own copy of the medical record, examination reports, and imaging
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Hospitals may charge a cost-recovery fee for copying in accordance with regulations, and the fee standard must be public. The hard part is remembering to copy after each visit
- Plain-language summary: Reviewing and copying your medical record is your legal right. Outpatient records, admission records, lab slips, imaging, surgical and anesthesia records, pathology materials, and fee schedules can all be copied. Hospitals have a dedicated window for this; only a cost-recovery fee may be charged. Changing hospitals, getting a second opinion, filing for commercial insurance, or in the event of a dispute all depend on this stack of materials
- Benefit: The regulation explicitly lists reviewing and copying medical records as a patient's right. The scope covers outpatient records, admission records, lab slips (test reports), medical imaging, surgical and anesthesia records, pathology materials, medical costs, and all other materials specified by the health authority under the State Council. Changing hospitals, getting a second opinion from another doctor, filing for commercial insurance, or in the event of a dispute — all depend on these
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2018). Regulation on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes (State Council Order No. 701), Article 16: "The patient has the right to review and copy their outpatient records, admission records, temperature charts, physician order sheets, lab slips (test reports), medical imaging examination materials, special examination consent forms, surgical consent forms, surgical and anesthesia records, pathology materials, nursing records, medical costs, and all other materials belonging to the medical record as specified by the health authority under the State Council." "Where a patient requests to copy medical record materials, the medical institution shall provide copying services and stamp a certifying mark on the copied medical record materials... a cost-recovery fee may be charged, and the fee standard shall be made public." "Where the patient has died, their close relatives may, in accordance with this regulation, review and copy the medical record materials." Article 15: "No unit or individual may tamper with, forge, conceal, destroy, or seize medical record materials." <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>; National Health and Family Planning Commission and State Administration of Traditional Chinese Medicine (2013). Provisions on the Administration of Medical Records in Medical Institutions (2013 edition): Medical institutions "shall designate a department or full-time or part-time personnel responsible for receiving applications for copying medical record materials," with copying done in the presence of the applicant and stamped with a certifying mark. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2600084.htm>
- Note: There is a dedicated window for copying; you do not need to plead with the doctor. For the list of items to copy, just recite Article 16 of the Regulation on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes. First, outpatient records, admission records, temperature charts, physician order sheets, lab slips, imaging materials. Then, surgical and anesthesia records, pathology materials, nursing records, and fee schedules. After receiving them, file them in chronological order in a folder. The most important inside are the discharge summary, surgical records, pathology reports, and key imaging. For chronic disease, keep each visit's indicators in the same notebook; see Section 16

### 7. If you have doubts about diagnosis or treatment, request on the spot that the medical record be sealed; both parties present, a list made, and one copy each
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Make the sealing request to the hospital, and wait for both parties to be present and complete the procedures. The hard part is saying this sentence in front of the doctor
- Plain-language summary: If you have doubts about treatment, you may request that the medical record be sealed. Sealing requires both medical staff and patient to be present, with a list made, signed and stamped, and each party holding one copy. If the medical record is not yet complete, the completed portion is sealed first. If you suspect the problem arose from IV fluids, blood transfusion, injection, or medication, the bag of fluid, the vial of medication, and the IV tubing must also be sealed together
- Benefit: The regulation provides that "where a medical dispute requires the sealing or unsealing of medical record materials, it shall be done in the presence of both the medical staff and the patient." What is sealed may be originals or copies, kept by the medical institution. Where a medical record is not yet complete and must be sealed, the completed portion is sealed first, and the subsequently completed portion is sealed afterward. The medical institution shall prepare a sealing list for the sealed medical record, signed or stamped by both medical staff and patient, with each party holding one copy. Where adverse consequences are suspected to have been caused by IV fluids, blood transfusion, injection, or medication, both medical staff and patient shall jointly seal and unseal the physical objects on site. Where testing is required, both parties shall jointly entrust an institution with testing qualifications. Where the parties cannot jointly entrust, the county-level people's government health authority at the medical institution's location designates one. Where adverse consequences are suspected to have been caused by blood transfusion and blood must be sealed, the medical institution shall notify the blood station to send personnel to the scene. There is also a hard constraint: "No unit or individual may tamper with, forge, conceal, destroy, or seize medical record materials." After the dispute is resolved following sealing, or where no further request for resolution is made within 3 years, the medical institution may unseal on its own (nationwide, effective October 1, 2018)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2018). Regulation on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes (State Council Order No. 701, Articles 15, 24, and 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Note: Sealing and copying are two different things. Copying is what you should do routinely; see Entry 6 (keep your own copy of medical records and imaging). Sealing fixes the evidence at the time of a dispute, guarding against after-the-fact writing and modification. If you suspect the problem arose from IV fluids, blood transfusion, or medication, in addition to the medical record, also seal together that bag of fluid, that vial of medication, and the IV tubing — do not let anyone take them away and throw them out. Medical institutions must publish in a prominent location the channels, procedures, and contact information for resolving medical disputes, and must set up a unified complaint management department. Approach that department first, do not argue with the on-duty doctor

### 8. For acute serious illness or injury, go straight to the emergency triage desk; do not queue at the registration window
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. Walk a few more steps inside to find the triage desk. State the most life-threatening symptom in one sentence
- Plain-language summary: Emergency is not first-come, first-served; it is ranked by severity in four tiers. The place that assigns the tier is the triage desk at the emergency entrance, not the registration window. Without triage, you have no tier in the system; no matter how serious your injury, you can only inch forward in the queue. On arrival, first state the most dangerous one: major bleeding, cannot catch breath, altered consciousness, an object embedded in the body, or crushing chest pain
- Benefit: The 2024 edition of the National Health Commission's emergency medical quality control indicators requires emergency triage to "classify patients into four tiers by severity." "Tier I: critical patients, requiring immediate rescue." "Tier II: urgent serious patients, often with assessment and rescue conducted simultaneously." "Tier III: urgent patients, requiring diagnosis and treatment within a short time." "Tier IV: non-urgent or sub-acute patients, seen in order in the ordinary diagnosis and treatment area." Triage is when, on entry to emergency, a dedicated person first assesses severity and sets the order of being seen. Beijing's implementation standard specifies the response requirements for each tier. Tier 1 "immediate assessment and rescue, place the patient in the resuscitation room or rescue room." Tier 2 "immediately monitor vital signs, receive rescue within 10 minutes, place the patient in the rescue room." Tier 3 "diagnosis and treatment prioritized over tier 4 patients, place the patient in the ordinary diagnosis and treatment area to wait; if the wait exceeds 30 minutes, reassess." Tier 4 "seen in order; absent a change in condition, the wait may be long; if the wait exceeds 4 hours, reassess." Triage must "adhere to the principle of priority for acute critical serious illness," and a triage area must be set up in a prominent location at the emergency department entrance (four-tier standard is nationwide; response times are Beijing standards)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: General Office of the National Health Commission (2024). Notice on Issuing Quality Control Indicators for Emergency Medicine and 5 Other Specialties (2024 Edition) (Guo Wei Ban Yi Zheng Han [2024] No. 150), Attachment 1, Indicator 4 "Emergency Tiered Triage Execution Rate." <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202405/content_6951260.htm>; Beijing Municipal Health Commission (2019). Beijing Work Plan for Strengthening Emergency Triage Classification and its attachment Beijing Hospital Emergency Triage Classification Standards (Trial): "Adhere to the principle of priority for acute critical serious illness. In accordance with the severity of the patient's condition, follow the principle of severe to mild, rapidly changing to relatively stable, and reasonably arrange the order of patient visits." "Set up an emergency triage area in a prominent location at the emergency department entrance." <http://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/ylws/201912/t20191216_1242338.html>
- Note: The four-tier division is unified nationwide. How quickly each tier must be handled is set by each province and city; this Beijing version is one that can be verified word for word. Tiers are based on indicators such as heart rate, blood pressure, and oxygen saturation, and on symptoms, not on how much pain you feel you are in. Hard indicators for tier 1 include the following. First, heart rate above 180 beats per minute, or below 40. Second, systolic blood pressure below 70 mmHg (systolic pressure is the high number in blood pressure). Third, oxygen saturation below 80%. Fourth, shock, confirmed myocardial infarction, or acute altered consciousness. If you feel triage has under-tiered you, supplement by clearly stating the most dangerous symptom. If your condition changes while waiting, immediately return to the triage desk for reassessment. In truly critical moments, calling 120 is faster than going yourself; the pre-hospital physician will first assign a tier by the same standards, and notify the hospital in advance to prepare for reception (see Section 13)

### 9. Without money, without documents, or unable to say who you are — emergency must still treat you first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money
- Plain-language summary: The state has a disease emergency relief fund that pays the emergency care costs of two types of people: those whose identity cannot be determined, and those who cannot pay. Hospitals may not refuse or delay rescue, and emergency centers may not decline to dispatch over money. So do not stay home enduring because of no money, and do not fear calling an ambulance because of debt. This fund covers only emergency care; subsequent inpatient care still goes through medical insurance and medical assistance
- Benefit: The disease emergency relief system's relief targets are "patients who suffer acute critical serious illness or injury in China and require emergency care but whose identity is unclear or who are unable to pay the corresponding costs." The fund pays two types of costs: "emergency care costs incurred by patients whose identity cannot be determined," and "emergency care costs owed by patients whose identity is clear but who are unable to pay." The document also specifies "all types of medical institutions at all levels and their staff must timely and effectively rescue patients with acute critical serious illness or injury, and may not refuse, shirk, or delay treatment for any reason." Pre-hospital emergency care also has rules: the nationwide pre-hospital medical emergency call number is "120." Emergency centers (stations) "may not refuse, shirk, or delay pre-hospital medical emergency services for command and dispatch reasons" and "may not refuse or delay pre-hospital medical emergency services for reasons of cost." Transport follows "the principles of nearest, most urgent, meeting professional needs, and taking into account patient wishes" (nationwide, since 2013)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: General Office of the State Council (2013). Guiding Opinions on Establishing a Disease Emergency Relief System (Guo Ban Fa [2013] No. 15). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2013-03/01/content_6069.htm>; National Health and Family Planning Commission (2014). Administrative Measures for Pre-Hospital Medical Emergency Care (Commission Order No. 3, Articles 13, 22, 23, and 25). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2014/content_2580977.htm>
- Note: The fund covers only the "emergency care cost" portion, and treatment comes first; afterward the medical institution applies to the fund — you do not need to collect money at the window. Those with medical insurance first go through medical insurance as usual. The application process and limits vary by province. In the transport principles, "nearest, most urgent" precedes "patient wishes," so the ambulance may not take you to the specific hospital you named; in critical moments, the nearest capable facility prevails. Where the remaining out-of-pocket portion is still heavy, go through medical assistance; see Section 7, Entry 10 (medical assistance)

### 10. Disability assessment should be done after treatment concludes; doing it too early will yield a low grade
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Assessment fee from a few hundred to two thousand yuan, usually advanced by the party applying. Must wait until treatment concludes, or clinical effects stabilize — usually 3 to 6 months after injury, sometimes longer. The hard part is resisting the urge to settle it before the injury has fully healed
- Plain-language summary: How much compensation is paid depends mainly on the grade; the grade depends on how much functional impairment remains after treatment. The standard specifies that assessment is to be done after treatment concludes or clinical effects stabilize. Going for assessment before the bone has healed or before the steel plate has been removed rates the condition at that moment, which means you have depressed your own grade. Ordinary fractures that have healed without affecting function are not rated
- Benefit: The Classification of Disability Caused by Human Injury provides that the timing of assessment "shall be conducted after treatment of the primary injury and its definitely associated complications has concluded or clinical treatment effects have stabilized." The standard "divides disability caused by human injury into 10 grades, from grade 1 (100% disability rate) to grade 10 (10% disability rate), with each grade differing by 10% in disability rate." The basis for judgment is "the degree of structural damage to the body's tissues and organs, functional impairment, and dependence on medical and nursing care, with appropriate consideration of the impact on social interaction and psychological factors caused by the disability." Grade 10 items related to fractures include the following. First, "compression fracture of one vertebral body (compression degree reaching 1/3) or comminuted fracture." Second, "fractures at two or more sites of the pelvis or comminuted fracture, with malunion." Third, "loss of function of any major joint of the four limbs (except the ankle joint) by 25% or more." Disability at the same site and of the same nature may not be calculated repeatedly. Where two or more disabilities occur, the grades shall be stated separately (nationwide, effective January 1, 2017)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Supreme People's Court, Supreme People's Procuratorate, Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of State Security, and Ministry of Justice (2016). Announcement on the Release of the Classification of Disability Caused by Human Injury (released April 18, 2016, effective January 1, 2017), Standard Articles 4.2, 4.4, 4.5, 5.10.6, and 6.2. <https://www.moj.gov.cn/pub/sfbgw/zwxxgk/fdzdgknr/fdzdgknrtzwj/201908/P020210316701681025917.pdf>
- Note: The three disability assessment systems do not interchange; do not confuse them. For assault, traffic accidents, and other personal injury, the judicial assessment under the Classification of Disability Caused by Human Injury applies, used to calculate disability compensation (see Section 9). Workplace injury goes through labor capacity assessment, using a separate ten-grade disability scale, governing workplace injury benefits (see Section 19). To enjoy the policy benefits available to disabled persons, a separate disability certificate (残疾人证) must be obtained (see Entry 11, disability certificate). Before assessment, gather medical records, surgical records, and follow-up imaging (see Entry 6, keep your own copy of medical records and imaging) — missing imaging is the most common cause of a depressed grade. When this injury is mixed with a pre-existing condition, the assessment opinion must also state how much of the disability outcome this injury accounts for. This column directly affects how much compensation is paid

### 11. If functional impairment does remain after treatment, apply for a disability certificate at the county-level Disabled Persons' Federation at the place of household registration
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The certificate itself is free of cost-recovery fee. Assessment and photo fees are in principle paid by yourself; the financially struggling may apply for reduction or waiver. Bring your ID, hukou booklet, and three 2-inch photos with white background to the county-level Disabled Persons' Federation at the place of household registration. After assessment passes, public posting is made in the village (community) for 5 working days, and the federation completes review within 10 working days. The hard part is making these trips and waiting through this period
- Plain-language summary: The disability certificate is the threshold for receiving disability benefits, not a proof of injury. It is divided into seven categories — visual, hearing, speech, physical, intellectual, mental, and multiple — each grades 1 to 4. The procedure is application, acceptance, assessment at a designated institution, public posting, review and approval, and issuance, with a 10-year validity, free renewal on expiry. If you do not qualify, you do not qualify — this is not something connections can secure
- Benefit: The disability certificate "is the legal credential for recognizing disabled persons and their disability category and grade, and is an important basis for disabled persons to lawfully enjoy preferential policies of the state and local governments." The assessment standard is the national standard Classification and Grading of Disability (GB/T 26341-2010). Issuance adheres to voluntary application and territorial management. The seven categories of disabled persons — visual, hearing, speech, physical, intellectual, mental, and multiple — may all apply, with grades 1 to 4. The county-level Disabled Persons' Federation is responsible for accepting applications and issuing, based on the assessment conclusions of hospitals or professional institutions designated by the province. Where the assessment conclusion meets the standard, public posting is made in the village (community) for five working days, and the county-level federation "completes review within ten working days." "No cost-recovery fee is charged for issuing a disability certificate." Assessment and photo fees are "in principle borne by the applicant; localities with the conditions may subsidize from the local finance, and for applicants with special difficulties the relevant departments shall be coordinated to provide reduction or waiver." The certificate is valid for ten years, with free renewal on expiry. Where the disability category or grade changes, re-assessment may be applied for. Where there is disagreement with the assessment conclusion, re-assessment may be applied for at the prefecture-level federation within ten working days. Where disagreement remains, the provincial disability assessment expert committee issues the final conclusion (nationwide, effective January 1, 2018)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: China Disabled Persons' Federation and National Health and Family Planning Commission (2017). Administrative Measures for the Disability Certificate of the People's Republic of China (released May 27, 2017, effective January 1, 2018; Articles 2, 3, 5, 7, 9, 17, 19, 22, and 25). <https://www.bdpf.org.cn/cms68/web1459/subject/n1/n1459/n1508/n1509/n1512/c68465/content.html>
- Note: Calculate the ledger before deciding whether to apply. The certificate itself does not directly pay money. It connects to the two subsidies for disabled persons: those in dibao households receive a living subsidy, and those at grades 1 and 2 requiring long-term care receive a nursing subsidy; see Section 7, Entry 8 (two subsidies). The certificate also connects to local rehabilitation, assistive devices, tax, employment, and transportation benefits. These standards vary widely by province; check clearly on the local Disabled Persons' Federation website before deciding whether to make the trip. It is one of three separate systems alongside the judicial disability grade and the labor capacity assessment for workplace injury, and they do not substitute for each other (see Entry 10, disability assessment should be done after treatment concludes). The certificate is for personal use only and may not be lent. Where the disability no longer meets the standard, the certificate is canceled, and re-application is not allowed within one year of cancellation. Multiple disabilities are graded by the most severe category

### 12. Thank the doctor who saved you through a thank-you letter, banner, or satisfaction survey — not through red envelopes: what the code prohibits is money and goods, not gratitude
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Write a thank-you letter, or fill out one satisfaction survey, ten-odd minutes
- Plain-language summary: Stuffing money in advance does not buy priority; appointment slots, beds, scarce drugs, and surgical scheduling are all prohibited from being exchanged for benefits. Wanting to thank the doctor who saved your life afterward is allowed, but do not give cigarettes, alcohol, or red envelopes — accepting them is a violation. Three ways of thanking that genuinely reach the doctor: fill out one satisfaction survey in earnest, write a thank-you letter naming the doctor or present a banner, and follow the doctor's orders at follow-up
- Benefit: The Nine Standards for Clean Conduct of Medical Institution Staff (Guo Wei Yi Fa [2021] No. 41), item 7: "Maintain the order of diagnosis and treatment, do not undermine fairness in seeking care. Adhere to the principle of equality, jointly build a fair medical care environment. Strictly prohibited is the use of medical resources such as appointment slots, beds, scarce drugs and consumables, or diagnosis and treatment arrangements such as examinations and surgeries to receive benefits or to profit at public expense." Item 8: "Jointly build a harmonious relationship, do not accept 'red envelopes' from the patient side. Abide by medical ethics, strictly self-discipline." The same item also specifies "strictly prohibited from soliciting or accepting gifts, gift money, shopping cards, marketable securities, equity, and other financial products from patients and their relatives." Patient-side paid dinners and entertainment likewise: "strictly prohibited from attending banquets, tourism, fitness, entertainment, and other activity arrangements arranged, organized, or paid for by them." The document also requires medical institutions to assume primary responsibility and conduct oversight, "resolutely investigate and punish violations." The satisfaction survey is incorporated into the performance evaluation of tertiary public hospitals. The basis is the General Office of the State Council's Opinions on Strengthening the Performance Evaluation of Tertiary Public Hospitals (Guo Ban Fa [2019] No. 4). The document states: "The performance evaluation indicator system for tertiary public hospitals consists of indicators in four aspects: medical quality, operational efficiency, sustainable development, and satisfaction evaluation." The document also states: "Hospital satisfaction consists of two parts: patient satisfaction and medical staff satisfaction..." It continues: "through outpatient patient, inpatient patient, and medical staff satisfaction evaluation, measure patient sense of gain and medical staff motivation." This evaluation also has a nationwide unified platform: "The state establishes a satisfaction management system for public hospitals..." The timeline is: "By the end of March 2019, all tertiary public hospitals nationwide were included in the National Health Commission's satisfaction survey platform. Localities shall apply the National Health Commission's satisfaction survey platform and incorporate the survey results into the performance evaluation of tertiary public hospitals." Hospitals must also manage complaints. The basis is the Administrative Measures for Medical Institution Complaints (National Health Commission Order No. 3, effective 2019), Article 2. Complaint management refers to the patient "raising opinions, suggestions, or complaint requests" regarding medical service conduct, medical management, and medical quality and safety. Hospitals are responsible for investigation, handling, and feedback. "Medical institutions at or above the secondary level shall set up a doctor-patient relationship office or designate a department (collectively, the complaint management department) to assume complaint management work in a unified manner." The responsibilities of the complaint management department include "uniformly accepting complaints, investigating and verifying complaint items, proposing handling opinions, and promptly replying to patients." Medical institutions "shall encourage staff to actively collect patient opinions and suggestions on medical services, medical quality and safety, and other aspects" (nationwide)

- Note: The beneficiary is yourself, and also the person who saved you. After an all-night rescue, wanting to thank is human nature — you may thank, only the form needs to change. Red envelopes and cigarettes-alcohol shift the risk to the other party; thank-you letters, banners, and satisfaction surveys are the means recognized by the system. Where you have already given and the other party returns it, do not read it as displeasure at the small amount or posturing — that is what they are required to do by regulation. Also distinguish two things: giving a doctor a red envelope is governed by industry discipline and the hospital; giving case-handlers or law enforcement personnel money or goods is the crime of bribery under the criminal law — these two are not the same order of magnitude; the latter, see Section 8, Entry 40. Thank-you letters and banners are generally accepted by hospitals, but "they will be entered into individual and department records" is an experiential statement with no documentary basis. What has documentary basis is the incorporation of satisfaction survey results into the performance evaluation of tertiary public hospitals. For doubts about diagnosis and treatment itself, request on the spot that the medical record be sealed (see Entry 7 in this section, seal the medical record), and retain the medical record and imaging (Entry 6, keep your own copy of medical records and imaging)
