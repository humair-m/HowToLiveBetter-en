# 1. Don't Die Early

This section covers three things: deaths from accidents and injuries like car crashes, fires, and poisoning; a small number of vaccines and screenings that have the hardest evidence; and what bodily signals should prompt you to go get checked. The numbers in the Benefit column come from randomized trials, studies that tracked large groups of people for many years, or official statistics. The numbers in the Cost column are the author's rough estimates based on market prices and are only for comparison with one another — don't cite them.

### 1. Wear a seat belt, in both the front and back seats
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Adds 2 seconds each time you get in the car.
- Plain-language summary: Buckle up in the front seat and your chance of being killed in a crash drops by about half. For those in the front seat of a light truck, it drops by about 60%. Of the people who died in cars in the United States in 2022 and for whom belt use could be determined, half were unbuckled.
- Benefit: The U.S. traffic safety authority (NHTSA) estimates that wearing a seat belt reduces the risk of fatal injury for front-seat occupants of passenger cars by 45%. For front-seat occupants of light trucks it drops by 60%. In 2022, among people who died in passenger cars in the U.S. and for whom belt use could be determined, 50% were unbuckled. The World Health Organization (WHO) uses the metric that seat belts reduce the risk of death for vehicle occupants by up to 50%. WHO estimates 248,099 road traffic deaths in China in 2021, equivalent to 17.4 deaths per 100,000 people.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: NHTSA (2024). Occupant Protection in Passenger Vehicles: 2022 Data (DOT HS 813 573). <https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813573> ; WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries> ; WHO GHO RS_196/RS_198 (China, 2021). <https://ghoapi.azureedge.net/api/RS_196?$filter=SpatialDim%20eq%20%27CHN%27>
- Note: Buckle up in the back too. In the same report, 60% of those killed in the second row were unbuckled. The 45% and 60% figures are estimates computed by the U.S. traffic safety authority (NHTSA) from accident databases (citing Kahane 2015), not from randomized trials. WHO's China death figure is an estimate that is several times higher than the figure registered by public security traffic management. The two statistics cover different scopes; here only the WHO figure is used.

### 2. Wear a helmet when riding a motorcycle or e-bike, and fasten the strap
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A helmet costs 100 to 300 yuan. Five seconds to put on each time.
- Plain-language summary: Wear a helmet and fasten the strap, and a motorcycle rider's chance of dying in a crash drops by about 40%. Head injury drops by about 70%. The strap has to be tight — a helmet perched loosely on the head does not count as worn.
- Benefit: Cochrane combined multiple studies. Motorcycle riders wearing helmets had a 42% lower risk of death. Original value OR 0.58, 95% CI 0.50 to 0.68. Head injury dropped 69% (OR 0.31, 95% CI 0.25 to 0.38).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Liu BC et al. (2008). Helmets for preventing injury in motorcycle riders. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD004333.pub3>
- Note: The helmet strap has to be fastened — hanging it on the handlebar does not count. The data above are from motorcycles. Electric bicycles have no direct studies; the figures are inferred because the way the head is injured in a crash is the same.

### 3. Install smoke alarms; if you burn coal or use gas for heating indoors in winter, also install a carbon monoxide alarm
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A smoke alarm is 30 to 100 yuan. A carbon monoxide alarm is 50 to 150 yuan. Replace the batteries once a year after installation.
- Plain-language summary: A working smoke alarm can cut the chance of dying in a residential fire by about 60%. When a fire starts, four in ten people are asleep — the nose alone will not wake them. If you burn coal or use gas indoors in winter, add a carbon monoxide alarm. In China in 2018, 11,500 people died of carbon monoxide poisoning, and in the three winter months 70% died at home.
- Benefit: A North Carolina study compared households that had a fire with those that did not. Households with a working smoke alarm had about a 60% lower risk of death. Original value OR 0.39, 95% CI 0.18 to 0.83. In the U.S. from 2018 to 2020, of fatal fires in occupied residences, 24% had no smoke alarm, and in 41% the people were asleep when the fire started. In 2018, China reported 11,523 carbon monoxide poisoning deaths. Of these deaths, 72.59% in December, 67.42% in January, and 66.48% in February occurred at home.
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Marshall SW et al. (1998). Fatal residential fires: who dies and who survives? JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.279.20.1633> ; USFA (2022). Fatal Fires in Residential Buildings (2018-2020), Topical Fire Report Series 22(2). <https://www.usfa.fema.gov/downloads/pdf/statistics/v22i2.pdf> ; You J et al. (2020). Number of Deaths due to Carbon Monoxide Poisoning by Month and by Place of Death — China, 2018. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2020.008>
- Note: Graded B because the smoke-alarm side has only one study, which compares households that had a fire with those that did not — not a randomized trial. The carbon monoxide alarm itself has no study showing reduced mortality. The benefit here is inferred from the fact that poisoning deaths concentrate in winter and at home.

### 4. Replace gas hoses and stoves when they expire, don't modify pipes yourself, and feel free to reject gas company door-to-door sales pitches
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A compliant gas hose is a few dozen yuan. Stoves are replaced every few years. While replacing, glance at whether it has expired.
- Plain-language summary: A compliant hose costs a few dozen yuan. Replacing it on time is a user obligation written into the Regulations on the Administration of Urban Gas. Modifying pipes yourself or removing the gas meter is explicitly prohibited by the same regulations. The same regulations also state that gas companies cannot require you to buy their designated products and services. Door-to-door pitches saying "you must replace" can be refused outright.
- Benefit: The regulations make "promptly replacing gas combustion appliances and connecting hoses that are nationally ordered to be eliminated or have reached the end of their service life" a user obligation: when stoves and hoses reach the end of their service life or are phased out by the state, you must replace them. The regulations also list "unauthorized installation, modification, or removal of indoor gas facilities and gas metering devices" as a prohibited act — modifying pipes yourself or removing the gas meter falls under this. The regulations also make clear that gas operators may not require users to buy their designated products, nor require users to accept the services they provide.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2010). Regulations on the Administration of Urban Gas (State Council Decree No. 583), Article 27: "Gas users shall abide by safe gas-use rules, use qualified gas combustion appliances and cylinders, and promptly replace gas combustion appliances, connecting hoses, and other items that are nationally ordered to be eliminated or have reached the end of their service life"; Article 28 prohibits "1) unauthorized operation of public gas valves", "2) using gas pipes as load-bearing brackets or grounding leads", "4) unauthorized installation, modification, or removal of indoor gas facilities and gas metering devices"; Article 20: gas operators may not "6) require gas users to purchase their designated products or accept their provided services"; Article 29: users may complain to gas management and other departments about fees and services, "the relevant departments shall process the complaint within 15 working days of receipt." <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2010/content_1758214.htm>
- Note: Some people knock on doors in the name of "safety inspection" and then push automatic-closing valves and alarms. Article 20, item 6 of the regulations forbids gas companies from requiring you to buy their designated products — you can refuse and you can complain. ### 5. Don't pick, don't buy, don't eat wild mushrooms — no "folk test" works
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is giving up a seasonal wild delicacy.
- Plain-language summary: In 2025 alone, there were 828 mushroom poisoning incidents nationwide, affecting 2,165 people, with 13 deaths. That year, 34 species previously unrecorded as poisonous were identified. The silver needle test, the "if bugs have eaten it, it's safe" test, and the "only brightly colored ones are poisonous" claim are all false.
- Benefit: China CDC investigated 828 mushroom poisoning incidents in 2025, involving 2,165 people and 13 deaths. Case fatality rate 0.6%, meaning 0.6% of those poisoned died. From 2019 to 2024 there were 276 to 676 incidents per year, with case fatality rates between 0.87% and 2.86%. In 2025 alone, 138 poisonous mushroom species were identified, of which 34 were first recorded in China as causing poisoning.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Mushroom Poisoning Outbreaks — China, 2025. China CDC Weekly (2026): "In 2025, China CDC investigated 828 mushroom poisoning incidents across 27 provincial-level administrative divisions (PLADs), affecting 2,165 individuals and causing 13 deaths - a case fatality rate of 0.6%, the lowest of the past six years. In total, 138 poisonous mushroom species were identified, including 34 newly recorded in poisoning incidents in China." "From 2019 to 2024, the annual number of incidents ranged from 276 to 676, and the case fatality rate ranged from 0.87% to 2.86%." <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2026.120>; the same journal's 2024 annual report <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2025.106>
- Note: If you have eaten the wrong mushroom, induce vomiting immediately, then bring the remaining mushrooms or photos to the hospital. Mushroom poisoning comes in several types, and identifying which mushroom it was directly determines how the doctor treats it. Every year new species are recorded as poisonous, so the premise that "locals can tell them apart" does not hold. The silver needle test, boiling with garlic, the "if bugs have eaten it, it's safe" test, and the "only brightly colored ones are poisonous" claim are all false.

### 6. Do not bring e-bikes into hallways or elevators, and do not charge them indoors
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is always parking at the ground-floor charging point — a few dozen meters more of walking each time.
- Plain-language summary: Pushing an e-bike into a hallway or elevator, or charging it indoors, is explicitly prohibited by the Ministry of Emergency Management's Provisions on Fire Safety Management of Tall Civil Buildings. Those who fail to correct the violation are fined 500 to 1,000 yuan for individuals and 2,000 to 10,000 yuan for businesses. The fine is the small matter; the serious one is that such a fire burns right on your only escape route.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Emergency Management's provisions explicitly state: "Parking electric bicycles or charging electric bicycles in the public lobbies, evacuation routes, stairwells, or safety exits of tall civil buildings is prohibited." Lobbies, corridors, stairwells, and safety exits — no parking, no charging at any of these. Those who refuse to correct the violation are fined 500 to 1,000 yuan for individuals and units not doing business, and 2,000 to 10,000 yuan for businesses.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ministry of Emergency Management (2021). Provisions on Fire Safety Management of Tall Civil Buildings (Ministry of Emergency Management Decree No. 5, Articles 37 and 47). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-08/01/content_5628815.htm>
- Note: Don't buy modified batteries, don't use no-brand chargers, don't leave it charging all night unattended. Once a lithium battery catches fire, it can fully ignite within tens of seconds while releasing highly toxic smoke. ### 7. Measure your blood pressure; if it's high, take medicine and bring it down to target
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: An electronic blood pressure monitor is 100 to 200 yuan. One measurement takes 1 minute. Most blood pressure medicines cost a few yuan to a few dozen yuan per month. The hard part is continuing to take it even without symptoms.
- Plain-language summary: For every 10 mmHg reduction in blood pressure, the probability of a major cardiovascular event drops by about 20%, stroke drops 27%, heart failure drops 28%, and the death rate drops 13%. Among Chinese people aged 35 to 75, 44.7% have high blood pressure, and only 7.2% have it under control.
- Benefit: A pooled analysis of 123 trials and over 610,000 people. For every 10 mmHg reduction in systolic pressure, major cardiovascular events drop by about 20%. Original value RR 0.80, 95% CI 0.77 to 0.83. Systolic pressure is the higher of the two blood pressure numbers. Stroke drops about 27% (RR 0.73). Heart failure drops about 28% (RR 0.72). Total mortality drops 13% (RR 0.87, 95% CI 0.84 to 0.91). China screened 1.7 million people aged 35 to 75: 44.7% had high blood pressure. Of those, 44.7% knew it, 30.1% were being treated, and only 7.2% had it under control.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ettehad D et al. (2016). Blood pressure lowering for prevention of cardiovascular disease and death: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(15)01225-8> ; Lu J et al. (2017). Prevalence, awareness, treatment, and control of hypertension in China (China PEACE Million Persons Project). Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(17)32478-9>
- Note: First, go measure your blood pressure once. Fewer than 1 in 10 Chinese people with high blood pressure have it under control, so this one measurement is one of the most cost-effective actions in the entire book. There is still debate over what target to bring it down to (130 or 140), but the fact of "knowing you have high blood pressure and bringing it down" itself is not in dispute.

### 8. After age 35, if you are overweight, get a fasting blood glucose test once; even if normal, repeat every three years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Fasting blood glucose is a few dozen yuan. Glycated hemoglobin is a few dozen yuan. A single blood draw at the hospital is enough.
- Plain-language summary: For a few dozen yuan, a tube of blood can detect prediabetes when there are zero symptoms. The official U.S. screening recommendation is: people aged 35 to 70 who are overweight or obese should be tested. If the result is normal, retest every three years.
- Benefit: The U.S. Preventive Services Task Force is the official U.S. body that issues screening recommendations. Its current recommendation is: screen adults aged 35 to 70 who are overweight or obese for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes, even with no symptoms. Overweight means body mass index ≥25, obese means ≥30. This recommendation is grade B. It also states: "For adults with normal blood glucose, screening every 3 years is a reasonable approach."
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Screening for Prediabetes and Type 2 Diabetes. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/screening-for-prediabetes-and-type-2-diabetes>
- Note: A prediabetes diagnosis does not mean you have to take medication. A fair number of people can reverse it through lifestyle changes alone. Among Chinese adults, those who know they have diabetes have always been a minority; many only discover it after complications appear.

### 9. Don't speed and don't drink-drive
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Driving a bit slower adds a few minutes per trip. The hard part is drinking less at meals.
- Plain-language summary: For every 1% increase in average vehicle speed, the probability of a fatal accident rises by about 4%. There is no safe level for alcohol — "a little is fine" does not exist. The risk starts rising even at very low blood alcohol concentrations.
- Benefit: According to the World Health Organization (WHO): for every 1% increase in average vehicle speed, the risk of a fatal accident rises by 4%. The risk from drink-driving starts rising even at very low blood alcohol concentrations.
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries>
- Note: The 1% and 4% figures for speed and death are derived from crash models, not randomized trials. For drink-driving, WHO only gives a direction, not numbers. "How much you drink, how much the risk rises" — the original literature for this question has not been verified in this section.

### 10. Use child safety seats for children under 4, don't hold them in your arms
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One seat costs 300 to 2,000 yuan and lasts several years. Strapping the child in takes an extra minute each time.
- Plain-language summary: Infants under 1 year in safety seats have about a 70% lower chance of fatal injury in a crash. Children aged 1 to 4 have about half the risk. Holding them in your arms has no such effect — you cannot hold on during a hard brake.
- Benefit: The U.S. traffic safety authority (NHTSA) estimates that using a safety seat reduces the risk of fatal injury for infants under 1 year in passenger cars by 71%. For children aged 1 to 4, it drops 54%. The WHO figure is a 71% reduction in infant deaths.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: NHTSA (2024). Occupant Protection in Passenger Vehicles: 2022 Data (DOT HS 813 573). <https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813573> ; WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries>
- Note: Secure the seat according to the manual — installing it wrong is the same as not installing it. Under 1 year, install it rear-facing, with the child facing the rear of the car. The figures above are official U.S. estimates from accident data.

### 11. If you have small children at home, install window restrictors on windows and balconies — window screens do not count as protection
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A pair of window restrictors or child-safety locks costs a few dozen yuan. Ten minutes to install.
- Plain-language summary: A pair of restrictors costs a few dozen yuan. New York City provided free window guards to high-risk households and went door to door to explain — reported childhood falls in one borough dropped by half in three years. New York later passed a law requiring landlords to install guards in apartments housing children. Window screens push open — they do not count as protection.
- Benefit: Starting in 1972, New York City ran a program called "Children Can't Fly," distributing free window guards in high-risk neighborhoods and explaining them door to door. Reported falls in the Bronx dropped 50% from 1973 to 1975. New York City subsequently amended its health code to require landlords to install window guards in apartments where children ten years old and younger reside.
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Spiegel CN, Lindaman FC (1977). Children can't fly: a program to prevent childhood morbidity and mortality from window falls. American Journal of Public Health: "Significant reduction in falls resulted, particularly in the Bronx, where reported falls declined 50 percent from 1973 to 1975", 1976 New York City Health Code amendment "to require that landlords provide window guards in apartments where children ten years old and younger reside". <https://doi.org/10.2105/AJPH.67.12.1143>; (2021). Unintentional Window Falls in Children and Adolescents. Academic Pediatrics: 38,840 emergency visits from the National Electronic Injury Surveillance System from January 2007 to August 2017, "The majority of falls occurred in children under the age of 6 and were related to falls from a second story or below". <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.acap.2020.07.008>
- Note: Do not put beds, sofas, boxes, or anything a child can climb on next to windows or balconies. Screens only block mosquitoes — push and they fall; do not treat them as guards. This is not only for those living on high floors: in the U.S. data, most falls occurred on the second floor or lower.

### 12. Don't take your eyes off children near water; wear life jackets when boating or swimming in open water
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A life jacket costs 50 to 200 yuan. Plus the attention it takes to watch the child.
- Plain-language summary: Wearing a life jacket when you fall into water cuts your chance of drowning to about half that of not wearing one. Drowning remains the leading cause of death for Chinese children aged 1 to 14. The drowning death rate in rural areas is about twice that of cities.
- Benefit: A study using U.S. Coast Guard data matched people of similar conditions into pairs. Among those who fell overboard from recreational boats, the drowning risk for those wearing a life jacket was about half. Original value adjusted RR 0.51, 95% CI 0.35 to 0.74. The drowning death rate among Chinese under 20 dropped from 6.60 per 100,000 in 2013 to 3.28 per 100,000 in 2021. The rural rate is about 2 times the urban rate. Drowning remains the leading cause of death for children aged 1 to 14. Among injury deaths in those aged 0 to 19 in 2021, drowning accounted for 31.1% and road traffic 27.9%.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Cummings P et al. (2011). Association between wearing a personal floatation device and death by drowning among recreational boaters. Injury Prevention. <https://doi.org/10.1136/ip.2010.028688> ; Li Z et al. (2023). Unintentional Drowning Mortality Among Individuals Under Age 20 — China, 2013–2021. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2023.198> ; Zhou J et al. (2024). Injury Mortality of Children and Adolescents Aged 0–19 Years — China, 2010–2021. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2024.057>
- Note: The A grade applies only to the life-jacket half. "Keep them in sight" is a consensus; on its own it is only C grade — no randomized trial, and there never will be. Drowning deaths in China concentrate in rural areas, in summer, and among males aged 15 to 19.

### 13. After age 60, train balance and leg strength, and modify your bathroom and stairs at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Two to three sessions per week, 30 minutes each of tai chi or balance training. Bathroom non-slip mats, grab bars, and night lights cost a few dozen to a few hundred yuan. The hard part is keeping it up long term.
- Plain-language summary: Practicing balance two or three times a week, such as tai chi, reduces the number of falls among older adults by about 23%, and the number of people who fall by 15%. Making the home non-slip and installing grab bars reduces falls another 20% or so. Falls are the leading cause of injury death among Chinese over 65, and more than half occur in their own homes.
- Benefit: Cochrane combined 108 trials and 23,407 people. Exercise reduced the rate of falls in older adults by 23%. Original value rate ratio RaR 0.77, 95% CI 0.71 to 0.83. The number of people who fell dropped 15% (RR 0.85). Having someone come to the home for a safety assessment and modification reduced the fall rate by about 20% (RR 0.81, 95% CI 0.68 to 0.97). Tai chi reduced fall risk by about 29% (RR 0.71). In China, falls are the leading cause of injury death among those over 65. Of the falls among older adults monitored in 2018, 55.97% occurred at home.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Sherrington C et al. (2019). Exercise for preventing falls in older people living in the community. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD012424.pub2> ; Gillespie LD et al. (2012). Interventions for preventing falls in older people living in the community. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007146.pub3> ; Lu Z et al. (2021). Characteristics of Falls Among Older People — China, 2018. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2021.013>
- Note: This applies to people over 60 living in their own homes, not in nursing homes. The studies above count the number of falls, not the number of deaths. The path from a fall to a hip fracture to death is short in older adults.

### 14. Test for hepatitis B (the "two-pair-half" panel); if you have no antibodies, get vaccinated
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A test is a few dozen yuan. Adult hepatitis B vaccine is 3 doses totaling 100 to 300 yuan. Completed within half a year.
- Plain-language summary: A test is a few dozen yuan. In the Qidong trial that followed people for 30 years, those vaccinated against hepatitis B at birth had an 84% lower chance of developing primary liver cancer, and the proportion carrying the hepatitis B virus was 72% lower. Adults found to have no antibodies get three shots, completed within half a year, for one or two hundred yuan.
- Benefit: The Qidong trial was cluster-randomized, not individually randomized, and followed people for 30 years. Neonatal hepatitis B vaccination reduced primary liver cancer by 84%. The 95% CI was 23% to 97%. Hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg) positivity dropped 72% (95% CI 68% to 75%); this positivity rate is the proportion found to be carrying the virus. The HBsAg positivity rate in the entire Chinese population dropped 52% from 1992 to 2014, and by 97% among children under 5.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Qu C et al. (2014). Efficacy of neonatal HBV vaccination on liver cancer and other liver diseases over 30-year follow-up of the Qidong hepatitis B intervention study. PLoS Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1001774> ; Cui F et al. (2017). Prevention of Chronic Hepatitis B after 3 Decades of Escalating Vaccination Policy, China. Emerging Infectious Diseases. <https://doi.org/10.3201/eid2305.161477>
- Note: This applies to adults born before 1992, those not vaccinated, or whose antibodies have faded. The 84% liver cancer reduction figure is from those vaccinated at birth. In the same trial, those who got catch-up vaccination only as adolescents had just 21% efficacy against carrying the virus. Adults get vaccinated mainly to avoid being infected — the 84% cannot be applied directly. Vaccination is useless for those already infected; they should see a liver disease specialist for regular follow-up. How to tell if already infected: on the lab report, the hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg) item is positive.

### 15. If pricked by a rusty nail or wood splinter, or your wound is exposed to soil, get it treated that day and ask whether you need a tetanus shot
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Registration plus wound care is a few dozen yuan. If the doctor says a shot is needed, the vaccine or immunoglobulin is a few dozen to a few hundred yuan.
- Plain-language summary: Once tetanus develops, about 1 in 10 cases die. Deep wounds and soil-contaminated wounds carry the highest risk. Getting it treated that day costs no more than a few dozen yuan — don't wait until the wound swells.
- Benefit: Among U.S. tetanus cases, about 1 in 10 die. Deep wounds and wounds contaminated with soil carry higher risk.
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Tetanus: "Tetanus bacteria can get into someone's body through broken skin, usually through injuries." "Tetanus can lead to death (1 in 10 cases in the United States are fatal)." "People who didn't complete the primary series or who aren't up to date with their 10-year tetanus booster shots are also at increased risk." "Vaccination also helps prevent tetanus in people with wounds, depending on their tetanus vaccination history." <https://www.cdc.gov/tetanus/about/index.html>; Office of the National Health Commission (2024). Notice on Issuing the Specifications for Diagnosis and Treatment of Non-Neonatal Tetanus (2024 Edition) (National Health Commission Medical Emergency Letter [2024] No. 381). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202410/content_6982262.htm>
- Note: Let the doctor judge at the hospital — don't decide yourself, and don't skip asking because it's a hassle. Not every wound requires a shot: the doctor will look at the nature of the wound, then at your last vaccination, and decide. "Vaccinated in childhood means lifelong protection" is wrong — booster shots are counted on a 10-year cycle. ### 16. Females should get the HPV vaccine, the earlier the better
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Domestic bivalent is about 300+ yuan per dose; imported nine-valent is about 1,300 yuan per dose. Two to three doses in total. Each visit takes half a day.

### 18. Women over 30 should get cervical cancer screening, with HPV testing preferred

- Cost: HPV testing is 100 to 300 yuan. If the result is negative, screening every 5 years is sufficient. Sampling takes a few minutes.
- Plain-language summary: In the rural India trial, women who had a single HPV test in their lifetime had about half the risk of dying from cervical cancer, and about half the risk of being detected with late-stage cervical cancer. One test costs one or two hundred yuan, and a negative result is good for 5 years.
- Benefit: The rural India trial was cluster-randomized, not individually randomized. The subjects were women aged 30 to 59. ### 19. Start colorectal cancer screening at age 45 to 50, with a fecal immunochemical test (FIT) or colonoscopy

### 21. Get the shingles vaccine after age 50
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: The recombinant zoster vaccine requires two doses, totaling about 3,000 to 4,000 yuan. This is fully out of pocket.
- Plain-language summary: In phase III trials, the vaccine blocked shingles 97%, similar across age groups. The drawback is that two doses cost three or four thousand yuan, all out of pocket. What that money buys is a 97% reduction in incidence.
- Benefit: A phase III trial across 18 countries enrolled 15,400 people. The trial randomized people into two groups — one got the vaccine, one a control shot — and followed them for an average of 3.2 years. Result: 6 cases in the vaccine group and 210 in the placebo group developed shingles. Per 1,000 person-years that is 0.3 vs 9.1; "per 1,000 person-years" means cases observed in 1,000 people over one year. The original paper states "the overall vaccine efficacy against shingles was 97.2% (95% CI 93.7–99.0, P<0.001)." Efficacy across age groups ranged from 96.6% to 97.9%.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Lal H, Cunningham AL, Godeaux O, et al. (2015). Efficacy of an adjuvanted herpes zoster subunit vaccine in older adults. New England Journal of Medicine, 372(22), 2087-2096. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1501184>
- Note: It ranks low on cost-effectiveness because it is expensive. Shingles itself is rarely fatal; the trouble is postherpetic neuralgia, which can hurt for months to years. After vaccination, pain at the injection site and fever are common. The heaviest tier (grade 3 symptoms) was 17.0% vs 3.2%.

### 22. Get the pneumococcal vaccine after age 65
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A few hundred yuan. Free for older adults in some regions.
- Plain-language summary: In a trial of 84,500 older adults, the 13-valent pneumococcal vaccine blocked about 46% of the pneumococcal pneumonia types it covers, and 75% of the most severe invasive infections. But it showed no effect on "all-cause pneumonia" — don't expect it to cover all pneumonia.
- Benefit: The Netherlands randomized 84,500 people aged 65 and older into two groups for comparison, using the 13-valent pneumococcal conjugate vaccine. Its efficacy against vaccine-type pneumonia was 45.6%. The 95.2% CI was 21.8–62.5. "Vaccine-type" refers to the bacterial strains covered by this vaccine. Efficacy against invasive pneumococcal disease was 75.0% (95% CI 41.4–90.8), the most severe class of infection. For all-cause pneumonia combined, it had no effect.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Bonten MJ, Huijts SM, Bolkenbaas M, et al. (2015). Polysaccharide conjugate vaccine against pneumococcal pneumonia in adults. New England Journal of Medicine, 372(12), 1114-1125. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1408544>
- Note: It only protects against the serotypes covered by the vaccine — getting the shot does not mean you won't get pneumonia. Older adults in China commonly get the 23-valent polysaccharide vaccine, which is not the same as the 13-valent conjugate vaccine used in this trial, so the efficacy figures above cannot be applied directly.

### 23. Test for Helicobacter pylori; if positive, eradicate it

- Plain-language summary: In the Shandong Linqu trial that followed people for 22 years, those who eradicated Helicobacter pylori had about half the risk of later developing gastric cancer, and about 40% lower risk of dying from gastric cancer. A test is one or two hundred yuan; eradication takes two weeks of medication.
- Benefit: The Shandong Linqu randomized trial followed people for 22 years. Those who received H. pylori eradication therapy had about half the risk of developing gastric cancer. ### 24. Heavy smokers should get a low-dose chest CT once a year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One scan is 200 to 400 yuan, 10 minutes to complete. Plus the cost of follow-up exams after a false alarm, and the anxiety during that period.
- Plain-language summary: Eligible heavy smokers who get an annual low-dose CT have a 20% lower chance of dying from lung cancer than those who only get chest X-rays, and 6.7% lower total mortality. The price is follow-up exams after suspicious findings, and the anxiety during that period.
- Benefit: The U.S. NLST randomized trial enrolled 53,454 people, all aged 55 to 74, with a smoking history of 30 pack-years or more, and quit no more than 15 years ago. A pack-year is a unit for smoking amount — one pack a day for one year counts as one pack-year. The results: those who got low-dose CT had 20.0% lower lung cancer mortality compared to those who only got chest X-rays. 95% CI 6.8 to 26.7. Total mortality dropped 6.7%, 95% CI 1.2 to 13.6.

### 27. If you see visible blood in your urine, even if it doesn't hurt and even if it's gone the next day, get checked once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A routine urinalysis is a few dozen yuan; a urinary tract ultrasound is one or two hundred yuan. If the doctor deems it necessary, add a cystoscopy, around a thousand yuan.
- Plain-language summary: For people over 60 who have one episode of visible blood in urine, the probability of ultimately being diagnosed with bladder cancer is about 2.8%, and for those 40 to 59 about 1.2%. This number isn't high, but it's high enough that you must go get checked once. Even if the bleeding stops the next day, you still need to be checked.
- Benefit: A U.K. study used electronic medical records from community primary care clinics, with 4,915 bladder cancer cases vs 21,718 controls. Among those with visible blood in urine, the proportion ultimately diagnosed with bladder cancer was: 2.8% for those over 60, 95% CI 2.5 to 3.1. For those 40 to 59, 1.2%, 95% CI 0.6 to 2.3. The technical term for this proportion is positive predictive value. Blood in urine visible only under the microscope is similarly meaningful. Odds ratio 20, 95% CI 12 to 33. For those over 60 the proportion was 1.6%, 95% CI 1.2 to 2.1.

### 28. If erectile function is having problems, get your cardiovascular system checked first — don't treat it as merely "a thing in that area"

- Cost: Registration is a few dozen yuan. Blood pressure measurement, blood draw for glucose and lipids is one or two hundred yuan. A hospital trip, then wait a few days for results.
- Plain-language summary: Men with erectile dysfunction later have about 40% more cardiovascular disease and about 60% more heart attacks than others. The younger the man, the bigger the gap. This condition often appears before chest pain. So check blood pressure, glucose, and lipids first — don't just buy something online to suppress it.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis pooled 14 cohort studies, 92,757 men, followed for an average of 6.1 years. Compared to men without erectile dysfunction, those with the condition had the following subsequent risks. Total cardiovascular events higher by about 44% (RR 1.44, 95% CI 1.27 to 1.63 — the confidence interval). Heart attack higher by about 62% (RR 1.62, 1.34 to 1.96). Cerebrovascular events higher by about 39% (RR 1.39, 1.23 to 1.57). All-cause mortality higher by about 25% (RR 1.25, 1.12 to 1.39). Cardiovascular mortality did not show a gap (RR 1.19, 0.97 to 1.46). The younger the age, the higher the multiplier. The multiplier was higher for people at medium baseline cardiovascular risk than for those at high or low risk. Studies that used a questionnaire to define the condition gave a multiplier of 1.61 (1.38 to 1.86). Studies that asked only one question gave 1.27 (1.18 to 1.37).
- Evidence grade: A

Circulation: Cardiovascular Quality and Outcomes, 6(1), 99-109. <https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCOUTCOMES.112.966903>; DailyMed (U.S. National Library of Medicine drug label database). VIAGRA (sildenafil citrate) tablet, CONTRAINDICATIONS. <https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=d905dc8d-917f-4ea3-a4ee-a1ecf6967d4e>
- Note: All 14 are cohort studies that track a group of people and observe outcomes — not randomized trials. So "40% more" is an association, not a causal effect. Occasional instances don't count; it is treated as a problem only when persistent for more than three months. The things to check are the same batch as Entries 7 and 8 of this section: measure blood pressure, check fasting glucose, plus lipids, smoking, and waist circumference. Don't buy "virility" supplements online. The legitimate drugs in this class are prescription drugs, and the label lists nitrates as a contraindication — people taking nitroglycerin and similar drugs cannot take them; combined use can cause blood pressure to crash. For how to improve, see Entry 29 of this section (weight loss and quitting smoking can improve erectile function). Benefit recorded as "medium" not "large": 44% is the risk gap between two groups, not "how much death is reduced by going to get checked once." Also not recorded as "small": the signal in Entry 27 of this section (visible blood in urine) has only detection rate, whereas the high blood pressure, diabetes, and dyslipidemia found here all have established treatments.

### 29. Lose weight, quit smoking, and bring blood pressure and glucose under control — erectile function can improve along with them
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free, and may even save money. The hard part is long-term adherence: controlling what you eat, getting moving, and quitting smoking.
- Plain-language summary: Manage cardiovascular risk factors and erectile function itself improves. On a questionnaire score, average improvement is 2 to 3 points. The questionnaire totals 25 points, with 22 and above considered normal.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis pooled 6 randomized controlled trials and 740 men. The intervention was lifestyle change, or medication to control cardiovascular risk factors, with follow-up of at least 6 weeks. Result: the erectile function questionnaire (IIEF-5) score improved by an average of 2.66 points (95% CI 1.86 to 3.47 — the confidence interval). Removing the 2 trials (143 people) that used statins, the remaining 4 trials that relied purely on lifestyle change (597 people) still improved by 2.40 points (1.19 to 3.61). The IIEF-5 is a 5-question questionnaire with a total score of 5 to 25. The original paper divides scores into five tiers, with 22 to 25 considered no dysfunction and 5 to 7 considered severe.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Gupta BP, Murad MH, Clifton MM, Prokop L, Nehra A, Kopecky SL (2011). The effect of lifestyle modification and cardiovascular risk factor reduction on erectile dysfunction: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Archives of Internal Medicine, 171(20), 1797-1803. <https://doi.org/10.1001/archinternmed.2011.440>; Rosen RC, Cappelleri JC, Smith MD, Lipsky J, Peña BM (1999). Development and evaluation of an abridged, 5-item version of the International Index of Erectile Function (IIEF-5) as a diagnostic tool for erectile dysfunction. International Journal of Impotence Research, 11(6), 319-326. <https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.ijir.3900472>
- Note: The interventions in these 6 trials were not the same — some were weight loss plus exercise, some were medication to control blood pressure and lipids. Total of only 740 people. So the direction is clear, but exactly how much improvement varies from person to person. Benefit recorded as "small" because the endpoint is only questionnaire score, with no figure for "how much disease was avoided later." The same actions have hard endpoints elsewhere: quitting smoking see Section 2, Entry 1 (quit smoking, the earlier the better); weight loss see Section 2, Entry 33 (keep BMI in 20–25); blood pressure see Entry 7 of this section (measure blood pressure; if high, take medicine to bring it to target). Medication itself is not forbidden, provided it is a doctor-prescribed prescription drug. "Health supplements" sold online often secretly add these ingredients at unknown doses; for how to tell, see Section 28, Entry 4 (don't buy weight-loss pills that promise fast slimming).

### 30. Use a condom throughout sex; don't share needles with anyone
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: One condom is one or two yuan. The hard part isn't the money — it's using one every time, and from the very start.
- Plain-language summary: Use one throughout every time, and heterosexual transmission of HIV drops by more than 70%. "Most of the time" is not much different from not using one. Syphilis, gonorrhea, and others are also blocked.
- Benefit: A study pooled 25 studies and 10,676 heterosexual couples. All were couples with one partner infected with HIV and one not — technically called serodiscordant. Compared to those who never used condoms, those who used one every time had about 71% lower risk of HIV transmission. Original value RR 0.29, 95% CI 0.20 to 0.43. Compared to those who used them intermittently, about 77% lower (RR 0.23, 95% CI 0.13 to 0.40). The authors concluded that condoms, while imperfect, can "reduce HIV transmission by more than 70% with consistent use." The same layer of protection also covers syphilis, gonorrhea, chlamydia, and other sexually transmitted infections.
- Evidence grade: A

### 32. When suicidal thoughts first arise, tell someone close to you — hand over those tens of minutes
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is opening up at the time, and admitting you can't be alone for these few hours.
- Plain-language summary: Nearly half of those who attempted suicide said the time from the first thought to acting was no more than 10 minutes, so what you have to get through is often just those tens of minutes. Three-quarters had some contact with people close to them during that time — they just didn't speak up. Among those hospitalized for self-harm, about 2% died of suicide 1 year later, about 7% after 9 years or more — the vast majority did not later die of suicide.
- Benefit: 82 people who had attempted suicide were interviewed within 3 days of admission: 47.6% (39 people) said the time from the first suicidal thought to actually acting was no more than 10 minutes. Those whose process was longer had significantly higher suicide-intent scores. Impulsivity scale scores were unrelated to the length of the process. 76.8% (63 people) said they had some kind of contact with a partner, family member, or friend during that time. The authors concluded that the window for intervention is usually very short and that professionals have limited room to intervene at this stage, so recognition signals and intervention methods must be disseminated to ordinary people. Another systematic review pooled 90 follow-up studies. Among those who presented for self-harm, the median proportion who repeated self-harm without dying within 1 year was 16%. The proportion who died of suicide within 1 year was 2%. Among those followed for 9 years or more, about 7% died of suicide.

- Note: Graded B because Deisenhammer's study had only 82 people, from one hospital, relying on subjects' recollection after the fact. ### 33. Don't treat "being saved" as a safety net: after drinking pesticide or inhaling gas, the ER can save your life but cannot save your lungs and brain
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. Just know this in advance.
- Plain-language summary: Paraquat has no antidote — more than 40% of those who drink it cannot be saved. It causes the lungs to become inflamed and slowly harden, with the kidneys and liver also injured. Among people saved from carbon monoxide poisoning, nearly half have worse brains six weeks later; even with hyperbaric oxygen, a quarter still do.
- Benefit: A systematic review of paraquat poisoning: the case fatality rate is extremely high because the substance itself is highly toxic and there is no effective treatment. It is actively transported into lung tissue against the concentration gradient — technically called active uptake against a concentration gradient — resulting in pneumonia and pulmonary fibrosis, while also causing kidney and liver injury. The review states: gastric lavage should not be performed. Hemodialysis and hemoperfusion are unlikely to alter the clinical course. The evidence for corticosteroids and immunosuppressants is weak. The review also states: "case fatality rates are high across all centers, despite wide variation in treatment." A Bangladesh series from 10 tertiary hospitals from 2013 to 2024 admitted 1,420 cases, of which 257 were analyzable: case fatality rate 43.2%. Common presentations were lower-limb swelling from acute kidney failure 71.2%, vomiting 65.8%, abdominal pain 58.0%. The acute carbon monoxide poisoning trial was double-blind randomized, 76 people in each group. Neither doctors nor patients knew which group they were in. At six weeks, cognitive after-effects remained in 19/76 (25.0%) of the hyperbaric oxygen group. The normobaric oxygen group had 35/76 (46.1%), P=0.007. After adjusting for other factors, OR 0.45 (95% CI 0.22 to 0.92 — the confidence interval). Another study consecutively observed 73 cases: 30% had cognitive after-effects, 12% had white matter hyperintensities. China revoked the registration and production license for paraquat aqueous solutions effective July 1, 2014, and stopped production. Effective July 1, 2016, aqueous solutions stopped being sold and used domestically. Effective September 26, 2020, soluble gel formulations are prohibited from sale and use within China. The documents state that "paraquat ingestion suicide incidents occur from time to time" is the direct reason for tightening regulation.
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Gawarammana IB, Buckley NA (2011). Medical management of paraquat ingestion. - Note: Graded B because these studies involve poisoning patients, not a uniform suicide-attempt population; the carbon monoxide trial also included accidental poisoning cases. ### 35. Don't trade "missing a kidney is no big deal" for money: the remaining kidney has to do the work of two, and 86% of those who sold a kidney later report poorer health

- Cost: Free. Just look at this accounting first.
- Plain-language summary: For legitimate donors who were screened layer by layer before donation and have follow-up afterward, 31 per 10,000 reach end-stage renal disease 15 years later, versus 4 per 10,000 for similarly healthy non-donors. Kidney sellers with no screening and no follow-up, 86% report poorer health 6 years later, and three-quarters are still in debt.
- Benefit: The U.S. compared 96,217 living kidney donors with 20,024 similarly healthy non-donors. The latter came from the U.S. National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES III). Looking at estimated risk of reaching end-stage renal disease (ESRD) 15 years after donation: donors 30.8 per 10,000, 95% CI 24.3–38.5; healthy non-donors 3.9 per 10,000, 95% CI 0.8–8.9, P<0.001. ### 36. Don't pick up, don't dismantle, don't sell metal parts of unknown origin; stay away from anything marked with the trefoil warning symbol
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free. If you see an unfamiliar metal cylinder, lead canister, or component removed from equipment, walk around it — don't pick it up, don't dismantle it, don't put it in your pocket.
- Plain-language summary: Radiation has no smell, color, or temperature, and most radiation sources look like an ordinary piece of metal, so "looks fine" cannot be the judge. The probability of an ordinary person encountering one is extremely low — fewer than 1 incident per 10,000 sources per year. But once you do, the outcome is irreversible. In 1987 in Brazil, someone dismantled a radiotherapy machine from an abandoned clinic as scrap metal; 4 people died.
- Benefit: As of 2025, China has approximately 178,000 radioactive sources in use and 355,000 ray devices. The annual incidence rate of radiation-source accidents is stable at less than 1 per 10,000 sources. The classification is as follows. Loss, theft, or loss of control of a Category I or Category II source constitutes a major radiation accident. Category III is a relatively major radiation accident. Loss or theft of a Category IV or V source, or causing a person to receive exposure exceeding the annual dose limit, is a general radiation accident. Official case: In June 2020, Ningxia Iron and Steel Group's ironmaking plant performed gamma-ray inspection work on a blast furnace pipe renovation project. A Category II iridium-192 radioactive source exposed three workers to radiation. Chen had acute radiation skin injury grade III on the left hand. Liu had grade IV on the left thumb and index finger, grade III on the middle, ring, and little fingers. Fan had grade I-II on the left hand. All three received doses seriously exceeding the national standard dose limits; the two responsible units had their radiation safety licenses revoked and were fined 70,000 yuan. International case: In 1987 in Goiânia, Brazil, a teletherapy machine from an abandoned clinic was dismantled, and the 50.8 TBq cesium-137 radioactive source inside was removed, breaking the sealed capsule. About 112,000 people were monitored. Of these, 249 were internally or externally contaminated. Of those 249, 129 had moderate to severe contamination, and 50 required close medical observation. Ultimately, 4 died (national / international).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ministry of Ecology and Environment (2025). 2024 China Ecological Environment Status Bulletin. <https://www.mee.gov.cn/ywgz/sthjjcgl/hjzljcypj/202506/t20250605_1120773.shtml>; National Nuclear Safety Administration (2026). Achievements of China's Nuclear and Radiation Safety Regulation in 2025. <https://nnsa.mee.gov.cn/tpxw/202601/t20260107_1140062.html>; Ministry of Ecology and Environment (2021). Notice on the Handling of a Radiation Accident Caused by Illegal Practices in Gamma-Ray Inspection Work. <https://www.mee.gov.cn/xxgk2018/xxgk/xxgk06/202101/t20210121_818216.html>; State Council (2019). Regulations on the Safety and Protection of Radioisotopes and Radiation Devices (2019 Revision, Articles 34, 42, 68). <https://www.mee.gov.cn/ywgz/fgbz/xzfg/201909/t20190918_734315.shtml>; Ministry of Environmental Protection (2009). Notice on Establishing a Grading and Reporting System for Radiation Accidents Involving Radioisotopes and Radiation Devices (Radiation Accident Grading Standards). <https://www.mee.gov.cn/gkml/zj/wj/200910/t20091022_172414.htm>; International Atomic Energy Agency (1988). The Radiological Accident in Goiânia. IAEA, Vienna. <https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/Pub815_web.pdf>
- Note: If you actually encounter one: keep your distance, remember the location, don't move it, call 110. The regulations require reporting to the ecology and environment authority, public security department, and health authority. Distance and time are the only effective protection — a pocket or plastic bag in between is useless. Devices that generate radiation by being powered, such as X-ray machines and CT scanners, leave no residual radioactivity after being unplugged. What is genuinely dangerous is the sealed radioactive source inside the equipment. Hospital radiotherapy machines, gamma inspection machines on pipes and boilers, well-logging instruments, and level gauges all contain sources. Removing the outer shell exposes the source. And their appearance is indistinguishable from ordinary metal parts. So don't count on recognizing a radiation source — parts of unknown origin from equipment should never be dismantled, sold, or brought home. People in scrap recycling, dismantling, hardware, and construction work have a much higher risk than ordinary people; on a construction site, walk around any area marked with a trefoil warning sign. Nuclear power plant leaks are a different kind of accident, with even lower probability for ordinary people. The beneficiary is mainly yourself and family members who were on the same scene.
