# 22. How to Relax: Entertainment Venues and Stress Relief

The first half of this section addresses entertainment venues — where money is wasted冤 and where the emergency exits are; the metric is money and personal freedom. The second half addresses stress relief; the metric is energy and overall mortality. The numbers in the two halves are not converted together. Section 3 has already covered what to do first when feeling low; here we give more concrete practices.

### 1. Before entering a KTV, bar, or escape room, take a glance at where the emergency exit is; if it is locked or blocked, change venues
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Look up for 10 seconds when entering
- Plain-language summary: By regulation, during operating hours, entertainment venues must keep evacuation routes and emergency exits clear. No blocking, no locking, no placing objects in the way, and no covering of indicator signs. Internet cafes likewise may not seal doors or windows. Failure to comply is a violation, and you may report it. If a private room is underground, has only one exit, and the corridor is stacked with clutter, change venues
- Benefit: This is the operator's statutory obligation. Failure to comply is a violation, and you may report it. The regulation reads: "During operating hours, entertainment venues shall ensure that evacuation routes and emergency exits are clear; they may not block or lock evacuation routes and emergency exits, and may not place barriers such as fences on evacuation routes and emergency exits that affect evacuation." The regulation also requires "clear indicator signs shall be placed on evacuation routes and emergency exits, and indicator signs may not be obscured or covered." Internet cafes likewise are required to "sealing or locking of doors, windows, safety evacuation routes, and emergency exits is prohibited during operating hours," and "open-flame lighting and smoking are prohibited"
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2006). Regulation on the Administration of Entertainment Venues (State Council Order No. 458, Articles 20 and 21). <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>; State Council (2002). Regulation on the Administration of Internet Service Business Venues (State Council Order No. 363, Article 21). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2002/content_61788.htm>
- Note: If any of the following is present, change venues: the private room is underground, there is only one exit, the corridor is stacked with clutter, or the indicator light at the emergency exit is not lit. When such venues catch fire, smoke is heavy, corridors are narrow, and people are many, and most have been drinking. The time you have to escape is measured in seconds. The reporting phone number, per regulation, should be written on the warning sign inside the venue

### 2. Before ordering, ask for the price list; entertainment venues must display prices openly — do not accept "play first, settle later"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Open your mouth and ask for the menu once. The hard part is finding it hard to ask about prices in front of others
- Plain-language summary: By regulation, entertainment venues must openly display prices for services and goods, and must proactively show you the price list. The so-called "sky-high drinks" almost always occur in situations where no price list was seen and the bill is settled afterward based on a verbal quote. When entering the private room, first ask for the menu and photograph it. After ordering, ask for a confirmed list. If there is a billing dispute, call 12315 on the spot
- Benefit: The regulation reads: "Where entertainment venues provide entertainment service items and sell goods, they shall openly display prices and present the price list to consumers; they may not compel or deceive consumers into accepting services or purchasing goods." Those who violate price laws and regulations are penalized by the relevant authorities in accordance with the law. The so-called "sky-high drinks" disputes almost all occur in situations where no price list was presented and the bill was settled afterward based on a verbal quote
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2006). Regulation on the Administration of Entertainment Venues (State Council Order No. 458, Articles 29 and 53). <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>
- Note: How to do it: when entering the private room, first ask for a price list — paper or electronic is fine — and photograph it as soon as you receive it. After ordering, ask for a confirmed list. For "special mixes" or "fruit plates" the server recommends, always ask the price before ordering. For billing disputes, call 12315 on the spot. Do not pay first and then seek redress; once paid, your burden of proof becomes much harder

### 3. If someone at the venue hands you "something," leave immediately; harboring and providing are not "helping a friend"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Stand up and walk out. The hard part is leaving the table in front of everyone
- Plain-language summary: Entertainment venues may not sell or provide drugs, nor allow people to consume them on premises; violators are ordered to suspend business for rectification for 3 to 6 months, with serious cases having their licenses revoked. Personal consumption incurs public security penalties. Allowing someone to "use it once" in a private room or lodging you have booked constitutes harboring another's drug use — a criminal offense. If someone produces unidentified powder, tablets, or vape pods and invites you to try, leave on the spot
- Benefit: The regulation expressly prohibits entertainment venues from "selling or providing drugs, or organizing, forcing, instigating, inducing, deceiving, or harboring others to consume or inject drugs." Staff also may not consume drugs and may not provide conditions for customers to do any of the above. Violators are punished by the public security organ through confiscation of illegal gains and unlawful property, and ordered to suspend business for rectification for 3 to 6 months. Serious cases result in revocation of the entertainment business license. The regulation also requires venues to display warning signs prominently in halls, private rooms, and booths prohibiting drugs, gambling, and prostitution, and to list the reporting phone number
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2006). Regulation on the Administration of Entertainment Venues (State Council Order No. 458, Articles 14, 30, and 42). <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6297.htm>
- Note: As long as someone produces unidentified powder, tablets, or vape pods and invites you to "try," leave on the spot; do not stay to watch. The personal risk you bear is heavier than the venue's. Personal drug use itself carries public security penalties. Allowing a friend to "use it once" in a private room you booked or in your own lodging constitutes harboring another's drug use — a criminal offense

### 4. Do not eat candy or snacks given by strangers, do not drink beverages that have left your sight, and do not accept vape pods handed by others
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Do not accept anything handed over by others. If your cup has left your sight, change to a new one
- Plain-language summary: Today's drugs do not look like drugs. The "upper" e-cigarettes spiked with synthetic cannabinoids look much like ordinary e-cigarettes; some people specifically hand them out in bars, KTVs, and hotels to try. Those who trick others into consuming face 10 to 15 days of detention and a fine of 1,000 to 5,000 yuan; those tricked into consuming also face 10 to 15 days of detention. If a beverage has left your sight, change to a new one
- Benefit: Today's drugs do not look like drugs. Synthetic cannabinoids as a class have been under integral listing since July 1, 2021 — this entire class is regulated as a drug. Some spike it into e-liquid to make "upper" e-cigarettes. In a case reported by the Supreme People's Procuratorate, the case officers described it: this e-liquid "can be made into various colors and flavors by adding food pigments," "in appearance it is essentially indistinguishable from an ordinary e-cigarette." Sellers hawked it in bars, KTVs, and hotels, inducing others to consume. In the report, of the consumption cases handled locally that year, the share of underage consumers was nearly 40%. Etomidate was added to the Schedule II psychotropic substances catalog on October 1, 2023. Tricking another into consuming is itself a category of violation: those who instigate, induce, deceive, or force another to consume or inject drugs face 10 to 15 days of detention and a fine of 1,000 to 5,000 yuan. Where a crime is constituted, the sentence is under 3 years; serious cases 3 to 7 years; coercive cases 3 to 10 years; targeting minors carries heavier penalties. The consequences also fall on the deceived: those who consume or inject drugs face 10 to 15 days of detention and may be fined up to 3,000 yuan. Less serious cases face up to 5 days of detention or a fine of up to 1,000 yuan. They may also be ordered not to enter entertainment venues for 6 months to 1 year. The addicted are ordered into community drug treatment for 3 years. Those who refuse community drug treatment, or who consume again during treatment, undergo compulsory isolated drug treatment for 2 years, with a possible extension of up to 1 year (nationwide, Public Security Administration Punishments Law effective January 2026)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Supreme People's Procuratorate (2021). Jiangsu Suzhou Wujiang District: Case Prosecutor Reveals New-Type Drugs Wearing a Trendy Disguise. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/zdgz/202110/t20211011_531831.shtml>; National Medical Products Administration, Ministry of Public Security, and National Health Commission (2023). Announcement on Adjusting the Catalogs of Narcotic and Psychotropic Substances (2023 No. 120). <https://yjj.sh.gov.cn/qtgzwj/20230911/3b612c94e35b4b63abba314c972a2593.html> (reproduced by the Shanghai Municipal Medical Products Administration; the original page nmpa.gov.cn returned 412 on this machine); Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2025). Public Security Administration Punishments Law (2025 revision, Articles 84 and 85). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml>; National People's Congress (1997). Criminal Law (Article 353). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007). Anti-Drug Law (Articles 33, 38, and 47). <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/post_2520706.html> (reproduced by the Guangdong Provincial People's Government)
- Note: This applies not only to entertainment venues; the same applies at school gates, friends' gatherings, and on long-distance buses. Whether it is candy, a beverage, or a vape pod, it is all the same. After being drugged, robbery, sexual assault, and extortion often follow; there are no public official statistics on this — it is an experiential judgment. Changing cups when a beverage has left your sight is much easier than pursuing the matter afterward. For what to do when someone at the venue produces unidentified powder, tablets, or vape pods, see Entry 3 in this section. For carrying things for others, see Section 8, Entry 29

### 5. Use your own ID at internet cafes; do not borrow or use someone else's
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money
- Plain-language summary: By regulation, internet cafes must verify and register the ID of customers going online, and must record online usage information, retaining it for no less than 60 days. Public security and cultural authorities may access it at any time. During that period the cafe may not modify or delete it. Whose ID is used to go online is whose name the activity on that machine during that period is recorded under
- Benefit: The regulation reads: "Operators of internet service business venues shall verify and register the IDs and other valid documents of customers going online, and record relevant online usage information. The retention period for recorded content and records shall not be less than 60 days." When cultural administrative departments or public security organs make inquiries in accordance with the law, the cafe must provide. During the retention period, modification or deletion is prohibited. So whose ID is used to go online is whose name the activity on that machine during that period is recorded under
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2002). Regulation on the Administration of Internet Service Business Venues (State Council Order No. 363, Article 23). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2002/content_61788.htm>
- Note: This is the same matter as Section 9's "do not lend your ID." Internet cafes may not serve minors, and must post a no-minors sign at the entrance prominently. Song-and-dance entertainment venues likewise do not serve minors. "Swiping an ID to help a minor into an internet cafe" — the cafe is fined, but the record remains on your ID

### 6. For script murder (jubensha) and escape rooms, do not go to those located inside residential buildings or below the second basement level
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Before booking, ask which floor the address is on
- Plain-language summary: A 2022 notice from five ministries states that script entertainment venues may not be located inside residential buildings, nor below the first basement level. Such venues are dark, partitioned, and use flammable props; doors often require puzzle-solving to open. Once a fire starts, escape is much harder than from ordinary shops. Before booking, look at the address and floor on the review page; non-compliant ones — change venues
- Benefit: A 2022 specialized notice from five ministries is very specific: "Script entertainment business venues may not be located inside residential buildings, below the first basement level of buildings (excluding the first basement level), etc." The notice also requires venues to implement the requirements of the Work Safety Law and the Fire Control Law, "regularly carry out self-awareness, self-inspection, and self-correction of fire risk, and improve the capacity for organized evacuation and initial fire suppression in emergencies." The same notice also requires age-appropriate labeling for scripts. Scenes unsuitable for minors may not allow minors to enter. Except on statutory holidays, rest days, and winter/summer breaks, script entertainment activities may not be provided to minors
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ministry of Culture and Tourism, Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, Ministry of Emergency Management, and State Administration for Market Regulation (2022). Notice on Strengthening the Administration of Script Entertainment Business Venues. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-06/27/content_5698021.htm>
- Note: Such venues are dark, partitioned, and use flammable props; doors often require "puzzle-solving" to open. Once a fire starts, escape is much harder than from ordinary shops. Before booking, you can see the floor on the review page, and whether it is a residential building. Non-compliant — change venues

### 7. When in a bad mood, walk or run; the effect size (effect magnitude) of antidepressant effect is proportional to intensity
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. About half an hour each time. The hard part is that when feeling low, you least want to move — you have to push yourself
- Plain-language summary: When in a bad mood, brisk walking or jogging shows the clearest improvement. Next are yoga, strength training, and tai chi or qigong, with little difference among them. The higher the intensity, the better the effect; strength training and yoga are the easiest to sustain. The evidence quality here is on the lower side; what is certain is that moving is better than not — pick the kind you can stick with
- Benefit: This is the result of pooling 218 randomized two-group controlled trials. Involving 495 groups and 14,170 participants meeting clinical criteria for major depression. The calculation allows various exercises to be compared against each other (Bayesian network meta-analysis). The comparators were usual care and placebo pills (active control). Effect size is expressed as Hedges' g — the more negative the number, the greater the symptom improvement. Brisk walking or jogging −0.62 (95% credible interval −0.80 to −0.45, meaning the true value roughly falls in this range). Yoga −0.55 (−0.73 to −0.36). Strength training −0.49 (−0.69 to −0.29). Mixed aerobic −0.43 (−0.61 to −0.24). Tai chi or qigong −0.42 (−0.65 to −0.21). The original text says "the effect of exercise is proportional to the prescribed intensity" — the higher the intensity, the better the effect. Strength training and yoga have the best acceptability and are the easiest to sustain
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Noetel M, Sanders T, Gallardo-Gómez D, et al. (2024). Effect of exercise for depression: systematic review and network meta-analysis of randomised controlled trials. BMJ, 384, e075847. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-075847>
- Note: Caveats. The authors themselves note that of the 218 studies, only 1 was clean enough to be at low risk of design bias. Under prevailing scoring, brisk walking/jogging is only "low" confidence, and the rest "very low." Exercise also cannot be blinded to participants — people know they are exercising, and tend to feel better, an expectation that cannot be removed. The directional conclusion remains usable: moving is better than not, do not set intensity too low, and choose the kind you can sustain. This is an elaboration of Section 3's "when feeling low, first get moving"; it cannot replace standard treatment after diagnosis

### 8. When anxiety affects your life, mindfulness-based stress reduction is non-inferior to a commonly used antianxiety medication
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Taking a course costs some money. The course is 8 weeks, with one class per week and daily home practice. The hard part is practicing every day for 8 straight weeks
- Plain-language summary: People with diagnosed anxiety disorders who take an 8-week mindfulness-based stress reduction course show symptom improvement nearly identical to taking the antianxiety medication escitalopram. Those on medication had nearly 80% experience adverse reactions, with some dropping out for that reason. Those in the course had about 15% experience adverse reactions, with no one dropping out. The course must be the standardized 8-week program — a random meditation app does not count
- Benefit: This is a trial conducted at three US urban academic medical centers, designed to test whether taking the course would be inferior to medication (non-inferiority randomized controlled trial). 276 adults with diagnosed anxiety disorders were randomized 1:1 into two groups. One group took the 8-week mindfulness-based stress reduction course. The other took escitalopram (flexible dose 10 to 20 mg). 208 people completed the full course. At the end, by the anxiety severity score rated by clinicians (Clinical Global Impression-Severity scale), the mindfulness group decreased 1.35, the medication group decreased 1.43. The difference between the two groups was −0.07 (95% CI −0.38 to 0.23, i.e., the true difference roughly falls in this range; P=0.65, i.e., this difference is indistinguishable from no difference). The lower bound of this interval did not cross the pre-specified "no longer considered inferior" line (non-inferiority margin −0.495). Side effects differed substantially: the medication group had 78.6% experience at least one treatment-related adverse reaction, with 10 dropping out as a result. The mindfulness group had 15.4%, with no one dropping out as a result
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Hoge EA, Bui E, Mete M, Dutton MA, Baker AW, Simon NM (2023). Mindfulness-based stress reduction vs escitalopram for the treatment of adults with anxiety disorders: a randomized clinical trial. JAMA Psychiatry, 80(1), 13-21. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2022.3679>
- Note: The trial used the standardized 8-week mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR) course; a random meditation app is not equivalent. It applies to people who have reached the diagnostic threshold for an anxiety disorder — you still need to see a doctor. If you have been delaying treatment because of concerns about medication side effects, this is an option with comparable strength of evidence

### 9. To recover on the spot, use 5 minutes of "cyclic sighing": inhale in two stages, extend the exhale
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. 5 minutes a day
- Plain-language summary: 5 minutes of breathing practice daily for a month produces better mood improvement and slower breathing than the same duration of mindfulness meditation. Of several breathing techniques, "cyclic sighing," which extends the exhale, works best. The method: inhale through the nose, take one additional small inhalation to fully fill the lungs, then slowly exhale through the mouth until empty. This is only one trial, and outcomes were self-reported, but it costs nothing
- Benefit: This is a randomized controlled at-home trial comparing three 5-minute daily breathing practices with the same duration of mindfulness meditation, over 1 month. The three breathing techniques were: cyclic sighing (emphasizing extended exhale), box breathing (equal inhale, hold, exhale), and cyclic hyperventilation with breath hold (long inhale, short exhale). Pooling individual-timepoint data (mixed-effects model), breathing practices all outperformed mindfulness meditation, especially cyclic sighing, which emphasizes exhale. It was better on mood improvement (P<0.05, i.e., this difference is unlikely to be by chance) and on respiratory rate reduction (P<0.05)
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Balban MY, Neri E, Kogon MM, et al. (2023). Brief structured respiration practices enhance mood and reduce physiological arousal. Cell Reports Medicine, 4(1), 100895. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100895>
- Note: The method: inhale through the nose, briefly take one additional small inhalation to fully fill the lungs, then slowly and lengthily exhale through the mouth until empty; repeat for 5 minutes. This is only one trial, conducted at home, with outcomes self-reported via questionnaire, and the sample size not large. The effect is only slightly better than meditation; it does not solve the problem. But it costs nothing, takes only 5 minutes, and can be done anywhere at any time — high cost-effectiveness

### 10. Treat "regular meetings with people" as a health expenditure; do not seek people out only when you are in a bad mood
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Costs some money and the time spent meeting. The hard part is also proactively arranging to meet when you are in a good mood
- Plain-language summary: People who rarely interact with others have about a 30% higher probability of dying over the same period. Those who feel lonely have about a 25% higher probability. Those living alone have about a 30% higher probability, more pronounced in those under 65. These are all observational longitudinal findings; it may also be that people with poorer health are more likely to live alone
- Benefit: This is the result of pooling 70 studies that first recorded conditions and then tracked outcomes (meta-analysis), totaling 3.4 million participants with an average follow-up of 7 years. Social isolation, loneliness, and living alone were all associated with increased mortality. After adjusting for confounders such as age and physical condition, the weighted average odds ratios were: social isolation 1.29, loneliness 1.26, living alone 1.32. In other words, the probability of dying over the same period was about 30% higher, about one-quarter higher, and about 30% higher, respectively. The effect sizes of the three were similar, and larger in those under 65 than in older populations
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Holt-Lunstad J, Smith TB, Baker M, Harris T, Stephenson D (2015). Loneliness and social isolation as risk factors for mortality: a meta-analytic review. Perspectives on Psychological Science, 10(2), 227-237. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691614568352>
- Note: Caveats. These are all associations observed by following populations, not randomized controlled trials; causality cannot be separated. People with poorer underlying health are more likely to live alone and have less social contact. The authors treat social relationships as a health factor on the order of smoking and obesity, but there is no randomized trial proving that "increasing social contact reduces mortality." This entry does not conflict with Section 3's "reduce the relationships that drain you": that entry cuts draining relationships, while this entry wants to preserve the few that restore your energy

### 11. If you live near green space, use it more; walking, strolling, sitting — all count
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Do it on the way
- Plain-language summary: Better greenery within 500 m of your home means lower probability of dying over the same period; per 0.1 increase in vegetation index, the probability is about 4% lower. What is measured is the greenery around your home, not how often you visit a park. Areas with good greenery tend to have wealthier residents and better air. So do not read this as "going to a park extends life"; just factor nearby green space into choosing a home
- Benefit: This is the result of pooling 9 studies tracking populations over years (meta-analysis of cohort studies), involving 7 countries and 8.32 million people. What was measured is the degree of greenery within 500 m of the home, using the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index (NDVI — a number that converts ground greenery into a single value). For every 0.1 increase in NDVI, the pooled risk ratio for total mortality was 0.96 (95% CI 0.94–0.97) — the probability of dying over the same period was about 4% lower, with the true value roughly in the range of 3% to 6% lower. Of the 9 studies, 7 found that better greenery was associated with lower mortality, and the difference was not likely to be by chance. The other 2 found no association
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Rojas-Rueda D, Nieuwenhuijsen MJ, Gascon M, Perez-Leon D, Mudu P (2019). Green spaces and mortality: a systematic review and meta-analysis of cohort studies. The Lancet Planetary Health, 3(11), e469-e477. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2542-5196(19)30215-3>
- Note: Caveats. The results across studies vary widely (I²=95% — the larger this number, the more the studies disagree). And it measures greenery around the residence, not your frequency of park visits. People who live in greener areas also tend to be wealthier, breathe better air, and exercise more — these factors cannot be separated from greenery in determining causality. Do not read this as "going to a park extends life"; the usable part is to factor green space into choosing a home, at zero cost. NDVI: bare ground and concrete approach 0, dense vegetation approaches 1. So "0.1" roughly corresponds to moving from a barren block to one with a row of street trees
