# 23. Which Skills Are Worth Learning

This section accounts for money and time; Entry 2 also accounts for mortality. The first six entries answer one question: whether to keep studying. Entry 1 first states which option the law has removed; Entry 2 calculates the relationship between schooling and lifespan; Entry 3 looks at where the educational attainment of all Chinese people falls. Entry 4 covers what financial aid exists when you cannot afford school; Entry 5 covers paths other than ordinary high school if you do not test in; Entry 6 frames "study or work" as a calculation and gives a do-it-yourself method. The next seven entries answer another question: how to choose once you have decided to learn something. China's wage statistics by occupation are difficult to verify item by item from public official sources. So here we cover only the things for which a source can be found: roughly how much one more year of schooling adds to wages (also called the return on education), how to verify whether a certificate is lawful, where training subsidies come from, and which dimension to look at when choosing a skill to be less easily replaced. Specific monthly earnings of a specific occupation are not given in this book. Entries 14 through 19 answer a third question: once you have decided what to learn, how to learn in a time-efficient way. These six entries account for time, not converted with money. Entries 20 to 23 answer a fourth question: once you are already doing professional technical work, where to apply for professional titles, how to take the exams, what pitfalls to avoid, and how much a title is worth.

### 1. Under age 16, "going to work" is not an option: a unit that hires you is fined 5,000 yuan per month; those willing to hire are operating illegally
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Take a minute to calculate your age
- Plain-language summary: Junior high graduates are typically 15 to 16, right at this line. Under 16, hiring you is illegal, with each child laborer costing the unit 5,000 yuan per month. So those willing to take you are units that knowingly violate the law. If something goes wrong, medical costs and compensation can only be sought from this unit; if it dodges or runs, no one covers for it
- Benefit: Compulsory Education Law, Article 11: "Children who have reached six years of age shall have their parents or other statutory guardians send them to school to receive and complete compulsory education." Article 14: "Employers are prohibited from hiring children and adolescents of the appropriate age who should be receiving compulsory education." Article 58: Those who, without justified reason, fail to send school-age children and adolescents to school as required by this law are subject to "criticism and education, and ordered to correct within a time limit" by the local township people's government or the education administrative department of the county-level people's government. Provisions on the Prohibition of Using Child Labor, Article 2: State organs, social organizations, enterprises, public institutions, private non-enterprise units, and individual industrial and commercial households "may not hire minors under 16 years of age." Article 6: "Penalty of 5,000 yuan per month for each child laborer used" — those who fail to correct within the time limit are fined 10,000 yuan per person per month. Article 7: Those who refer child labor for employment are "fined 5,000 yuan per referral." Article 10: Where a child laborer falls ill or is injured, the employer "shall be responsible for sending them to a medical institution for treatment and shall bear all medical and living costs during treatment"; disability or death also entails one-time compensation. Article 13 leaves only one exception: literary and sports units may, with the consent of parents or other guardians, hire professional literary or artistic performers and athletes under 16 (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2018). Compulsory Education Law (2018 second amendment, Articles 11, 14, and 58). <http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/sjzl_zcfg/zcfg_jyfl/202110/t20211029_575949.html> (published by the Ministry of Education); State Council (2002). Provisions on the Prohibition of Using Child Labor (State Council Order No. 364, Articles 2, 6, 7, 10, and 13). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2002/content_61798.htm>
- Note: The beneficiary is yourself. Working after age 16 is lawful. But positions willing to take a 16-year-old without educational credentials are exactly the kind described in Entry 10 of this section (choose automation-resistant skills): fixed procedures, most easily replaced by machines. The overtime pay, annual leave, and workplace injury benefits discussed in Section 19 all require a labor relationship and evidence. Illegal work has neither, so those sums are unobtainable

### 2. Also fold "is schooling useful" into the mortality ledger: each additional year of education reduces adult mortality risk by about 1.9%
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. What is spent is the time and energy of those additional years of study; the tuition portion is in Entry 4 of this section (vocational school tuition and stipends). The hard part is continuing to study, without dropping out midway
- Plain-language summary: Each additional year of schooling reduces adult mortality risk by an average of about 1.9%. The effect is most pronounced for those 18 to 49, at about 2.9% per year; those over 70 only 0.8%. Those who can keep studying usually have better underlying conditions; how much of this is the effect of schooling itself cannot be separated out
- Benefit: A systematic review and meta-analysis, gathering prior studies and pooling them. Search range January 1980 to May 2023; ultimately including 603 studies, 70 sites in 59 countries, and 10,355 observations. Education and adult mortality (all-cause mortality) show a dose-response relationship (the more schooling, the lower the mortality). Each additional year of education reduces mortality risk by an average of 1.9% (95% uncertainty interval 1.8–2.0, true value roughly in this range). For those 18 to 49, each additional year reduces risk by 2.9% (2.8–3.0). For those over 70, 0.8% (0.6–1.0). No difference was seen between men and women. Stratifying by country development level (sociodemographic index) also showed no difference
- Evidence grade: A (contested)
- Source: IHME-CHAIN Collaborators (2024). Effects of education on adult mortality: a global systematic review and meta-analysis. The Lancet Public Health, 9(3), e155-e165. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-2667(23)00306-7>
- Note: Contested. The authors themselves report publication bias (p<0.0001), meaning that studies with favorable results are more likely to be published. Results across studies also vary widely. The included studies are all observational follow-ups, not randomized trials. Years of schooling are entangled with family conditions, region, and occupation; how much of this 1.9% is the schooling itself, and how much is "those who can keep studying had better conditions to begin with," cannot be separated by such studies. So it strongly indicates "more schooling and living longer go in the same direction," but does not equal "if you go to school you will extend your life by 1.9%." The beneficiary is yourself

### 3. Before judging whether "degrees have depreciated," first look at the national educational structure: only 15,467 per 100,000 people have college-level education or above
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Just glance at the figures below
- Plain-language summary: The 2020 census shows that nationwide, only 15,467 per 100,000 people have reached junior college (dazhuan) or above — about 15%. Those with junior high education total 34,507 — the most populous tier. Those aged 15 and above have an average of 9.91 years of schooling, just past junior high graduation. So junior high education in China is not "good enough" — it is the most ordinary tier
- Benefit: The standard reference time of the Seventh National Census is November 1, 2020. Of the national population, 218,360,767 people have university (junior college or above) education. Senior high (including secondary specialized) 213,005,258. Junior high 487,163,489. Primary 349,658,828. Compared with the Sixth Census in 2010, per 100,000 people those with university education rose from 8,930 to 15,467. Senior high rose from 14,032 to 15,088. Junior high fell from 38,788 to 34,507. Primary fell from 26,779 to 24,767. Average years of schooling of those aged 15 and above rose from 9.08 to 9.91. Illiteracy rate fell from 4.08% to 2.67%
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: National Bureau of Statistics and the Leading Group Office of the Seventh National Census of the State Council (2021). Communiqué of the Seventh National Census (No. 6) — Educational Attainment of the Population. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/tjgb/rkpcgb/qgrkpcgb/202302/t20230206_1902006.html>
- Note: These figures only indicate how rare or common degrees are in the population. Whether studying is worth it for any specific person should be calculated using the method in Entry 6 of this section (how to calculate "study or work"). Also note: the census counts the entire national population, including the elderly and children. The proportion of young people with university education is much higher than this figure, so comparing yourself to your peers using this number will overstate your advantage. Conversely, using it to refute "studying is useless" is valid, because the denominator is exactly the entire labor force competing with you for jobs. The beneficiary is yourself

### 4. Calculate "cannot afford school" against policy first: vocational school tuition is mostly already waived; stipends are 2,300 yuan; student loans up to 20,000 yuan per year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before the school year begins, go to the school's student financial aid office and ask once, submit materials once
- Plain-language summary: In public vocational schools, rural students, urban agricultural-major students, and financially struggling students already have tuition waived. Vocational and ordinary high school stipends are 2,300 yuan per year starting spring 2025. In university, stipends are 3,700 yuan per year and student loans up to 20,000 yuan per year. Money shortages can be deferred or resolved another way; rarely must you drop out and work right now
- Benefit: The vocational school tuition waiver has been policy since fall 2012. The original text reads: "For all rural (including county and town) students in grades one, two, and three of full-time formal-status enrollment in public secondary vocational schools, urban agricultural-major students, and students with financial difficulties, tuition is waived (excluding students in arts-related performance majors)." In 2024, the student financial aid policy was adjusted. The national stipend for undergraduate and junior college students rose from 3,300 yuan to 3,700 yuan per student per year. The National Endeavor Scholarship rose from 5,000 yuan to 6,000 yuan. Both effective from fall 2024 (Endeavor Scholarship from 2024). The national stipend for secondary vocational schools and the national stipend for ordinary high schools both rose from 2,000 yuan to 2,300 yuan per student per year, effective spring 2025. National student loans were also adjusted, from fall 2024. For undergraduate and junior college students, "the maximum loan amount per student per year increased from no more than 16,000 yuan to no more than 20,000 yuan." For graduate students, "from no more than 20,000 yuan to no more than 25,000 yuan." The interest rate is the Loan Prime Rate (LPR) for the same period and tier minus 70 basis points (i.e., 0.7 percentage points lower) (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ministry of Finance, National Development and Reform Commission, Ministry of Education, and Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (2012). Opinions on Expanding the Scope of Tuition Waiver Policy for Secondary Vocational Education and Further Improving the National Student Stipend System (Cai Jiao [2012] No. 376). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2013/content_2332779.htm>; Relevant bureau heads of the Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Education, and Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (2024). Q&A on Adjusting the Student Financial Aid Policy. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202410/content_6983672.htm>; Ministry of Finance, Ministry of Education, People's Bank of China, and National Financial Regulatory Administration (2024). Notice on Adjusting and Improving National Student Loan Policy (Cai Jiao [2024] No. 188). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202411/content_6984812.htm>
- Note: The beneficiary is yourself and your children. Exactly whom the tuition waiver covers, and the tiers of stipends, are set by each province. Private schools are subsidized at the local public-school standard for comparable majors; the difference still has to be paid by you, so verify against local documents before enrollment. Student loans are loans, not grants, and must be repaid after graduation. But they separate "cannot afford tuition right now" from "whether these years are worth it" into two separate questions — do not treat money shortage as the conclusion. Section 7 has covered training subsidies and temporary relief during unemployment; if household income has truly stopped, apply for those first

### 5. Not testing into ordinary high school is not the end of the road: vocational schools have articulated enrollment and separate exams; skill positions may also reduce educational requirements
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before filling out the high school entrance exam preferences, check your province's vocational education enrollment platform once
- Plain-language summary: The 2022 Vocational Education Law states plainly: vocational education and general education have equal status. Secondary vocational schools can enroll in articulated programs with higher vocational schools; higher vocational schools must also set aside enrollment quotas or hold separate exams specifically for secondary vocational graduates. Employers may not set thresholds that block you because you graduated from a vocational school. Insufficient score for ordinary high school is switching paths, not stopping at junior high education
- Benefit: Vocational Education Law (effective May 1, 2022), Article 3: "Vocational education is a type of education with the same important status as general education." Article 37: Secondary vocational schools "may, in accordance with relevant state provisions, implement articulated enrollment and training with higher vocational school education in relevant majors." Higher vocational schools "may, in accordance with relevant state provisions, adopt a combined assessment of cultural literacy and vocational skills to enroll students." Provincial and above education administrative departments shall establish a unified vocational education enrollment platform, aggregating and publishing schools, major offerings, and enrollment, and providing query and application services. Article 53: "Students of vocational schools enjoy equal opportunities with students of ordinary schools at the same level in advancement, employment, and career development." Higher vocational schools and ordinary higher education institutions implementing vocational education "shall set aside an appropriate proportion in enrollment plans or adopt separate examination methods to specifically enroll vocational school graduates." "Employers may not set admission, hiring, or employment conditions that impede the equal employment and fair competition of vocational school graduates." In public institution open recruitment, positions with vocational skill level requirements "may appropriately lower the educational requirement" (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2022). Vocational Education Law (2022 revision, Articles 3, 37, and 53). <http://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/sjzl_zcfg/zcfg_jyfl/202204/t20220421_620064.html> (published by the Ministry of Education)
- Note: The beneficiary is yourself and your children. These are statutory channels, not guarantees of admission. Which majors have articulated programs, what proportion is set aside for separate enrollment, and which subjects are tested in the vocational college entrance exam are all set by each province. Before applying, you must check your province's current vocational education enrollment platform and enrollment plans — do not extrapolate from other provinces' experience. When choosing secondary vocational majors, apply the dimension from Entry 10 of this section (choose automation-resistant skills): majors that require on-site presence and operate in non-standard environments are harder to replace. To choose a school, see whether it is in your province's current vocational education enrollment plan. Verify any certificate it issues using the three steps in Entry 8 (check whether the certificate is in the catalog). Any enrollment promotion promising "guaranteed placement" or "guaranteed pass" is to be rejected outright

### 6. Frame "study or work" as a calculation: three years of early wages set against the annual income gap over the following decades
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Sit down and calculate once, about half an hour
- Plain-language summary: Lay the two options side by side on paper. Early work earns three years of low-threshold wages, only this once. Continuing study returns the additional annual income earned over each following year, multiplied by the remaining 30 to 40 years. In urban China, each additional year of schooling raises income by an average of 4.0% (in 1988) to 10.2% (in 2001). The data are old; treat them as a rough indication
- Benefit: This is an estimate for urban China from 1988 to 2001. How much one additional year of schooling raises income is called the return on education. The original text reads "rising substantially from 4.0% per year of schooling in 1988 to 10.2% in 2001." The rise occurred mainly after 1992. It reflects the widening gap (higher education wage premium) earned by those who reached university over those who did not. This is the China-local magnitude available when converting "one more year of study" into an annual income gap. For the global average, see Entry 7 of this section (the global average return per additional year of education)
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Zhang J, Zhao Y, Park A, Song X (2005). Economic returns to schooling in urban China, 1988 to 2001. Journal of Comparative Economics, 33(4), 730-752. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jce.2005.05.008>
- Note: Before calculating, first deduct the tuition waivers and stipends from Entry 4 of this section (vocational school tuition and stipends), and additionally the ledger from Entry 2 of this section (more years of education and mortality). The data go up to 2001 and cover only urban areas; they cannot be used directly as today's figures. The 9% global average in Entry 7 of this section is the same. If you truly apply this method, the conclusion will vary by person. High tuition, mismatch between major and position, and long study years may indeed make it not worth it, but that only shows this specific program is not worth it, not that studying is useless. Several other factors cannot fit into the formula: statutory educational thresholds, see Entry 5 of this section (articulated enrollment and separate exams for vocational schools); replaceability, see Entry 10 (choose automation-resistant skills); whether you can change industries. The beneficiary is yourself

### 7. First memorize the baseline: the global average private return per additional year of education (the portion that lands on your own income) is about 9% per year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Memorize this number; use it as a yardstick against various "learn something" proposals
- Plain-language summary: By the worldwide average, one additional year of schooling raises personal income by about 9% per year; the return for women has consistently been higher than for men. This number refers to formal schooling; enrolling in a course cannot be directly compared. Use it as a yardstick: a course that asks you to spend 30,000 yuan and half a year should be evaluated by whether the income change it brings matches one year of schooling
- Benefit: A review covering 1950 to 2014, 139 countries, and 1,120 estimates calculated that "the global average private return per additional year of education is 9% per year." Private return is the portion that lands on the individual's own income: one additional year of schooling raises income by an average of about 9%. The private return on higher education is rising. The social return on education (the portion that lands on society as a whole rather than the individual) remains high. The average return for women remains consistently higher than for men
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Psacharopoulos G, Patrinos HA (2018). Returns to investment in education: a decennial review of the global literature. Education Economics, 26(5), 445-458. <https://doi.org/10.1080/09645292.2018.1484426>; World Bank Policy Research Working Paper 8402. <https://doi.org/10.1596/1813-9450-8402>
- Note: This is the global average for "one more year of schooling"; it cannot be treated as the return on "taking a course," nor is it a China-specific figure — it can only be used as a magnitude reference. Its use is to give all training proposals a yardstick: a course that asks you to spend 30,000 yuan and half a year should be evaluated by whether it can produce an income change equivalent to one year of formal education. Also note that the global average masks enormous variation across majors and across countries

### 8. Before paying for a certificate, first check whether it is in the National Vocational Qualification Catalog or on the list of evaluation institutions registered with the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Check once, ten-odd minutes
- Plain-language summary: Some organizations, in the name of new occupations and skill levels, run irregular classes, charge irregular fees, and issue certificates excessively, even impersonating authoritative bodies — the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security has specifically targeted this. Before registering, check in three steps: whether the occupation is in the National Vocational Qualification Catalog; whether the issuing body is an evaluation organization registered with the human resources and social security department; and whether the certificate number can be found on the official platform. If not found, the registration fee is wasted
- Benefit: The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security specifically conducted a nationwide campaign for this. The document names the irregularities: "some institutions and units, in the name of new-occupation vocational qualifications and vocational skill levels, arbitrarily run training, evaluation, and certification activities, charging irregular fees and issuing certificates excessively; some institutions and units engage in false or exaggerated promotion, and even impersonate authoritative bodies to organize training, evaluation, and the issuance of certificates." Campaign measures include joining with the cyberspace administration, public security, and market regulation departments to investigate, "severely cracking down on the evaluation and certification activities of fake appraisal institutions and fake MOHRSS-registered evaluation organizations." Non-compliant promotions on websites and new media are shut down in accordance with the law. The document also requires "guiding the public to understand certificate query channels and methods, and to judge the lawful compliance and value of certificates"
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (2022). Notice on Carrying Out Special Governance of "Mountain-Fortress Certificates" for Technical and Skill Categories. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/18/content_5679641.htm>
- Note: Verify in this order. First check whether the occupation is in the National Vocational Qualification Catalog; if not, it is not a national vocational qualification. Then check whether the issuing body is an evaluation organization registered with the human resources and social security department. Finally, on the official skill talent evaluation certificate query platform, check whether the certificate number can be found. Anything not findable — do not register. Those bearing the words "international," "senior," or "registered," and those promising "guaranteed pass" or "income from hanging the certificate," carry the highest risk

### 9. Prioritize government-subsidized training channels; do not start by paying out of pocket for a commercial class
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money, or substantially reduced. Training itself takes time. The hard part is resisting the impulse to enroll and pay out of pocket; first check the subsidy catalog
- Plain-language summary: From 2025 to the end of 2027, the state plans to conduct more than 30 million person-times of subsidized training. The money comes from multiple channels: the employment assistance fund, the unemployment insurance fund, and enterprise employee education funds, among others. The more in-demand the occupation and the higher the training cost, the higher the subsidy. Those employed should first ask their employer; everyone else should first look through the local human resources and social security bureau's subsidy catalog, then decide what to learn
- Benefit: The national scale is clear: from 2025 to the end of 2027, "focusing on high-end, precision, advanced, and urgent industries, key employment groups, and the like, conduct 30 million person-times or more of subsidized training." Funding sources were consolidated into a single package: employment assistance funds, unemployment insurance funds, industry development funds, east-west cooperation funds, enterprise employee education funds, and so on. Subsidy levels are set by the urgency and shortage of the occupation (trade) and the training cost — different occupations get different amounts. Enterprises are also required "to set aside and use employee education funds in accordance with regulations, ensuring that 60% or more goes to frontline employee education and training"
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Leading Group for Employment Promotion and Labor Protection of the State Council (2025). Guiding Opinions on Carrying Out Large-Scale Vocational Skill Enhancement Training. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032222.htm>
- Note: Exactly how to claim and how much can be claimed is set by each province. The entry point is the "vocational training subsidy catalog" on the local human resources and social security bureau's website and the public training bases. Section 7 has covered the vocational training subsidy during unemployment (the same person may receive up to 3 times) and the social insurance subsidy. Those employed should first ask about their employer's employee education fund. Checking the subsidy catalog before deciding what to learn saves far more money than choosing first and then looking for subsidies

### 10. When choosing a skill, prioritize "does it require manual dexterity and on-the-spot judgment" — these are the hardest to automate away
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before choosing, filter the work you want to learn through this dimension
- Plain-language summary: By US estimates, about 47% of jobs are at high risk of being taken over by computers; later estimates that broke down by specific tasks yielded much lower proportions. The barriers machines struggle with are three: manual dexterity, coming up with new things, and dealing with people. So work that requires on-site presence, non-standard environments, and on-the-spot judgment is harder to replace; positions with fixed procedures and primarily information-processing tasks are at the highest risk
- Benefit: Estimating "how likely this job can be handed to a computer" for 702 US occupations one by one (computability probability assessment), about 47% of jobs fall in the high-risk range. The model's determinants of risk are three barriers that machines currently cannot cross, which the authors call "engineering bottlenecks." First, perception and manipulation, including finger dexterity, hand dexterity, and working in tight spaces and awkward postures. Second, creative intelligence (the ability to come up with new things). Third, social intelligence (the ability to deal with people). So work in non-standard environments requiring manual skill and on-the-spot judgment has consistently lower replacement probability — not an accident of individual occupations. Positions with fixed procedures and primarily information-processing tasks are at the highest risk
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Frey CB, Osborne MA (2017). The future of employment: how susceptible are jobs to computerisation? Technological Forecasting and Social Change, 114, 254-280. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.techfore.2016.08.019>
- Note: Contested. This study is a 2013 unpublished working paper; the scoring rates "whether an entire occupation can be taken over by machines." Later studies revised to estimate by individual tasks and yielded much lower high-risk proportions. It also does not say in what year this will happen. When using it, do not fixate on the 47% number; focus on the three barriers it identifies that block machines. On-site installation, inspection, and care are more automation-resistant than pure paperwork — that is why. So "learning a skill" should prioritize those that require on-site presence and face non-standard environments

### 11. Before learning, confirm whether this skill has a grade evaluation, and whether the grade can be converted into money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Check once and that is enough
- Plain-language summary: The state is promoting "new eight-grade worker," requiring enterprises to link position wages to skill grades; outstanding employees may even be awarded grades directly without regard to seniority or qualifications. This is a policy direction; localities and enterprises vary widely. Skills with national occupational standards and grade pathways are the only ones that will allow salary increases by grade going forward — check before learning
- Benefit: The state is promoting the "new eight-grade worker" vocational skill grade system. The documents explicitly require linking grades with compensation: "strengthen a skill-oriented compensation distribution system... guide enterprises to implement a distribution method that doubly links position compensation with position value and skill grade." Localities should "expand the scale and frequency of evaluation and hiring above senior worker level." Eligible enterprises may conduct their own skill grade evaluations of employees. For outstanding employees, "corresponding vocational skill grades may be recognized directly, breaking conditions of years of work, grade, qualifications, and the like" (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Leading Group for Employment Promotion and Labor Protection of the State Council (2025). Guiding Opinions on Carrying Out Large-Scale Vocational Skill Enhancement Training. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032222.htm>; Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (2021). Implementation Plan for the "Skill China Action." <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-07/06/content_5622619.htm>
- Note: This is a policy direction, not a wage figure already realized; implementation varies widely across localities and enterprises. What you can act on is: when choosing a skill, see whether it has a corresponding national occupational standard and grade pathway; skills with a pathway can later have salary increases by grade. "Skills" without grades and without industry entry barriers can only rely on you finding work after learning them

### 12. Do not treat "high wages online" as a sign of shortage; go check the local shortage occupation catalog and subsidy catalog
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Check two local documents
- Plain-language summary: How much subsidy a locality gives for each trade is set by the urgency of shortage and the training cost, so the subsidy amount reflects whether the locality is short of workers. "How much someone makes in a certain trade" online is mostly the listed price on recruitment platforms or self-reports by individuals. What you see is mostly those who have made money, and you have not figured in hours, commuting, occupational disease, or peak/off-peak seasons
- Benefit: The subsidy amount itself is the official pricing of "which trades are short of workers." Policy requires "improving a differentiated subsidy mechanism set according to the urgency of shortage and training cost of the occupation (trade)," and localities determine their local subsidy-based vocational skill training project scope accordingly. So how much the subsidy for the same trade differs across cities directly reflects the local shortage level
- Evidence grade: C
- Source: Leading Group for Employment Promotion and Labor Protection of the State Council (2025). Guiding Opinions on Carrying Out Large-Scale Vocational Skill Enhancement Training. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032222.htm>
- Note: TODO (to verify: official data sources for average wages by occupation and shortage occupation rankings nationwide — relevant MOHRSS pages could not be obtained this time). The "how much someone makes in a certain trade" online almost all comes from listed prices on recruitment platforms or personal self-reports. Those you can see are mostly those who have made money, and neither includes hours, commuting, occupational disease, or peak/off-peak seasons. To see real demand, check the subsidy catalog on the local human resources and social security bureau's website, then check the job demand rankings of public employment service agencies. ### 13. With the same money and time, prioritize short-cycle programs that can lead directly to a job

### 14. After studying, first close the book and test yourself — do not reread
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money; total time does not increase either. Take the time you would have spent rereading, and convert it to closing the book and writing from memory or doing practice questions. The hard part is that self-testing exposes what you have not retained on the spot, which feels worse than rereading
- Plain-language summary: Reading a second time after reading feels familiar, but the retention is weak. Closing the book and testing yourself once is clearly more effective; adding quizzes to class produces a medium-magnitude improvement in grades. When ranking ten commonly used study methods, self-testing ranks in the highest tier. After reading a section, first write out the key points from memory, then go back and check
- Benefit: A meta-analysis of 222 independent studies and 48,478 students: classroom quizzing raises student academic performance, with an effect size of medium magnitude (g = 0.499). The magnitude of effect is influenced by several conditions: what method the control group used, whether the quiz format matches the final exam, whether materials are matched, whether corrective feedback is given, how many times quizzing is repeated, where and when it is measured, intervention duration, and what experimental design is used. Another review evaluating ten commonly used study methods rates "practice testing" and "distributed practice" as the highest tier (high utility), on the grounds that they are effective across learners of different ages and abilities, and have been validated across multiple assessment formats and in real educational settings
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Yang C, Luo L, Vadillo MA, Yu R, Shanks DR (2021). Testing (quizzing) boosts classroom learning: A systematic and meta-analytic review. Psychological Bulletin, 147(4), 399–435. <https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000309>; Dunlosky J, Rawson KA, Marsh EJ, Nathan MJ, Willingham DT (2013). Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: Promising Directions From Cognitive and Educational Psychology. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266>
- Note: Self-testing is for forcing retrieval from your brain, not for grading. Method: after reading a section, close the book, first write the key points from memory, then go back and check. Corrective feedback makes it more effective, so look carefully at where you went wrong when checking answers. The benefit magnitude is rated "high" based on the effect size and tier. These two pieces of evidence measure grades, not hours, so the threshold for time saved is not applied. Self-testing and Entry 15 (spread the same time across several days) are the two highest-rated methods in the same review and work best combined

### 15. Spread the same time across several days; do not learn it all at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money; total time also unchanged. What changes is only the arrangement: the same 3 hours, divided into 1 hour per day over 3 days. The hard part is starting a few days earlier and not putting it off until the last night
- Plain-language summary: The same time, spread across several days, is remembered better than learned all at once. 317 experiments have tested this, and the conclusion is consistent. One practical rule: the longer you need to remember it, the longer the review interval should be. If the test is tomorrow, review today; if the test is a month away, review every few days
- Benefit: A meta-analysis aggregating 184 articles, 317 experiments, and 839 assessments of distributed practice effects. It compared two things: learning all at once versus spread across sessions (spacing effect), and short versus long intervals (delay effect). The conclusion: the review interval and "how long you need to remember it" together determine final-test retention: the longer the retention required, the longer the optimal review interval. Another review evaluating ten commonly used study methods rates distributed practice as the highest tier (high utility)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Cepeda NJ, Pashler H, Vul E, Wixted JT, Rohrer D (2006). Distributed practice in verbal recall tasks: A review and quantitative synthesis. Psychological Bulletin, 132(3), 354–380. <https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354>; Dunlosky J, Rawson KA, Marsh EJ, Nathan MJ, Willingham DT (2013). Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266>
- Note: Combine with Entry 14 (close the book and self-test) for best effect: spread the self-testing itself across several days. The materials in the Cepeda meta-analysis are mostly word-list memory; the optimal interval for complex content has no general-purpose number of days. "The further off the test, the longer the interval" is the rule the original text gives, without a specific schedule. Cramming the night before works on the spot, but the forgetting is fast

### 16. Do not treat highlighting, rereading, or writing summaries as your main study method
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money; in fact it saves time. The hard part is that these are the most顺手 things to do, and they give the most "I am studying" feeling
- Plain-language summary: Students most commonly use highlighting and rereading, but in the ranking of ten commonly used methods, both fall in the lowest tier. Writing summaries, keyword mnemonics, and imagery for text are also in the lowest tier. They are not completely useless, but the conditions under which they work are too narrow; the same time is better spent on self-testing
- Benefit: A review evaluating ten commonly used study methods assigns tiers based on "whether the effect generalizes across study conditions, learner characteristics, materials, and assessment formats." Rated in the highest tier are practice testing and distributed practice. Rated in the middle tier are elaborative interrogation, self-explanation, and interleaved practice. Rated in the lowest tier of five are: writing summaries, highlighting (or underlining), keyword mnemonics, imagery for text, and rereading. The review states plainly: most students say they use rereading and highlighting, but these two do not reliably improve performance, and should be replaced with other methods, such as using practice testing instead of rereading
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Dunlosky J, Rawson KA, Marsh EJ, Nathan MJ, Willingham DT (2013). Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: Promising Directions From Cognitive and Educational Psychology. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266>
- Note: Highlighting can be used to "mark which spots to self-test later"; what is problematic is treating highlighting itself as learning. The review also notes that the lowest tier does not equal zero — it is just that the conditions under which they work are narrow and need more research. For replacement actions, see Entry 14 (close the book and self-test) and Entry 15 (spread the same time across several days)

### 17. Mix several problem types when practicing; do not do twenty of one kind in a row
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. Change "doing the practice book in order by problem type" to "doing it shuffled." The hard part is that mixed practice has lower on-the-spot accuracy, which feels like regression
- Plain-language summary: Doing a large string of the same type at once feels smooth on the spot, but later you may not be able to distinguish which method to use. Mixing several types yields better long-term results, a medium magnitude. But this technique is selective by material: most effective for images and classification, smaller for math problems, and for vocabulary it is actually worse than blocked practice
- Benefit: A multilevel meta-analysis including 59 studies, 238 effect sizes, and 158 samples. The overall effect of interleaved practice relative to blocked practice is of medium magnitude (Hedges' g = 0.42). By material: painting best (g = 0.67), other visual materials also good; math tasks smaller effect (g = 0.34); the overall effect for expository text and taste materials was not significant; for vocabulary, blocked practice was actually better (g = −0.39). Regression analysis showed: the more similar between categories, the more dissimilar within categories, and the more complex the material, the greater the benefit of interleaving
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Brunmair M, Richter T (2019). Similarity matters: A meta-analysis of interleaved learning and its moderators. Psychological Bulletin, 145(11), 1029–1052. <https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000209>
- Note: The review cited in Entry 16 (do not treat highlighting as your main method) rates interleaved practice in the middle tier, on the grounds that research was not yet sufficient at the time. Combining the two, the use is: content that requires "recognizing which kind and which method" should be mixed — e.g., math problem types, case diagnoses, statute application; content requiring rote should be done in blocks — e.g., vocabulary, term definitions

### 18. Do not choose study methods based on "I am a visual type, he is an auditory type"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. Just stop choosing materials and courses by this classification, and stop paying for style assessments
- Plain-language summary: Many people believe each person has their own learning style — visual, auditory, and so on — and that teaching to style produces better learning. Studies that rigorously test this are very few, and almost none support it; several produce the opposite result. People do have preferences, but there is no evidence that teaching to preference produces better learning. Do not pay for a style assessment
- Benefit: A review commissioned to evaluate the evidence on learning styles points out: to prove that "teaching to learning styles" is effective, you would first have to group students by style, then randomly assign each group to different teaching methods, then test with the same exam. The criterion for effectiveness is a specific interaction: the method most effective for one style is not the most effective for another. The review concludes: such interactions are almost never found; although the literature on learning styles is vast, studies with sound experimental design are very few, and several of them produce results directly contrary to the "matching hypothesis." The authors therefore conclude that the existing evidence is insufficient to support the use of learning style assessment in educational practice
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Pashler H, McDaniel M, Rohrer D, Bjork R (2008). Learning Styles: Concepts and Evidence. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 9(3), 105–119. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x>
- Note: The review also notes that this does not mean all versions of learning style theory have been tested and refuted — many have not been tested at all. What to avoid is paying for style assessments and choosing training courses by style. For choosing training, see Entry 9 (prioritize government-subsidized training channels) and Entry 13 (prioritize short-cycle programs that lead directly to a job)

### 19. For content to memorize, test yourself in the form you will use it later — do not just memorize it once and call it done
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money; no extra time either. What changes is only the practice form: swap "memorize it once as is" for "test yourself in the form you will use it later." The hard part is first thinking through how this material will actually be used
- Plain-language summary: Closing the book and testing yourself has benefits that extend beyond the few words you memorized. A meta-analysis of 192 contrasts, pooling over ten thousand people, finds it also works for items where the question type is changed and for application and reasoning problems, with a small-to-medium magnitude. But there are two situations where it is almost useless: swapping the stimulus and the response, and content only ever seen in the textbook that was never tested
- Benefit: A meta-analysis aggregating over 40 years of research, including 67 published and unpublished articles, 122 experiments, and 192 transfer effect sizes, totaling 10,382 people. Compared to a "see it again but no test" control, practice testing produces learning that does transfer to other contexts, with effect size d=0.40 (95% CI 0.31 to 0.50). The strongest transfer occurs when: testing in a different question format, on application and reasoning problems, on medical diagnosis problems, and on meaning-adjacent cue words. The weakest transfer occurs when: swapping the stimulus and response, on materials seen at initial study but never tested, and on example problems. Among the moderators, whether the response format at practice matches the test, whether retrieval practice with detailed feedback was done, and whether the initial test was answered correctly all strongly influence whether positive transfer occurs. The authors also corrected for publication bias in two ways; the moderator effect sizes are little affected, but the intercept estimate is markedly suppressed: when none of the above conditions hold, positive transfer is often not observed
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Pan SC, Rickard TC (2018). Transfer of test-enhanced learning: meta-analytic review and synthesis. Psychological Bulletin, 144(7), 710-756. <https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000151>
- Note: The word "memorize" usually conflates two things: going over material again (rereading, highlighting), and closing the book to actively recall. The former is rated lowest in the review that ranks ten study methods; for the rating itself, see Entry 16 (do not treat highlighting, rereading, or writing summaries as your main method). What to do is the latter: close the book and actively recall, specifically in Entry 14 (close the book and self-test), and spread across several days, see Entry 15 (spread across days). The method is to first think clearly about how it will be used later: do you need to pick it out on an exam, or recognize it on the spot — then test yourself in that form. Reverse-direction testing is also worth doing, because in this meta-analysis transfer in the reverse direction was weakest, so both directions must be practiced
