# 31. What Paths Exist After Eighteen

This section calculates money and time; the entry on refusing military service also affects personal freedom. This is a route map, not a manual for any single path. This section first lays out the legal thresholds for each path side by side, then item-by-item writes what is only useful once you know about it: what you can get from military service, what channels besides the civil service exam lead into the system, which two majors at college-application time lead directly to a staffing quota (编制), whether a credential can be made up later, what kind of company to find for overseas work, what happens to social insurance if you do not enter an organization, and whether the state will cover part of the interest on a loan if you have no starting capital.

The paths available after eighteen extend far beyond what this section covers. Only those with national-level public documents whose thresholds and benefits can be verified item by item are included here. Joining state-owned enterprises, factories, working as a seafarer, freelancing, continuing on to high school and university — these are also viable paths. They simply lack unified, verifiable documents, and this book will not write conditions for them from impression.

Which path to choose is your own business. This section provides only two things: where the threshold is written, and what deadlines and qualifications lapse once the path is taken. For how to calculate the question of studying versus working, and how to choose a skill, see Section 23. For overtime pay, work injury, and severance after entering a workplace, see Section 19. For startup capital and exit for opening a shop or company, see Section 12. For study abroad, see Section 32. For protections after unemployment, see Section 7. None of these are repeated.

### 1. First match against the thresholds: the age and education requirements for the twelve paths are all set in writing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Spend ten minutes matching yourself to see whether you can reach them.
- Plain-language summary: The age and education thresholds for these twelve paths are all written into documents. Work requires being at least 16. Military service requires men to be 18, with the age relaxed to 24 for university graduates. Civil service requires ages 18 to 35 with junior-college education or above. The two paths of self-study exam (自考) and registering as an individual business have neither education nor age requirements.
- Benefit: Military service: per the Military Service Law, "male citizens who have reached the age of eighteen shall be enlisted for active service; those not enlisted in that year may be enlisted for active service up to age twenty-two. The enlistment age for graduates of regular institutions of higher education may be relaxed to twenty-four, and for graduate students to twenty-six." Civil service: Article 18 of the Provisions on Civil Servant Recruitment. Application conditions include "age eighteen or above and thirty-five or below" and "with educational attainment at the junior-college level or above." These two may be adjusted with approval from the provincial or higher civil service authority. Article 19 lists five categories of persons who may not apply, such as those with criminal penalties, those dismissed from public office, and those on the list of joint-punishment subjects for serious credit violations. Firefighters: per the Measures for Recruiting Firefighters for the National Comprehensive Fire and Rescue Force. Article 5: "age 18 or above and 22 or below" and "with educational attainment at the senior-high level or above." Article 6: "those with junior-college education or above, retired soldiers of the PLA and the People's Armed Police... age may be relaxed to 24." Military civilian personnel: Article 9 of the Regulations on Civilian Personnel of the Chinese People's Liberation Army, with basic conditions including "age 18 or above." Article 19 specifies that for first recruitment to lower-rank positions, the maximum age is 35. "Lower-rank positions" means entry-level positions. Public institutions: Article 8 of the Regulations on Personnel Management of Public Institutions — "new hiring of staff by public institutions shall be openly recruited to the public." Individual businesses: Regulations on Promoting the Development of Individual Industrial and Commercial Households — "citizens with business capability... who are lawfully registered as individual industrial and commercial households are subject to these Regulations." Self-study exam (自考): the Interim Regulations on Higher Education Self-Study Examinations. Article 3: "Citizens of the People's Republic of China, without restriction by sex, age, ethnicity, race, or level of education already attained, may take the higher education self-study examination in accordance with these Regulations." Article 20: "currently enrolled students at full-time schools of all types and levels may not apply." State-funded normal students: Article 2 of the Implementation Measures for Integrated Undergraduate-Graduate State-Funded Normal Student Education at Ministry of Education-affiliated Normal Universities. It enrolls only at six MOE-affiliated normal universities — Beijing Normal University, East China Normal University, Northeast Normal University, Central China Normal University, Shaanxi Normal University, and Southwest University. The undergraduate and graduate phases are designed as a continuum with staged assessment, proceeding from undergraduate directly into graduate study. Tuition and accommodation during the enrollment period are borne by central finance, with a living allowance also provided. Article 5: "state-funded normal students are admitted through an early batch" — admitted before the regular batches. Directed medical students: Notice on Properly Conducting the Employment Placement and Performance Management of Rural Order-directed tuition-free medical students. It states: "after admission and before receiving the admission notice, the student must sign an agreement with the training school and the county-level health administrative department... and human resources and social security department at the directed place of employment." Overseas work: Regulations on the Administration of Foreign Labor Cooperation. Article 5: engaging in foreign labor cooperation requires approval by the commerce authority and obtaining a business qualification. Article 7, item 4: "those who have not lawfully obtained a Foreign Labor Cooperation Business Qualification Certificate and completed registration may not engage in foreign labor cooperation." A company without this qualification certificate that organizes workers to go abroad is itself operating illegally. Article 8, item 2: "no unit or individual may organize labor workers to work abroad under the name of business, tourism, or study." Platform gig work and flexible employment have no statutory education threshold; the basis for self-enrollment in social insurance and for occupational injury protection is in Entries 11 and 12 of this section. The Compulsory Education Law "prohibits employers from recruiting children of compulsory-education age who should be receiving compulsory education" (national).

### 2. Complete draft registration (兵役登记) by October 31 of the year you turn eighteen; conscript (义务兵) active service is two years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Registration is completed online in a few minutes. Actually enlisting costs two full-time years; during those two years you cannot simultaneously do anything else.
- Plain-language summary: Draft registration (兵役登记) is a legal obligation, not enlistment; registering does not mean you must serve. Males who turn 18 in the current year must complete online registration by October 31. Without registration, the county will order you to correct within a deadline; if still not done, you will be compelled to perform military service duty and fined. Actual service lasts two years for conscripts (义务兵).
- Benefit: Registration: per the Provisions on Draft Registration Work. It states: "the military service organs of the county, autonomous county, city without districts, or municipal district people's governments shall, before October 31 each year, organize initial draft registration for male citizens of the administrative region who reach age 18 by December 31 of the current year." The same provisions also state: "initial draft registration is primarily conducted online, with citizens completing it independently through the draft registration network platform." What happens if you do not register: Article 57, item 1 of the Military Service Law. This item covers those who "refuse or evade draft registration." The penalty is "the county-level people's government shall order correction within a deadline; if correction is not made by the deadline, the county-level people's government shall compel performance of military service duty and impose a fine." The Military Service Law also states that "the active service term for conscripts (义务兵) is two years." "Based on the needs of the military and voluntary consent, citizens aged seventeen and under eighteen may be enlisted for active service." For non-commissioned officers (军士), "those who have served on active duty for twelve years or who meet other conditions stipulated by the state shall be placed in work by the county-level or higher local people's government at the place of resettlement." For non-commissioned officers, "those who have served on active duty for thirty years, or who reach age fifty-five, or who meet other conditions stipulated by the state, are placed in retirement." "Placed in work" means the government arranges a job; "retirement placement" means processing retirement. The Conscription Work Regulations state that "priority shall be given to enlisting graduates of regular institutions of higher education and those with special requirements for political, physical, or professional skills." The regulations also state: "citizens who are full-time enrolled students or recent graduates of regular institutions of higher education may enlist at the place of household registration before enrollment or at the location of the school" (national).

Conscription Work Regulations (State Council and Central Military Commission Order No. 759, Article 5). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2023/content_5753313.htm>
- Note: Registration and application are two different things, with very different costs. The set of sanctions prohibiting departure from the country for two years applies only to those who apply and then go back on it — see Entry 3 (refusing military service). Women are not required to complete initial draft registration; they are enlisted based on military needs. The conditions for enlisted soldiers to take military academy examinations and for outstanding soldiers to become officers (commissioning from the ranks) are written into internal military regulations that are not accessible externally. The Military Service Law only states two sentences: active-duty officers are selected from among "outstanding active-duty soldiers" and others; military academies admit cadets without restriction by enlistment age. Specific scores and quotas are not given in this book.

### 3. After applying, refusing military service means no exit abroad or returning to school for two years, and no civil service or state-owned enterprise employment
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. The cost is deciding whether to go before applying.
- Plain-language summary: Applying and then refusing to serve carries a chain of consequences. Civil service may not hire you; state-owned enterprises and public institutions may not recruit you. For two years you may not leave the country, may not continue schooling, and may not return to school. You will also be placed on the list of subjects with serious credit violations in fulfilling national defense obligations. So "apply first and decide later, and just back out if it doesn't work" is a very expensive idea.
- Benefit: Article 57, item 1, sub-item 2 of the Military Service Law covers "applicants for active service who refuse or evade enlistment." The penalty is the same: "the county-level people's government shall order correction within a deadline; if correction is not made by the deadline, the county-level people's government shall compel performance of military service duty and impose a fine." Item 2 of the same article: "those who commit the act described in item 2 above and refuse to correct may not be hired as civil servants or as staff managed under the Civil Servant Law of the People's Republic of China, may not be recruited or hired as staff of state-owned enterprises and public institutions, may not leave the country, continue schooling, or return to school for two years, and shall be placed on the list of subjects with serious credit violations in fulfilling national defense obligations for joint punishment." "Joint punishment" means multiple departments apply the above restrictions to the same list. Article 58 extends the same sanctions to those already enlisted. A military service member who refuses to perform duties or flees the forces with the intent of evading service is subject to the consequences depending on the outcome. Those expelled from the military, dismissed from military membership, or criminally prosecuted are punished under Article 57, item 2. Those expelled are also fined (national, effective October 1, 2021).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2021). Military Service Law (2021 revision, Articles 57, 58). <http://www.mod.gov.cn/regulatory/2021-08/20/content_4892491.htm> (published by the Ministry of National Defense)
- Note: This entry calculates personal freedom and employment eligibility, not money. This chain of sanctions applies only to two categories. One is "applicants for active service who refuse or evade enlistment." The other is those who, after enlistment, are expelled, dismissed from military membership, or criminally prosecuted. Those who simply failed to register are not included — draft registration is in Entry 2. The precondition for sanction is "refusal to correct," determined and punished by the county-level people's government. The fine amount is set locally and not stated in this book. Whether to go is your decision, but make it before applying.

### 4. The two most tangible benefits for university students in military service: tuition covered up to 20,000 yuan per year, student status retained for two years, and a dedicated graduate admissions plan
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. After discharge, submit materials per the school's requirements to apply — a trip or two.
- Plain-language summary: Currently enrolled students, recent graduates, and incoming students who enlist have their tuition during service paid by the state. For undergraduate/junior-college students, the maximum is 20,000 yuan per person per year; for graduate students, 25,000 yuan per person per year; over two years, that is up to 40,000 or 50,000 yuan. Student status is preserved, and you may enroll or return to school within two years of discharge. For graduate admissions, there is a dedicated plan that admits only veterans who were university students.
- Benefit: A 2024 notice from the Ministry of Finance and three other departments states that higher-education students serving in the military receive three benefits: tuition compensation, state student loan repayment for tuition, and tuition reduction. "Loan repayment" means the state repays the student loan for you. The standards "are correspondingly adjusted to no more than 20,000 yuan per person per year for undergraduate/junior-college students and no more than 25,000 yuan per person per year for graduate students." The new standards take effect from the fall 2024 semester. How much is actually compensated is determined by this sentence: "executed based on the higher of the tuition actually paid by the student or the state student loan obtained (including principal and all interest accrued before full repayment)." A further sentence: "the portion exceeding the standard is not eligible for compensation, repayment, or reduction." Student status: per the Veterans Support Law. It states: "active-duty military personnel who, before enlistment, had been admitted to a regular institution of higher education or were studying at one, retain admission eligibility or student status during active service, may enroll or return to school within two years of discharge, and may transfer to another major at the same institution in accordance with relevant state regulations." The law also states: "institutions of higher education, based on state-coordinated arrangements, may admit veterans through separately listed plans and separate recruitment." For graduate admissions: per a 2026 Ministry of Education notice. The plan is called the "Veteran University Student Soldiers Plan." The notice states: "within the overall scale of the national master's degree admissions plan, separately listed and dedicated for use; may not be converted to ordinary plans or other dedicated plans. Admissions institutions independently set... minimum initial-exam score requirements (generally no higher than the corresponding discipline/program ordinary-plan candidates' initial-exam score requirement)... with priority given under equal conditions to candidates with longer service." "Separately listed, dedicated for use" means these slots are set aside and may only be used to admit veteran university student soldiers. In 2026, this plan was undertaken by 532 regular institutions of higher education (national).

### 5. After discharge, only six categories of cases receive government job placement; those entering self-employment must report within 30 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Bring your discharge certificate and make a trip to the veterans affairs department at the place of resettlement.
- Plain-language summary: "Guaranteed assignment after military service" has long not been the general rule. Now only six categories receive government job placement; conscripts (义务兵) discharged after two years of service are, in the vast majority of cases, not among the six. The category that does not receive government job placement is called self-employment, and it has a hard deadline: within 30 days of discharge from active service, you must report. Without justifiable cause, exceeding 30 days constitutes forfeiture of resettlement benefits.
- Benefit: Article 25 of the Veterans Resettlement Regulations lists six categories to be placed in work by the people's government at the place of resettlement. The original text: "(1) non-commissioned officers who have served on active duty for 12 years; (2) those who personally received a medal or honorary title during active service; (3) those who personally received a third-class combat merit or above, or a second-class merit or above, during active service; (4) those who personally received a first-class commendation during active service; (5) those disabled by war and assessed at disability grade 5 to 8; (6) children of martyrs." The regulations also state: "the people's government at the place of resettlement shall complete the work placement of discharged NCOs and conscripts within 6 months of receiving them." Employers "shall sign a mid- to long-term employment contract or labor contract of no less than 3 years. Where an enterprise accepts a discharged NCO with 10 or more years of military service, it shall sign an open-ended labor contract." "Open-ended" means the contract has no end date. During the waiting period for placement, "a living subsidy shall be paid monthly at the local monthly minimum wage standard." Where you are not placed on the job on time for reasons not attributable to yourself, "a living allowance shall be paid monthly at no less than 80% of the average wage of personnel under equivalent conditions at the unit, until you take up the post." Cases not eligible for placement by work assignment include being expelled from the Chinese Communist Party and having received criminal punishment. The deadline for self-employment is Article 51: "discharged NCOs and conscripts entering self-employment shall, within 30 days from the date of approval of discharge, report to the veterans affairs authority at the place of resettlement. Failure to report on time without justifiable cause for more than 30 days shall be deemed forfeiture of resettlement benefits" (national, effective September 1, 2024).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council and Central Military Commission (2024). Veterans Resettlement Regulations (State Council and Central Military Commission Order No. 787, published July 29, 2024, effective September 1; Articles 25, 31, 32, 35, 51, 75). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2024/issue_11526/202408/content_6969192.html>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2020). Veterans Support Law (Articles 13, 14, 15). <http://www.mod.gov.cn/gfbw/fgwx/flfg/16048597.html> (published by the Ministry of National Defense)
- Note: On the same day these regulations took effect, the 2011 Regulations on Resettlement of Discharged Soldiers (State Council and Central Military Commission Order No. 608) were repealed. Many online guides still cite the old regulations, and the conditions have changed — the old version required "ordinary-time second-class merit or above, or wartime third-class merit or above." The 30-day deadline applies only to the self-employment category; other placement types require reporting "at the prescribed time." At reporting, you will be issued a Veterans Preferential Treatment Certificate, which connects to subsequent retirement pay, employment and entrepreneurship support, and education assistance.

### 6. Three ledgers after discharge: one-time retirement pay, military years counted as work years, and 20,000 yuan annual tax deduction for self-employment
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. At discharge, the military transfers out your social insurance relationship and funds. You then go to the social insurance handling agency (the social insurance window) at the place of resettlement to continue coverage.
- Plain-language summary: After discharge there are three ledgers. First is the one-time retirement pay for self-employment, paid according to active service years, with additional payments for those who received merits. Second, the years of service are not blank social insurance — military years count as work years, with the contribution years combined with pre-enlistment and post-discharge years. Third is tax: those who open their own shop can deduct up to 20,000 yuan in taxes per year for 3 years.
- Benefit: The first ledger: per the Veterans Resettlement Regulations. "For discharged NCOs and conscripts entering self-employment, a one-time retirement pay is issued based on active service years." Who pays: "specifically arranged by central finance, with the specific standard determined jointly by the State Council veterans work authority, the Political Work Department of the Central Military Commission, and the Ministry of Finance... and adjusted from time to time." The increment percentage has five tiers. Receiving a medal or honorary title: 25%; first-class combat merit or first-class commendation: 20%. Second-class combat merit, first-class merit, or second-class commendation: 15%. Third-class combat merit or second-class merit: 10%. Fourth-class combat merit or third-class merit: 5%. The regulations also state: "veterans who are not promptly employed may apply to the human resources and social security department at the place of household registration for unemployment insurance benefits in accordance with the law; active service years are deemed as contribution years, except for veterans placed in retirement or under support." "Deemed contribution years" means that even though you did not pay in during those years, it is counted as if you had. The second ledger: per the Veterans Support Law. "Active service years of veterans are counted as work years, and after discharge are combined with the work years at the employer." "Work years" is the number of years of work; service years and post-discharge years add together. The law also states that active service years, pre-enlistment, and post-discharge pension, medical, and unemployment contribution years are "combined in accordance with the law," and "the active service experience of discharged NCOs and conscripts is deemed grassroots work experience." The Military Insurance Law: "the active service years of military personnel are deemed as basic medical insurance contribution years for employees, combined with the contribution years of basic medical insurance for employees before enlistment and after discharge." The third ledger: per a 2023 announcement from the Ministry of Finance, State Tax Administration, and Ministry of Veterans Affairs (No. 14 of 2023). The announcement addresses self-employed discharged soldiers: "within 3 years (36 months, similarly hereinafter), a limit of 20,000 yuan per household per year shall be applied to deduct in sequence the VAT, urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharge, local education surcharge, and individual income tax actually payable for that year. The limit standard may be increased upward by up to 20%." "Deduction" means subtracting from the year's payable taxes in order, up to the limit — not a payment to you. For enterprises that hire such veterans, "the quota standard is 6,000 yuan per person per year, with an upward adjustment of up to 50%." The execution period is January 1, 2023 to December 31, 2027. Those not reaching the 3-year mark by expiration may continue to enjoy the benefit until the period is complete (national).

### 7. Entering the system has more than the civil service exam: completing two years in a grassroots service project makes you eligible for the annual ~10% dedicated recruitment plan
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. The cost is two years of grassroots service in townships and villages. During these two years you receive a fiscal subsidy, not a market wage — the hard part is enduring these two years.

### 13. Two paths that lock in formal staffing on college application day: state-funded normal students and directed medical students, at the cost of 6 years of service

### 14. To work overseas, first check whether the company has a Foreign Labor Cooperation Business Qualification: it may not collect a deposit from you
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->

Article 7: the list of qualification certificate holders is reported to the commerce authority under the State Council and notified to Chinese embassies and consulates abroad. This article also states: "those who have not lawfully obtained a Foreign Labor Cooperation Business Qualification Certificate and completed registration may not engage in foreign labor cooperation." Article 8: "no unit or individual may organize labor workers to work abroad under the name of business, tourism, or study." Article 9: enterprises shall, within 5 working days of registration, deposit in a designated bank "a foreign labor cooperation risk-disposition reserve of no less than 3 million yuan RMB"; the deposit list is published to the public. Article 10: the reserve is used to pay service fees that should be refunded, labor compensation, and legally compensated losses that the enterprise refuses or is unable to bear. It is also used "for expenses required for the return of labor workers or emergency rescue in the event of an incident." Article 11: organizing labor workers to engage in gambling- or sex-related work abroad is prohibited. Article 12: those who have not arranged training may not organize overseas deployment. Article 13: shall purchase personal accident injury insurance for labor workers during their overseas employment. Articles 21, 23: shall enter into written contracts with the foreign employer and with the labor workers respectively; without contracts, organizing overseas deployment is prohibited. Article 25: "may not collect a deposit under any name from labor workers or require labor workers to provide property as a guarantee." Article 26: within 10 working days of contract signing, report to the commerce authority for record. Article 29: where the actual rights and interests of labor workers do not match the contract, the enterprise shall assist in claiming compensation; where proper compensation is not obtained, the worker has the right to require the enterprise to bear compensation liability. Article 31: where the safety situation in a relevant country or region is insufficient to safeguard personal safety, the enterprise may not organize labor workers to go. Article 37: the commerce authority establishes a bad-credit record and announcement system. Penalties are in subsequent articles. Article 39: those without qualifications to engage in foreign labor cooperation are investigated and shut down; where a crime is constituted, criminal liability is pursued. Article 40: those who organize overseas deployment under the name of business, tourism, or study, who lend qualifications, or who organize work related to gambling or sex, have their business qualification certificate revoked. Article 43: those who organize overseas deployment without signing a contract, or who conceal information or provide false information, are fined 100,000 yuan or more and 200,000 yuan or less; the principal person in charge is also fined 20,000 yuan or more and 50,000 yuan or less (national).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2012). Regulations on the Administration of Foreign Labor Cooperation (State Council Order No. 620, Articles 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 21, 23, 25, 26, 29, 31, 37, 39, 40, 43). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2012-06/11/content_1091.htm>
- Note: The compliant process is as follows: the company first signs a written labor cooperation contract with the foreign employer, then signs a written service contract or labor contract with you. The contract must specify the work content, duration, compensation, working hours, leave, social insurance, and personal safety risks. Within 10 working days of signing, the company must report to the commerce authority for record. The company must also arrange for you to receive training in skills, safety precautions, foreign language, and local law and customs, and purchase personal accident injury insurance covering the overseas work period. Companies that will not let you see the contract, demand a deposit, or urge you to depart first and complete paperwork later — these are the companies to avoid. Overseas high-salary recruitment scams and telecom-fraud zones are in Section 21, Entry 5; pre-departure safety alerts and 12308 are in Section 21 and not repeated here. The regulations set no education or age threshold for those going overseas to work; the threshold is on the company's qualifications and on whether the destination country grants a permit. The company may sign contracts only with enterprises or institutions approved by that country, not with individuals abroad. The reserve is used by the authority when the company defaults; you cannot withdraw it yourself. So if something does happen, you must first complain to the commerce authority — Article 20 gives you this right to complain. For dispatches of more than 100 people to the same country or region, accompanying management personnel must be arranged and reported to the embassy/consulate for record. Smaller projects lack this layer of protection; you must keep your own contract and transfer records.

### 15. Working remotely from home for an overseas company: this income is domestic for tax purposes; you report individual income tax yourself, and foreign-exchange receipts go through "employee compensation"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Once a year, complete the consolidated annual tax reconciliation, from March 1 to June 30 of the following year. When foreign-exchange receipts exceed the annual total, you must also prepare an employment contract and income proof. The hard part is remembering to report this tax yourself.
- Plain-language summary: Whether this income counts as domestic or foreign depends on where you work, not where the money comes from. Working in China remotely for an overseas company counts as domestic income and is subject to Chinese individual income tax. The overseas company has no entity in China, so no one withholds on your behalf — you must report the tax yourself. Money remitted in goes through the "employee compensation" pathway.
- Benefit: Whether this income counts as domestic: Article 3 of the Implementing Regulations of the Individual Income Tax Law (State Council Order No. 707, effective January 1, 2019). It states: "unless otherwise specified by the competent finance and tax authority under the State Council, the following income, regardless of whether the place of payment is within China, is income sourced from within China: (1) income obtained from providing labor within China due to employment, engagement, or performance of obligations." Article 6 of the same regulations defines two tax categories. One is wages and salaries: "wages and salary income refers to wages, salaries, bonuses, year-end additional pay, labor dividends, allowances, subsidies, and other income related to employment or engagement that an individual receives due to employment or engagement." The other is personal service income: "personal service income refers to income obtained by an individual from providing labor, including income from design, decoration, installation, drawing, laboratory testing, testing, medical care, law, accounting, consulting, lectures, translation, manuscript review, calligraphy and painting, sculpture, film and television, audio recording, video recording, performance, advertising, exhibition, technical services, brokerage services, agency services, and other labor." Article 6 of the Individual Income Tax Law specifies how to calculate taxable income: "for resident individuals' comprehensive income, the balance after deducting 60,000 yuan, special deductions, special additional deductions, and other deductions determined in accordance with the law from the year's income is the taxable income." The same article also states: "for personal service income, manuscript income, and royalty income, the balance after deducting 20% of the income is the income amount." The "minus 20%" sentence lists only these three categories — wages and salaries are not included. So wages and salaries are included in income in full; the two tax categories yield different tax amounts. Article 9: "for individual income tax, the person obtaining the income is the taxpayer, and the unit or individual paying the income is the withholding agent." Article 10: "in any of the following circumstances, the taxpayer shall handle tax declaration in accordance with the law: ... (2) obtaining taxable income without a withholding agent; ... (4) obtaining income from overseas." Article 11: "resident individuals who obtain comprehensive income shall calculate individual income tax annually... those who need to handle consolidated annual reconciliation shall do so between March 1 and June 30 of the following year." Money remitted in: per the Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange (People's Bank of China Order [2006] No. 3, effective February 1, 2007). Article 9: "individual foreign-exchange settlement and domestic individual foreign-exchange purchase are subject to annual total-amount management. Within the annual total, the individual handles it at a bank with a valid identity document; for amounts exceeding the annual total, under the current account, the individual handles it at a bank with a valid identity document and relevant proof of the transaction amount." Article 7: "banks and individuals handling individual foreign-exchange business shall comply with these Measures, may not evade quota supervision by splitting, and may not use false commercial documents or certificates to evade authenticity management." Article 2 of the Implementing Rules of the Measures for the Administration of Individual Foreign Exchange (Hui Fa [2007] No. 1): "annual totals for individual foreign-exchange settlement and domestic individual foreign-exchange purchase are managed; the annual totals are 50,000 US dollars equivalent per person per year." Article 10: "for domestic individuals' non-commercial settlement under the current account exceeding the annual total, the following proof materials and a valid identity document are presented at the bank: ... (7) employee compensation: employment contract and income proof" (national).

- Note: Which tax category applies depends on your relationship with the other party. If you signed an employment contract and work under the other party's direction, it is wages and salaries. If you take work project by project and deliver results, it is personal service income. The two yield different tax amounts — figure out which you are before reporting. When the money comes in, up to 50,000 US dollars equivalent per year can be settled at the bank with just an ID card; for the portion exceeding, you must give the bank the employment contract and income proof. Do not split receipts across people to evade the quota. The above describes the situation of being in China, not departing. Actually going abroad to work for an overseas employer is a different setup — see Entry 14 of this section. The following four items are not covered in this book. They may have real costs, but no verifiable basis could be found this time. First, whether this income requires VAT or requires you to issue a VAT invoice on behalf of the payer. Second, whether doing this long-term requires a business license or registration as an individual business. Third, whether labor law can be invoked against the overseas employer — for example, annual leave, overtime pay, severance. Fourth, data export and confidentiality.

### 16. Going solo without entering an organization: for startup capital, look first at the entrepreneurship guarantee loan — up to 300,000 yuan for individuals, with the treasury covering half the interest
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Consultation is free. First make a trip to the human resources and social security authority for qualification review, then to the guarantee agency and the handling bank (the lending bank) for one procedure each. The hard part is that borrowed money must eventually be repaid.
- Plain-language summary: If you must borrow to start, look first at this treasury-subsidized loan. Individuals can borrow up to 300,000 yuan, for up to 3 years, with the treasury paying half the interest. One hard condition is often overlooked: at application, neither you nor your spouse may have other loans — only student loans, poverty-alleviation microloans, housing loans, car loans, and small consumer loans under 50,000 yuan are excluded.
- Benefit: Individual application conditions are in Article 6 of the Management Measures for Special Funds for Inclusive Finance Development (Cai Jin [2023] No. 75). The first condition is belonging to a key employment group. The document lists: urban-registered unemployed persons, employment-difficulty persons (including persons with disabilities), veterans, persons released from criminal sentences; also higher-education graduates (including college student village officials and students returning from study abroad), workers and unemployed persons from enterprises in industries with excess capacity being reduced; also returning-to-countryside entrepreneurial migrant workers, online merchants, poverty-alleviation populations, rural self-employed entrepreneurs. The second condition is having no other loans: "apart from student loans, poverty-alleviation microloans, housing loans, car loans, and small consumer loans under 50,000 yuan (including credit card consumption), at the time of application for an entrepreneurship guarantee loan, the applicant and their spouse shall have no other loans." Article 9: "for qualifying entrepreneurship guarantee loans, the finance department provides a fiscal interest subsidy of 50% of the actual loan interest rate. For extended or overdue entrepreneurship guarantee loans, the finance department generally does not provide an interest subsidy." "Interest subsidy" means the treasury pays half the interest on your behalf. "Extended" means extending beyond the maturity date; "overdue" means past due and unpaid — for these two, the treasury no longer pays. Article 10: for individual entrepreneurship guarantee loans of 100,000 yuan or below, counter-guarantees are generally eliminated — no need to find someone or pledge property to the guarantee agency. Article 11: individual entrepreneurship guarantee loans within the scope of award and subsidy support "have an amount no more than 300,000 yuan and a loan term of no more than 3 years." Partnership entrepreneurship may be appropriately increased, up to no more than 110% of the sum of individual limits. The loan interest rate ceiling for award-subsidized loans has three tiers: LPR+50BPs for eastern regions, LPR+150BPs for central and western regions, and LPR+250BPs for poverty-alleviation areas. LPR is the Loan Prime Rate, the bank lending market quoted rate; BPs is the unit of measurement for interest rates — the higher the addition, the higher the interest. Those who "repay actively, drive strong employment, and have good entrepreneurial projects" may apply again after maturity, with a cumulative total of no more than 3 times. Article 8: applicants first apply to the local human resources and social security department for qualification review, then submit the application to the guarantee agency and handling bank. Article 12: with the consent of the provincial people's government, localities may expand the scope of support, raise the amount, and increase the subsidy proportion (national).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ministry of Finance (2023). Notice on Issuing the Management Measures for Special Funds for Inclusive Finance Development (Cai Jin [2023] No. 75, Articles 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202309/content_6903691.htm>
- Note: The treasury paying half the interest does not change the fact that "borrowed money must be repaid." This book's position in Section 12, Entry 1 is unchanged: in entrepreneurship, only put in money you can afford to lose; do not touch household reserves; do not sign personal guarantees for company loans; and a spouse especially should not co-sign. If you have already decided to borrow, this kind of policy loan is the lowest-interest option — do not touch consumer loans or private lending. The amount and scope can be raised by each province; how much can actually be approved is subject to the local human resources and social security department's statement for the year. The "up to 200,000 yuan for individuals" notice from the 2020 pandemic period was a temporary document — follow this set of measures now. For training subsidies, social insurance subsidies, and employment internships during unemployment, see Section 7, Entry 13 (vocational training subsidy).
