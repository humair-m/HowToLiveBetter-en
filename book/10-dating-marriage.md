# 10. Is Dating and Marriage Worth It

This section breaks "is marriage worth it" into separate accounts — money, time, health, legal risk, and relationship quality — and tallies each separately. Each account uses only numbers traceable to a source, and sources are limited to official statistics or meta-analyses (which pool data from multiple studies and recompute). No moral judgment is made here, and no conclusion is drawn for you. The health accounts use mortality risk; the time accounts use minutes per day; the legal accounts use what the statutes say. These three methods are not interconvertible. For the accounting method, fill-in checklist, and common mistakes, see [docs/marriage-worth-it.md](../docs/marriage-worth-it.md).

### 1. Meet more people instead of fixating on one: whether two people click cannot be predicted before meeting
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=some benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free. Spend time meeting more people, not on repeatedly rethinking the same person. The hard part is resisting the urge to fixate.
- Plain-language summary: Two speed-dating studies had participants fill out more than 100 mate-preference questionnaires before meeting. The questionnaires could only predict who generally tends to like others and who is generally popular; they could not predict whether two specific people would click. Compatibility can only be known after meeting. So meeting more people is more cost-effective than betting everything on one person.
- Benefit: In two speed-dating studies, participants filled out more than 100 self-report mate-selection scales (questionnaires on which they rated themselves) before meeting. The researchers used a random-forest model (a pattern-finding algorithm) to compute. It could compute "how easily a person generally likes others" — these questionnaires explained 4%–18% of the variance, where variance refers to the differences between people, and the questionnaires can only explain a small portion. It could also compute "how generally popular a person is" — explained 7%–27%. But "whether these two specific people click" could not be computed; no combination of pre-meeting traits and preferences could predict it. The same group of researchers had earlier run another speed-dating experiment. Participants self-reported ideal-partner preferences before the meeting, such as valuing looks or valuing economic prospects. This preference failed to predict their interest in any specific person at the event
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Joel S, Eastwick PW, Finkel EJ (2017). Is romantic desire predictable? Machine learning applied to initial romantic attraction. Psychological Science, 28(10), 1478-1489. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797617714580>; Eastwick PW, Finkel EJ (2008). Sex differences in mate preferences revisited: do people know what they initially desire in a romantic partner? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 94(2), 245-264. <https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.94.2.245>
- Note: The two studies only show that "it cannot be predicted before meeting"; they do not say "no amount of effort helps". Compatibility can only be known by meeting, so spending time meeting more people, and actually meeting each one, is more cost-effective than betting everything on a single person. The study participants were all European and American college students. China's dating and marriage environment differs; discount accordingly when applying to yourself.

### 2. Once the other person clearly refuses, stop; continuing to pester is a public-order violation, not "sincerity"
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=yes benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free, takes no time. The hard part is holding back: no more messages, no more waiting for them.
- Plain-language summary: If the other party clearly says no, and you keep messaging or waiting for them, from January 2026 onward this is a public-order violation. Detention up to 5 days, or a fine up to 1000 yuan. More serious circumstances: detention 5 to 10 days. Police can also issue an order barring contact with the other party; violating it adds another 5 to 10 days' detention.
- Benefit: The Public Security Administration Punishment Law in force from January 1, 2026, lists pestering and stalking as a separate item. The article reads: "repeatedly sending obscene, insulting, or threatening information, or using harassment, pestering, stalking, or other methods that interfere with another person's normal life". Such acts are punished with up to 5 days' detention, or a fine up to 1000 yuan. More serious circumstances: detention 5 to 10 days, with a possible concurrent fine up to 1000 yuan. This item adds another layer: with the approval of the person in charge of the public security organ, the actor may be ordered not to contact the victim for a specified period. Violating the no-contact order adds another detention of 5 to 10 days
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2025 revision). Public Security Administration Punishment Law (Article 50 paragraph 1 item 5, paragraph 2). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202506/t20250627_699863.shtml> (reposted by Supreme People's Procuratorate)
- Note: "Relentless pursuit will eventually move the other person" has no research support, but detention and no-contact orders are written in black and white. The record left behind also affects civil-service exams, public-institution exams, political vetting, and qualification for some professions. The line between pursuing someone and harassing someone is not based on what you think but on whether the other party has clearly said no.

### 3. Judge whether the other person is interested by behavior, not "signals": who initiates, who plans, who follows up
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free, takes little time. The hard part is changing the habit: stop reading their expressions and tone, and start recording facts.
- Plain-language summary: 52 pairs of strangers of the opposite sex chatted for about ten minutes, then each judged whether the other was flirting. The result: "the other person is not flirting" was judged fairly accurately; "the other person is flirting" was missed. Switching to 261 people watching videos gave the same conclusion. So don't guess from expressions and tone — look at behavior: who initiates, who plans the next time, whether the other person follows up when you don't push.
- Benefit: Two studies measured the accuracy of recognizing flirting. The first had 52 pairs of unacquainted opposite-sex pairs talk for 10 to 12 minutes. Afterward, each reported whether they had been flirting and whether they thought the other was flirting. The result: "interactions in which no flirting occurred were judged more accurately; interactions in which flirting occurred were judged more poorly". The second used 26 one-minute videos, judged by 261 bystanders; the conclusion was the same. People can fairly reliably tell "the other person is not flirting" but struggle to tell "the other person is flirting"
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Hall JA, Xing C, Brooks S (2015). Accurately detecting flirting: error management theory, the traditional sexual script, and flirting base rate. Communication Research, 42(7), 939-958. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0093650214534972>; Perilloux C, Kurzban R (2015). Do men overperceive women's sexual interest? Psychological Science, 26(1), 70-77. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614555727> (disputing party)
- Note: Disputed. Other research argues that it is not that men overestimate women's sexual intent, but that women underreport their own intent on questionnaires. Who judges more accurately remains unsettled. But both sides agree on one thing: inferring the other person's mind from micro-cues like expressions and tone has very low accuracy. Visible behavior is far more reliable: whether the other person initiates, whether they plan the next meeting, whether they follow up when you do not push.

### 4. Whether a relationship is good depends mainly on your own feelings in it, not on the other party's conditions
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free, takes little time. The hard part is shifting perspective: put away the mate-selection checklist and look at how the relationship actually unfolds.
- Plain-language summary: What best predicts whether a relationship is good is your own feelings, not the other party's conditions. For example, whether you feel the other person is committed and appreciates you, and how often you fight. Your own feelings are two to four times more powerful than the partner's statements. But whether the relationship will get better or worse later cannot be predicted by any questionnaire.
- Benefit: A machine-learning analysis across 29 labs and 43 longitudinal couple datasets — longitudinal meaning the same couples are tracked over time. The items that best predicted relationship quality were all from the partner's own feelings about the relationship: perceived commitment, appreciation, sexual satisfaction, perceived partner satisfaction, conflict. Among the individual-difference items, the strongest were life satisfaction, negative emotion, depression, avoidant attachment, anxious attachment. The relationship-related items explained up to 45% of the variance at the start of the study. Variance refers to the difference in relationship quality from one couple to another; 45% is the portion explainable by these items. By the end of the study, the maximum explained was 18%. And the predictive power of "self-reported items" was two to four times that of "partner-reported items". Adding individual differences and partner reports on top of self-reported relationship feelings yielded no additional predictive power
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Joel S, Eastwick PW, Allison CJ, et al. (2020). Machine learning uncovers the most robust self-report predictors of relationship quality across 43 longitudinal couples studies. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 117(32), 19061-19071. <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1917036117>
- Note: The same study also found that whether the relationship got better or worse during the study could not be predicted by any set of self-report items from the questionnaire. So to judge whether your current relationship is good, asking your own feelings is more accurate than checking the other party's conditions list, but do not expect to predict how it will go later. The "don't just ask whether there is emotional value" entry in this section covers the same thing; here we list the specific items.

### 5. To get closer, take turns answering a set of progressively deeper questions; don't study pickup lines
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=none benefit=small metric=time -->
- Cost: Free. Takes 45 minutes.
- Plain-language summary: Two strangers take turns answering 36 progressively deeper questions; after 45 minutes, they are significantly closer than those who merely chitchat for the same duration. What works is taking turns telling the truth and going deeper layer by layer, not pickup lines. This only shows they were closer at the time; it does not mean a long-term relationship will develop.
- Benefit: Two unacquainted people were paired and asked to take turns answering three sets of progressively deeper questions, 36 in total. Questions ranged from "who would you most like to have dinner with" to "when did you last cry in front of someone". Closeness after 45 minutes was significantly higher than the control group that chitchatted for the same duration. It was also higher than the average rating that college students in the study gave their closest actual relationship
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Aron A, Melinat E, Aron EN, Vallone RD, Bator RJ (1997). The experimental generation of interpersonal closeness: a procedure and some preliminary findings. Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, 23(4), 363-377. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167297234003>
- Note: Only one experiment was conducted, on college students, measuring only short-term closeness, not whether a long-term relationship could develop. The closeness in the experiment came from both people taking turns, going deeper layer by layer, telling the truth about themselves — not from pickup lines, scripts, or one-sided performance. So in conversation you can "say a bit of something true about yourself, then ask a question at the same depth", taking turns.

### 6. Long-distance is not worse than same-city; the real hurdle is the first three months after moving in together
<!-- cost-tag: money=high time=high willpower=yes benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Travel costs money, and the round-trip travel time. You meet less often than same-city couples. The hard part is enduring not seeing each other for long stretches.
- Plain-language summary: Long-distance relationships are actually more stable during the separation, because meeting less often makes it easy to idealize the other person beyond reality. The real hurdle comes after moving in together. In tracking data, about half of long-distance couples made it to the same-city stage; the other half ended during the separation. Of the half that reunited, one-third broke up within 3 months.
- Benefit: Two studies reached the same conclusion. Long-distance relationships are actually more stable than same-city ones during the separation. The reason is that idealization of the partner beyond reality is significantly higher in long-distance relationships — the study measured idealization distortion, romantic love, relationship recall, and perceived agreement. And the less often they meet, the more idealized. But this stability holds only while both sides remain apart; once they become same-city, long-distance couples are more likely to break up. The study that tracked long-distance-to-same-city gave these proportions: about half of long-distance couples reached the move-in-together stage, and the other half ended during the separation. Of the half that reunited, one-third broke up within 3 months of reunion. The longer the interval between meetings during separation and the stronger the idealization, the less stable after reunion
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Stafford L, Merolla AJ, Castle JD (2006). When long-distance dating partners become geographically close. Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, 23(6), 901-919. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0265407506070472>; Stafford L, Merolla AJ (2007). Idealization, reunions, and stability in long-distance dating relationships. Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, 24(1), 37-54. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0265407507072578>; Dargie E et al. (2015). Journal of Sex & Marital Therapy, 41(2), 181-202. <https://doi.org/10.1080/0092623X.2013.864367> (the long-distance study mentioned in the note)
- Note: The subjects are American college students' dating relationships, not marriages, and the era is earlier, so do not apply the absolute proportions directly. Another study surveyed 717 people in long-distance relationships and 425 in same-city relationships. The difference in relationship quality between the two types was small; whether a relationship is good depends mainly on each person's circumstances and on the relationship itself. So when deciding whether to do long-distance, do not use "long-distance always ends" as the reason. What you should prepare for in advance is the months after reunion, when autonomy decreases, mutual understanding increases, and conflict and jealousy also increase.

### 7. Look at registration data before listening to elders: what exactly do marriage rate and divorce rate measure
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=none benefit=small metric=time -->
- Cost: Free. Read the marriage-registration section of the Ministry of Civil Affairs' annual statistical bulletin; takes a few minutes.
- Plain-language summary: In 2024, 6.106 million couples registered marriages nationwide, down 20% from the previous year; 3.513 million divorced. The online claim that the divorce rate exceeds 50% comes from dividing these two numbers, about 57.5%. But the people divorcing and the people marrying are not the same cohort, so it cannot be read as "half of all marriages end in divorce". The bulletin's marriage rate of 4.3‰ and divorce rate of 2.5‰ are computed against the total population.
- Benefit: In 2024, 6.106 million couples registered marriages nationwide, down 20.5% from the previous year; the marriage rate was 4.3‰. In the same year, 3.513 million couples divorced: 2.622 million registered at civil-affairs departments, and 891,000 by court judgment or mediation; the divorce rate was 2.5‰. The bulletin specifies: marriage (divorce) rate = number of couples marrying (divorcing) in the year / average total population in the year × 1000‰. This is a "crude rate"; the denominator is the total population, not the married population
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ministry of Civil Affairs (2025). 2024 Statistical Bulletin on the Development of Civil Affairs (Section 5(2)1 Marriage Registration Services, Note 5), web version and PDF version. <https://www.mca.gov.cn/n1288/n1294/n1554/c1662004999980006190/content.html>, <https://www.mca.gov.cn/gdnps/n2445/n2451/n2458/n2681/c1662004999980006189/attr/400985.pdf>
- Note: The commonly cited "divorce rate over 50%" online comes from dividing the number of couples divorcing in a year by the number of couples marrying in that year, 351.3 / 610.6 ≈ 57.5%. This is called the "divorce-to-marriage ratio". The two numbers divided are not the same cohort, and the bulletin does not contain this indicator, so it cannot be read as "half of all marriages end in divorce". To compute "the probability that a given marriage eventually ends in divorce", you would need to follow the same cohort of marriages over time; this section did not find such official data.

### 8. Factor in the health benefits, but discount them as observational data
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=none benefit=small metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free, takes no time. Just remember: these numbers say that mortality is lower among married people — the two occur together; it does not mean marriage makes you live longer.
- Plain-language summary: Married people have about a 12% lower probability of dying in the same period than unmarried people; never-married people have about a 24% higher mortality than married people. But this is just the two occurring together, not the effect of marriage itself. People who are healthier, higher-income, and more socially active are both more likely to marry and more likely to live longer. These numbers are mostly from Europe and America; for China, treat them only as order-of-magnitude references.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis of older adults pooled and recomputed data from multiple studies, totaling 53 comparisons and over 250,000 people. The all-cause mortality RR for married relative to unmarried was 0.88, 95% CI 0.85–0.91 (RR is risk ratio, CI is confidence interval), about a 12% lower probability of dying in the same period. In the same study, widowed 1.11, divorced or separated 1.16, never-married 1.11, respectively about 11%, 16%, 11% higher. No difference by sex. The most conservative estimate was RR 0.94, only about 6% lower. Another meta-analysis compared never-married to married, using 95 papers and over 500 million people. The result was HR 1.24 (1.19–1.30), about a 24% higher probability of dying in the same period. And the older the age, the smaller the gap. Another meta-analysis stratified by sex, with 21 studies and over 7.89 million people: for both sexes, being unmarried co-occurs with total mortality, cancer mortality, and cardiovascular mortality. The gap in cardiovascular mortality and total mortality was larger for men. Unmarried men relative to unmarried women had 31% more stroke mortality and 9% more all-cause mortality
- Evidence grade: A (disputed)
- Source: Manzoli L, et al. (2007). Marital status and mortality in the elderly: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Soc Sci Med 64:77–94. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socscimed.2006.08.031>; Roelfs DJ, et al. (2011). The rising relative risk of mortality for singles: meta-analysis and meta-regression. Am J Epidemiol 174(4):379–389. <https://doi.org/10.1093/aje/kwr111>; Wang Y, et al. (2020). Sex differences in the association between marital status and the risk of cardiovascular, cancer, and all-cause mortality: a systematic review and meta-analysis of 7,881,040 individuals. Glob Health Res Policy 5:4. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s41256-020-00133-8>
- Note: The dispute is that married people are already a selected group: healthier, higher-income, more socially active people are both more likely to marry and more likely to live longer. All three meta-analyses used observational data from existing populations — only tracking, no grouped controls. The authors themselves note publication bias or methodological limitations. Roelfs also found that the more rigorous the study, the smaller the gap. These numbers are mostly from European and American populations; for China, treat them only as order-of-magnitude references.

### 9. The time account uses "unpaid labor"; agree on a division of labor before registering the marriage
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=some benefit=large metric=time -->
- Cost: Free. Spend an hour or two before marriage turning household chores, eldercare and childcare, and whose career gives way into a clear division of labor, and execute it afterward. The hard part is laying it out on the table; don't keep dragging it out unspoken.
- Plain-language summary: In the National Time Use Survey, unpaid labor such as housework and accompanying and caring for family members averaged 1 hour 52 minutes per day for men and 3 hours 29 minutes for women. The survey was not broken down by marital status, so "how much more housework after marriage" has no official number; this gender gap can only serve as an upper-bound reference. The division of labor can be negotiated differently before marriage.
- Benefit: 2024 National Time Use Survey. Unpaid labor refers to housework, accompanying and caring for family members, and similar unpaid work. Those who did such work on the survey day averaged 2 hours 45 minutes per day, of which men averaged 1 hour 52 minutes and women 3 hours 29 minutes. The proportion of people who did such work on a given day was 67.5% for men and 83.9% for women. Residents' household labor averaged 1 hour 17 minutes per day; counting only those who did it, 1 hour 59 minutes, 28 minutes less than in 2018. Accompanying and caring for family members averaged 30 minutes per day; counting only those who did it, 1 hour 46 minutes. The 2018 figures: household labor 45 minutes for men and 2 hours 6 minutes for women; accompanying and caring for children 17 minutes for men and 53 minutes for women
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: National Bureau of Statistics (2024). Third National Time Use Survey Bulletin (No. 2, No. 3). <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202410/t20241031_1957216.html>, <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202410/t20241031_1957215.html>; National Bureau of Statistics (2019). 2018 National Time Use Survey Bulletin. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfb/202302/t20230203_1900224.html>
- Note: Both surveys were broken down only by sex, urban-rural, and age, not by marital status. So "how much more housework after marriage" has no official number; only the gender gap can be used as an upper-bound reference. The numbers are national averages; the division of labor between the two of you can be negotiated entirely differently.

### 10. For the money account, first read the legal default rules, then decide whether to make a written agreement
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Read the articles in the marriage and family section of the Civil Code on property. If needed, the two of you sign a written property agreement; the law only requires it to be in writing — notarization is unnecessary. The hard part is talking about money openly in advance.
- Plain-language summary: Without a written agreement, the law defaults to making post-marital wages, bonuses, business and investment income, and inherited or gifted property jointly owned by both. Pre-marital property, and property designated by will or gift contract to one party only, remains personal. The two of you can sign a written agreement that each keeps their own; once signed, it is binding. The party that contributes more to childcare or eldercare can claim compensation upon divorce.
- Benefit: The default rule without other agreement: post-marital wages, bonuses, labor remuneration, production and business investment income, intellectual property income, and inherited or gifted property are jointly owned by the spouses (Article 1062). Pre-marital property, personal injury compensation, property designated by will or gift contract to one party only, and one party's personal-use daily necessities are personal property (Article 1063). The two parties may agree in writing that pre-marital and post-marital property belongs to each individually, jointly, or partly to each individually and partly jointly; this agreement is legally binding on both parties (Article 1065). The party that bears more obligations due to raising children, caring for the elderly, or assisting the other party's work may claim compensation upon divorce (Article 1088)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (in force from January 1, 2021; Articles 1062, 1063, 1065, 1088). National Law and Regulation Database <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?title=%E4%B8%AD%E5%8D%8E%E4%BA%BA%E6%B0%91%E5%85%B1%E5%92%8C%E5%9B%BD%E6%B0%91%E6%B3%95%E5%85%B8&id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>; full text of articles reposted by Supreme People's Court Gazette <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/7f184078694d811fb3314f6af9accf.html>
- Note: Bride price (彩礼), wedding, and marital home have no official statistics; this entry gives no numbers. How much compensation under Article 1088 is first negotiated between the two of you; if negotiations fail, the court decides — there is no fixed formula. A written property agreement works for both sides: the higher-earning party has use for it, and so does the party who does more unpaid work like housework and caregiving.

### 11. When parents fund a home purchase, write clearly at the time of transfer whether it is a loan or a gift
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=none benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. On the day of funding, write a one-page statement specifying whether the money is a gift to whom or a loan to whom, signed by both the funding parent(s) and the child. If it is a loan, have both spouses sign; the transfer memo should carry the same statement.
- Plain-language summary: Pre-marital parental funding for a home is by default a gift only to their own child. Post-marital funding without agreement is treated as joint spousal property. From February 2025 onward, when one party's parent fully funds a home post-marriage without specifying it is only for their own child, the court can award the home to the funding parent's child upon divorce, and then decide whether to compensate the other party. If it is to be treated as a loan, write it clearly on the day and have both parties sign.
- Benefit: Pre-marital parental funding for a home purchase is recognized as a gift to the parent's own child individually, unless the parents explicitly state it is a gift to both. For post-marital funding, the agreement governs; absent an agreement or where the agreement is unclear, it is handled under the principle of joint spousal property. From February 1, 2025, there is an additional rule. When one party's parent fully funds the purchase during the marriage, and the gift contract does not specify that the gift is only to their own child, the court may, upon divorce and division, award the home to the child of the funding parent. After awarding, the court considers several factors to determine whether and how much to compensate the other party: the situation of cohabitation and joint children, divorce fault, the magnitude of contribution to the family, and the market price of the home at divorce. If one party's parent partially funds the purchase, or both sets of parents contribute, the home is awarded to one party based on the source and proportion of funding, with reasonable compensation (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Supreme People's Court (2020). Interpretation (I) on the Application of the Civil Code Marriage and Family Section (Fa Shi [2020] No. 22, Article 29). <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282071.html>; Supreme People's Court (2025). Interpretation (II) on the Application of the Civil Code Marriage and Family Section (Fa Shi [2025] No. 1, Article 8). <https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/452771.html>; National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Article 1062 paragraph 1 item 4). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Note: Writing it clearly helps both sides. The funding parents no longer need to rely on producing a post-divorce IOU to protect the funding. After Interpretation (II) takes effect, who provided the money is itself the basis for dividing the home; whose name is on the title no longer decides ownership. The party receiving the funding also does not have to wait until divorce to learn what the money counts as. If you want it to be a loan, treat it as a loan in full: both parties agree it is a loan, agree on repayment, the transfer notes it, and where necessary keep repayment records. Missing any of these means explaining on the witness stand.

### 12. A large amount borrowed by one spouse does not automatically become your debt if you did not sign or ratify
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Do not sign "joint borrower" or "guarantor" on your spouse's IOU or guarantee. Do not write things like "we will repay this money" in chats either — that can be taken as your subsequent ratification of the debt. The hard part is holding back when pressed.
- Plain-language summary: Money borrowed by your spouse behind your back, as long as it clearly exceeds household daily expenses and you did not sign or subsequently acknowledge it, does not automatically become your debt. The creditor must prove the money was used for joint living or joint business. But money for a home purchase, if it actually becomes a home you live in together or co-own, the other party can usually prove that level.
- Benefit: The following situations constitute joint spousal debt. First, both spouses sign together. Second, one party ratifies afterward — accepts the debt after the fact, with both acknowledging it. Third, one party borrows in their own name during marriage for the daily needs of the family. Money borrowed in one party's own name that clearly exceeds household daily expenses is not joint spousal debt. The exception is where the creditor (the party seeking repayment) can prove the money was used for joint spousal living, joint production and business, or that both parties acknowledged it. Home-purchase money clearly exceeds household daily needs; the burden of proof falls on the party asserting the claim — the creditor must produce the evidence. But if this money actually became a home that the spouses jointly live in or co-own, the creditor can usually prove the "used for joint spousal living" level. So more effective is the step in Entry 11 (parents writing clearly whether funding is a loan or a gift) (nationwide, from 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Article 1064). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>; Supreme People's Court (2020 second revision). Provisions on Several Issues Concerning the Application of Law in the Trial of Private Lending Cases (Articles 15, 16). <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/94b6623974526df7d2430a3c73f050.html> (Supreme People's Court Gazette)
- Note: When dividing property at divorce, the other party's parent suddenly producing a loan agreement signed only by your spouse requires a two-step look. First, examine whether the loan actually occurred. If you say the money was never handed over and can plausibly explain, the court weighs several things: the loan amount, how the money was handed over, both parties' financial capacity, then how transactions normally happened, whether assets changed correspondingly, and what witnesses say. If the other party produces only a transfer record, they must still prove the loan was established. Second, after the loan is established, determine whether it is joint debt. General rules on IOUs and guarantees are in Section 8, Entry 18.

### 13. Registering a marriage takes only an ID card plus a signed declaration; no hukou booklet, no fee, certificate issued on the spot
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=none benefit=small metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Both parties must appear in person. The whole procedure usually takes tens of minutes.
- Plain-language summary: From May 2025 onward, registering a marriage requires only two things: your own ID card, plus a signed declaration. The declaration states that you have no spouse and that you and the other party have no direct blood kinship or collateral blood kinship within three generations. You do not need to bring the hukou (household registration) booklet, and you do not need to return to one party's hukou location to register. Registration is free of charge; if you meet the conditions, the certificate is issued on the spot.
- Benefit: The new marriage registration regulation in force from May 10, 2025, changed the materials required for mainland residents to marry. The article requires only two items: "(1) the person's own resident ID card; (2) the person's signed declaration that they have no spouse and that they and the other party have no direct blood kinship or collateral blood kinship within three generations". The hukou booklet is no longer a required material. The registration authority is "the civil-affairs department of the county-level people's government, or the township (town) people's government designated by the provincial, autonomous region, or municipal people's government on the principle of convenience". The old regulation's "go to the location of one party's habitual hukou residence" restriction has been removed. The regulation also specifies "marriage registration authorities shall not charge fees for handling marriage registration" and "where the parties meet the conditions for marriage, registration shall be carried out on the spot and the marriage certificate issued"
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2025). Marriage Registration Regulation (State Council Order No. 804, Articles 2, 6, 8, 10). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202504/content_7017752.htm>
- Note: The registration authority may refuse only in four situations: not having reached the legal marriage age, not being fully voluntary on both sides, one or both parties already having a spouse, or the two being direct blood kin or collateral blood kin within three generations. Possible charges are only for peripheral services like photography and photocopying. Registration itself charges nothing; if "processing fee" or "expedited fee" is demanded, refuse outright and file a complaint. The 30-day cooling-off period for divorce registration is in the exit-cost entry of this section.

### 14. Get a pre-marital checkup before registering; if you have a major illness, you must truthfully inform the other party before registration

- Cost: Free or reduced for age-eligible people in most regions. Self-pay is only a few hundred yuan. Takes half a day.

Must be filed within one year from the date of knowing. The pre-marital checkup itself has not been a prerequisite for marriage registration since 2003; going or not is voluntary. Cost depends on local policy; takes half a day.
- Benefit: The legal consequence of concealment is clear. The new marriage registration regulation states "where one party has a major illness, they shall truthfully inform the other party before marriage registration; if they fail to inform truthfully, the other party may petition the people's court to annul the marriage in accordance with Article 1053 of the Civil Code". The Civil Code provides that a party with a major illness shall truthfully inform the other party before marriage registration; failure to truthfully inform allows the other party to petition the people's court to annul the marriage. ### 15. Don't just ask "is there" emotional value; look at relationship quality
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=some benefit=medium metric=time -->
- Cost: Free. Before marriage, use a few conflicts and a few months of being together to evaluate relationship quality; don't just check the conditions list. The hard part is resisting the urge to judge by checklist.
- Plain-language summary: Higher marriage quality correlates with better health, but the relationship is weak, similar in magnitude to the effect of habits like diet on health. To judge whether a relationship is good, look at a few conflicts and a few months of being together; don't just check a conditions list. Whether a bad marriage is worse for health than not marrying has no data that directly compares them.
- Benefit: A meta-analysis of 126 studies and over 72,000 people showed that higher marriage quality correlates with better health. The effect size r ranged from 0.07 to 0.21. r is the degree to which two things vary together; 0 means no relationship, 1 means complete synchrony. Mortality risk had r = 0.11, cardiovascular reactivity during conflict had r = −0.13. This magnitude is comparable to the effect of health behaviors like diet on health. Studies that explicitly tested sex differences found essentially no male-female differences. Reviews in the cardiovascular field note that marital dissatisfaction and marital quality significantly affect cardiovascular risk
- Evidence grade: B (disputed)
- Source: Robles TF, et al. (2014). Marital quality and health: a meta-analytic review. Psychol Bull 140(1):140–187. <https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031859>; Dhindsa DS, et al. (2020). Marital status and outcomes in patients with cardiovascular disease. Trends Cardiovasc Med 30(4):215–220. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcm.2019.05.012>
- Note: The dispute is that the association itself is weak. Some results show publication bias. And most studies surveyed at one time point, so causality cannot be established; Robles' own paper lists these limitations. This section did not find a meta-analysis that directly compares the mortality of "low-quality marriage" versus "unmarried", so "bad marriage is worse than not marrying" can only be an inference for now, not a numerical conclusion.

### 16. Calculate the exit cost: consensual divorce has a 30-day cooling-off period; divorce litigation has statutory grounds
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Spend some time before marriage reading: if you have to exit, how long will it take, and what procedure. The hard part is thinking about breaking up before the wedding.
- Plain-language summary: Consensual divorce is not "sign and done". Within 30 days after filing the application, either party may withdraw. After that period, both parties must appear in person within 30 days to claim the divorce certificate; not showing up counts as withdrawal. If the other party refuses, only litigation remains. The court first mediates; only if there is domestic violence, bigamy, separation of two years, or similar circumstances and mediation fails does the court grant divorce.
- Benefit: Consensual divorce requires a written divorce agreement and personal appearance at the marriage registration authority to apply (Article 1076). Within 30 days from the date the authority receives the application, either party may withdraw. Within 30 days after that period, both parties must personally claim the divorce certificate; failure to claim counts as withdrawal (Article 1077). If one party refuses, litigation is the route; the court should first mediate. Where there is bigamy or cohabitation with another, domestic violence or abuse or abandonment, gambling or drug use and refusal to reform after repeated education, or separation due to relationship incompatibility for two years, and mediation fails, divorce should be granted. If after a non-divorce judgment the parties separate for another year and one sues again, divorce should be granted (Article 1079). In 2024, 3.513 million couples divorced nationwide, of which 891,000 were by court judgment or mediation
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (Articles 1076, 1077, 1079). <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/7f184078694d811fb3314f6af9accf.html>; Ministry of Civil Affairs (2020). Notice on Implementing the Provisions of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China on Marriage Registration (Min Fa [2020] No. 116). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-12/04/content_5567010.htm>; Ministry of Civil Affairs (2025). 2024 Statistical Bulletin on the Development of Civil Affairs. Citation same as Entry 7 (what marriage rate and divorce rate measure)
- Note: The cooling-off period applies only to consensual divorce. Litigated divorce has no cooling-off period, but the cycle is longer. Court fees and lawyer fees vary by region; this entry gives no numbers. A high exit cost does not mean you should not marry, but you should know these things before marrying.

### 17. Keep a separate account for "marrying to satisfy elders"; don't mix it with your own accounts

- Cost: Free. Write it in two columns on paper. One column is which benefits in this marriage are yours; the preceding entries can compute those. The other column is which benefits accrue to the elders: peace of mind, face, the cessation of pressure to marry. The hard part is keeping the elder column separate, not folding it into yours.
- Plain-language summary: The elder-side benefits — peace of mind, face, no more pressure — are real. But they do not automatically turn into your health benefits or relationship quality. And the time account, money account, and exit cost are all borne by you. Split into two columns on paper, separating which benefits are yours and which are the elders', and you can see at a glance whether this account is worth it.
- Benefit: The elders' benefits are real. But they do not automatically translate into your health benefits (Entry 8) or relationship quality (Entry 15). And the time account (Entry 9), money account (Entries 10–12), and exit cost (Entry 16) are all borne by the parties themselves. After splitting the accounts, whether "task-driven marriage" has high or low cost-effectiveness becomes clear to you

### 18. Treat relationship quality as a health account: argue about the issue, not the person; don't insult, don't dig
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=small metric=mortality -->
- Cost: Free. When arguing, only talk about the matter at hand; don't insult, don't dig, don't dredge up old grievances. The hard part is holding your tongue in the heat of the moment.
- Plain-language summary: Couples with good relationships are somewhat healthier and have somewhat lower mortality risk. The association is small, comparable in magnitude to the effect of diet on health. In one experiment, couples who maintained hostility throughout an argument had experimental blister wounds heal at only 60% the speed of low-hostility couples.
- Benefit: Pooling 126 studies and over 72,000 people (meta-analysis). Higher marriage quality correlates with better health, correlation coefficient r between .07 and .21. A correlation coefficient of 0 means no relationship, 1 means complete synchrony. The correlation with lower mortality risk was r = .11. Smaller cardiovascular reactivity during arguments, r = -.13. The authors note that these effects are small, comparable in magnitude to the association of health habits like diet with health. In another crossover experiment, 42 healthy couples were each hospitalized twice for 24 hours. Once for a mutually supportive conversation, once for discussing a contentious issue. After the argument, experimental blister wounds healed more slowly. Couples who displayed more hostility on both visits had wounds heal at only 60% the speed of low-hostility couples. The morning after the argument, their blood inflammatory markers (IL-6, tumor necrosis factor) also rose more
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Robles TF, Slatcher RB, Trombello JM, McGinn MM (2014). Marital quality and health: a meta-analytic review. Psychological Bulletin. <https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031859>; Kiecolt-Glaser JK et al. (2005). Hostile marital interactions, proinflammatory cytokine production, and wound healing. Archives of General Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1001/archpsyc.62.12.1377>
- Note: Reason for B: most of the pooled studies only tracked, without grouped controls. So it cannot be determined whether a poor relationship makes people sick or whether poor health makes the relationship poor. Some studies still show the association after controlling for age and income. The wound experiment had only 42 couples. "Argue about the issue, don't insult, don't dig" is the author's practice extrapolated from "hostile behavior" in the experiment; no study has directly compared it. If you fight badly and cannot change it, the two of you can go together for marriage and family counseling — also no separate literature on this. If fighting escalates to hitting, or there is long-term verbal abuse or threats, it is no longer a communication problem but domestic violence; see Section 8, Entry 43 (domestic violence). To think through whether to leave, exit costs are in this section, Entry 16 (exit cost).
