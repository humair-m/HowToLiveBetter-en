# 19. On the Job, Leaving the Job, and Workplace Injury

This section accounts for money: how to calculate it, what to sign, how deadlines are counted. The first three entries cover money you should get while still on the job; the middle six cover leaving the job; the last seven cover occupational disease and workplace injury (工伤). The money in workplace injury is an order of magnitude larger than a layoff, and the deadlines are harder. Damage from occupational disease cannot be reversed.

### 1. Overtime pay is calculated in three tiers — 1.5x, 2x, and 3x; if not paid, file a complaint with labor inspection, and overdue payment triggers an additional 50% to 100% on top
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Routinely save a copy of your clock-in records, shift schedules, and overtime approval forms, and screenshot messages from group chats where the boss assigns work. The hard part is remembering to do this every month
- Plain-language summary: Regular overtime is paid at 1.5 times wages. Rest-day overtime without compensatory time off is paid at 2 times. Working on statutory holidays such as New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Labor Day, or National Day is paid at 3 times, and compensatory time off cannot be substituted for it. If the company doesn't pay, complain to the labor inspector. After being ordered to pay within a time limit and still not doing so, the unit must additionally pay 50% to 100% of the amount owed
- Benefit: The legal standard work time is no more than 8 hours per day and an average of no more than 44 hours per week. Regular overtime generally may not exceed 1 hour per day. With special reasons to work more, no more than 3 hours per day, and no more than 36 hours total per month. Overtime pay is divided into three tiers. For arranging extended working hours on regular days, not less than 150% of wages. For arranging work on rest days when compensatory time off cannot be arranged, not less than 200%. For arranging work on statutory holidays, not less than 300%. For the statutory-holiday tier, the statute provides no "compensatory time off substitution" option. When a unit arranges overtime but does not pay overtime, the labor administrative department orders it to pay in full within a time limit. If still unpaid after the deadline, the unit is ordered to pay the worker, in addition to the arrears, a compensation amount of 50% to 100% of the amount payable (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2018). Labor Law (2018 second amendment, Articles 36, 41, 43, and 44). <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/bgt/art/2023/art_d9aa750028b14b99a776cb93726a360d.html> (published by the State Administration for Market Regulation); Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007). Labor Contract Law (Article 85, item 3). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: Rest days and statutory holidays must be calculated separately. Statutory holidays are limited each year to New Year's Day, Spring Festival, Labor Day, National Day, and the few other days specified by law. Days stitched together by leave-swap arrangements count as rest days at 200%. "Company policy says overtime requires approval" and "I stayed voluntarily" do not mean the company can avoid paying, but they make it harder for you to produce evidence, so keep records of task assignments and approvals. A company that wants to implement comprehensive working-hour calculations or non-fixed working-hour arrangements must first obtain approval from the labor administrative department; it is not a unilateral decision. The arbitration statute of limitations for disputes, see Section 8, Entry 19

### 2. Annual leave is calculated by cumulative years of service as 5, 10, or 15 days; unused leave is converted at 300% of daily wages
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Add up the years worked across all employers; the social insurance payment record serves as proof. The hard part is being able to refuse to sign when the company hands you a "voluntary waiver of annual leave" form
- Plain-language summary: Cumulative service across all employers of 1 year up to 10 years entitles you to 5 days of annual leave per year. Reaching 10 years gives 10 days; reaching 20 years gives 15 days. Even if you have changed jobs, it still accumulates upward. If the unit cannot arrange leave and you agree not to take it, those days are paid at 3 times your daily wage. Once you sign a "voluntary waiver of annual leave," only your regular wage remains
- Benefit: Continuous work of 12 months entitles you to annual leave. The number of days is based on **cumulative** working time, summing years worked across all employers. 1 to under 10 years: 5 days; 10 to under 20 years: 10 days; 20 years or more: 15 days. Statutory holidays and rest days do not count toward annual leave days. Where the unit genuinely cannot arrange leave due to work needs and obtains your consent not to take it, the days not taken "are paid as annual leave wages at 300% of the worker's daily wage income." The 300% already includes the share that would have been paid for a normal working day, so the additional amount actually received is 200%. Daily wage = your monthly wage ÷ 21.75. Monthly wage is calculated as the average over the 12 months before payment, after removing overtime pay. Those who joined the unit during the year are prorated by the remaining calendar days at that unit. On leaving, the year's amount is prorated by how long you have already worked and settled; already-taken excess leave is not clawed back. If the unit neither arranges leave nor pays, it is ordered to correct within a time limit. If still uncorrected past the deadline, in addition to that annual leave wage, an equal amount of compensation must also be paid (nationwide, effective January 1, 2008)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2007). Regulation on Paid Annual Leave for Employees (State Council Order No. 514, Articles 2, 3, 4, 5, and 7). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2008/content_859865.htm>; Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (2008). Implementing Measures for Paid Annual Leave of Enterprise Employees (MOHRSS Order No. 1, Articles 3, 4, 5, 10, 11, 12, and 15). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1265995.htm>
- Note: Cumulative working time must be summed across employers; those who have changed jobs should not calculate by current-employer tenure alone. **Only when the employee "does not take leave for personal reasons and so requests in writing" may the unit pay only regular wages**, so do not sign a "voluntary waiver of annual leave" form. Personal leave totaling 20 days or more in a year, where the unit does not dock your pay, disqualifies you from annual leave that year. Sick leave exceeding the corresponding number of months also disqualifies: under 10 years of service is 2 months, 10 to 20 years is 3 months, 20 years or more is 4 months. Family visit leave, bereavement leave, maternity leave, and the wage-paying suspension period for workplace injury are not counted toward annual leave

### 3. Probation has a statutory cap, the wage may not be below 80%, and probation may be agreed only once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before signing, spend a few minutes checking that the contract period matches the probation length
- Plain-language summary: For a contract of less than 1 year, the probation period may be at most 1 month. For 1 to 3 years, at most 2 months. For 3 years or more, at most 6 months. The same company may put you on probation only once; putting you on probation again after a position transfer is illegal. Probation wages may not be lower than 80% of the lowest grade for the same position or the wage stipulated in the contract, nor below the local minimum wage
- Benefit: For a contract of 3 months to less than 1 year, probation may not exceed 1 month. For 1 year to less than 3 years, no more than 2 months. For 3 years or more, and for open-ended contracts without a fixed end date, no more than 6 months. Contracts that are for completion of a specific task, or that total less than 3 months, may not stipulate a probation period. "The same employer and the same employee may agree only one probation period" — the same company may put the same person on probation only once. If the contract stipulates only a probation period, that probation is invalid and the period becomes the labor contract period. Probation wages "may not be lower than 80% of the lowest grade wage for the same position at the unit, or the wage stipulated in the labor contract, and may not be lower than the minimum wage standard at the employer's location." Where the probation agreed exceeds the statutory length and the person actually worked, the unit must pay an additional compensation for the excess period, based on the monthly wage standard after completion of probation. If the unit wishes to terminate the contract during probation, it may do so only on the grounds listed by law and must explain the reason to the worker (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007). Labor Contract Law (Articles 19, 20, 21, and 83). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: Three common illegal practices. First, the contract is only for 1 year but stipulates 3 months of probation. Second, after a position transfer, a new probation period is imposed. Third, "no social insurance during probation" — the social insurance obligation runs from the first day of work and is unrelated to whether there is probation; for the employer's obligations, see Section 12, Entry 16. Being dismissed during probation does not mean receiving nothing; it depends on whether the reason given by the unit is one of those permitted by law. For illegal dismissals, see Entry 6 for 2N

### 4. On being laid off, first calculate N: one month of wages per full year; under six months counts as half a month
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Run the calculation once with a calculator, ten minutes
- Plain-language summary: The economic compensation the company must pay on layoff is calculated as follows: one year of service yields one month of wages; six months to under one year counts as one year; under six months yields half a month. The monthly wage here is the average over the 12 months before leaving; bonuses, allowances, and subsidies are all included. Wages above three times the local average monthly wage for the prior year are capped at three times, and the years counted are capped at 12
- Benefit: The economic compensation defined by the Labor Contract Law — the money the unit owes when laying you off — is commonly called N. The formula is: "one month's wages paid for each full year... for six months to less than one year, calculated as one year; for less than six months, half a month's wages is paid to the worker." Where the monthly wage exceeds three times the local average monthly wage of employees for the prior year, it is capped at three times, and the years of service are capped at twelve
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007). Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China (effective January 1, 2008) (Article 47). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: Monthly wage refers to the average wage over the twelve months before the labor contract was dissolved. Bonuses, allowances, and subsidies must all be included, not just the base wage. This point is most often reduced by the company

### 5. If the company dismisses you without 30 days' notice, it must pay an extra month's wages
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money
- Plain-language summary: If the company makes you leave without 30 days' written notice, it must pay an extra month's wages on top of N — this is the so-called N+1. In negotiation, list this one month separately; do not let the company fold it into N
- Benefit: The Labor Contract Law provides that when the employer dissolves the labor contract under this article, there are two options. One is "to notify the worker in writing thirty days in advance." The other is "to dissolve the labor contract after paying the worker an extra month's wages." If it wants you to leave immediately, it must pay an extra month's wages. This extra month is commonly called substitute-notice pay, calculated separately from N
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007). Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China (effective January 1, 2008) (Article 40). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: So a lawful layoff commonly results in N+1. In negotiation, separate N and 1; do not let the company fold the extra month into N

### 6. If the company terminates illegally, the compensation is double the economic compensation standard
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Going through arbitration takes months. The hard part is keeping up with the case for those months, without giving up midway
- Plain-language summary: When the company's termination is illegal, the compensation is double the economic compensation standard — the so-called 2N. Whether the termination is illegal depends on the specific circumstances. First consult legal aid or a lawyer, then decide whether to negotiate with the company or go to arbitration
- Benefit: The Labor Contract Law provides that where the employer dissolves or terminates the labor contract in violation of this law, "it shall pay compensation to the worker at twice the economic compensation standard stipulated in Article 47 of this law." The formula is: first calculate one tranche by the economic compensation standard, then double it and pay it to you
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007). Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China (effective January 1, 2008) (Article 87). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: This is the so-called 2N. What counts as illegal termination depends on the specific circumstances. First consult legal aid or a lawyer, then decide whether to negotiate or go to arbitration; the path is in Section 7

### 7. Do not sign "voluntarily resigning for personal reasons"; once signed, N is gone
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is resisting the pressure to sign on the spot
- Plain-language summary: Signing "voluntarily resigning for personal reasons" usually forfeits both economic compensation and unemployment insurance benefits. Resigning on your own initiative is not among the situations that trigger compensation, except for employer faults such as wage arrears or failure to pay social insurance. You do not have to sign that day; sleep on it for a night
- Benefit: The Labor Contract Law restricts the situations that trigger economic compensation to those listed in the law. A worker who resigns on their own initiative is not among them, unless it is because of statutory employer faults such as wage arrears or failure to pay social insurance. Signing "voluntarily resigning" usually forfeits both economic compensation and unemployment insurance benefits
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007). Labor Contract Law of the People's Republic of China (effective January 1, 2008) (Articles 38 and 46). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>
- Note: A common company line is "sign first to speed up the process; we'll pay the compensation separately." If so, write it into the agreement on the spot; otherwise do not sign. Not signing that day carries no legal consequence; sleep on it for a night

### 8. Before leaving, save your pay stubs, attendance records, labor contract, social insurance records, and chat logs
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Spend half an hour saving the materials. The hard part is remembering to save them before leaving
- Plain-language summary: Once you hand back your computer and accounts, pay stubs, attendance, contracts, social insurance records, and chat logs can never be retrieved. In arbitration, how much your wages were and whether there was overtime still depend on the materials you hold. What you are saving are your own employment records; do not take the company's source code or client list
- Benefit: Once computers and accounts are handed back, these materials can never be retrieved. In arbitration, although the burden of proof tilts toward the unit on certain matters, how much your wages were and whether there was overtime still rely on the evidence in your hands
- Evidence grade: C
- Source: Author's experience, no direct literature; the path to defending your rights is in Section 7
- Note: What you save are your own employment materials. Do not casually take the company's source code, client lists, or technical documents; the risks of taking them are in Section 11

### 9. Right after leaving, do two things immediately: register as unemployed to claim unemployment insurance, and review any non-compete agreement
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money
- Plain-language summary: Right after leaving, do two things. First, register as unemployed and claim unemployment insurance. Second, review any non-compete agreement. Unemployment insurance is counted from the day you register; every month you delay is one month less of payment. Only those who lost their job without wanting to leave can claim it — this is another reason for Entry 7 (do not sign "voluntarily resigning for personal reasons")
- Benefit: Claiming unemployment insurance has four thresholds: unemployment insurance paid for at least 1 year, the job loss not being of your own volition (interruption of employment not due to personal willingness), already registered as unemployed, and still actively looking for work. The number of years paid determines whether you can claim for up to 12, 18, or 24 months. How to apply online, see Section 7, Entry 1 (apply for unemployment insurance online first when unemployed). Non-compete restrictions run for up to 2 years. During those 2 years, the company must compensate you monthly. If the contract does not specify the amount, it is calculated as 30% of your average monthly wage over the 12 months before leaving, and may not be lower than the local minimum wage. Only if the company itself fails to pay for 3 consecutive months may you request to be released from the agreement; see Section 11, Entry 12 (what to do if the company doesn't pay after you've signed a non-compete). Non-compete has a time limit, but the obligations you owe before expiry must still be honored
- Evidence grade: C
- Source: Author's experience, no direct literature; the figures cited come from State Council (1999). Unemployment Insurance Regulation (State Council Order No. 258, Articles 14, 16, and 17: "Unemployment insurance is calculated from the date of unemployment registration"). <https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=517> and Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2012). Labor Contract Law (2012 amendment, Articles 23 and 24). <https://yjglj.fushun.gov.cn/008/20260622/d846d3c2-df3d-4c4c-af16-898ede43e1ee.html> (reproduced by the Fushun Municipal Emergency Management Bureau)
- Note: Rated C because "go register the very day you leave" has no direct literature; the figures in the Source line are themselves the original statutory text. Unemployment benefits do not expire, but they are counted from the day of registration, so each day earlier is one more day's money. For application materials and the online portal, see Section 7. For non-compete compensation standards and termination conditions, see Section 11. Signing "voluntarily resigning for personal reasons" forfeits not only N but also unemployment insurance; see Entry 7 (do not sign "voluntarily resigning for personal reasons")


### 10. Before taking a job with dust, noise, or chemicals, check whether the contract discloses the hazards; the three occupational health exams are arranged and paid for by the unit
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. When signing the contract, read the "occupational disease hazard notification" section line by line. Keep a copy of every exam report. The hard part is asking out loud and getting the materials in person
- Plain-language summary: When signing the contract, the unit must truthfully write into the contract what occupational disease hazards exist, how to protect against them, and what benefits apply. If it conceals anything, you may refuse to do the work, and the unit may not dismiss you for it. The three occupational health exams — pre-employment, in-service, and on-leaving — are arranged and paid for by the unit. Without a leaving exam, the unit may not dissolve your contract
- Benefit: The Occupational Disease Prevention Law provides that when signing the labor contract, the unit must explain four things clearly: what occupational disease hazards this work may produce, what consequences may result, how to protect against them, and what benefits apply. The statutory text reads "truthfully inform the worker and specify in the labor contract; concealment or deception is prohibited." If you are reassigned after signing to a hazardous job not disclosed in the contract, the unit must truthfully inform you anew and negotiate a contract amendment with you. If the unit fails to do so, "the worker has the right to refuse to engage in work with occupational disease hazards, and the employer may not, for that reason, dissolve the labor contract concluded with the worker." There are three exams in total: pre-employment, in-service, and on-leaving. "Occupational health examination costs are borne by the employer" — the unit pays; results must be communicated to you in writing. Persons who have not had a pre-employment exam may not be assigned to work involving contact with occupational disease hazards. Persons whose physical conditions are inherently unsuited to such work (with occupational contraindications) may not be assigned to do these contraindicated operations. If work-related health damage is detected, the person must be transferred out of the original post and properly placed. **"Workers who have not undergone a pre-leaving occupational health examination may not have their labor contract dissolved or terminated."** On leaving, you "have the right to request a copy of your occupational health monitoring file, and the employer shall truthfully and free of charge provide it, and stamp the copies provided." The unit must truthfully make a copy for you free of charge, and stamp it (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2018). Occupational Disease Prevention Law (2018 amendment, Articles 33, 35, and 36). <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201711/t20171104_779851.html> (reproduced by the Beijing Municipal People's Government)
- Note: Three questions in the interview let you roughly judge whether the workplace is legitimate: whether the position has occupational disease hazards, whether the contract discloses them, and who pays for the pre-employment exam. Evasive answers or "just start and we'll see" — weigh the risk yourself. The leaving exam is the most easily overlooked: without a leaving exam, the unit may not dissolve or terminate the labor contract. This exam is also a key piece of evidence for later applying for an occupational disease diagnosis; the file copy is the same — get it before leaving. Occupational disease itself is handled as a workplace injury; benefits begin at Entry 12

### 11. Damage from dust, noise, and chemical toxins is irreversible: protective equipment must be provided by the unit; you may refuse work without protective measures
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is always wearing the particulate respirator, earplugs, and goggles, and refusing work without protection, at the cost of being called "high maintenance" by colleagues
- Plain-language summary: Pneumoconiosis, noise-induced hearing loss, and chemical eye burns are all statutory occupational diseases; once the lungs and hearing are damaged, they cannot be restored. You have the right to know what hazards the position entails, and the right to demand that the unit provide protective facilities and personal protective equipment. You may refuse to do work without protection. If the unit cuts your pay or dismisses you for that, those actions are invalid
- Benefit: Statutory occupational diseases, effective August 1, 2025, comprise 12 major categories and 135 types, including 4 open-ended clauses. The first category is occupational pneumoconiosis. It includes silicosis, coal workers' pneumoconiosis, graphite pneumoconiosis, carbon black pneumoconiosis, asbestosis, and talc pneumoconiosis. It also includes cement pneumoconiosis, mica pneumoconiosis, potter's pneumoconiosis, aluminum pneumoconiosis, welder's pneumoconiosis, and foundry worker's pneumoconiosis, plus one open-ended clause. Occupational ear, nose, throat, and oral diseases include noise-induced hearing loss and blast-induced deafness. Occupational eye diseases include chemical eye burns and electric ophthalmia. These injuries are all irreversible; no treatment can restore the lungs or hearing. The law provides four rights you can invoke on the spot. First, to know what occupational disease hazards exist in this workplace, what consequences they may cause, and what protective measures should be taken. Second, to require the unit to provide qualified protective facilities and personal protective equipment and to improve working conditions. Third, to criticize, report, and accuse practices that violate occupational disease prevention laws and regulations, as well as practices that endanger life and health. Fourth, "to refuse unlawful directives and orders to undertake work without occupational disease protective measures" — you may refuse non-compliant directives and work without protection. Moreover, "if wages, benefits, or other treatment of a worker are reduced, or the labor contract concluded with the worker is dissolved or terminated, because the worker lawfully exercised these rights, the action is invalid." Cutting pay, cutting benefits, or dismissing you because you invoked these rights is invalid (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: National Health Commission, Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, National Disease Control Bureau, and All-China Federation of Trade Unions (2024). Classification and Catalog of Occupational Diseases (effective August 1, 2025). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202412/content_6992843.htm>; for the 12 categories and 135 types in the new catalog, see National Health Commission (2024). New catalog adjusts occupational diseases to 12 major categories and 135 types. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202412/content_6992838.htm>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2018). Occupational Disease Prevention Law (Article 39). <https://www.beijing.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengcefagui/qtwj/201711/t20171104_779851.html> (reproduced by the Beijing Municipal People's Government)
- Note: Choose the right mask — only particulate respirators block dust; ordinary gauze masks and disposable surgical masks do not. If the unit does not provide earplugs for a noisy job, buy your own — a few yuan a pair; once hearing is gone, it cannot come back. "Two years to save enough money, then leave" does not hold for pneumoconiosis. Silicosis can develop years after leaving dust work, and will continue to worsen. So the leaving exam is especially important (see Entry 10, three occupational health exams arranged and paid for by the unit). The new catalog also adds two categories. One is occupational musculoskeletal diseases, consisting of carpal tunnel syndrome, limited to manufacturing workers who use the wrists in long, repetitive, or forceful work. The other is occupational mental and behavioral disorders, consisting of post-traumatic stress disorder, limited to people's police, medical and health personnel, fire and rescue, and other emergency responders involved in incident response. On-scene treatment of chemical splashes on the body, see Section 13, Entry 21

### 12. Injured at work or hit on the way to or from work — first thing is to apply for workplace injury recognition; if the unit does not report, report it yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money; one trip to the social insurance administrative department. Bring three pieces of material: a workplace injury recognition application form, proof of the labor relationship, and a medical diagnosis certificate
- Plain-language summary: An injury at work counts as a workplace injury. Being hit on the way to or from work, where you do not bear primary responsibility, also counts. But without workplace injury recognition, medical costs, the wages paid during recovery, and the disability benefit all disappear. The unit should report within 30 days. If the unit does not report, you or your family or the union may report directly to the social insurance administrative department within one year
- Benefit: Without workplace injury recognition, medical costs, the wages paid during recovery (suspension-with-pay period wages), and the disability benefit all disappear. If the unit fails to report within 30 days, the workplace injury benefit costs during that period are borne by the unit itself
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2010 revision). Regulation on Workplace Injury Insurance (State Council Order No. 586). Article 14 lists seven situations that "shall be recognized as workplace injury," including "(6) being injured on the way to or from work by a traffic accident or urban rail transit, passenger ferry, or train accident in which the person does not bear primary responsibility"; Article 17: "The unit shall, within 30 days from the date of the accident injury or the date of diagnosis or appraisal as an occupational disease, submit a workplace injury recognition application to the social insurance administrative department of the pooling region"; "Where the employer fails to submit a workplace injury recognition application as provided in the preceding paragraph, the injured worker, or a close relative, or a labor union organization may, within 1 year from the date of the accident injury or the date of diagnosis or appraisal as an occupational disease, directly submit a workplace injury recognition application to the social insurance administrative department of the pooling region at the employer's location"; "Where the employer fails to submit a workplace injury recognition application within the time limit specified in paragraph 1 of this article, the workplace injury benefits and related costs incurred during that period in accordance with this Regulation shall be borne by that employer"; Article 19: "Where the worker or a close relative maintains it is a workplace injury and the employer disagrees, the employer bears the burden of proof"; Article 20: "A workplace injury recognition decision shall be made within 60 days from the date of accepting the workplace injury recognition application." <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Note: 1 year is a hard deadline; once past, no application is possible. If the unit says it is not a workplace injury, the unit must prove it. But you should also keep your own materials: photos of the day of the incident, contact information for colleagues, treatment records, and attendance. Article 16 lists three situations that are not recognized: intentional crime, drunkenness or drug use, self-injury or suicide. Article 15 separately provides for situations "deemed to be workplace injury": sudden death from illness at work during working hours and at the work post, or death within 48 hours after emergency rescue failed

### 13. Do not believe "as long as you make it to your desk, it counts as a workplace injury": when suddenly unwell, first call 120 — not rush to clock in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money
- Plain-language summary: Rushing to the office when suddenly unwell does not get you extra money. Deemed-workplace-injury requires that the person fall ill during working hours and at the work post; falling ill on the way before arriving does not qualify. Rescue lasting more than 48 hours also does not qualify. The dozens of minutes lost to fitting those conditions are paid for with your own survival rate
- Benefit: Deemed-workplace-injury requires that the person "suddenly die from illness during working hours and at the work post" or die within 48 hours after emergency rescue failed. Delaying medical care to meet these two conditions costs your own chance of survival, and the compensation may not even be obtained
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Regulation on Workplace Injury Insurance, Article 15, item (1): "Sudden death from illness during working hours and at the work post, or death within 48 hours after emergency rescue failed" is deemed a workplace injury; Article 14, item (6) lists only "being injured on the way to or from work by a traffic accident or urban rail transit, passenger ferry, or train accident in which the person does not bear primary responsibility" — sudden illness on the way is not included. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Note: Both conditions must be met simultaneously: during working hours, and at the work post. Falling ill on the way and not reaching the post does not apply. Falling ill at the post, being sent home or to the hospital, and dying within 48 hours after emergency rescue failed may still apply. What matters is where the person was when falling ill, not where they ultimately die. Short videos that turn this into "as long as you make it to the company, there's money" have it exactly backwards. What changes the outcome is whether those dozens of minutes after falling ill are spent getting to a hospital. Also, 48 hours is a hard line; rescue beyond 48 hours does not qualify

### 14. If the unit did not enroll you in workplace injury insurance, the workplace injury benefits still apply — the unit pays in full at the same standard
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. If the unit does not acknowledge, you have to go through arbitration or even lawsuit, starting from several months. The hard part is keeping up with the case for those months
- Plain-language summary: If the unit did not enroll you in workplace injury insurance, the workplace injury benefits still apply. It is simply that the unit pays in full at the same items and standards, not one cent less. Non-enrollment triggers additional penalties for the unit: ordered to make up the contributions, late payment surcharges of 0.05% per day, and if still unpaid past the deadline, an additional fine of 1 to 3 times the underpaid amount. "The company didn't buy workplace injury insurance so it can't pay" is a lie
- Benefit: The regulation explicitly states: a unit that should have participated in workplace injury insurance but did not, when an employee suffers a workplace injury, that unit pays out the money according to the items and standards stipulated by the regulation. Which tranches apply and how much each is, are identical to those for an insured unit; the only difference is that the unit pays out of its own pocket
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Regulation on Workplace Injury Insurance, Article 62, paragraph 2: "Where an employee of an employer that should have participated in workplace injury insurance but did not suffers a workplace injury, that employer shall pay the costs according to the workplace injury benefit items and standards stipulated in this Regulation." Same article, paragraph 1: ordered to enroll within a time limit and make up the contributions, "from the date of underpayment, late payment surcharges of five ten-thousandths per day shall be accrued; if still unpaid past the deadline, a fine of 1 to 3 times the underpaid amount shall be imposed." <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Note: "The company didn't buy workplace injury insurance for you, so it can't pay" is the most common lie in these situations. Non-enrollment only triggers additional penalties for the unit; your benefits are not reduced

### 15. After the injury stabilizes, undergo labor capacity assessment; the disability grade converts directly into money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Going through a labor capacity assessment takes some waiting
- Plain-language summary: After the injury stabilizes, undergo labor capacity assessment; the grade converts directly into money. The one-time disability benefit is calculated from your wages: grade 5 — 18 months, grade 6 — 16 months, grade 7 — 13 months, grade 8 — 11 months, grade 9 — 9 months, grade 10 — 7 months. Before the assessment result is out, do not sign a "one-time settlement" agreement
- Benefit: The one-time disability benefit is converted into several months of wages based on disability grade, paid out as a lump sum. Grade 5 — 18 months, grade 6 — 16 months, grade 7 — 13 months of the worker's own wages. Grade 8 — 11 months, grade 9 — 9 months, grade 10 — 7 months of the worker's own wages
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Regulation on Workplace Injury Insurance, Article 21: "After treatment, when the injury is relatively stable and there is disability affecting labor capacity, labor capacity assessment shall be conducted"; Article 22: "Labor function impairment is divided into ten disability grades, the most severe being grade 1 and the least severe being grade 10"; Article 36, grade 5 and 6 benefits (one-time disability benefit 18/16 months of own wages, monthly disability allowance at 70%/60% of own wages); Article 37, grade 7 to 10 benefits (13/11/9/7 months of own wages). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>
- Note: Grades 5 and 6 retain the labor relationship — the person is still an employee of that unit and receives a monthly disability allowance. For grades 7 to 10, when the contract expires or the person requests termination, two further sums apply: a one-time workplace injury medical benefit, paid by the fund, and a one-time disability employment benefit, paid by the unit; how much is set by the province. Before the assessment result is out, do not sign a "one-time settlement" agreement

### 16. The three sums for work-related death must be distinguished: funeral subsidy, dependent relative pension, and one-time work-death benefit
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money; one set of procedures
- Plain-language summary: Work-related death is three sums; do not negotiate only one. The funeral subsidy is 6 months of the local average monthly wage of employees for the prior year. The dependent relative pension is paid monthly out of the deceased's wages — spouse 40% per month, other relatives 30% each per month. The one-time work-death benefit is 20 times the national per-capita disposable income of urban residents for the prior year — there is only this one number nationwide
- Benefit: The money for work-related death is divided into three sums. The first is the funeral subsidy, an amount equal to 6 months of the average monthly wage of employees in the pooling region (the participating region) for the prior year. The second is the dependent relative pension, paid monthly out of the deceased's own wages — spouse 40% per month, other relatives 30% each per month. The third is the one-time work-death benefit, an amount equal to 20 times the national per-capita disposable income of urban residents for the prior year; there is only this one number nationwide, regardless of region. For work-related death in 2026, this sum is about 1.13 million yuan (1,130,040 yuan). The calculation is the prior year's national per-capita disposable income of urban residents of 56,502 yuan multiplied by 20
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Regulation on Workplace Injury Insurance, Article 39: "(1) The funeral subsidy is 6 months of the average monthly wage of employees in the pooling region for the prior year; (2) The dependent relative pension is paid, in a certain proportion of the worker's own wages, to relatives who were mainly supported by the deceased worker and who lack labor capacity. Standard: spouse 40% per month, other relatives 30% each per month, with an additional 10% per month for elderly without spouse or orphans on top of the above standard... (3) The one-time work-death benefit standard is 20 times the national per-capita disposable income of urban residents for the prior year." <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>; National Bureau of Statistics (2026). Income and consumption expenditure of residents in 2025. <https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202601/t20260119_1962321.html>
- Note: The one-time work-death benefit is uniform nationwide; the amount is updated each year with the figures published by the National Bureau of Statistics. In negotiation, do not accept "we have a lower local standard here"

### 17. If you are bullied, insulted, or obstructed at work over a long period, do not just endure: first record and preserve evidence, then pursue a complaint, police report, or arbitration depending on the nature of the matter
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Time is spent recording: dates, people present, the other party's exact words; keep originals of chat screenshots and emails. Arbitration and litigation take months. Changing jobs is the highest cost; whether to do so is your own calculation
- Plain-language summary: People bullied at work later have about a 60% higher risk of heart disease and stroke; the more bullying, the higher the risk. Bullied people are about twice as likely to develop suicidal ideation years later compared to others. Being scolded and bullied is not a small matter that just passes if you endure it
- Benefit: Health: three Swedish and Danish cohorts combined, totaling 79,201 employed persons aged 18 to 65. None had cardiovascular disease at baseline; mean follow-up 12.4 years. 9% reported being bullied at work in the past year. Bullied persons had about 59% higher risk of cardiovascular disease (coronary heart disease and cerebrovascular disease) (HR 1.59, 95% CI 1.28–1.98). Those who experienced workplace violence had about 25% higher risk (HR 1.25, 1.12–1.40). Both showed a dose-response with greater exposure. A nationwide random-sample longitudinal survey in Norway, 1,846 employed persons, measured once every two to three years, three times in total. Bullied persons were about twice as likely as others to later develop suicidal ideation (OR 2.05, 95% CI 1.08–3.89). Conversely, those who first had suicidal thoughts were not more likely to be bullied later. Legal: China has no specific law governing "workplace bullying," so each behavior is matched to its specific category. A unit that insults, physically punishes, beats, unlawfully searches, or detains a worker is subject to administrative penalties, must compensate for damages, and where a crime is constituted, is subject to criminal liability. A unit that uses violence or threats to coerce you to work — you may immediately dissolve the contract without prior notice and still receive economic compensation. The unit must take measures to prevent and stop sexual harassment carried out using power or superior-subordinate relationships. The victim may require the harasser to bear civil liability. Labor arbitration cases close within 45 days from the date of acceptance; complex cases may be extended by up to 15 days
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Xu T et al. (2019). Workplace bullying and workplace violence as risk factors for cardiovascular disease: a multi-cohort study. European Heart Journal. <https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehy683>; Nielsen MB, Nielsen GH, Notelaers G, Einarsen S (2015). Workplace Bullying and Suicidal Ideation: A 3-Wave Longitudinal Norwegian Study. American Journal of Public Health. <https://doi.org/10.2105/AJPH.2015.302855>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007). Labor Contract Law (Article 38, paragraph 2; Articles 46 and 88). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2007/content_711013.htm>; National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Article 1010). Full text reproduced by the Ankang Municipal Human Resources and Social Security Bureau <https://rsj.ankang.gov.cn/Content-2150407.html>
- Note: Rated B because: the health figures come from longitudinal records, not randomized experiments, and "being bullied" was self-reported. Four steps you can take first. First, start recording today; for audio recording, see Section 8, Entry 41 (recording). Second, report in writing through internal unit channels, leaving a record of having reported.
