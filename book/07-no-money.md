# 7. How to Live When You Have No Money

This section counts money and security. How much money you can receive, how much you can be exempted from, what services you can get, and what conditions you must meet are all written here; they are not converted to lifespan. Policies change every year and differ by region, so amounts and thresholds are subject to what your locality publishes for the current year. Entries are ordered as follows: those that cost nothing, require few procedures, and take effect quickly come first; those that require accumulating long-term eligibility or differ widely by region come later; those that rest only on experience and have no official documentation come last.

### 1. When unemployed, first claim unemployment insurance benefits online
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. You can submit an application from your phone; any of three entry points works: the "Palm 12333" App, the electronic social security card mini-program inside WeChat or Alipay, or the National Social Insurance Public Service Platform. With a social security card or ID card you can do it; in most areas no on-site visit is required. The precondition is to first complete unemployment registration.
- Plain-language summary: If your employer and you together have paid in for at least 1 year and you didn't resign voluntarily, after completing unemployment registration you can receive money. With 1 to under 5 years of contributions, you can receive up to 12 months. With 5 to under 10 years, up to 18 months. With 10+ years, up to 24 months. During the benefit period, your employee medical insurance is paid for you by unemployment insurance.
- Benefit: Three conditions must be met simultaneously. First, your employer and you together have paid unemployment insurance cumulatively for at least 1 year. Second, you did not voluntarily resign (the law calls it "interruption of employment for reasons not attributable to the worker (非因本人意愿中断就业)"). Third, you have completed unemployment registration and have the willingness to look for work. How long you can receive is calculated by contribution years. Cumulative 1 to under 5 years: up to 12 months. 5 to under 10 years: up to 18 months. 10+ years: up to 24 months. The monthly amount is set by the provincial government and differs by province. The amount is lower than the local minimum wage and higher than the urban minimum living guarantee. The national target is to gradually raise it to 90% of the minimum wage. During the unemployment insurance benefit period, you continue to participate in employee basic medical insurance and enjoy basic medical insurance benefits. The basic medical insurance premium that would have been paid is paid out of the unemployment insurance fund; you do not pay it personally (nationwide; the regulation has been in effect since 1999)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (1999). Unemployment insurance benefit standards, to be gradually raised to 90% of the minimum wage. <https://www.ndrc.gov.cn/fggz/jyysr/jysrsbxf/202206/t20220627_1328819.html>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2010). Social Insurance Law (Article 48). <https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5192937/content.html> (State Taxation Administration policy and regulation database)
- Note: Those who voluntarily resign are not counted as "not attributable to the worker" and cannot receive benefits. Employee medical insurance during the benefit period is paid for you by unemployment insurance. Provincial amounts vary widely; use what your locality publishes. The "90% of minimum wage" claim comes from the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security's 2017 guidance. ### 2. If owed wages, first complain to labor inspection, then apply for labor arbitration; both routes are free, and most cases resolve within months
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free; both complaint and arbitration charge nothing. You'll need to gather labor contract, pay stubs, attendance records, transfer records, and chat logs, and visit the human resources and social security bureau or arbitration committee 1 to 3 times. The arbitration statute of limitations is 1 year (called the arbitration limitation period in law). Those still working at the same employer with unpaid wages are not subject to this 1-year limit. Those who have already left must file within 1 year of the departure date. The hard part is making trip after trip and gathering evidence piece by piece; it's easy to lose steam midway.
- Plain-language summary: Both routes are free. First call 12333 or go to the labor inspection office at the local human resources and social security bureau to complain; after the case is filed, it must be investigated within 60 working days, and the company can be ordered to pay within a deadline, with an additional 50% to 100% damages for late payment. If that doesn't work, apply for labor arbitration, which closes within 45 days. But "case closed" does not equal "money received": if the company has no assets or the boss has fled, you may win and still get nothing.
- Benefit: The first step is the complaint. Call 12333, or go to labor inspection at the local human resources and social security bureau. The law requires investigation to be completed within 60 working days after filing; complex cases may extend by another 30 working days. Once verified, the company can be ordered to pay within a deadline. If payment is still not made past the deadline, an additional 50% to 100% of the payable amount must be added as damages. In 2024, nationwide inspection agencies handled 137,000 cases, recovering 8.65 billion yuan in wages for 654,000 people. If you hold an IOU or pay sheet and the wage arrears are clear, you can also directly apply to a basic court for a payment order. A payment order is a court-issued pay directive; the court issues it within 15 days of accepting. The second step is to apply for arbitration. The law requires cases to close within 45 days of acceptance, extendable by another 15 days. Wage claims can apply for advance execution: executing part of the award before the case is fully tried. If the amount owed does not exceed 12 times the local monthly minimum wage, it qualifies as final arbitration (一裁终局): once arbitrated, it's final, and the company cannot file suit. In 2024, nationwide, 4.257 million cases were handled, with 4.156 million closed. Settlement amount 93.47 billion yuan. Mediation success rate 79.6%, arbitration closure rate 98.2%, arbitration finalization rate 73.7%. Some say this drags on "half a year, a year" — that refers to cases that go from arbitration to court: first instance 6 months (3 months under summary procedure), plus second instance 3 months. In 2024 courts adjudicated 88,000 labor remuneration cases and recovered 28.54 billion yuan; 998 people were sentenced for the crime of refusing to pay labor remuneration (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A

- Note: Complaint and arbitration work, but distinguish "case closed" from "money received." The 98.2% closure rate only means the procedure was completed. If the company has no assets or the boss has fled, you can win and still get nothing. The Supreme People's Court only publishes the total amount actually executed (2.3 trillion yuan in 2024) and "execution-completion rate remained high"; how much of the wage-claim money actually reached the parties' pockets has no official data. "Arbitration finalization rate" is not defined in the bulletin; this entry understands it as "arbitrated and not taken to court." Wage arrears above a certain threshold reach the criminal filing standard. One type: owing one person for 3+ months, with the amount between 5,000 and 20,000+ yuan. The other: owing 10+ people, with cumulative amount between 30,000 and 100,000+ yuan. In either case, if payment is still refused after being ordered, it meets the filing threshold. Provincial high courts and provincial procuratorates set and publish the specific amount for their province within these ranges; check your province's number before complaining, and raise it explicitly at the time of complaint. Migrant workers on construction sites have separate provisions for wage-dedicated accounts and general contractor advance payment; going to the general contractor is more useful than going to the labor contractor. Rural migrant workers applying for legal aid for wage claims are not subject to financial-hardship screening. ### 3. If you can't afford to sue, apply for legal aid; wage claims, alimony, and workplace injury cases are already covered
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Legal aid does not charge the aided person a cent.
- Plain-language summary: Wage claims, alimony claims, social insurance or minimum-living-guarantee benefits, and losses from acting heroically can all be applied for as free legal aid. Call 12348, or go to the local legal aid center. You no longer need to go back to your home town to get a financial-hardship certificate; you just need to truthfully state it yourself. Defendants who may face the death penalty, as well as blind, deaf, mute, and minor defendants, are assigned lawyers across the board.
- Benefit: The State Council's regulation specifies the six categories of civil legal aid. The regulation's original wording is: "requesting state compensation according to law; requesting social insurance benefits or minimum living guarantee benefits; requesting pensions and relief funds; requesting alimony, child support, or support payments; requesting payment of labor remuneration; asserting civil rights and interests arising from acting heroically." In plain language: requesting state compensation, social insurance or minimum-living benefits, pensions and relief funds, alimony and child support, wage claims, losses from acting heroically. On the criminal side, defendants who may face the death penalty, as well as blind, deaf, mute, and minor defendants, are assigned lawyers regardless of financial status
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2003). Legal Aid Regulations (State Council Decree No. 385, Articles 10, 11, 12). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6944.htm>; Officials of the Ministry of Justice (2021). Q&A on Implementing the Legal Aid Law. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2021-12/29/content_5665114.htm>
- Note: The Legal Aid Law took effect on January 1, 2022, with one change. Previously you had to "submit a financial-hardship certificate." Now you only need to truthfully state your financial situation yourself; no need to go back to your home town for a certificate. Entry points are local legal aid centers and public legal service centers; you can also call 12348

### 4. When there's no way out, go to a relief station; they provide food, shelter, and a ticket home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Go to the local relief management station yourself to ask for help, or tell a police officer or civil affairs department to take you there. On entry, truthfully register your name and belongings. The hard part is opening your mouth to tell someone you have no money and nowhere to go.
- Plain-language summary: Relief stations provide food and shelter, send you to hospital if you fall ill, and help contact your family. Those without money to get home are issued tickets. Stays are generally no more than 10 days. Voluntary entry, voluntary departure; it is not custody, and no cash is given. Tickets are issued to your hukou (household registration) location or your place of residence; think clearly about where you want to return before going.
- Benefit: Relief stations provide food and shelter meeting sanitary requirements. Those who fall acutely ill while at the station are sent to hospital for treatment. They also help contact your relatives and workplace. Those without money to buy a ticket back to their hukou location or place of residence are issued a travel voucher (for boat or train) — i.e., a ticket. The hukou location is your home town; the place of residence is where you currently live. The relief period is generally no more than 10 days. Those waiting for family to pick them up, awaiting treatment, or awaiting placement may be extended. Police and other administrative personnel, when carrying out their duties and encountering vagrants and beggars, should inform them they can seek help. For people with disabilities, minors, the elderly, and those with limited mobility, they should guide and escort them there (nationwide, since 2003)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2003). Measures for the Administration of Relief for Vagrants and Beggars with No Means of Living in Cities (State Council Decree No. 381, Articles 5, 6, 7). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62246.htm>; Ministry of Civil Affairs (2003). Implementing Rules for the Measures for the Administration of Relief for Vagrants and Beggars with No Means of Living in Cities (Ministry of Civil Affairs Decree No. 24, Articles 11, 12). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2003/content_62510.htm>
- Note: Voluntary help-seeking, voluntary departure; not custody. What's provided is basic food and shelter; no cash is given. Travel vouchers are issued for the hukou location or place of residence; think clearly about where to go before seeking help

### 5. To find work, first use free public employment services and the gig-work market; don't use paid intermediaries
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Register at a public employment service agency at or above the county level, such as an employment service center or a human resources market. You can also register at the local gig-work market. If you don't know the address, call 12333 and ask.
- Plain-language summary: Public institutions like employment service centers and human resources markets provide job introductions, career guidance, policy consultation, and employment and unemployment registration, all for free. The law explicitly forbids them from monetizing these services. Gig-work market registration and information posting are also free. Take the opportunity to complete unemployment registration, since you'll need it for unemployment benefits and employment-difficulty-person certification.
- Benefit: The law requires the following to be free across the board: employment policy consultation; information on where jobs are being offered and where workers are needed; career guidance and job introduction; employment registration and unemployment registration; and employment assistance for employment-difficulty persons. These institutions may not engage in business activities or profit from these services. Gig-work job and recruitment information, registration and posting are also provided free to the public and have been brought under public employment information services. For those with long idle time (i.e., who can't get gigs for long stretches) and low-income households among gig workers, enhanced assistance is also required (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2007, 2015 amendment). Employment Promotion Law (Article 35). <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647636.htm>; Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security and four other departments (2022). Opinions on Strengthening the Construction of Gig-Work Markets and Improving Job-Seeking and Recruitment Services (Ren She Bu Fa [2022] No. 38). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-07/09/content_5700177.htm>
- Note: Unemployment registration is a prerequisite for claiming unemployment insurance benefits and certifying employment-difficulty status; get it done at the same time. For day-rate gig work, settle wages on the spot, and keep the other party's contact information and a record of work done

### 6. For sudden misfortune, first apply for temporary assistance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Apply to the township government or subdistrict office, explaining what happened in your household — for example, fire, traffic accident, sudden serious illness of a family member, or a sudden surge in unavoidable daily expenses. After review and public notice, the county-level civil affairs department approves. Prepare materials per the local checklist; usually 1 to 2 trips.
- Plain-language summary: When something sudden happens to the household — such as fire, traffic accident, sudden serious illness of a family member — that overwhelms basic living for a time, you can apply to the township government or subdistrict office for temporary assistance. You don't need to already be a minimum-living-guarantee household. Mostly it's a one-time payment; the amount varies widely by locality.
- Benefit: Two categories of households can receive temporary assistance. One is households that, due to accidents, sudden serious illness of a family member, and similar reasons, cannot make ends meet even for the most basic expenses of food, shelter, and medical care for a time. The other is minimum-living-guarantee households whose unavoidable expenses have suddenly increased beyond what they can bear. What kinds of events qualify and how much is given are set and published by local people's governments at or above the county level (nationwide system, amount set locally, since 2014)
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: State Council (2014, 2019 revision). Interim Measures for Social Assistance (State Council Decree No. 649, Articles 47, 48, 49). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5468952.htm>
- Note: You don't need to already be a minimum-living-guarantee household. Mostly a one-time payment; amounts vary widely by locality. Use what your locality publishes for the current year

### 7. If your income is below the local minimum living guarantee line, apply for the minimum living guarantee (dibao)
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. A household member who lives with you submits a written application to the township government or subdistrict office where your hukou is registered. If applying yourself is inconvenient, you can entrust the villagers' committee or residents' committee to act for you. You must undergo household income and asset verification. Usually 2 to 3 trips, with ID card, hukou book, and income/asset documentation. The hard part is laying bare your household finances for inspection and waiting for public notice.
- Plain-language summary: If total household income divided by number of people falls below the local minimum-living-guarantee line, and your household assets also meet requirements, you can apply. At end of 2024, the national urban dibao average was 798.1 yuan per person per month, and rural was 593.9 yuan. You must apply at your hukou location. Having a house, car, or savings, or having children able to support you, may mean denial.
- Benefit: If total household income divided by number of people is below the local minimum-living-guarantee standard, and household assets are also within the stipulated range, you can receive dibao. At end of 2024, the national urban dibao average was 798.1 yuan/person·month, and rural 593.9 yuan/person·month. Urban dibao covered 6.250 million people; rural dibao covered 33.615 million. The dibao standard is set this way: take the local previous-year per-capita consumption expenditure, multiply by a quantified ratio, and adjust at intervals. In plain language, take how much the average person in the locality spent in the previous year, and apply a discount (national average; varies by locality)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2014, 2019 revision). Interim Measures for Social Assistance (State Council Decree No. 649, Articles 9, 10, 11). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2019/content_5468952.htm>; National Bureau of Statistics (2025). Social undertakings developing well, people's livelihood continually improving — Social and livelihood statistics report since the 14th Five-Year Plan. <https://www.gov.cn/lianbo/bumen/202509/content_7042627.htm>; Ministry of Civil Affairs and three other departments (2024). Guiding Opinions on Further Improving the Setting and Adjustment of Minimum Living Guarantee Standards (Min Fa [2024] No. 16). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202403/content_7007237.htm>
- Note: Dibao is the last line of protection; you must apply at your hukou location. If household house, car, or savings exceed local standards, or if children are able to support you, approval may be denied. Once approved, other benefits follow: subsidization for resident medical insurance contributions (resident medical insurance see Entry 9), medical assistance for treatment (medical assistance see Entry 10), and exemption from financial-hardship screening for legal aid (legal aid see Entry 3)

### 8. Holders of a disability certificate can apply for the two subsidies for persons with disabilities
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Bring your disability certificate to the subdistrict or township and submit one application.
- Plain-language summary: People with disabilities in minimum-living-guarantee households can receive a living subsidy. Those with disability grade 1 or 2 who need long-term care can receive a care subsidy. Both can be received simultaneously. Standards are set by each province and vary widely; ask at your subdistrict for specifics. Bring your second-generation disability certificate and apply at the subdistrict or township once.
- Benefit: The State Council established two subsidies. The first is the living subsidy for persons with disabilities in financial difficulty; the document's stated recipients are "persons with disabilities in minimum-living-guarantee households; localities with conditions may gradually extend coverage to low-income persons with disabilities and other persons with disabilities in difficulty." In other words, first cover persons with disabilities in minimum-living-guarantee households; localities with the fiscal capacity can extend further. The second is the care subsidy for severely disabled persons; the document's stated recipients are "severely disabled persons whose disability grade is assessed as level 1 or 2 and who need long-term care." The two can be received simultaneously
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2015). Opinions on Comprehensively Establishing a Living Subsidy for Persons with Disabilities in Difficulty and a Care Subsidy for Severely Disabled Persons (Guo Fa [2015] No. 52). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2015-09/25/content_10181.htm>
- Note: Standards are set by provincial governments and vary widely by locality, from tens to several hundred yuan per month. The second-generation disability certificate is required for application. If applying yourself is inconvenient, a family member may act for you

### 9. Don't let the 400-yuan annual resident medical insurance lapse; hardship households can be exempted
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: The portion you pay personally is no less than 400 yuan per person per year. This is the national standard for 2024 and 2025; localities may set higher. You must pay during the fixed annual centralized contribution period. Extremely-poor people are fully subsidized — this money is paid entirely by the government. Minimum-living-guarantee recipients are subsidized at a fixed amount, with the government paying a fixed portion.
- Plain-language summary: Individuals pay 400 yuan a year and up; the government subsidizes about 700 yuan per head. Extremely-poor people are fully subsidized; minimum-living-guarantee recipients are subsidized at a fixed amount. Lapses have a cost: if you don't enroll during the centralized period or don't pay continuously, after paying there is a 3-month period during which medical care isn't reimbursed, with an additional month added in principle for each additional year of lapse.
- Benefit: Besides the portion you pay personally, the government subsidizes an additional amount per head. For 2024, no less than 670 yuan; for 2025, no less than 700 yuan (the 700 yuan figure comes from a Hebei provincial document's restatement of the national standard). The 2026 Government Work Report said it would be raised by another 24 yuan. Minimum-living-guarantee recipients and extremely-poor people, for medical expenses that meet the regulations, after medical insurance reimbursement, the remaining portion can be reimbursed again at no less than 70% through medical assistance. The cost of lapse is a waiting period. Those who didn't enroll during the centralized contribution period or who didn't pay continuously have a fixed 3-month benefit waiting period after paying, during which medical care is not reimbursed. For each additional year of lapse, an additional month is added in principle (effective nationwide, 2025)
- Evidence grade: A

### 10. For serious illness, first go through medical insurance, major illness insurance, medical assistance, and cross-province filing; don't touch online lending
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. The order is fixed; do it step by step. First, before cross-province hospitalization, file using the "National Cross-Province Medical Treatment Filing" mini-program inside the "National Healthcare Security Administration" WeChat official account. Second, settle directly at discharge. Third, if what you still owe after reimbursement is too heavy, apply to the township government or subdistrict office for medical assistance. Fourth, if you're still employed, take sick leave under the medical treatment period — the medical treatment period is the time you can stop work due to illness without being dismissed; workplace injury cases go through workplace injury insurance. Fifth, if you can't pay credit card debt, take the initiative to negotiate installments with the bank. Sixth, if still not enough, raise money on a personal-help-seeking platform designated by the Ministry of Civil Affairs. Materials are mainly diagnostic certificate, fee schedule, and settlement statement; 2 to 4 trips. The hard part is being sick and still making trip after trip for procedures.
- Plain-language summary: The order is fixed. File in the "National Healthcare Security Administration" official account before cross-province hospitalization, settle directly at discharge, no need to advance the full amount first. If what you still owe after reimbursement is more than you can bear, apply to the township or subdistrict for medical assistance. Employed people get 3 to 24 months of medical treatment period based on years of work; during this time the company cannot dismiss you. Online lending and high-interest private loans are not to be touched.
- Benefit: The state calls basic medical insurance, major illness insurance, and medical assistance collectively the "three-tier system," with each tier catching what the previous one didn't. Minimum-living-guarantee recipients and extremely-poor people, for medical expenses that meet regulations, are covered at no less than 70%. Seriously ill patients who weren't poor but became poor from medical bills can self-apply for further assistance. With cross-province hospitalization filing completed before admission, you can settle directly at discharge, no need to advance the full amount and then return to your enrollment locality for reimbursement. For sick leave there are 3 to 24 months of medical treatment period, depending on years of work. During this time, the company may not terminate the labor contract, and sick-leave pay may not be lower than 80% of the local minimum wage. Workplace-injured workers have a suspension-of-work-with-pay period — the time off work to recover from injury — during which original wages and benefits continue to be paid monthly by the employer, generally no more than 12 months.
