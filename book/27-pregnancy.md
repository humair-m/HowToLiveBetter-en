# 27. Pregnancy and Childbirth: From Discovering Pregnancy to Hospital Discharge and Paperwork

The first half of this section counts the risk of death for pregnant women and fetuses. The second half counts money and legwork time. The two ledgers are not converted into each other. The content only addresses "what to do when." Things like nutrition during pregnancy and prenatal education, which lack hard evidence or have very small effects, are not covered here. For maternity allowance, maternity leave, and the cost of raising a child, see Section 18; for how to care for a child after birth, see Section 20; for medical emergencies, see Section 13.

The numbers from the World Health Organization: In 2023, approximately 260,000 women worldwide died from pregnancy and childbirth. About 75% of them died from these five categories of causes: severe postpartum bleeding, infection, hypertensive disorders of pregnancy (a class of diseases where blood pressure rises after pregnancy, including preeclampsia and eclampsia), complications during delivery, and unsafe induced abortion. The World Health Organization also states, "Most maternal deaths are preventable, because the medical measures to prevent and manage complications are known." The most effective entries in this section are the ones that intercept these problems in advance.

### 1. Start supplementing 0.4 mg of folic acid daily when preparing to conceive, and continue through the first 3 months of pregnancy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A few dozen yuan per bottle. Rural women do not need to pay — the state's free folic acid supplementation program provides it. One pill a day; the hard part is remembering every day.
- Plain-language summary: Taking one folic acid pill a day before and during pregnancy reduces the probability of neural tube defects in the child by about 70%. Neural tube defects are birth defects such as anencephaly and spina bifida. For those who have already had one such child, the probability of having another with the same problem drops to about one-third of the original.
- Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review pooled 5 trials with 6,708 deliveries, and rated the evidence quality as high. Supplementing folic acid daily before and during pregnancy gives a neural tube defect risk to the fetus of RR 0.31 (95% CI 0.17 to 0.58, confidence interval), about a 70% reduction. For those who have already had a child with a neural tube defect, the risk of having another with the problem is RR 0.34 (95% CI 0.18 to 0.64), dropping to about one-third of the original. By population subgroup, 0.4 mg and higher doses show no difference in effect. Taking folic acid alone and taking multivitamins show no difference in effect either.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: De-Regil LM, Peña-Rosas JP, Fernández-Gaxiola AC, Rayco-Solon P (2015). Effects and safety of periconceptional oral folate supplementation for preventing birth defects. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (12), CD007950. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007950.pub3>
- Note: The key is "starting before pregnancy." The fetal neural tube closes within 28 days after conception. By the time you test positive for pregnancy, most of this window has already passed. So if you are planning to have a child, start taking it 3 months in advance. In the same pooled review, folic acid had no clear effect on cleft lip and palate, congenital heart disease, or miscarriage — do not count on it to treat anything else.

### 2. Register the Maternal and Child Health Handbook at the community health service center before 13 weeks of pregnancy, and use up the free prenatal checkup quota
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge — this is part of the National Basic Public Health Services program. You will need to make several trips according to gestational week.
- Plain-language summary: At least five prenatal checkups during pregnancy, plus postpartum home visits and a 42-day postpartum checkup, are paid for by the state. The way to get them is to register a Maternal and Child Health Handbook at the community before 13 weeks of pregnancy. If you do not register, you miss these free visits for nothing.
- Benefit: The National Basic Public Health Services Specification (Third Edition) includes maternal health management services. Resident pregnant women within the jurisdiction can receive these services: 1 health checkup in early pregnancy, 2 in mid-pregnancy, 2 in late pregnancy, 1 postpartum visit, and 1 postpartum 42-day health checkup. Registration must be done before 13 weeks of pregnancy, and the first prenatal checkup is done at the same time. The two mid-pregnancy visits fall in weeks 16 to 20 and weeks 21 to 24. The two late-pregnancy visits fall in weeks 28 to 36 and weeks 37 to 40. The locally published service list also includes a full first-visit lab panel and a postpartum depression screening.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: National Health and Family Planning Commission (2017). National Basic Public Health Services Specification (Third Edition). <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2017/04/20170417104506514.pdf>; Jingjiang Municipal People's Government. National Basic Public Health Services Program — Maternal Health Management Services (locally published service list). <https://www.jingjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zdlyxxgk/wsjk/art/2023/art_383017a2e7794892bc772bfbe042f379.html>
- Note: These 5 visits are the free minimum, not the entirety of prenatal care. Hospitals will add ultrasound, blood glucose, and fetal heart monitoring as needed, and these you pay for yourself. The earlier you register, the earlier your pregnancy risk grading is done. Pregnancy risk grading classifies pregnant women by risk level; only those identified as high-risk can be transferred early to a hospital that can handle them. If you live in a different district, register where you currently live — no need to return to your registered household location (hukou).

### 3. Get tested for HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B at the first prenatal checkup; if positive, there is free blocking treatment
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge — the prevention of mother-to-child transmission program provides free testing.
- Plain-language summary: Get tested for HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B at the first prenatal checkup; you do not pay for these tests. A positive result is not a dead end. The state provides free medication and protocols to block the route by which the disease passes from mother to child — this is called prevention of mother-to-child transmission.
- Benefit: Mother-to-child transmission is when a disease passes from mother to child. The Specification for Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission of HIV, Syphilis, and Hepatitis B (2020 Edition) sets the requirements. During early pregnancy or at the first prenatal checkup, the hospital must inform the patient of relevant information and "provide appropriate, standardized free testing" — these three tests cost you nothing. Based on the results, post-test counseling or referral is then provided. A positive result is not a dead end. Article 44 of the Regulations on AIDS Prevention and Treatment requires that pregnant women infected with HIV be provided with "free treatment and counseling for the prevention of mother-to-child transmission of AIDS." Article 43 requires the provision of blocking treatment (preventing the disease from passing to the child), treatment, postpartum visits, infant follow-up and testing, and other services. For mothers who are positive for hepatitis B surface antigen, the newborn should receive both the hepatitis B vaccine and hepatitis B immunoglobulin at birth (see Section 20, Entry 2).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: General Office of the National Health Commission (2020). Specification for Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission of HIV, Syphilis, and Hepatitis B (2020 Edition). <https://wjw.xinjiang.gov.cn/hfpc/zhgl6/202012/08b7de496f0b40e586d4b99d00cf79c4.shtml> (reproduced by the Health Commission of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region); State Council (2006). Regulations on AIDS Prevention and Treatment (Articles 43, 44). <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/zwgk_20040/zcwj2022/flfg/202304/t20230408_2992986.html> (reproduced by the Beijing Municipal Health Commission)
- Note: The earlier these three are tested, the more useful, because blocking and treatment both take time. Article 3 of the Regulations on AIDS Prevention and Treatment also states explicitly that "no unit or individual may discriminate against people living with HIV, AIDS patients, and their families," and that legitimate rights such as marriage, employment, medical care, and education are protected by law. For routine prevention and testing, see Section 1. For post-exposure prophylaxis after high-risk behavior, see Section 13.

### 4. Not a single cigarette or sip of alcohol during the entire pregnancy; household members should not smoke indoors either
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge — you also save money on tobacco and alcohol. The hard part is getting those who live with you to quit too.
- Plain-language summary: Smoking during pregnancy raises the probability of premature birth, low birth weight, and cleft lip and palate in the child. The risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and delivery doubles. The risk of sudden infant death is also higher. Family members smoking indoors counts the same. It is never too late to quit.
- Benefit: Per the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention: Smoking during pregnancy can leave the child "born small even if born at term" and "may cause premature birth." It "doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and delivery." It raises the risk of birth defects such as cleft lip and palate. It can also "harm the developing lungs and brain of the fetus, with harm that can persist into childhood." Infants born to mothers who smoked during pregnancy, and infants exposed to smoke after birth, have a higher risk of sudden infant death syndrome. The official recommendation is that "it is best to quit before pregnancy, but if you are already pregnant, quitting still works" and "it is never too late to quit."
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Smoking During Pregnancy. <https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/diseases/pregnancy.html>
- Note: When others smoke indoors, the secondhand smoke the pregnant woman breathes counts the same for the fetus. So household members should not smoke indoors either. The same for alcohol: no amount of drinking is known to be safe, and the simplest approach during pregnancy is not a single sip. For the ledger of tobacco and alcohol for adults themselves, see Section 2.

### 5. For those at high risk of preeclampsia, start one low-dose aspirin tablet daily after 12 weeks of pregnancy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A few dozen yuan. One tablet a day, continued until delivery. The hard part is not forgetting.
- Plain-language summary: Preeclampsia is a hypertensive disorder of pregnancy. Those considered high-risk include anyone who had it in a previous pregnancy, those carrying multiples, those with chronic hypertension, those with pre-pregnancy diabetes, and those with kidney disease or autoimmune disease. For high-risk individuals, starting one low-dose aspirin tablet daily from 12 weeks of pregnancy reduces preeclampsia by about 15%, premature birth and perinatal death by about 20%. Others should not take it on their own.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force gives a Grade B recommendation: for those at high risk of preeclampsia, start low-dose aspirin (81 mg/day) after 12 weeks of pregnancy for prevention. Preeclampsia is a hypertensive disorder of pregnancy. Pooling multiple studies, the results are as follows; 95% CI is the confidence interval. Preeclampsia RR 0.85 (95% CI 0.75 to 0.95, 16 studies), about 15% lower. Premature birth RR 0.80 (95% CI 0.67 to 0.95, 13 studies), about 20% lower. Perinatal mortality RR 0.79 (95% CI 0.66 to 0.96, 11 studies), about 21% lower; perinatal mortality is death during the period around delivery. Small for gestational age / intrauterine growth restriction RR 0.82 (95% CI 0.68 to 0.99, 16 studies), about 18% lower; this refers to the fetus growing smaller than expected for gestational age.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Aspirin Use to Prevent Preeclampsia and Related Morbidity and Mortality: Preventive Medication. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/low-dose-aspirin-use-for-the-prevention-of-morbidity-and-mortality-from-preeclampsia-preventive-medication>
- Note: Those considered high-risk include: previous preeclampsia, twin or multiple pregnancy, chronic hypertension. Also those with type 1 or type 2 diabetes before pregnancy, kidney disease, and autoimmune diseases such as systemic lupus erythematosus and antiphospholipid syndrome. Those not in these categories should not take it on their own. This medication requires a doctor's prescription; explain your medical history clearly during prenatal checkups and let the doctor decide. Most domestically sold versions are 100 mg enteric-coated tablets; take as prescribed.

### 6. Get screened for gestational diabetes at 24 weeks of pregnancy or later; do not skip the sugar-water test because it is a hassle
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A few dozen to over a hundred yuan. You must go in fasting, get blood drawn several times, and it takes up half a morning.
- Plain-language summary: Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that appears only after pregnancy. Half a morning after 24 weeks — drinking sugar water once and getting several blood draws — can detect it. If undetected and unmanaged, the risks of macrosomia (overly large fetus), difficult labor, a C-section cut, preeclampsia, and neonatal hypoglycemia all rise.
- Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force's Grade B recommendation: screen asymptomatic pregnant women (those who feel no discomfort) for gestational diabetes at 24 weeks of pregnancy or later. Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that appears only after pregnancy. For screening done only once, that one time should be at 24 weeks or later. Whether screening before 24 weeks is beneficial — the Task Force's conclusion is that existing evidence is insufficient to judge the benefits and harms (Grade I). If gestational diabetes is left unmanaged, it raises the risk of: macrosomia (overly large fetus), difficult labor, cesarean delivery, preeclampsia, and neonatal hypoglycemia.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Gestational Diabetes: Screening. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/gestational-diabetes-screening>
- Note: Among those diagnosed, most can control their blood sugar through diet and exercise adjustments, and only a minority need insulin. Do not skip this test for fear of needing injections. Blood sugar must be rechecked after delivery: those who have had gestational diabetes have a significantly higher future risk of type 2 diabetes.

### 7. Memorize this "go to the hospital immediately" list; it applies throughout pregnancy and for one year postpartum
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Spend five minutes memorizing it; ideally, those who live with you should also know it.
- Plain-language summary: If any one appears, go to the hospital that day — do not wait a night at home. Headache that does not improve or gets worse, vision changes, fever of 38 °C. Severe swelling of hands or face, shortness of breath, chest pain or fast heartbeat. Severe abdominal pain that does not ease, fetal movement stops or markedly decreases, vaginal bleeding or fluid leakage. One arm or leg swollen and painful, thoughts of harming yourself or the baby. Applies within one year postpartum too.
- Benefit: The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention lists these urgent maternal warning signs: headache that does not improve or gets worse, dizziness or fainting, vision changes, temperature of 38 °C or higher; severe swelling of hands or face, thoughts of harming yourself or the baby, difficulty breathing, chest pain or fast heartbeat; severe nausea and vomiting, severe abdominal pain that does not ease, fetal movement stops or markedly decreases during pregnancy; vaginal bleeding or fluid leakage during pregnancy, postpartum vaginal bleeding or abnormal discharge; severe swelling, redness, or pain in one arm or leg. These problems can all occur within one year postpartum — it is not "once the first month is over, you are fine."
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Hear Her — Urgent Maternal Warning Signs. <https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html>; World Health Organization. Maternal mortality fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/maternal-mortality>
- Note: The use of this list is to separate "tough it out" from "must go now." Headache plus vision changes plus upper abdominal pain points to preeclampsia (a hypertensive disorder of pregnancy). A swollen and painful calf points to deep vein thrombosis (see Section 13). Per the World Health Organization, postpartum hemorrhage can take the life of a healthy woman within hours. If any one appears, go to the hospital directly — do not wait a night at home first.

### 8. If your water breaks, lie flat where you are, elevate your hips, and call 120; do not walk around or shower
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge.
- Plain-language summary: When water breaks, it is not "slowly gather your things and head out." The correct approach is to lie down where you are, prop up your buttocks, and call 120. Standing and walking makes the amniotic fluid flow faster, and the umbilical cord may be washed down with it. That is an emergency that can cost the baby's life.
- Benefit: The Beijing Municipal Health Commission's instructions: After discovering water breaking, "find a place to lie flat right away, to prevent amniotic fluid from flowing out," "do not walk around casually," and "immediately call 120 for emergency transport." While waiting, notify family to bring things and meet at the hospital. Medical institutions' patient education further explains why: when standing, amniotic fluid is lost faster, and the fetal umbilical cord may prolapse — called umbilical cord prolapse — which is a fetal emergency. So lie down, prop up the hips; a left lateral position can be used.
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Beijing Municipal Health Commission. Weekly First Aid Topic: What to Do When a Mother-to-Be's Water Breaks Early? <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/bmfw_20143/jkzs/jzjj/202001/t20200115_1575632.html>; Beijing Shunyi District Maternal and Child Health Hospital. What to Do if Your Water Breaks at Home in Late Pregnancy? <https://www.bch-syfy.cn/Html/News/Articles/9453.html>
- Note: The three signs of imminent delivery are regular contractions, bloody show, and water breaking. The first two allow for unhurried packing; water breaking is the only one that requires "lie down where you are and call for transport." Showering, using the toilet, or driving yourself after water breaks are all classic mistakes. Note the time of water breaking and the color of the amniotic fluid, and tell the doctor. Yellow-green amniotic fluid, or fluid containing meconium particles, suggests fetal distress (the fetus is hypoxic in the uterus). If accompanied by regular contractions, markedly decreased fetal movement, or fever with chills, emphasize to the doctor that this is an emergency.

### 9. If you want labor analgesia, just ask for it; it does not increase the risk of cesarean delivery
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: Several hundred to over a thousand yuan, varying by region. Ask the hospital and the anesthesiology department in advance whether they can do it. The hard part is speaking up to ask.
- Plain-language summary: Labor analgesia is an injection of anesthetic in the back that suppresses the pain of childbirth. It works better than other pain relief methods, and does not make you more likely to need a C-section. The claim that "epidural causes a lifetime of back pain" is also unsupported. It now has its own billing item, and hospitals are more willing to perform it.
- Benefit: The Cochrane systematic review concludes that epidural analgesia is more effective than non-epidural methods at reducing labor pain and improving maternal satisfaction with pain relief. Labor analgesia uses epidural analgesia. The same review also states, "Epidural analgesia has no effect on the risk of cesarean delivery and long-term back pain." The previously observed increase in instrumental delivery (using instruments to deliver the baby) is no longer seen in studies after 2005. Starting in 2024, the National Healthcare Security Administration's service price project guidelines for obstetrics established separate billing items for "labor analgesia," "doula delivery," and "family-attended labor." The rationale was precisely that without an independent price item, hospitals' willingness to provide labor analgesia had been suppressed.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Anim-Somuah M, Smyth RMD, Cyna AM, Cuthbert A (2018). Epidural versus non-epidural or no analgesia for pain management in labour. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (5), CD000331. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000331.pub4>; National Healthcare Security Administration (2024). Restructuring Obstetric Service Price Items to Help Build a Family-Friendly Society. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202406/content_6957553.htm>
- Note: The two most widespread claims — "epidural causes a lifetime of back pain" and "epidural causes difficult labor requiring a C-section" — are both unsupported. Cochrane pooled epidural analgesia with other analgesia methods and concluded it has no effect on cesarean risk or long-term back pain. The real side effects are: low blood pressure, fever, leg weakness, inability to urinate, as well as prolonged first and second stages of labor and greater likelihood of needing oxytocin. The decision is yours, but do not forgo it based on rumors. Whether it is available also depends on whether the hospital has an anesthesiologist on site 24 hours; ask during prenatal checkups.

### 10. Do not request a cesarean delivery without medical indication, and do not schedule surgery to pick a date
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. The hard part is withstanding pressure from family.
- Plain-language summary: The World Health Organization's position is that once the cesarean rate in a region exceeds 10%, maternal and neonatal deaths no longer continue to decline. When a C-section is needed, it must be done. But choosing a C-section to pick a date or to avoid pain is just undergoing a major abdominal surgery for nothing.
- Benefit: The World Health Organization's 2015 statement: When the cesarean rate in a population rises to about 10%, maternal and neonatal deaths decline. Beyond 10%, there is no evidence that mortality improves further. The WHO also stresses that every effort should be made to provide cesarean delivery to mothers who need it, rather than chasing a particular rate.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: World Health Organization (2015). WHO Statement on Caesarean Section Rates (WHO/RHR/15.02). <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-RHR-15.02>
- Note: When medically necessary, a C-section is a life-saving surgery; do it when needed, do not push through. What to avoid is surgery that is not medically needed — for example, to avoid pain, pick an auspicious time, or time the child's school age. A C-section is a major abdominal surgery with slower recovery, and a higher risk of placenta previa, placenta accreta, and uterine rupture in the next pregnancy. This directly affects whether you will want to have a second child.

### 11. Confirm your maternity insurance enrollment status before getting pregnant; an unemployed spouse can also claim maternity medical expenses
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Make one phone call to the social security or medical insurance consultation line.
- Plain-language summary: When an employer has paid maternity insurance, the medical costs of childbirth and the allowance during maternity leave are paid from this fund. For an unemployed spouse, the medical costs portion can also be reimbursed. Whether your enrollment months are sufficient, and whether you need to file for out-of-area delivery in advance — these must be asked beforehand; they cannot be made up afterward.
- Benefit: Article 54 of the Social Insurance Law: Where an employer has paid maternity insurance premiums, its employees are entitled to maternity insurance benefits. An employee's spouse who has no work is entitled to maternity medical expense benefits in accordance with state regulations, with the funds paid from the maternity insurance fund. In plain terms, when the employer has paid maternity insurance, the employee is entitled to benefits, and the medical costs of childbirth for the employee's spouse who has no work can also be paid from this fund. Maternity insurance benefits come in two parts: one is maternity medical expenses, the medical costs of childbirth itself; the other is the maternity allowance, the money paid to you during maternity leave. Article 56 lists the three circumstances under which a maternity allowance can be claimed: a female employee takes maternity leave, takes leave for a family planning procedure, or other circumstances provided by laws and regulations. The maternity allowance is "calculated based on the average monthly wage of employees of the employer in the previous year" — this refers to your employer's average employee monthly wage last year, not your own wage.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2010). Social Insurance Law of the People's Republic of China (Articles 54, 56). <https://guangdong.chinatax.gov.cn/gdsw/qysw_gkwj/2020-02/12/content_8e8c485d0db34b989531f3ba57cec5a9.shtml> (reproduced by the Guangdong Provincial Tax Service of the State Taxation Administration)
- Note: Two things must be asked in advance, because they cannot be made up afterward. First, whether the consecutive enrollment months are sufficient: every region sets a minimum, and if insufficient, you are not eligible. Second, whether out-of-area delivery requires prior filing: without it, you may have to pay in full yourself and seek reimbursement later, or even be unable to claim. For how maternity leave days and the maternity allowance are calculated, see Section 18, Entry 2.

### 12. Process the Birth Medical Certificate before hospital discharge; decide the name in advance and do not misspell it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Done during hospital discharge paperwork.
- Plain-language summary: This certificate is a prerequisite for household registration (hukou), insurance enrollment, and school enrollment, and only the delivering institution can issue it the first time. If you leave the hospital and try to apply afterward, you must go through a different process and submit more documents. For the characters in the name, confirm in advance that the household registration system can type them.
- Benefit: Article 23 of the Maternal and Infant Health Care Law: Medical and health care institutions and personnel who attend home births shall, in accordance with the regulations of the health administrative department under the State Council, issue the uniformly formatted neonatal birth medical certificate. This certificate is a prerequisite for everything that follows: household registration, insurance enrollment, getting a medical insurance card, kindergarten and school enrollment all require it. And the first issuance can only be done by the delivering institution. Applying for replacement after leaving the hospital requires a different process and additional supporting documents.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (1994, revised 2017). Maternal and Infant Health Care Law of the People's Republic of China (Article 23). <https://www.gov.cn/guoqing/2021-10/29/content_5647619.htm>
- Note: Before filling it out, verify the parents' names, ID numbers, and the child's name character by character. The characters in the name must be typeable in the household registration system; rare characters will cause difficulties later. Changing a name or the certificate requires a reissuance or replacement process — far more troublesome than checking on the spot. Once you have the certificate, store it separately and as carefully as your household register and ID card.

### 13. Do not refuse the newborn heel-stick blood screening or hearing screening
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free in most regions, or a few dozen yuan. Some extended items are at your own expense. One heel stick after birth.
- Plain-language summary: One heel-stick blood draw and one hearing test can detect hypothyroidism, phenylketonuria, and hearing impairment while the child appears completely normal. By the time symptoms appear, the intellectual damage is irreversible.
- Benefit: Article 3 of the Management Measures for Neonatal Disease Screening: "Neonatal disease screening nationwide includes congenital hypothyroidism, phenylketonuria, and other neonatal inherited metabolic diseases, as well as hearing impairment." These two categories share a common feature: they show no abnormalities early on, and by the time symptoms are obvious, intellectual damage is already irreversible. Early detection and early intervention work very well — methods include thyroid hormone supplementation, special formula feeding, hearing aids, or cochlear implants. Article 10 requires that institutions promptly notify guardians of positive screening results for confirmation.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ministry of Health (2009). Management Measures for Neonatal Disease Screening (Ministry of Health Order No. 64, Articles 3, 10, 11). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2009/content_1371363.htm>
- Note: Article 11 requires that before screening, the project, conditions, methods, sensitivity (how much it can detect), and cost be truthfully explained to the guardian, with signed consent. So you will receive an informed consent form. Before signing, read it carefully: which items are the state-mandated diseases, and which are self-paid additions. A positive initial screen does not equal a diagnosis; the vast majority are ruled out on retest — do not collapse at this step. If the hearing initial screen is not passed, attend the rescreening as notified; do not judge for yourself that "the child can hear, so let it be."

### 14. Once you have the Birth Medical Certificate, enroll the child in resident medical insurance — do not wait for household registration to be completed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Annual resident medical insurance premium of a few hundred yuan.
- Plain-language summary: Once you have the birth certificate, you can enroll the child in insurance online — no need to wait for household registration. Once enrolled, the inpatient costs at discharge can be reimbursed on the spot. Newborn inpatient costs are not low, and the difference between being insured and not insured is large.
- Benefit: The Chinese government's position in 2024: "Newborns can enroll in insurance online using the birth medical certificate," without needing household registration first. "After discharge, newborns can have their medical expenses directly reimbursed." Previously, enrollment required household registration, and inpatient costs had to be paid in full up front and reimbursed afterward. The average processing time for enrollment has been reduced from 28.7 working days at the start of the year to 6.4 working days.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Chinese Government Network (2024). State Council Policy Briefing on Improving the Long-Term Mechanism for Basic Medical Insurance Enrollment. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202409/content_6973567.htm>
- Note: Prematurity, phototherapy for jaundice, neonatal pneumonia — these are common reasons for newborn hospitalization, often running into the tens of thousands of yuan, and the difference between being insured and not insured is large. Regulations on "how many days after birth you can enroll, with coverage retroactive to the date of birth" differ by region. How it is actually executed is determined by the local medical insurance authority. Calling 12393 before discharge is the simplest approach.

### 15. Within one month after the child's birth, file the birth registration at the police station
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. One trip to the household registration window at the police station.
- Plain-language summary: The law allows one month, not "whenever you have time." Household registration gates vaccination, kindergarten enrollment, school enrollment, and obtaining an ID card. The longer you delay, the more documents you will need to make up.
- Benefit: Article 7 of the Household Registration Regulations: "Within one month after the birth of a baby, the household head, a relative, the foster parent, or a neighbor shall file a birth registration with the household registration authority at the infant's usual place of residence." This is the legally set time limit, not a suggestion. Household registration is a prerequisite for the following: vaccination, kindergarten, school enrollment, getting an ID card. The longer it is delayed, the more documents need to be supplemented.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (1958). Household Registration Regulations of the People's Republic of China (Article 7). <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/post_2531969.html> (reproduced by the People's Government of Guangdong Province)
- Note: Bring all of these: birth medical certificate, both parents' ID cards and household registers, marriage certificate. Whether the child registers under the father or the mother is your choice, but the two locations have different policies, and both school district and insurance enrollment are affected. Decide before going — changing it is troublesome. Children born to unmarried parents can also be registered.

### 16. Do not skip the 42-day postpartum checkup; it is also a postpartum depression screening
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge, part of basic public health services. Takes up half a day. The hard part is being willing to move just after the first month postpartum.
- Plain-language summary: This checkup is free, includes a complete blood count and routine vaginal discharge test, and also includes a postpartum depression screening. Serious complications can occur up to one year postpartum — do not assume you are safe once the first month is over.
- Benefit: The National Basic Public Health Services Specification (Third Edition)'s maternal health management includes 1 postpartum visit and 1 postpartum 42-day health checkup. The locally published service list specifies that this visit includes laboratory tests (complete blood count, routine vaginal discharge) and 1 postpartum depression screening. Serious complications can occur within one year postpartum. In the "go to the hospital immediately" list in Entry 7, "thoughts of harming yourself or the baby," "postpartum vaginal bleeding or abnormal discharge," and "severe swelling or pain in one limb" all fall in the postpartum period.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: National Health and Family Planning Commission (2017). National Basic Public Health Services Specification (Third Edition). <https://www.nhc.gov.cn/ewebeditor/uploadfile/2017/04/20170417104506514.pdf>; Jingjiang Municipal People's Government. National Basic Public Health Services Program — Maternal Health Management Services (locally published service list). <https://www.jingjiang.gov.cn/xxgk/zdlyxxgk/wsjk/art/2023/art_383017a2e7794892bc772bfbe042f379.html>
- Note: Postpartum depression is not weakness of character, and a family member's "look on the bright side" will not solve it. It has specific screening questionnaires, treatments, and recovery is possible. For what to do when suicidal thoughts appear, see Section 1. For the legal pathway when you genuinely cannot raise the child after birth, see Section 9, Entry 20. This checkup will also examine how the wound is healing, how the uterus is recovering, and pelvic floor function. The contraception plan is also discussed at this visit: no menstruation after delivery does not mean you cannot get pregnant.
