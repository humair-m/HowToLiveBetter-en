# 20. How to Care for a Newborn

Metric: Infant mortality and money. This section only includes matters that bear on whether the child can survive, or how much you will spend, and only where the difference is large enough and the evidence is hard. Parenting details such as how to soothe and how to feed are not in this book. What to do before birth and on the day of discharge, see Section 27.

### 1. Have the baby sleep on its back, on a firm flat surface, in the same room but not in the same bed, with nothing soft in the bed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The mattress must be changed to a firm one, a few hundred yuan. The hard part is resisting the elders' advice and keeping to this
- Plain-language summary: Have the baby lie on its back to sleep, on a firm flat surface. Adults and baby sleep in the same room but not in the same bed. No pillow, quilt, crib bumper, plush toy, or shaping pillow in the bed. Tilted rockers and infant seats are for sitting, not for sleeping. In the United States alone, about 3,500 babies die each year in sleep
- Benefit: About 3,500 infants die each year in the United States from sleep-related causes. This includes sudden infant death syndrome, deaths of undetermined cause, and accidental suffocation in bed. The American Academy of Pediatrics' safe sleep environment is "back position; firm, non-inclined sleep surface; same room but not same bed; avoid soft bedding and overheating." Several other things also reduce risk: breastfeeding, keeping the baby away from nicotine and alcohol, vaccinating on schedule, and using a pacifier
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Moon RY, Carlin RF, Hand I, Task Force on Sudden Infant Death Syndrome and the Committee on Fetus and Newborn (2022). Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment. Pediatrics, 150(1), e2022057990. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-057990>
- Note: "Nothing soft in the bed" means no pillow, no quilt, no crib bumper, no plush toy, no shaping pillow. Even a nursing pillow that looks safe is not used. Tilted rockers and infant seats are not for sleeping. In this section, this matter has the largest effect size and is the one where you most need to withstand pressure from elders

### 2. Within 24 hours of birth, get the first dose of hepatitis B vaccine
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The hepatitis B vaccine is part of the immunization program and is free to begin with. The baby is given the shot at the hospital on the day of birth; no separate trip is needed
- Plain-language summary: The first dose of hepatitis B vaccine should be given within 24 hours of birth. Of those infected with hepatitis B in infancy and early childhood, about 95% develop chronic hepatitis. Those infected as adults mostly clear it on their own. So the earlier this shot, the more useful. If the mother is hepatitis B surface antigen positive, the baby must also receive hepatitis B immunoglobulin at the same time
- Benefit: The World Health Organization states: "All infants should receive the hepatitis B vaccine as soon as possible after birth (within 24 hours)." The reason is "about 95% of infections in infancy and early childhood progress to chronic hepatitis." Most adult infections clear on their own (resolve without treatment)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: World Health Organization. Hepatitis B fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hepatitis-b>
- Note: If the mother is hepatitis B surface antigen positive, the baby must also receive a dose of hepatitis B immunoglobulin after birth. This should be checked during prenatal screening — do not wait until after birth to ask. The mother's own screening is in Section 1

### 3. Complete all vaccines in the national immunization program; the whole series is free, and missed doses are only made up, not restarted
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The law states "vaccination units may not charge any fee for immunization program vaccines." Several trips to the vaccination clinic are required by age month. Keep the vaccination certificate safe; do not lose it
- Plain-language summary: Vaccines in the national immunization program are free throughout; the vaccination unit may not charge. Childcare institutions and schools check vaccination certificates at enrollment; missed doses are caught up. If missed, only the unfinished doses are made up — you do not restart the series, and switching manufacturers does not block continuation. Eczema, hives, allergic rhinitis, and asthma, as long as the condition is stable, and likewise neonatal jaundice, can all be vaccinated normally
- Benefit: At childcare enrollment and school enrollment, childcare institutions and schools must verify the vaccination certificate. If a child is found not to have received immunization program vaccines as required, the institution must report to the vaccination unit and cooperate in urging the guardian to make up the missed doses. So missed vaccines cannot be postponed; they will be caught at enrollment. The version now in effect is the 2026 schedule. Human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine has been incorporated into the national immunization program. The starting age for the DTaP vaccine has been moved from 3 months to 2 months, with 5 doses total, at 2 months, 4 months, 6 months, 18 months, and 6 years. Eligible children in Tibet, Qinghai, Xinjiang, and the Xinjiang Production and Construction Corps also routinely receive Japanese encephalitis vaccine starting March 2026. Several vaccines can be given on the same day: all immunization program vaccines can currently be co-administered according to the schedule or make-up principles. Two or more injectable vaccines must be given at different sites, "strictly prohibited from mixing two or more vaccines into the same syringe for administration." If not given on the same day, two or more injectable live-attenuated vaccines must be separated by at least 28 days. Inactivated vaccines, oral live-attenuated vaccines, and other vaccines have no required interval. The make-up principle for those under 18 is to make up as early as possible — "only the unfinished doses need to be made up; there is no need to restart the full series," and a switch of manufacturer for the same vaccine can be continued (nationwide, 2026 schedule)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2019). Vaccine Administration Law (Articles 48 and 49). <http://ynswsjkw.yn.gov.cn/html/2019/faguiguizhangxin_0731/6509.html> (reproduced by the Yunnan Provincial Health Commission); National Disease Control Bureau and National Health Commission (2026). Interpretive Q&A on the National Immunization Program Vaccine Schedule for Children and Explanatory Notes (2026 Edition). <https://www.ndcpa.gov.cn/jbkzzx/c100013/common/content/content_2073002930292035584.html>
- Note: The most common misunderstanding is "the child has a weak constitution; hold off on vaccination." The official statement: so-called "allergic constitution," family history of allergic disease, prior food or drug allergy in the child, are not reasons to withhold vaccination. Atopic dermatitis (eczema), hives, allergic rhinitis, and bronchial asthma can all be vaccinated as long as the condition is stable. Taking antiallergy medication or using inhaled corticosteroids does not prevent vaccination. Physiological jaundice and breast-milk jaundice in newborns can also be vaccinated normally. The only two true contraindications are prior acute severe allergic reaction to the same vaccine, and severe allergy to a known component of the vaccine. Children with immune deficiency, and children undergoing chemotherapy, radiation therapy, or immunosuppressant therapy, must be evaluated individually — go to the vaccination clinic, explain the situation, and decide. For the first dose of hepatitis B, see Entry 2. Self-paid non-immunization-program vaccines incur separate vaccine and service fees; they are unrelated to the free vaccines discussed here

### 4. For the first 6 months, breastfeed only — not even water is needed; from 6 months, add complementary foods and continue breastfeeding
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money; it also saves on formula. What is spent is time; feeding takes a chunk out of every day. The hard part is keeping it up without stopping for the first 6 months
- Plain-language summary: Start breastfeeding within the first hour after birth. For the first 6 months, breastfeed only — even water is not needed. From 6 months, add complementary foods, with iron-rich animal-source foods daily; continue breastfeeding to age 2 or beyond. If breast milk is insufficient or cannot be given, use formula — do not feel guilty. What you feed matters far less for the child's safety than sleep position
- Benefit: The World Health Organization recommends "initiating breastfeeding within the first hour after birth." For the first 6 months, "exclusive breastfeeding — that is, providing no other food or liquid, including water." Then "from 6 months of age, begin adding safe and sufficient complementary foods while continuing breastfeeding to 2 years or beyond." The National Health Commission's service guideline also promotes exclusive breastfeeding for ages 0 to 6 months. This guideline also requires that, starting at 6 months, "the daily complementary food must include iron-rich animal-source foods"
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: World Health Organization. Breastfeeding. <https://www.who.int/health-topics/breastfeeding>; General Office of the National Health Commission (2024). Service Guideline for Nutrition and Feeding Assessment of Infants and Toddlers (Trial) (Guo Wei Ban You You Han [2024] No. 452). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202502/content_7002872.htm>
- Note: If breast milk is insufficient, or the body does not permit breastfeeding, use formula. The gap between breastfeeding and formula is far smaller than the safe-sleep gap. Frequency of complementary feeding: 1 to 2 times per day at 6 to 8 months, 2 to 3 times per day at 9 to 12 months. Daily intake should cover at least four of seven food groups. For children with severe eczema or egg allergy, whether to avoid peanuts, see Entry 12 in this section (do not avoid peanuts)

### 5. Mix formula with water at 70°C or above; let it cool before feeding; discard any leftover
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Wait a few extra minutes each time for the milk to cool. The hard part is waiting for the water temperature to drop before mixing, even when the baby is crying from hunger
- Plain-language summary: Formula is not sterile and may carry a bacterium called Cronobacter sakazakii. Once a newborn is infected, reported case fatality is between 40% and 80%. When mixing, first pour hot water into the bottle, wait for the water to cool to about 73.8°C, then add the formula. At this point the milk is still above 70°C, which can reduce this bacterium to less than one hundred-thousandth of its original count. Cool after mixing, then feed; discard any leftover
- Benefit: Formula is not sterile and may carry Cronobacter sakazakii. Reported case fatality after neonatal infection is 40% to 80%. Experimental studies show that first pouring hot water into the bottle, waiting for the water to cool to about 73.8°C, then adding formula keeps the milk temperature above 70°C in the vast majority of cases. This can reduce the bacterium by more than 5 log units, to less than one hundred-thousandth of the original count
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Beary MA, Daly SE, Baker J, Snyder AB (2025). Assessing Hot Water Reconstitution Instructions and Labeling of Powdered Infant Formula to Ensure Cronobacter spp. Reduction. Journal of Food Protection, 88(9), 100571. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfp.2025.100571>; Sima WG, Legesse T, Girma S, et al. (2025). Emerging microbial risks: Cronobacter sakazakii in powdered infant formula for infants under six months of age in Ethiopia. BMC Microbiology, 26, 307. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s12866-025-04380-y>; World Health Organization & FAO (2007). Safe preparation, storage and handling of powdered infant formula: guidelines. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241595414>
- Note: The 70°C threshold comes from public health guidelines. Beary et al. write that "public health guidelines for caregivers of high-risk infants recommend using water heated to at least 70 °C (158 °F) to reconstitute powdered infant formula in order to inactivate microorganisms." Premature infants, low-birth-weight infants, and infants under 2 months are at the highest risk. Do not use water that has sat in a thermos all day. Do not leave prepared milk at room temperature to wait for the next feeding

### 6. Do not feed honey to infants under 1 year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money
- Plain-language summary: Do not feed honey to a child under 1 year. Honey water, honey mixed into complementary food, and pastries containing honey all count. Honey may contain bacteria that cause botulism. The adult gut flora can suppress it; the infant's cannot. The same applies to home-pickled vegetables and fermented foods
- Benefit: The US CDC states "do not feed honey to children under 1 year" because "honey may contain bacteria that cause botulism." The adult gut flora can suppress these spores (the dormant form of this bacterium); the infant's cannot
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: US CDC. Preventing Botulism. <https://www.cdc.gov/botulism/prevention/index.html>
- Note: What must not be fed includes honey water, complementary foods mixed with honey, and pastries containing honey. By the same logic, home-pickled vegetables and fermented foods should also not be given to infants

### 7. The vitamin K shot at birth must be given
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: 0 yuan to tens of yuan. This cost is usually already included in the hospitalization fee for childbirth and need not be paid separately
- Plain-language summary: Newborns have very little stored vitamin K. Without supplementation, bleeding may occur; the late-onset form often presents as intracranial bleeding. In places that do not routinely give this shot, 478 of every 100,000 newborns had this bleeding. With this shot, the incidence is estimated to drop by more than 78.5%. Regular hospitals in China routinely give it; for home births or births at unregulated facilities, ask actively whether it was given
- Benefit: Newborns have very little stored vitamin K. Without supplementation, vitamin K deficiency bleeding may occur; the late-onset form often presents as intracranial bleeding. Belgian consensus recommends "intramuscular injection of 1 or 2 mg of vitamin K once at birth." In areas without systematic prophylaxis, the incidence of vitamin K deficiency bleeding is 478 per 100,000 — this figure comes from 42,000 newborns in Tashkent. The authors estimate that prophylaxis can reduce the incidence by "more than 78.5%"
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Fiesack S, Smits A, Rayyan M, et al. (2021). Nutrients, 13(11), 4109. <https://doi.org/10.3390/nu13114109>; Tursunov D, Yoshida Y, Yrysov K, et al. (2018). Nagoya Journal of Medical Science, 80(1), 11. <https://doi.org/10.18999/nagjms.80.1.11>
- Note: When giving birth at a regular hospital in China, the hospital routinely gives this shot; you do not need to worry about it yourself. For home births, or births at unregulated facilities, you must actively confirm. Oral administration is less effective than injection; the injection referred to here is intramuscular

### 8. If an infant under 3 months reaches a body temperature of 38°C, go straight to the hospital; do not observe at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Registration plus examination, a few hundred yuan
- Plain-language summary: For an infant under 3 months, if body temperature reaches 38°C, go straight to the hospital. Do not observe at home, and do not first give fever-reducing medicine and wait to see. A child of this age, even with a serious bacterial infection, may present with only fever. So "looks alert enough" cannot be a reason to wait at home. For an infant under 8 days old, even the guidelines do not cover; even more so, go immediately
- Benefit: The American Academy of Pediatrics has developed a specific guideline for well-appearing full-term infants aged 8 to 60 days. This guideline defines fever as "body temperature ≥38.0°C." It splits into three tiers — 8 to 21 days, 22 to 28 days, and 29 to 60 days — each with its own management pathway. Infants under 8 days old are not even in the scope of this guideline, meaning that at this stage it is even more imperative to seek care immediately
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Pantell RH, Roberts KB, Adams WG, et al. (2021). Evaluation and Management of Well-Appearing Febrile Infants 8 to 60 Days Old. Pediatrics, 148(2), e2021052228. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2021-052228>
- Note: A child of this age, even with a serious bacterial infection, may present with only fever. So looking "alert enough" cannot be a reason to observe at home. Also, do not first administer fever-reducing medicine yourself and wait to see

### 9. No matter how tired or angry, never shake the baby
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is stopping your hands when you are too tired or too angry
- Plain-language summary: A baby's head is large and the neck is weak. Forceful shaking causes bleeding in the brain and eyes; it may be fatal or leave lifelong disability. Gentle rocking to soothe sleep is fine; what is dangerous is uncontrolled violent shaking. When you are about to lose your temper, put the baby back in the crib, walk away for a few minutes — let the baby cry for a while is safer than holding and shaking
- Benefit: The infant's head is proportionally large and neck muscles are weak. Violent shaking causes intracranial bleeding and retinal bleeding, possibly fatal or leaving lifelong disability. Gentle rocking to soothe sleep is not what we mean; what is dangerous is uncontrolled violent shaking. Medically, this category of injury from shaking and impact is called "abusive head trauma." It is the leading cause of death from head trauma in children under 2. In the United States from 1999 to 2014, it caused nearly 2,250 deaths in children under 5
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Spies EL, Klevens J (2016). Fatal Abusive Head Trauma Among Children Aged <5 Years — United States, 1999–2014. MMWR 65(20):505-509. <https://doi.org/10.15585/mmwr.mm6520a1>; Choudhary AK et al. (2018). Consensus statement on abusive head trauma in infants and young children. Pediatric Radiology 48(8):1048-1065. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00247-018-4149-1>
- Note: When you truly reach the moment of losing control, first put the baby back in the crib and walk away for a few minutes. Letting the baby cry for a while is far safer than holding and shaking. Those who care for a baby alone, chronically sleep-deprived, must remember this most. For how to handle your own emotions, see Section 3

### 10. For diapers, do not look at the brand; look at three things: fit, how often they are changed, and whether they have been flagged in spot-check notifications
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: A few hundred to over a thousand yuan per month, depending on the tier you use
- Plain-language summary: No official agency ranks "which brand is best." What you can check is only two things: the State Administration for Market Regulation's product quality spot-check notifications, and regulators' investigations of specific incidents. Expensive does not equal safe; imported does not equal compliant. First buy a small pack and try for a week; if the baby has no diaper rash or pressure marks, then stock up
- Benefit: No official agency has published a ranking of "which brand is best," so this book makes no brand recommendations. What can be verified is only two categories. One is the State Administration for Market Regulation's product quality spot-check notifications. The other is regulators' investigations of specific incidents. After the June 2026 "infant diaper formamide issue" drew attention, the State Administration for Market Regulation led the formation of a joint investigation team to verify
- Evidence grade: C
- Source: Chinese Government Network (2026-06-22). State Administration for Market Regulation leads formation of joint investigation team to verify the "infant diaper formamide issue." <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202606/content_7072931.htm> (this page has only a title; the body is not displayed, so this entry cites only the fact of the investigation, not any conclusion)
- Note: TODO (to verify: the conclusion of that investigation, and the original formal notification by the State Administration for Market Regulation). How to actually buy: expensive does not equal safe; imported does not equal compliant. First buy a small pack and try for a week; if the baby has no diaper rash or pressure marks, then stock up. Diaper rash is mostly caused by infrequent changing and lack of breathability; the brand matters less than is imagined. How to check spot-check notifications is in Section 5

### 11. For big-ticket items, consider in the order "borrow, buy secondhand, buy new"; do not equip everything at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No additional money; what is saved is usually in the thousands of yuan. The hard part is resisting the urge to equip everything at once
- Plain-language summary: Strollers, cribs, swaddling blankets, and toys are all used briefly; the secondhand market is plentiful. First ask to borrow; if not, buy secondhand; only if that fails, buy new — you can usually save over a thousand yuan. Car seats and mattresses should not be bought secondhand; the structural damage to a car seat after a crash is invisible to the eye. For big-ticket items like postpartum care centers and early education classes, give yourself a 24-hour cool-down before deciding
- Benefit: Strollers, cribs, swaddling blankets, and toys are all used briefly; the secondhand market has supply far exceeding demand. What you genuinely cannot skimp on, and is not advisable to buy secondhand, is two things: car seats and mattresses. Structural damage to a car seat after a crash is invisible to the naked eye
- Evidence grade: C
- Source: Author's experience, no direct literature; for car seat evidence, see Section 1; for impulse spending, see Section 5
- Note: Postpartum care centers, early education classes, and infant swimming facilities are neither recommended nor dismissed by this book. But they are all a large expense that can be either spent or skipped; the 24-hour cool-down period discussed in Section 5 applies

### 12. If the child has severe eczema or egg allergy, do not avoid peanuts; introduce them early under medical guidance, but never feed whole peanuts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A jar of peanut butter, tens of yuan. Feed a few times a week, easy to do. Before starting, take the child to a doctor for an assessment
- Plain-language summary: Infants with severe eczema or egg allergy who regularly consume peanuts from 4 to 11 months have a peanut allergy rate of 1.9% at age 5; those who completely avoid have 13.7%. Children whose skin test already shows some reaction have 10.6% if consuming, and 35.3% if avoiding. Take the child to a doctor before introducing. Whole peanuts must never be fed — they can block the windpipe
- Benefit: The UK LEAP randomized trial. 640 infants enrolled, with criteria of severe eczema, egg allergy, or both. At enrollment they were 4 to 11 months old. Randomized into two groups: one regularly consumed peanut products, one completely avoided; peanut allergy rate was assessed at 60 months. Of the 530 who were peanut skin-test negative at enrollment, the avoidance group had 13.7% allergy, the consuming group 1.9% (P<0.001). Of the 98 who were weakly skin-test positive at enrollment, the avoidance group had 35.3%, the consuming group 10.6% (P=0.004). There was no difference in serious adverse events between the two groups
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Du Toit G, Roberts G, Sayre PH, et al. (2015). Randomized trial of peanut consumption in infants at risk for peanut allergy. New England Journal of Medicine, 372(9), 803-813. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1414850>; General Office of the National Health Commission (2020). Core Information on Feeding and Health Education for Infants and Toddlers. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2020-08/01/content_5531915.htm>; Perkin MR et al. (2016). Randomized Trial of Introduction of Allergenic Foods in Breast-Fed Infants. New England Journal of Medicine, 374(18), 1733-1743. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1514210> (the EAT general-infant trial referenced in the note)
- Note: **The form can only be thinned peanut butter or peanut powder mixed into complementary food; never give whole peanuts.** The National Health Commission's feeding core information states plainly: "Whole peanuts, nuts, jelly and similar foods are easily inhaled into the windpipe and cause choking; infants and toddlers should avoid eating them." What to do when choking occurs, see Section 13, Entry 26 (someone choking and unable to speak). This applies only to high-risk children: those with severe eczema or already allergic to eggs. **Before introducing, you must see a doctor for an assessment; do not try this at home yourself**: the LEAP trial gave each child a skin test before enrollment; children whose skin-test reaction exceeded 4 mm were excluded from the trial and were not given peanuts. The evidence for general-population children is much weaker. In another trial of 1,162 ordinary breastfed infants, by intention-to-treat, the early-introduction group had food allergy 5.6%, the routine group 7.1%, not significant (P=0.32); significance appeared only among those who actually complied (2.4% vs 7.3%). This kind of analysis tends to overstate effect; the authors themselves say intention-to-treat did not prove efficacy, but it also shows that early introduction is safe. China's official feeding documents currently have no statement on "whether to introduce allergenic foods early or avoid them," so this entry is written based on international trial evidence. Pay attention to timing too: LEAP started at 4 months, whereas in China complementary foods begin at 6 months; see Entry 4 in this section (breastfeed only for the first 6 months). At what month and how to start for high-risk children — follow the doctor's guidance
