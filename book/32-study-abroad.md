# 32. Study Abroad: Status, Work, Insurance, and Credential Recognition After Return

This section calculates money and personal freedom; it does not discuss whether to go abroad or compare schools. The four things you will lose out on if you do not know them: how visa status is maintained, what the cap on working hours is, which insurance and fees are mandatory, and whether the diploma can be recognized after returning home.

Coverage spans the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia; figures are listed country by country. Sources are official web pages from each country's immigration authority and China's Ministry of Education. **Each country changes its study-abroad policies frequently — more frequently than Chinese regulations. The cutoff date for all figures in this section is September 2026. Before departure and before each visa renewal, re-check the figures yourself via the links in the Source field.**

For how to seek help from embassies and consulates when something happens overseas, what 12308 can do, and how to buy overseas medical and medical-evacuation insurance, see Section 21. For the question of studying versus working, see Section 23. For account theft, see Section 14; for telecom fraud, see Section 8. These are not repeated.

### 1. Before paying tuition, check whether the school is on the MOE Service Center credential recognition (留服认证) accredited institutions list
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Enter the school name once in the MOE Service Center's "accredited institution lookup" and the check is done in a few minutes.
- Plain-language summary: After returning, this diploma must pass a credential recognition to be used for civil service exams, state-owned enterprise employment, household registration, and professional title evaluation. Recognition is done by the MOE Service Center for Scholarly Exchange. If the school is not on the recognizable list, tuition and time are wasted, and the diploma cannot be used domestically. Checking the list costs nothing; check before paying tuition.
- Benefit: The Chinese (Ministry of Education) Service Center for Scholarly Exchange has an accredited institution lookup. You can search by country or by school name. What you are checking is whether the academic degrees issued by the school fall within the recognition scope. Additionally, the Ministry of Education's Cross-Border Education Supervision Information Network publishes supervision information on Chinese-foreign cooperative education and overseas education.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Chinese (Ministry of Education) Service Center for Scholarly Exchange. Accredited Institution Lookup. <http://yxcx.cscse.edu.cn/>; Ministry of Education Cross-Border Education Supervision Information Network. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/>
- Note: An agent's verbal claim of "MOE recognition" does not count; the result of your own online lookup is authoritative. The list changes — being on the list at enrollment does not guarantee being on it at graduation, so re-check once each year during study. Chinese-foreign cooperative education programs have a separate recognition and registration system. For those enrolled after 2008, you can look up your registration number on the Cross-Border Education Supervision Information Network by name and ID number.

### 2. Track the US F-1 new-rule lawsuit: "max four years, leave within 30 days of finishing" was to take effect September 15, 2026; the day before, a court paused it; the current rule remains "duration of status"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Copy the program end date on your I-20 into your calendar with a 90-day advance reminder. The I-20 is the study-eligibility document the school issues you. Also check the school's international student office notices once each semester to track this lawsuit's progress.
- Plain-language summary: The US had planned to change F-1 to a maximum of four years, with departure within 30 days of completion. The day before it was to take effect, a federal court paused it. So the old rule still applies: you stay as long as you are studying, with 60 days after completion to prepare to leave. A pause is not a repeal — the government can still appeal, and the rule may change again at any time.
- Benefit: On July 17, 2026, the Department of Homeland Security published a rule originally scheduled to take effect September 15, 2026. The content: F-1 students would no longer enter under "duration of status," but on a fixed term of up to four years, with a separate extension application required upon expiration. After completion of study or practical training, only 30 days would be allowed to prepare to depart. On September 14, 2026, Judge Saylor of the US District Court for the District of Massachusetts, under Section 705 of the Administrative Procedure Act, postponed the rule's effective date. The postponement applies nationwide and to the entire rule. The court did not revoke the rule — the revocation request was denied, but with leave to renew. The case is still being heard. Under the court's order, those on F, J, and I visas still enter under "duration of status," the 60-day departure grace period remains, and no separate extension application is required.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: DHS (2026). U.S. Department of Homeland Security, No. 1:26-cv-13799-FDS (D. Mass. Sept. 14, 2026), court docket. <https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/74661796/presidents-alliance-on-higher-education-and-immigration-v-united-states/>; Yale Office of International Students & Scholars (2026-09-14). Important Update: Court Action on the D/S Rule. <https://oiss.yale.edu/news/important-update-court-action-on-the-ds-rule>; AILA (2026). One Day Before Taking Effect, Federal Court Postpones the F, J, and I Fixed Admission Period Rule. <https://www.aila.org/blog/think-immigration-one-day-before-taking-effect-federal-court-postpones-the-f-j-and-i-fixed-admission-period-rule>; 8 CFR 214.2(f). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2>
- Note: This entry is current as of September 25, 2026. The government may appeal to the First Circuit Court of Appeals, and DHS may revise and reissue the rule. So if you are studying for several years, do not assume the old rule will remain unchanged. The court scheduled a status hearing for October 2, 2026 — after that date, check the school's notice again. If the new rule takes effect later, extensions will require a separate application before the term expires, with no automatic renewal. Under the old rule, those who leave the program early have different departure deadlines than those who complete — ask the school's international student office first. J visas are in the same rule, with details different from F-1 — check according to your visa category.

### 3. Working hours are a red line written into law in all four of the US, Canada, UK, and Australia — exceeding by even one hour violates visa conditions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Track your own hours by week. The hard part is that employers may say "no one checks a few extra hours" — do not believe it.
- Plain-language summary: All four countries cap the working hours for student visa holders. Exceeding them is a visa violation that may result in visa cancellation, deportation, and scrutiny on future applications. The caps: US 20 hours per week; UK 20 hours per week for degree-level study, 10 hours for sub-degree; Canada 24 hours per week; Australia 48 hours per two weeks. Two jobs are combined for the calculation.
- Benefit: US F-1 students may not exceed 20 hours per week of on-campus work during the academic term. Approved off-campus part-time work is likewise 20 hours per week. Full-time is permitted during breaks. Per 8 CFR 214.2(f)(9). UK student visa: for degree-level or above courses, 20 hours per week during term; for sub-degree, 10 hours per week. Part-time courses prohibit work entirely. Self-employment is not permitted, nor work as a professional athlete or entertainer. Per the student-related portion of the UK Immigration Rules, Appendix Student ST26.1, ST26.5. Canada: qualifying full-time students may work off-campus up to 24 hours per week, per IRPR section 186(v). Australia: student visa holders may work up to 48 hours per two weeks during course sessions. Research master's, doctoral students, and their dependents are not subject to this limit.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: 8 CFR 214.2(f)(9). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2>; UK Home Office. Immigration Rules Appendix Student (ST26.1, ST26.5). <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student>; IRCC. Work off campus as an international student. <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html>; Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. Student visa (subclass 500). <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500>
- Note: US off-campus work requires prior authorization. You may not begin work while the I-765 employment authorization application is pending. Canada's old permits state 20 hours per week, but as long as you continue to qualify, you may actually work 24 hours — current regulations govern. The UK's prohibition on self-employment is broad: taking private clients, opening an online shop, and freelancing all count.

### 4. The root of status is "full-time enrolled": during leaves of absence, withdrawal, or transfer gaps, no work is permitted
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Before pausing studies, taking leave, or transferring, first contact the school's international student office to clarify your status.
- Plain-language summary: Whether you can work depends on whether you are currently studying full-time. Once you take leave, withdraw, are dismissed, or are between schools without current enrollment, your work eligibility is gone immediately. Continuing to work at that point is illegal employment. Many people assume that as long as the visa has not expired, they are fine — but status has already lapsed.
- Benefit: Canada explicitly states that during an approved leave of absence, off-campus work is not permitted. During a transfer period without active enrollment, off-campus work is also not permitted. Work may resume only after studies resume. US on-campus work eligibility applies only to F-1 students whose SEVIS status is Active. SEVIS is the US student-status record system; Active means currently enrolled and valid. These students must also maintain a full-time course load. UK work permission depends on course type — part-time courses prohibit work.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: IRCC. Work off campus as an international student. <https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/work/work-off-campus.html>; U.S. Department of Homeland Security, Study in the States. Working in the United States. <https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/students/work/working-in-the-united-states>; UK Home Office. Immigration Rules Appendix Student (ST26.1). <https://www.gov.uk/guidance/immigration-rules/immigration-rules-appendix-student>
- Note: When course load becomes heavy and you want to reduce it, ask first: below how many credits does it stop being full-time? This status lapses earlier than work eligibility. Those dismissed from school or whose programs are terminated typically lose status that same day — do not wait for an official letter before acting.

### 5. US: within 10 days of moving, you must report your new address
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Submit one address change online, ten minutes.
- Plain-language summary: In the US, when you change residence, the law requires notification of immigration within 10 days. Not reporting is not a small matter. First, it is itself a violation; second, mail from immigration may go missing.
- Benefit: 8 CFR 265.1 provides that aliens required to register by law must, within 10 days of an address change, report it. Report to USCIS, by the method it requires, both the fact of the change and the new address.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: 8 CFR 265.1. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-265/section-265.1>; USCIS. AR-11, Alien's Change of Address Card. <https://www.uscis.gov/ar-11>
- Note: After reporting to immigration, the SEVIS record (your student-status record) at the school must be updated separately. The other three countries also require notification of the immigration authority or school upon address change. Deadlines and methods differ by country — follow your host country's immigration authority website.

### 6. Before departure and during study, check the MOE's study-abroad alerts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Look once before departure and once each semester thereafter.
- Plain-language summary: The Ministry of Education issues dedicated alerts for specific countries or regions, stating that recent security or policy conditions there are unfavorable to Chinese students. These are official, publicly issued risk notices, verifiable in writing — more reliable than agents' statements. A look costs nothing.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Education issues study-abroad alerts through the Cross-Border Education Supervision Information Network. Four alerts were issued in 2025. Alert No. 1 noted that higher-education bills passed by certain US states contain provisions unfavorable to China (2025-04-09). Alerts No. 2 and No. 3 twice noted a high incidence of cases targeting Chinese citizens in the Philippines and a poor study environment (2025-07-18, 2025-08-30). Alert No. 4 noted a poor security situation and study environment in Japan and recommended caution in planning study in Japan (2025-11-16).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ministry of Education Cross-Border Education Supervision Information Network. Alert Information. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/>; Ministry of Education issues 2025 Alert No. 4 on study abroad. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/n2/2/2/2060.shtml>; Alert No. 1. <https://jsj.moe.gov.cn/n2/2/2/2001.shtml>
- Note: An alert is not a ban and does not mean you cannot go — it gives you an official basis for weighing local risks. The alert list changes with circumstances; this section does not track it long-term — the current page on the website is authoritative. For overseas personal safety and consular protection, see Section 21.

### 7. Australia: OSHC must cover the entire period with no gap; arriving without insurance may result in denial of entry
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Several thousand to over ten thousand RMB. Purchased at once for the visa duration — buy for as many years as you study.
- Plain-language summary: Australia writes overseas student health insurance into visa conditions — it is not optional. The insurance must cover the entire stay. When switching policies on visa renewal, no gap is permitted. Arriving without proof of insurance may result in on-the-spot denial of entry. Family members accompanying you on a student visa each require their own policy.
- Benefit: The Australian Department of Home Affairs requires student visa applicants and their dependents to purchase Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC), and to hold it throughout the stay in Australia, with no gap. The insurance must be provided by an Australian-approved insurer. Exceptions apply for those meeting exemption criteria. No gap is permitted between the new insurance and the insurance under the prior visa. Arriving without proof of insurance may result in denial of entry. Those entering earlier than the course start date must set the insurance start date as the actual date of arrival in Australia.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Australian Government Department of Home Affairs. Student visa (subclass 500). <https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/student-500>
- Note: If the school arranges it on your behalf, ask clearly for the insurer name, policy start and end dates, and policy number — these are required on the visa application. Buying too short a period causes visa renewal problems; better to buy a bit beyond graduation.

### 8. UK: in addition to the visa fee, there is an annual healthcare surcharge — students pay 776 pounds per year, paid in full at application
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Visa fee 558 pounds. Healthcare surcharge 776 pounds per year. Both paid in full at application.
- Plain-language summary: Going to the UK costs more than just the visa fee. There is also an annual healthcare surcharge based on visa duration that you must prepay to use the UK's National Health Service. A two-year visa means paying 1,552 pounds at once. Combined with the visa fee, this sum is paid upfront before departure — do not omit it from your budget.
- Benefit: The UK student visa fee is 558 pounds. This is the same whether applying outside the UK or extending or switching within the UK. There is also a healthcare surcharge, paid in advance at application based on the visa duration, which is required to use the UK's National Health Service. The student and dependents pay 776 pounds per year — a 2-year visa is 1,552 pounds. Other categories of applicants pay 1,035 pounds per year. For visas exceeding 6 months but less than 1 year, the full year is charged.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: UK Government. Student visa. <https://www.gov.uk/student-visa>; UK Government. Pay for UK healthcare as part of your immigration application. <https://www.gov.uk/healthcare-immigration-application>
- Note: Amounts and exchange rates change — calculate using the current figures on the two pages in the Source field. Those on degree courses and aged 18 or above can generally stay up to 5 years; sub-degree courses 2 years. Use those years to estimate the surcharge.

### 9. Before returning, allow time for credential recognition: MOE Service Center recognition takes 10 to 20 working days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: Online application and online payment. Preparing materials plus review, counted in working days, takes about a month.
- Plain-language summary: Overseas academic credentials must first undergo credential recognition to be used domestically. Review alone takes 10 to 20 working days. During recruitment season, or if materials are returned for correction, it takes longer. Autumn recruitment, civil service exam registration, and household registration all have deadlines — do not wait until you need it to begin processing.
- Benefit: Recognition of academic degrees from outside China is handled at the online service hall of the Chinese (Ministry of Education) Service Center for Scholarly Exchange. The process has four steps: register and verify identity; submit application and materials online; pay online; wait for evaluation and review. The published processing time is 10 to 20 working days. Working days exclude weekends and holidays; this is the time the Service Center uses for review.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Chinese (Ministry of Education) Service Center for Scholarly Exchange. Online Service Hall · Academic Degree Recognition. <http://zwfw.cscse.edu.cn/>
- Note: The materials to submit are: diploma certificate, passport or travel permit, residence card or visa endorsement, ID photo, and authorization statement. Exit-entry records during the overseas period are pulled automatically by the system, so do not lose the visa pages from your time abroad. The Service Center also posts announcements on which recognition certificates have been invalidated and which schools' recognition applications are being suspended. Check before applying whether your school is affected.

### 10. The list of schools under "enhanced scrutiny" grows every year; the path of buying a diploma is closed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Before applying, search the school name once in the Service Center's notices and announcements.
- Plain-language summary: The Service Center names certain overseas institutions for enhanced credential-recognition scrutiny, and also suspends recognition applications for individual schools. The named ones are often exactly the kind advertised as "no language requirement, no need to leave China, master's in a few months." Searching the school name before applying is much easier than appealing afterward.
- Benefit: The Chinese (Ministry of Education) Service Center for Scholarly Exchange has been issuing announcements enhancing credential recognition scrutiny for certain overseas institutions (an additional check on these schools' diplomas). The most recent is the ninth announcement (2025-10-28). The Service Center has also issued announcements suspending recognition applications for individual overseas institutions, and has issued warnings about scams in which people use the credential-recognition process to defraud.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Chinese (Ministry of Education) Service Center for Scholarly Exchange. Announcement on Enhanced Credential Recognition Scrutiny for Certain Overseas Institutions (IX) (2025-10-28). <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/cscse/sy/tzgg/2025102809225023345/index.html>; Chinese (Ministry of Education) Service Center for Scholarly Exchange. <https://www.cscse.edu.cn/>
- Note: "Enhanced scrutiny" does not mean recognition is impossible, but it will require additional documentation and take longer. Any agent promising "guaranteed recognition" or "internal expedited channel" is a scam — the Service Center has issued a dedicated anti-fraud notice. Cross-border arrangements to have someone write papers or attend online classes on your behalf, if detected, can void the credential and may affect your visa record.
