# 33. How to Live After Disability

This section addresses life after disability has already occurred. For how to prevent disability, see Sections 1 and 13 — not repeated here.

Four metrics are calculated separately and not converted into one another. The entries on emergencies and complications are calculated by mortality. Those on subsidies, benefits, and lawsuits are calculated by money. Those on schooling and procedures are calculated by time. Those on psychiatric hospitalization, capacity, and guardianship are calculated by personal freedom.

The World Health Organization's reference gives three numbers. About 1.3 billion people worldwide have significant disability — about 16% of the world's population, roughly one in six. Persons with disabilities die up to 20 years earlier than those without. Persons with disabilities have double the risk of depression, asthma, diabetes, stroke, obesity, or poor oral health compared with persons without disabilities. Much of this gap is not caused by disability itself, but by lack of access to medical care, schooling, employment, and the ability to leave the house. So the entries in this section are half about the body, half about paperwork and rights.

Five things already addressed elsewhere are only pointed to here, not repeated. How to obtain a disability certificate (残疾人证), the seven categories, and how grades 1 through 4 are assessed, see Section 24, Entry 11 (disability certificate). When disability assessment can be done, see Section 24, Entry 10 (disability assessment). How to claim the two disability subsidies, see Section 7, Entry 8 (two subsidies). For work-injury labor capacity assessment and how the disability grade converts to money, see Section 19, Entry 15 (labor capacity assessment). For how to apply for long-term care insurance with severe disability, see Section 17, Entry 7 (long-term care insurance). That entry is in the "having an elderly person at home" section, but this benefit has no age threshold — younger people can receive it too.

Most entries in this section have you and your family as the primary beneficiaries, in the highest two tiers. Where others are involved, the note addresses this separately.

### 1. After spinal cord injury, sudden severe headache and sweating — sit the person upright, loosen clothing, and call 120
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Three actions, done in tens of seconds.
- Plain-language summary: For those with spinal cord injury, the body's alarm wiring is severed in part. A full bladder or tight clothing may not be felt, but blood pressure can surge. The signals are sudden severe headache and sweating. Sit the person upright, loosen tight clothing, check whether the bladder is full, and call 120 at the same time.
- Benefit: This kind of episode is called autonomic dysreflexia. A systematic review searched four databases — Medline, CINAHL, EMBASE, and PsycINFO — and included 31 studies, 6 of which were randomized controlled trials. The review states that the non-pharmacological management at the time of an episode is three steps: position the patient upright, loosen tight clothing, and remove the triggering stimulus. These three steps are based on clinical consensus and physiological data — Level 5 evidence. Antihypertensive medication is used only when blood pressure remains elevated; among these, prazosin has Level 1 evidence, and nifedipine and prostaglandin E2 have Level 2 evidence. The review also notes that strategies for preventing episodes mainly target the two common triggers — the genitourinary system and surgery — and most are supported only by Level 4 and Level 5 evidence.
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Krassioukov A, Warburton DE, Teasell R, Eng JJ; Spinal Cord Injury Rehabilitation Evidence Research Team (2009). A systematic review of the management of autonomic dysreflexia after spinal cord injury. Archives of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation, 90(4):682-695. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apmr.2008.10.017>
- Note: Rated B because there is no randomized controlled trial behind those three on-scene actions — only consensus and physiological reasoning. The review itself states that these methods are mostly supported only by non-controlled studies. The medication portion is the doctor's responsibility — do not administer antihypertensives at home. Laying the person flat raises blood pressure further — sit them upright. Family members and caregivers must also know these three steps. Do not treat it as an ordinary headache and tough it out.

### 2. The first decade after disability is a high-risk period for suicide — do not treat it as something "looking on the bright side" can fix
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. A phone call, or one consultation.
- Plain-language summary: In the first years after disability, the breakdown of one's own or family's mood is common — not a matter of willpower. Long-term follow-up at two UK spinal cord injury centers found this group's suicide rate was five times that of the general population. Over 90% of suicides occurred within the first decade after injury. Those whose injury cause itself was a suicide attempt had an even higher risk.
- Benefit: The study followed 2,304 traumatic spinal cord injury patients newly admitted between 1991 and 2010 who survived the first year after injury and had neurological deficits at discharge, with follow-up through end of 2014. Among them, 63 (2.7%) had sustained the injury as a result of suicide attempt. By the end of follow-up, 533 had died, of whom 4.2% died by suicide; 91% of suicides occurred within the first decade after injury. The age-standardized suicide mortality rate was 62.5 per 100,000 per year (95% CI 36.4–88.6, confidence interval), five times the 2014 suicide rate of the general population in England and Wales (12.2 per 100,000). For those whose injury was caused by suicide attempt, compared with other injury causes, the odds ratio for death was 4.32 (P<0.001), and for suicide was 9.46 (P<0.001).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Savic G, DeVivo MJ, Frankel HL, Jamous MA, Soni BM, Charlifue S (2018). Suicide and traumatic spinal cord injury - a cohort study. Spinal Cord, 56(1):2-6. <https://doi.org/10.1038/sc.2017.98>
- Note: This study examined only spinal cord injury; extending the conclusion to other disability causes such as blindness, amputation, or burns is an extrapolation. What it shows is the time window: risk does not end at discharge — it must be watched for a decade. For talking to someone, call 12356 — see Section 29, Entry 11 (12356). For not stockpiling sleeping pills and pesticides at home, see Section 1, Entry 25 (suicidal thoughts). For grief and mood stuck for half a year, see Section 29, Entry 8 (grief stuck after half a year).

### 3. Psychiatric hospitalization is voluntary in principle; only two circumstances allow admission without the person's consent
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Memorize the two circumstances so you can articulate them when needed.
- Plain-language summary: A family member or employer cannot keep someone in a psychiatric hospital by a single statement. The law recognizes only two circumstances: the person has already harmed themselves or is at risk of doing so; the person has already harmed others or is at risk of doing so. The first requires the guardian's consent for admission. If you dispute the diagnosis, you can request a re-diagnosis within three days, and thereafter you may commission an independent assessment yourself.
- Benefit: Article 30 of the Mental Health Law states that "hospitalization for mental disorder treatment is voluntary in principle." Only when the diagnostic conclusion and condition assessment indicate that the patient is a person with severe mental disorder and one of the following circumstances applies, should hospital treatment be implemented. First: "has already engaged in behavior harming themselves, or poses a risk of harming themselves." Second: "has already engaged in behavior endangering the safety of others, or poses a risk of endangering the safety of others." Article 31: for the first circumstance, the medical institution implements hospital treatment only with the guardian's consent; if the guardian does not consent, it may not be implemented. Article 32: for the second circumstance, if the patient or guardian disputes it, a re-diagnosis may be requested, to be submitted within three days of receiving the diagnostic conclusion, and to be conducted by psychiatric practicing physicians other than the two who made the initial diagnosis; if still disputed, an independent psychiatric medical assessment may be commissioned at a qualified assessment institution. Article 35: if the re-diagnosis conclusion or assessment report indicates that the patient cannot be confirmed as having severe mental disorder, or that the patient does not require hospital treatment, the medical institution may not implement hospital treatment. Article 78: those who deliberately admit a person without mental disorder as a person with mental disorder bear compensation liability in accordance with the law (national, effective May 1, 2013, amended 2018).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Mental Health Law of the People's Republic of China (2018 amendment, Articles 30, 31, 32, 35, 78). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7448a066d>
- Note: Conversely, it is not the case that no one can bring someone in for evaluation. Article 28 provides that where a suspected person with mental disorder has already engaged in behavior harming themselves or endangering the safety of others, or poses such risk, the close relatives, the employer, or the local public security organ shall immediately take measures to stop the behavior and bring the person to a medical institution for psychiatric diagnosis. A medical institution that receives a person brought for diagnosis may not refuse to make a diagnosis. So "brought for diagnosis" and "kept for hospitalization" are two gates — the first is low, the second is high. Article 75 lists the responsibilities of medical institutions: violations in implementing restraint or isolation, or diagnosing a person without mental disorder as having one, lead to sanctions up to revocation of the practice certificate. For what to do when someone close has violent impulses, see Section 8.

### 4. The person providing long-term care for a disabled family member — their own body must be watched
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Off-load part of the care work, and get a checkup yourself.
- Plain-language summary: Caregiver burnout is not weakness. A US study followed over 800 elderly people for four years. Those who cared for a disabled spouse while reporting mental strain had a mortality risk about 60% higher than non-caregivers. Caregivers who did not feel strain did not show this elevation. So what to watch is not caregiving itself, but chronic tension.
- Benefit: The study was a prospective population cohort, 1993 to 1998, mean follow-up 4.5 years, conducted in four US communities. 392 caregivers and 427 non-caregivers, ages 66 to 96, all cohabiting with a spouse. Within four years, 103 (12.6%) died. After adjusting for sociodemographic factors, existing disease, and subclinical cardiovascular disease, those who provided care while reporting mental or emotional strain had a mortality risk 63% higher than non-caregiver controls (RR 1.63, 95% CI 1.00–2.65, confidence interval). Caregivers who did not report strain (RR 1.08, 95% CI 0.61–1.90) and those whose spouse was disabled but who did not provide care (RR 1.37, 95% CI 0.73–2.58) showed no elevated adjusted mortality.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Schulz R, Beach SR (1999). Caregiving as a risk factor for mortality: the Caregiver Health Effects Study. JAMA, 282(23):2215-2219. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.282.23.2215>
- Note: Controversy: the lower bound of the confidence interval sits exactly at 1.00, so the "no difference" possibility is not entirely excluded. The population is US elderly cohabiting with a spouse, and the care recipients are disabled elderly spouses — extending to those caring for younger disabled family members is an extrapolation. What it does show is the direction: those feeling strain have higher risk. Off-load as much care work as possible; ask the local Disabled Persons' Federation and Civil Affairs about respite and day-care services. Caregivers must not stop their own chronic disease medications — see Section 16, Entry 1 (taking medications as prescribed to completion). The beneficiary of this entry is you yourself.

### 5. For long-term wheelchair users, switch to a pressure-relief cushion and check the ischial and coccygeal areas daily
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: A pressure-relief cushion costs several hundred to over a thousand yuan. Daily check, one minute.
- Plain-language summary: Ordinary foam cushions, when sat on for long periods, can break down the skin; broken skin heals very slowly and is prone to infection. Among nursing-home residents using pressure-relief cushions, pressure ulcers at the ischial area were significantly fewer, but no difference was found when the coccyx was included. The wheelchair itself must be fitted to the person's dimensions — do not just buy a generic model.
- Benefit: This randomized clinical trial was conducted at 12 nursing homes, enrolling 232 residents aged 65 or older who used a wheelchair for 6 or more hours per day and had a Braden score of 18 or below, between 2004 and 2008. All were first fitted with a wheelchair appropriate to their body, then randomized into two groups. One group used skin-protection cushions (air, visco-fluid foam, or gel-foam), and the other used 7.6 cm segmented foam cushions. Follow-up was 6 months or until a pressure ulcer developed. Pressure ulcers at the ischial tuberosity: segmented foam group 8 cases (6.7%), skin-protection cushion group 1 case (0.9%), P=0.04. Combining ischial and sacrococcygeal pressure ulcers: segmented foam group 21 cases (17.6%), skin-protection cushion group 12 cases (10.6%), P=0.14 — no significant difference.
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Brienza D, Kelsey S, Karg P, et al. (2010). A randomized clinical trial on preventing pressure ulcers with wheelchair seat cushions. Journal of the American Geriatrics Society, 58(12):2308-2314. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1532-5415.2010.03168.x>
- Note: Rated B for two reasons. First, the sample was only 232 people, with the primary endpoint P-value of 0.04 just clearing the threshold, and the secondary endpoint showed no difference. Second, the population was elderly nursing-home residents; extending to younger wheelchair users is an extrapolation. In the trial, all participants were first fitted with an appropriate wheelchair, so "proper fit" and "cushion" work together — do not just change the cushion. For pressure ulcer prevention in long-term bedridden patients, air mattresses and regular turning — see Section 17, Entry 8 (pressure ulcers). For how to apply for assistive device configuration subsidies — see Entry 7 of this section (ask at the county-level Disabled Persons' Federation for the full list).

### 6. Do not buy therapies and devices claiming to "cure paralysis, blindness, or deafness"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge — it saves money. The hard part is that family members hold out hope and cannot be dissuaded.
- Plain-language summary: The Advertising Law is unambiguous. Advertising for medical, pharmaceutical, and medical-device products may not guarantee efficacy, may not state cure rates or effectiveness rates, and may not use testimonials. So "stand up after three courses" or "X number of people have already recovered" are themselves illegal advertisements — no need to study their claimed principles. Non-medical-device products may not even mention treating disease.
- Benefit: Article 16 of the Advertising Law provides that advertisements for medical, pharmaceutical, and medical-device products may not contain: "(1) assertions or guarantees of efficacy or safety; (2) statements of cure rate or effectiveness rate; (3) comparisons with other pharmaceuticals, medical devices, or medical institutions in terms of efficacy and safety; (4) recommendation or endorsement by advertising spokespersons." Article 17 provides that, other than medical, pharmaceutical, and medical-device advertisements, "no other advertisement may address disease-treatment functions, nor may it use medical terminology or terminology that would confuse the marketed product with pharmaceuticals or medical devices." Article 4 provides that advertisements may not contain false or misleading content, may not deceive or mislead consumers, and that the advertiser is responsible for the truthfulness of the advertisement content (national, 2021 amendment).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Advertising Law of the People's Republic of China (2021 amendment, Articles 4, 16, 17). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8081817ab231eb017abd6bd860052d>
- Note: There is only one way to judge: see whether the seller dares to make absolute claims. The rehabilitation department of a legitimate hospital will not promise you that you will stand up after a certain number of courses. When you encounter such marketing, report it to 12315 or the local market supervision authority. Health-supplement sales pitches use the same pattern — see Section 6, Entry 10 (health supplements). If already purchased and seeking a refund, follow the online-shopping and prepaid-consumption process — see Section 5, Entry 29 (online shopping and platform rules). The beneficiary of this entry is you and your family.

### 7. After the disability certificate (残疾人证) is issued, go to the county-level Disabled Persons' Federation and ask about everything you can apply for in one visit
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. A trip to the county-level Disabled Persons' Federation, a morning's questioning. The offices to visit afterward are more than one.
- Plain-language summary: The disability certificate itself pays nothing — it is a key. Attached to it are several separate benefits, each of which you must apply for individually; they do not arrive automatically. On your first visit, work through the items on the list one by one — this saves trips back and forth.
- Benefit: The certificate connects to at least six benefits, each with its own source. First: the living-difficulty disability subsidy and the severe-disability care subsidy — see Section 7, Entry 8 (two subsidies). Second: rehabilitation assistance for children with disabilities — see Entry 8 of this section (rehabilitation assistance). Third: subsidy for basic-model assistive device configuration, per Article 26 of the Regulations on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation. Fourth: subsidy for home accessibility modifications — see Entry 9 of this section (ramps, handrails, bathroom modification). Fifth: proportional employment and employment services — see Entry 10 of this section (voluntarily disclosing your certificate). Sixth: individual income tax reduction — see Entry 11 of this section (income tax reduction). Article 26 of the Regulations on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation also states that the state provides basic rehabilitation services for urban and rural poor persons with disabilities and severe persons with disabilities through key rehabilitation projects, "providing subsidies for basic-model assistive device configuration in accordance with relevant state regulations." Article 20 provides that the relevant departments of county-level and higher people's governments and the Disabled Persons' Federation shall use community resources to set up rehabilitation venues or purchase services to carry out community rehabilitation work including rehabilitation guidance, daily living ability training, rehabilitation nursing, assistive device configuration, information consultation, and referral (national, effective July 1, 2017).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2017). Regulations on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation (Articles 20, 26). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-02/27/content_5171308.htm>
- Note: The process of obtaining the certificate itself is in Section 24, Entry 11 (disability certificate). Subsidy standards and specific methods are mostly set at the provincial level — the same benefit can differ severalfold between two provinces. When asking, note the name of each item; when you get home, look up the original document by name on the local Disabled Persons' Federation and government websites — do not rely solely on the counter's verbal statement. Do not count on getting everything in one visit; return once a year to ask again, as policies change.

### 8. Children under 7 with a disability or autism: apply to the county-level Disabled Persons' Federation for rehabilitation assistance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Submit one application to the county-level Disabled Persons' Federation, then choose a designated institution.
- Plain-language summary: The state has established a rehabilitation assistance system for children with disabilities aged 0 to 6 and children with autism, covering surgery, assistive devices, and rehabilitation training. The target population first prioritizes families in difficulty; localities with sufficient fiscal capacity may relax the age and family economic conditions. This is the window where rehabilitation is most effective — do not wait until school age. Institutions must be chosen from the Federation's designated list.
- Benefit: The State Council Opinion on Establishing a Disability Rehabilitation Assistance System for Children states that the assistance targets are children aged 0–6 with visual, hearing, speech, physical, or intellectual disabilities and children with autism, who meet the criteria. This includes children in urban and rural minimum-livelihood households, registered poverty-alleviation households, children in child-welfare institutions, disabled orphans, disabled children included in extreme-poverty support, and disabled children in other economically disadvantaged families. The specific recognition method for other economically disadvantaged families is set by county-level and higher local people's governments. The document also states: "localities with capacity may expand the age range for disability rehabilitation assistance, and may also relax the restrictions on the family economic conditions of the assistance targets." The funding guarantee standard is set by county-level and higher local people's governments based on local fiscal capacity, the number of persons to be protected, and disability category, with a dynamic adjustment mechanism. The higher-level basis is Article 26 of the Regulations on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation: the state establishes a disability rehabilitation assistance system for children, "gradually achieving free surgery, assistive device configuration, and rehabilitation training for children aged 0–6 with visual, hearing, speech, physical, or intellectual disabilities and children with autism" (national, from 2018).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2018). Opinion on Establishing a Disability Rehabilitation Assistance System for Children (Guo Fa [2018] No. 20). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2018-07/10/content_5305296.htm>; State Council (2017). Regulations on Disability Prevention and Rehabilitation (Article 26). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-02/27/content_5171308.htm>
- Note: The assistance standard is set by each province; first look up the "Implementation Measures for Disability Rehabilitation Assistance for Children" on the local Disabled Persons' Federation website. The designated institution list is held by the Federation. The document requires the Federation, with relevant departments, to strengthen admission and exit supervision of designated rehabilitation institutions, establish regular inspection and comprehensive assessment mechanisms, and establish a blacklist system. Institutions not on the list require you to pay in full, and recourse is difficult if something goes wrong. The beneficiary of this entry is your child, in the second tier. Institutions promising "guaranteed cure" should be handled per Entry 6 of this section (curing paralysis).

### 9. Home ramps, handrails, and bathroom modifications: subsidies can be applied for from county-level and higher governments
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Application is free. The modifications themselves are mostly subsidized, but you may need to cover part. You must visit the Federation and the subdistrict.
- Plain-language summary: The Law on the Building of a Barrier-Free Environment provides: county-level and higher governments shall support and guide home barrier-free facility modification, and shall provide appropriate subsidies to qualified families of persons with disabilities and the elderly. Such projects are generally led by the Federation and run by annual quota, so register early. How much is subsidized and which items are covered is set locally.
- Benefit: Article 19 of the Law on the Building of a Barrier-Free Environment: "county-level and higher people's governments shall support and guide home barrier-free facility modification. Appropriate subsidies shall be given to qualified families of persons with disabilities and the elderly." Article 18 provides that, for existing residential buildings, residential areas, public buildings, public places, transportation facilities, and urban and rural roads that do not meet the barrier-free facility engineering construction standards, county-level and higher people's governments shall, based on actual conditions, formulate targeted barrier-free facility modification plans and organize their implementation. Article 62 provides that any organization or individual has the right to submit opinions and suggestions on strengthening and improving barrier-free environment construction to the relevant competent departments of the government, and to complain about or report violations of this Law; the competent department, upon receiving a complaint or report, "shall handle it promptly and reply." Article 63 provides that for acts violating this Law that harm the public interest, the People's Procuratorate may issue procuratorial suggestions or file public interest litigation (national, effective September 1, 2023).
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Law of the People's Republic of China on the Building of a Barrier-Free Environment (Articles 18, 19, 62, 63). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff80818188d7430b0189018493370940>
- Note: Rated B because the law only states "shall provide appropriate subsidies" — how much and who qualifies is left to localities, with no national uniform figure. First look up "barrier-free modification for difficult and severely disabled families" on the local Disabled Persons' Federation website. Quotas are mostly allocated annually; queue early. Adding elevators in residential complexes and modifying corridors is a separate process through the housing and construction department. For public places with non-compliant barrier-free facilities, complain to the competent department per Article 62, which must reply.

### 10. When job-hunting, voluntarily disclose your certificate — hiring you saves the employer money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. One extra sentence at the interview. The hard part is speaking up.
- Plain-language summary: Employers must arrange for persons with disabilities to make up no less than 1.5% of the total workforce, with the specific proportion set by the province. Falling short requires payment into the disability employment fund (残保金), calculated per missing person at the employer's average employee wage from the previous year. So hiring you is not just charity — it is real savings. Mentioning your certificate at the interview lets the employer do this math.
- Benefit: Article 8 of the Regulations on the Employment of Persons with Disabilities: "the proportion of employees with disabilities arranged by an employer shall be no less than 1.5% of the total on-the-job workforce. The specific proportion shall be stipulated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the central government based on local conditions." Article 9: those falling short of the proportion stipulated by the provincial government shall pay the disability employment security fund. Article 8 of the Management Measures for the Collection and Use of the Disability Employment Security Fund gives the formula: "annual fund payment = (previous-year on-the-job workforce × proportion stipulated by the provincial government − previous-year actual number of employees with disabilities) × previous-year average annual wage of on-the-job employees." Article 16: small and micro enterprises registered for under 3 years with a workforce of 20 or below (inclusive) that do not meet the disability employment proportion are exempt from the fund. Article 36 of the Law on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities provides that the state grants tax preferences to employers that meet or exceed the prescribed proportion, or that centrally employ persons with disabilities (national, Regulations on the Employment of Persons with Disabilities effective May 1, 2007).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2007). Regulations on the Employment of Persons with Disabilities (Articles 8, 9). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_6646.htm>; Ministry of Finance, State Tax Administration, China Disabled Persons' Federation (2015). Management Measures for the Collection and Use of the Disability Employment Security Fund (Cai Shui [2015] No. 72, Articles 6, 8, 16). <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2015-09/15/content_5650063.htm>; Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities (Article 36). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080816f135f46016f1d134c88132b>
- Note: For newly registered small and micro enterprises under 3 years old with 20 or fewer employees, this rationale does not hold — do not count on it. The fund's reduction policies are continuously adjusted; rely on the year's Ministry of Finance announcements. Additionally, proportional employment requires genuine employment. Being listed on the payroll while the employer uses you to offset the fund is fraud, and you can be drawn into it. For employment-difficulty person status and social insurance subsidies, see Section 7, Entry 12 (employment-difficulty persons). For vocational training subsidies, see Section 7, Entry 13 (vocational training subsidies). The beneficiary of this entry is you yourself.

### 11. Individual income tax for persons with disabilities may be reduced — call the provincial tax bureau for how much
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. Check a box at filing, or submit documentation once.
- Plain-language summary: The Individual Income Tax Law states that income of persons with disabilities may be subject to reduced tax. But how much and for how long is set by the provincial government, so it varies by province. Some set an annual reduction limit, others reduce by percentage. The method is to call 12366 to ask the local tax bureau, or find the reduction items in the Individual Income Tax App.
- Benefit: Article 5 of the Individual Income Tax Law: "in any of the following circumstances, individual income tax may be reduced, with the specific amount and duration stipulated by the people's government of the province, autonomous region, or municipality directly under the central government, and filed with the standing committee of the people's congress at the same level: (1) income of persons with disabilities, elderly without support, and family members of martyrs; (2) those who have suffered significant losses due to natural disasters." Article 36 of the Law on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities additionally provides that the state grants tax preferences to persons with disabilities engaging in self-employment and exempts them from administrative and public-service fees (national, Individual Income Tax Law effective January 1, 2019).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Individual Income Tax Law of the People's Republic of China (2018 amendment, Article 5). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf724bd0609>; Law of the People's Republic of China on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities (Article 36). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff8080816f135f46016f1d134c88132b>
- Note: The benefit magnitude is rated "Small" because the law itself gives no figure; most provinces' reductions are on the order of several hundred to several thousand yuan per year. The amount is not large, but applying once works long-term — worth the trip. How much reduction applies in your province is determined only by the local tax bureau's announcement; comparison tables circulating online are perennially outdated. For those running their own small shop, administrative and public-service fees are exempted under Article 36 of the Law on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities — raise this when obtaining the license.

### 12. Guide dogs may be brought into public places and onto public transportation; the blind ride intra-city public transit for free
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. When stopped, you must speak up and cite the law, and may need to hold your ground for a while.
- Plain-language summary: The law specifies that public places and public transportation shall provide convenience to persons with disabilities bringing guide dogs, hearing dogs, or assistance dogs. The blind, with valid documentation, ride intra-city buses, subways, and ferries for free; persons with disabilities may carry their necessary assistive devices for free. When stopped, state these two provisions; if that does not work, file a complaint.
- Benefit: Article 46 of the Law on the Building of a Barrier-Free Environment: "operators and managers of public places, transportation facilities, and public transportation vehicles shall provide convenience to persons with disabilities carrying service dogs such as guide dogs, hearing dogs, and assistance dogs." Article 35 provides that government service convenience hotlines and emergency call systems such as police, fire emergency, traffic accident, and medical emergency shall gradually incorporate accessibility functions such as voice, large text, Braille, and one-touch calling. ### 15. Even without a right lower limb or both lower limbs, you can obtain a driver's license — the qualifying category is C5
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->

- Plain-language summary: Those missing a right lower limb or both lower limbs, or unable to move them, but who can sit upright independently and meet upper-limb requirements, may obtain a license for a disability-specific automatic small car, designated C5. ### 16. Rehabilitation is done at a legitimate institution with a rehabilitation medicine department; intensity follows the therapist — more is not better
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Inpatient rehabilitation costs several hundred yuan per day, with the out-of-pocket portion varying by region. Time is counted in months. Daily practice is required and very tedious.
- Plain-language summary: Bringing someone home to lie in bed and wait for natural recovery is the most common mistake. After a stroke, organized multidisciplinary team care results in more people alive and living at home one year later. But do not add intensity on your own — earlier, more frequent, and more intense exercise within 24 hours of stroke produced worse outcomes at three months. How much, and when — listen to the rehabilitation department and therapist.
- Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review included 29 trials, 5,902 participants, comparing organized inpatient stroke unit care with other care models. At final follow-up (median 1 year), the odds ratio for poor outcome (death, disability, or need for institutional care) was 0.77 (95% CI 0.69–0.87, confidence interval); for death, OR 0.76 (95% CI 0.66–0.88); for death or dependency, OR 0.75 (95% CI 0.66–0.85); evidence quality moderate. In absolute terms, per 100 people receiving stroke unit care, 2 more survived, 6 more lived at home, and 6 more were independent in daily living. On the other side, the AVERT trial randomized 2,104 patients across 56 acute stroke units in 5 countries, comparing "usual care" with "usual care plus very early high-intensity mobilization." At three months, the proportion with good outcome (modified Rankin Scale 0–2) was 480 (46%) in the very-early-mobilization group vs 525 (50%) in the usual-care group; adjusted OR 0.73 (95% CI 0.59–0.90, P=0.004). Death was 88 vs 72, with no significant difference (OR 1.34, 95% CI 0.93–1.93, P=0.113).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Langhorne P, Ramachandra S; Stroke Unit Trialists' Collaboration (2020). Organised inpatient (stroke unit) care for stroke: network meta-analysis. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 4:CD000197. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000197.pub4>; AVERT Trial Collaboration group (2015). Efficacy and safety of very early mobilisation within 24 h of stroke onset (AVERT): a randomised controlled trial. Lancet, 386(9988):46-55. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(15)60690-0>
- Note: Controversy: both bodies of evidence come from stroke; extending them to spinal cord injury, amputation, or burns is an extrapolation. AVERT shows that "earlier and more intense" is not supported — not that "early mobilization is useless." In the trial, the vast majority of both groups got out of bed within 24 hours; the difference was in frequency and total volume. When selecting an institution, check whether it has a rehabilitation medicine department and dedicated rehabilitation therapists and occupational therapists — do not just look at the word "rehabilitation" on the sign. For how to transfer between primary care and tertiary hospitals and how the deductible threshold is calculated, see Section 24, Entry 1 (tiered referral). For community rehabilitation and assistive device configuration, see Entry 7 of this section (ask at the county-level Disabled Persons' Federation for the full list).

### 17. If hearing declines, get a hearing aid — the reason to fit one is to hear, not to prevent dementia
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: Hearing aids cost several thousand to tens of thousands of yuan, mostly self-paid. Fitting and several return visits for adjustment are required.
- Plain-language summary: "Hearing aids prevent dementia" is not supported at present. A three-year trial of nearly a thousand people showed no overall difference. A difference was seen only in the subgroup whose baseline risk of cognitive decline was already elevated. So the reason to fit a hearing aid is to hear in the present, to converse with people, and to hear traffic when crossing the road — not for the brain decades from now.
- Benefit: The ACHIEVE trial was conducted in four US communities, enrolling 977 adults aged 70 to 84 with untreated hearing loss and no significant cognitive impairment. Randomized into two groups: one received a hearing intervention (hearing counseling and hearing aids), the other received health-education control, with follow-up every 6 months. The primary endpoint was change in global cognitive score over 3 years. In the primary analysis pooling two source populations, no significant difference was found: hearing intervention −0.200 (95% CI −0.256 to −0.144), control −0.202 (95% CI −0.258 to −0.145), difference 0.002 (95% CI −0.077 to 0.081), P=0.96. A prespecified sensitivity analysis showed that the intervention effect differed between the two source populations (interaction P=0.010). Participants from the long-running cardiovascular health observational study were older, had more cognitive decline risk factors, and lower baseline cognitive scores. The investigators' conclusion is that the hearing intervention may reduce 3-year cognitive change in populations at higher risk of cognitive decline, but not in those at lower risk. The trial reported no serious adverse events attributable to the study.
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Lin FR, Pike JR, Albert MS, et al.; ACHIEVE Collaborative Research Group (2023). Hearing intervention versus health education control to reduce cognitive decline in older adults with hearing loss in the USA (ACHIEVE): a multicentre, randomised controlled trial. Lancet, 402(10404):786-797. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(23)01406-X>
- Note: Controversy: the commonly circulated claim that "hearing aids reduced cognitive decline by nearly half" comes from a subgroup of this trial, not from its main result — the main result was no difference between groups. "Getting a hearing aid" and "preventing dementia" must be considered separately: the former should be done; the latter should not be the reason. For hearing-impaired candidates taking the college entrance exam, foreign-language listening can be exempted — see Entry 13 of this section. For those with hearing impairment who drive, hearing aids must be worn — see Entry 15 of this section (C5).

### 18. Declaring an adult "unable to manage their own affairs" requires a court application; the family's say does not suffice
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: A court application requires a filing fee, generally a few dozen to one hundred yuan. Assessment fees are separate, usually several thousand yuan, advanced by the applicant. The process takes months.
- Plain-language summary: Whether an adult is "without civil capacity" or "with limited civil capacity" can be determined only by a court. Family members, employers, and residents' committees cannot decide this themselves. The application must be filed with the court by an interested party or a relevant organization. Conversely, if intelligence or mental health recovers, an application may also be made to recognize restoration of capacity. This procedure is both a restriction and a protection.
- Benefit: Article 21 of the Civil Code: "an adult who cannot recognize their own actions is a person without civil capacity, and civil juridical acts are carried out on their behalf by their legal representative." Article 22: "an adult who cannot fully recognize their own actions is a person with limited civil capacity, and civil juridical acts are carried out on their behalf by their legal representative or with the legal representative's consent or ratification; however, civil juridical acts that purely confer benefits or are suited to their intellectual or mental health condition may be carried out independently." Article 24: "for an adult who cannot recognize or cannot fully recognize their own actions, interested parties or relevant organizations may apply to the people's court for a determination that the adult is without civil capacity or has limited civil capacity." The same article also provides that, after such a determination, upon application by the person, an interested party, or a relevant organization, the people's court may determine that the person has been restored to limited civil capacity or full civil capacity, based on the condition of their intellectual or mental health recovery. Relevant organizations include: residents' committees, villagers' committees, schools, medical institutions, women's federations, disabled persons' federations, lawfully established elderly persons' organizations, and civil affairs departments. Article 23 provides that the guardian of a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity is their legal representative (national, effective January 1, 2021).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (Articles 21, 22, 23, 24). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>
- Note: The application goes through the court's special procedure, faster than ordinary civil cases but still taking months. A hospital diagnosis certificate must be submitted, and the court usually also commissions a forensic assessment, costing several thousand yuan, advanced by the applicant. Signing on someone's behalf without this procedure, or disposing of their property, may later be declared invalid, and even sold houses may need to be returned. One more point: limited civil capacity does not mean nothing can be done. Acts suited to the person's intellectual and mental condition, and acts that purely confer benefits, can be done by the person themselves — do not handle everything on their behalf. ### 19. The guardian of an adult is determined by statutory order; if the ward harms another, the guardian pays compensation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free of charge. In disputes, resolution is through the residents' committee, villagers' committee, civil affairs department, or the court.
- Plain-language summary: When an adult loses capacity, the guardian is determined in order: spouse, parents and children, other close relatives, and finally other willing individuals or organizations. In a dispute, the residents' committee, villagers' committee, or civil affairs department designates; those who disagree may apply to the court. While still lucid, one may designate a future guardian in writing. If the ward harms another, the guardian pays compensation.
- Benefit: Article 28 of the Civil Code: for adults without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity, guardianship is held in order by persons with guardianship capacity: "(1) spouse; (2) parents, children; (3) other close relatives; (4) other individuals or organizations willing to serve as guardian, provided that the consent of the residents' committee, villagers' committee, or civil affairs department at the ward's place of residence is obtained." Article 31: where there is a dispute over the determination of the guardian, the residents' committee, villagers' committee, or civil affairs department at the ward's place of residence designates; those dissatisfied with the designation may apply to the people's court for designation, or may directly apply to the people's court. When designating, "the true wishes of the ward shall be respected, in accordance with the principle most beneficial to the ward." Where rights and interests are unprotected before designation, the residents' committee, villagers' committee, the relevant organization stipulated by law, or the civil affairs department serves as temporary guardian. Article 33: "an adult with full civil capacity may, after consultation with close relatives, other individuals willing to serve as guardian, or organizations, determine their own guardian in writing in advance." Article 1188: "where a person without civil capacity or with limited civil capacity causes harm to another, the guardian bears tort liability. Where the guardian has fulfilled guardianship duties, their tort liability may be reduced." If the ward has property, compensation is paid from the ward's own property, with any shortfall borne by the guardian. Article 1189: where the guardian delegates guardianship duties to another, the guardian still bears tort liability; the delegate bears corresponding liability only if at fault (national, effective January 1, 2021).
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Civil Code of the People's Republic of China (Articles 28, 31, 33, 1188, 1189). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808081729d1efe01729d50b5c500bf>
- Note: Article 33 is voluntary guardianship (意定监护). Selecting the person in writing while still lucid can eliminate most later disputes — for how to write it, see Section 17, Entry 1 (voluntary guardianship). The compensation paragraph warrants care: entrusting the person to a respite institution or a relative leaves liability with the guardian; the delegate bears corresponding liability only when at fault. So purchasing a guardian liability insurance policy for the ward is worthwhile. Article 78 of the Mental Health Law also provides that a guardian who abandons the patient or fails to perform guardianship duties, causing harm, bears compensation liability in accordance with the law.

### 20. If you are refused hiring, denied regularization, or underpaid because of disability, you can sue — but first calculate the process cost

- Benefit: Article 3 of the Law on the Protection of Persons with Disabilities: "discrimination on the basis of disability is prohibited. - Evidence grade: A
