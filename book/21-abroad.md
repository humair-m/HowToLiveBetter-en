# 21. Going Abroad, Travel, and Overseas Safety

This section accounts for money and personal freedom; a few entries also bear on personal safety. Once abroad, it is the law of the host country that governs you, not China's. The line of what Chinese embassies and consulates can do for you is clearly drawn.

### 1. Before departure, check the Ministry of Foreign Affairs safety advisory once; places marked "do not travel" are to be avoided
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Open the Chinese consular service website and skim through; it takes a few minutes
- Plain-language summary: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs divides overseas safety risks into four tiers; the highest is "do not travel." Starting September 15, 2026, avoiding high-risk countries and regions has been written into administrative regulation. When applying for documents and at exit border checks, staff will also remind you, and where necessary, advise you not to go. Before departure, skim that page; a few minutes' work
- Benefit: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs publishes overseas safety advisories by risk level. From low to high, roughly four tiers: "pay attention to safety," "strengthen safety precautions," "travel with caution," "do not travel." The new regulation effective September 15, 2026 writes the individual's obligation into administrative regulation. The original text reads: "Chinese citizens should pay attention to overseas safety advisories and safety risk alerts for tourist destinations, and avoid going to or staying in high-risk countries or regions." The new regulation also requires immigration authorities (the agencies that issue entry-exit documents and operate border checks) to remind, at document issuance and exit border inspection, those heading to high-risk regions. The original text: "For Chinese citizens preparing to travel to countries or regions at the highest risk level or with frequent outbreaks of cases that seriously endanger personal safety, advice against travel shall be given where necessary"
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2026). Provisions on Exit and Entry Administration (State Council Order No. 841). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm> (Article 2); State Council (2023). Regulation on Consular Protection and Assistance (State Council Order No. 763). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm> (Articles 19 and 21); Department of Consular Affairs, Ministry of Foreign Affairs. Safety Advisories. <https://cs.mfa.gov.cn/zggmzhw/lsbh/aqtx/>
- Note: Advisories change with conditions; this book does not reproduce the list — check that page yourself before traveling. As of September 7, 2026, the following "do not travel"-class advisories remained on the page: Eswatini (2026-08-25), Palestine (2023-10-10), Sudan (2023-04-17). Four more were Afghanistan (2023-02-17), Syria (2023-01-04), Peru (2022-12-29), Somalia (2022-11-24). Additional targeted advisories apply to parts of northern Myanmar and certain provinces of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Travel agencies also have a notification obligation. Before joining a tour group, you may require the agency to provide written information on destination risks

### 2. Save 12308 and the local embassy or consulate's consular protection number in your phone, and copy one onto paper in your wallet — do not wait until something happens to look for them
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Save two numbers and write one down; a few minutes
- Plain-language summary: There is one help number when something happens. Inside China, dial 12308; abroad, dial +86-10-12308. Since this hotline launched, it has received more than 3 million calls. Over the same period, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and overseas missions have handled more than 500,000 consular protection cases
- Benefit: The Ministry of Foreign Affairs has a global consular protection and service emergency hotline staffed 24 hours. Inside China, dial 12308. Abroad, dial +86-10-12308 or +86-10-65612308. Since this hotline launched in 2014, it has cumulatively received more than 3 million calls. Over the same period, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and overseas missions have handled more than 500,000 consular protection cases of various types
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Chinese Embassy and Consulates in Myanmar (2023). Consular Advisory on the Security Situation in Northern Myanmar. <https://cs.mfa.gov.cn/zggmzhw/lsbh/aqtx/202311/t20231109_11176682.shtml>; State Council Information Office (2023). State Council Regular Policy Briefing on Consular Protection and Assistance. <https://www.gov.cn/xinwen/2023zccfh/11/index.htm>
- Note: The local embassy or consulate also has its own consular protection number, often faster than the main switchboard. Before departure, look it up by country on the Chinese consular service website, and save it together with the main switchboard number in your phone. If your phone is lost or out of battery, the saved numbers are useless. So write one copy on paper in your wallet, or write the number down for a family member

### 3. Know what consular protection can and cannot do: it can visit, it cannot get you out; you still pay the costs
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Spend a few minutes reading this line clearly
- Plain-language summary: If you are detained abroad, the embassy or consulate can: verify the situation with local authorities, demand lawful and fair handling, visit you, and observe court hearings. It cannot overturn a verdict, nor walk you out. Food, lodging, transport, and medical costs advanced by others are ultimately yours to pay. So before going abroad, prepare your money and insurance; do not treat "if something happens, the state will cover it" as a plan
- Benefit: When you are detained, the embassy or consulate can do several things. Verify the situation with the relevant authorities of the host country, and demand lawful and fair handling. The regulation reads "conduct visits or maintain contact with [the citizen] in accordance with the laws of the receiving country and treaties concluded or jointly acceded to by China and the receiving country, learn their relevant needs, and require the relevant authorities of the receiving country to accord the Chinese citizen humanitarian and fair treatment." In plain language, this means: in accordance with local law and the treaties signed by both countries, visit you, contact you, ask what you need, and require the local side to treat you by humanitarian and fair standards. The embassy or consulate may attend court hearings and may demand that your litigation rights be protected. If you are injured or caught in a disaster, it can urge local emergency rescue and help family members handle aftermath. But on costs the regulation is equally clear: "Where Chinese citizens, legal persons, or non-legal-person organizations, in the course of consular protection and assistance, receive materials or services such as food, lodging, transport, or medical care provided by third parties, they shall pay the costs they themselves are obliged to bear." Food, lodging, transport, and medical care advanced by others — the part you owe is yours to pay
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2023). Regulation on Consular Protection and Assistance (State Council Order No. 763). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm> (Articles 9, 10, 14, and 26)
- Note: The embassy or consulate does not pay your fines, does not pay your medical bills, does not buy your tickets, and does not change the conclusion of the host country's court. What it can give you is information on local lawyers, translators, medical providers, and funeral services, and can help you contact relatives and friends and find relief channels. So before going abroad, prepare your own money and insurance; do not treat "if something happens, the state will cover it" as a plan

### 4. Buy insurance that includes overseas medical care and medical evacuation; do not buy only flight delay insurance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: A short-term overseas travel insurance policy, tens to a few hundred yuan
- Plain-language summary: Medical care abroad is paid out of your own pocket. Domestic medical insurance generally does not reimburse it, and the embassy or consulate does not advance it. What truly bankrupts a family is not the outpatient fee but hospitalization after serious injury, and the transport of the person back home, which requires charter aircraft or medical escort. When buying travel insurance, look at two coverage amounts: overseas medical expenses, and emergency medical evacuation and repatriation
- Benefit: Medical care abroad is paid out of pocket. Domestic medical insurance generally does not reimburse overseas care. Consular protection does not advance this money either (see Entry 3, what consular protection can and cannot do, based on Article 26 of the regulation). What truly bankrupts a family is not the outpatient fee but hospitalization after serious injury and medical evacuation. Transporting a person from an overseas hospital back home requires a chartered plane or medical escort — among the most expensive of self-paid items. When buying insurance, prioritize two coverage amounts. One is the overseas medical expense coverage. The other is the emergency medical evacuation and repatriation coverage, which governs the maximum payout for transferring hospitals and bringing the person home
- Evidence grade: C
- Source: State Council (2023). Regulation on Consular Protection and Assistance (State Council Order No. 763). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm> (Article 26); author's experience, no direct literature
- Note: TODO (to verify: typical cost ranges for overseas medical care and medical evacuation — official or industry statistics needed). Before buying, confirm three things. First, whether the country you are going to is within the coverage area. Second, whether high-risk activities such as diving, skiing, or riding motorcycles are excluded. Third, whether claims are settled directly between the insurer and the hospital, or whether you advance the money and seek reimbursement on return. Trips to visa-free countries also require insurance

### 5. Treat "overseas high-salary recruitment" as fraud; those deceived into doing telecom fraud may be barred from leaving the country on return
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is personally declining a job that looks very good
- Plain-language summary: Monthly salary far above the market, no education or experience required, no proper contract signed, asking you to gather in a border city first, and handling your visa and keeping your passport for you. Take this kind of job, and neither you nor your passport are in your own hands. With forged materials at exit, border inspection will stop you from leaving. Those who participated in illegal or criminal activity abroad may be barred from exiting China for 6 months to 3 years upon return
- Benefit: The official characterization of such cases is direct. In recent years, "those deceived into leaving the country, or leaving the country under fabricated pretexts to engage in cross-border gambling, telecom network fraud, and other illegal or criminal activity, seriously endangering the lives, property, and security of the people." This refers to being deceived into going out, or fabricating a reason to sneak out, to engage in gambling and telecom fraud. The new regulation tightens both ends on this point. On the exit side: "The reason for applying for exit, entry, stay, or residence shall be truthful and lawful." Those who submit false materials or make false statements, the immigration authorities may refuse to issue documents and refuse to let them exit. On the return side: "Chinese citizens who engage in illegal or criminal activity abroad that endangers national security and interests may... be barred from exiting for between 6 months and 3 years from the date of return." Those subject to administrative detention for fraudulent document acquisition or unlawful exit-entry are likewise barred from exiting for 6 months to 3 years
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2026). Provisions on Exit and Entry Administration (State Council Order No. 841). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm> (Articles 3 and 4); Ministry of Justice, Ministry of Public Security, and National Immigration Administration (2026). Q&A on the State Council's Provisions on Exit and Entry Administration. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202608/content_7077270.htm>
- Note: The identifying features are basically fixed: monthly salary far above comparable jobs, no education or experience required, no proper contract, asking you to gather in a border city first, applying for the visa on your behalf and keeping your passport. Northern Myanmar has a dedicated consular advisory. If you actually go, your personal freedom and your passport are no longer in your own hands; you cannot leave even if you want to

### 6. Overseas cash withdrawals may not exceed 100,000 yuan per year, calculated as the sum of all cards under your own name
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Before departure, estimate how much cash this trip will need; cover the rest by card. The hard part is changing the habit — use card where you can, do not withdraw cash
- Plain-language summary: The overseas cash withdrawal limit is not per card — it is the total of all bank cards under your name (including supplementary cards), at 100,000 yuan per calendar year. Once reached, you may not withdraw cash overseas for the rest of the year and the following year. Only cash withdrawals are suspended; card spending is unaffected, and does not consume your annual 50,000 USD foreign exchange purchase quota
- Benefit: The basis is the State Administration of Foreign Exchange's Notice on Regulating Large-Value Overseas Cash Withdrawal Transactions by Bank Cards (Hui Fa [2017] No. 29, effective January 1, 2018). Article 1: "Individuals holding domestic bank cards withdrawing cash overseas — the total of bank cards under their own name (including supplementary cards) may not exceed the equivalent of 100,000 yuan per calendar year. Those exceeding the annual quota will be suspended from withdrawing cash overseas with domestic bank cards in the current year and the following year." Same article, paragraph 2: "Individuals may not circumvent or assist in circumventing overseas cash withdrawal management by borrowing others' bank cards or lending their own." Article 2: SAFE sends the suspension list to issuing banks daily; issuing banks shall, no later than 17:00 Beijing time that day, suspend persons on the list from withdrawing cash overseas using that bank's cards. Article 3: Persons placed on the suspension list may bring valid identification to SAFE branches to check their overseas cash withdrawal details. Article 4: The per-card daily cap for foreign-currency cards is adjusted from the equivalent of 1,000 USD to the equivalent of 10,000 yuan; yuan-denominated cards remain at the equivalent of no more than 10,000 yuan per card per day. Article 6: Issuing banks, domestic yuan card clearing organizations, and individuals who violate this notice shall be penalized in accordance with the relevant provisions of the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations of the People's Republic of China. SAFE's Q&A also clarifies several points. The Notice does not affect individuals' card spending overseas. Food, lodging, transport, and shopping for tourism, business, and study may all be paid by bank card. These expenditures do not consume the individual's annual 50,000 USD foreign exchange purchase quota. The annual quota is calculated by SAFE aggregating data from issuing financial institutions; because real-time control would slow transaction response, "SAFE uses delayed control measures" (no real-time interception). Separately, statistics indicate that in 2016, 81% of overseas cash withdrawals by domestic bank cards were below the equivalent of 30,000 yuan (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (2017). Notice on Regulating Large-Value Overseas Cash Withdrawal Transactions by Bank Cards (Hui Fa [2017] No. 29, Articles 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 7, effective January 1, 2018). <https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/2017/1230/21873.html>; State Administration of Foreign Exchange (2017). Q&A by SAFE Officials on Regulating Large-Value Overseas Cash Withdrawal Transactions by Bank Cards. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2017-12/31/content_5251958.htm>; UnionPay International. Overseas ATM Withdrawals. <https://m.unionpayintl.com/wap/cn/serviceCenter/cardUsingInstructions/805.shtml>
- Note: The quota is calculated per calendar year; January 1 begins a new accumulation. Only overseas cash withdrawals are suspended; card spending is unaffected. If you genuinely need to use large amounts of cash overseas, first purchase foreign exchange under the Individual Foreign Exchange Administration Measures, then carry foreign currency notes out of the country. There are two pitfalls with cash withdrawals themselves, explained on the UnionPay International "Overseas ATM Withdrawals" page. First, dual-logo cards cannot select the UnionPay channel for cash withdrawal at some ATMs in some countries. Second, some overseas ATMs display a prompt for an additional fee (surcharge) on the screen, which is collected by the local acquirer and "is unrelated to UnionPay." The same page also notes that "because credit card withdrawals incur overdraft interest, cardholders are advised to use UnionPay debit cards for overseas withdrawals." For lost cards, retained cards, or stolen-card fraud, see Section 14, Entry 5 (on stolen card fraud, first freeze and report, then call police)

### 7. Photograph passport, visa, and ID card and store a copy in the cloud; if lost, first call the police, then apply for a travel document
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Take a few photos before departure
- Plain-language summary: If your passport is lost, the embassy or consulate can issue a travel document for return. The premise is that you can prove who you are. Having photos of the data page, visa page, and entry-exit records speeds up the replacement greatly. You must also first report to local police and get a receipt — exit inspection and insurance claims both recognize this paper. Phones are often lost together with passports; do not store photos only on the phone
- Benefit: If your passport is lost, the overseas embassy or consulate can issue a travel document for return, but you must first prove your identity. Having clear photos of the passport data page, visa page, and entry-exit records makes a large difference in how fast replacement happens. At the same time, you must first report to local police and obtain a report receipt. Exit inspection and insurance claims in many countries require this material. The entry point for finding the embassy or consulate is the two consular protection numbers saved in Entry 2
- Evidence grade: C
- Source: State Council (2023). Regulation on Consular Protection and Assistance (State Council Order No. 763). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm> (Article 16); author's experience, no direct literature
- Note: The method is to take the photos and store them in two different places — for example, one copy in a cloud drive, one copy sent to a family member. Also print a paper copy and keep it separately from the original. Bank cards and prescriptions for regular medications should also be photographed. Phones and passports are often lost together; storing photos only on the phone is the same as not storing them at all

### 8. Before driving abroad, confirm whether the destination recognizes the Chinese driver's license; most "international driving permits" sold online are worthless paper
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Before departure, check the destination's rules once
- Plain-language summary: The "international driving permit" handled by online agents does not exist under Chinese mainland law. That document is a translation recognized between parties to the 1949 Geneva Convention; mainland China is not among the 103 parties. So no matter who handles it for you domestically, it has no legal force. Whether you can drive with a Chinese driver's license depends on the destination's own rules — if not clearly confirmed, do not drive
- Benefit: The so-called "international driving permit" (IDP) is a translation recognized between parties to the 1949 Convention on Road Traffic. This Convention was concluded in Geneva on September 19, 1949, and entered into force on March 26, 1952. There are currently 103 parties (signatory countries and regions). Mainland China is not on this list. So China does not issue such international driving permits. Any agency "handling an international driving permit" domestically produces no legal effect. Whether you can drive with a Chinese driver's license depends on the rules of the destination country or region itself: some recognize a Chinese driver's license with a notarized translation, some require exchange for a local license, and some do not recognize it at all
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: United Nations Treaty Collection. Convention on Road Traffic, Geneva, 19 September 1949 — status of treaties. <https://treaties.un.org/pages/ViewDetailsV.aspx?src=TREATY&mtdsg_no=XI-B-1&chapter=11&clang=_en>
- Note: In most countries, driving without a license is a criminal offense, or carries a heavy penalty. And in the event of an accident, insurance will most likely refuse to pay — that is the true cost. Two ways to check: on the Chinese consular service website, find the destination country's "Notes for Chinese Citizens"; or have the rental company provide written confirmation. Hong Kong, Macao, and Taiwan regions, and some countries, have separate arrangements

### 9. When using an agency for visa, study abroad, or immigration, first ask whether it is registered with the immigration authority
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Ask one more question about whether it is registered, and keep the written materials
- Plain-language summary: From September 15, 2026, agencies handling visa, study abroad, and immigration must register with the immigration authority. Phrases like "guaranteed pass," "guaranteed visa," and "we'll prepare the materials for you" are themselves among the practices expressly prohibited. If you fill out a form incorrectly yourself, the worst case is rejection this time. If you use false materials and are found to have engaged in fabrication, the blemish remains in your future entry-exit records
- Benefit: In 2018, the admission approval for private entry-exit intermediary agencies was abolished (opening an agency no longer required prior approval). The number of agencies grew rapidly, and "the base of agencies is unclear, some agencies conduct business in violation of laws and regulations, harming the lawful rights and interests of those exiting and entering" — the official count of how many there were unclear. From September 15, 2026, such agencies switched to a registration system. An agency must, within 15 days from the date of establishment, register with the local immigration authority. Those already operating must complete registration within 90 days of the effective date. The expressly prohibited behaviors are these. First, "publishing false information, or soliciting clients through exaggerated or misleading promotion." Second, "providing or assisting in providing false materials, assisting others to illegally obtain visas, stay or residence permits, passports, or other entry-exit documents or procedures." Third, "leaking, selling, or unlawfully providing... personal privacy or personal information." Fourth, "organizing or assisting others in cross-border illegal or criminal activity." Overseas enterprises and institutions may not provide such intermediary services within China
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2026). Provisions on Exit and Entry Administration (State Council Order No. 841). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm> (Articles 7, 8, 10, 12, and 13)
- Note: Agencies that bandy about "guaranteed pass," "guaranteed visa," and "we'll prepare the materials for you" are doing what is expressly prohibited. Once discovered, the blemish is on your visa record. Problems with self-filled applications result at most in rejection this time. Use of false materials found to be fabrication carries much heavier consequences. Policy consultation and information queries that do not seek profit are not intermediary services

### 10. Do not issue invitation letters for strangers, and do not let others use your name to handle entry-exit procedures
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is declining a personal favor on the spot
- Plain-language summary: Issuing an invitation letter or application materials for someone else with false content — the issuer is penalized. Individuals are fined 5,000 to 10,000 yuan, with illegal gains confiscated. Units are fined 10,000 to 50,000 yuan, and the person in charge and the person handling it are each additionally fined 5,000 to 10,000 yuan. One favor exchanged for paying this money yourself
- Benefit: The person issuing the invitation letter is responsible for whether the content is true. The original text: "Where an individual issues a false invitation letter or other application materials for another's application for exit, entry, stay, or residence, the immigration authority shall impose a fine of 5,000 yuan or more and 10,000 yuan or less; where there are illegal gains, the illegal gains shall be confiscated." Where a unit issues false materials, the fine is 10,000 yuan or more and 50,000 yuan or less. At the same time, the directly responsible person in charge and other directly responsible persons (the managing leadership and the handler) are each additionally fined 5,000 yuan or more and 10,000 yuan or less
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2026). Provisions on Exit and Entry Administration (State Council Order No. 841). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202607/content_7077173.htm> (Articles 3 and 11)
- Note: This is the same category of risk as Section 8's "do not carry things for strangers": if the materials are issued under your name, the consequences fall on you. Public officials and military personnel who seek to obtain foreign nationality or overseas residence status through an intermediary in violation of regulations — the intermediary may not only refuse to handle it, but must also report to the supervisory organ

### 11. Before departure, send your itinerary, lodging, and travel companions to a family member, and agree on how often to be in contact
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- Cost: No money. Send one message before departure
- Plain-language summary: If you lose contact while abroad, family members can seek help from the embassy or consulate; the embassy or consulate will tell the family how to report to local police, and after a case is filed will urge follow-up. The premise is that someone at home knows where you should be and when you should be in contact. Send the flight number, lodging address, travel companions' phone numbers, and the agreed contact frequency to one family member — that is enough
- Benefit: When a Chinese citizen is missing overseas, relatives may seek help from overseas diplomatic missions. The regulation states "overseas diplomatic missions shall provide information on how to report to local police and other means of obtaining relief." After local police file a case, the embassy or consulate will urge timely handling. Whether these reliefs can be activated depends on whether someone at home knows where you should be and when you should be in contact
- Evidence grade: C
- Source: State Council (2023). Regulation on Consular Protection and Assistance (State Council Order No. 763). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202307/content_6891761.htm> (Article 13); author's experience, no direct literature
- Note: Four items are enough: flight number, lodging address, travel companions' names and phone numbers, and the agreed contact frequency. If you are heading to a place with poor signal or poor security, also make clear "how long without news before calling the police." The relief procedures above all require that a family member knows your itinerary in order to begin
