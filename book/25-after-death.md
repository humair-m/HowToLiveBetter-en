# 25. What to Do After Someone Passes Away

Metric: Money. This section covers only how to handle procedures and how to obtain money — including what to do first when someone has just passed away, and which fees have a list you can check against. It also covers which accounts' money can be withdrawn, and which matters close relatives have the right to handle. The first five entries are arranged in the order in which events occur: the scene, the death certificate, the body and cremation, what to do when disagreeing on the cause of death, and hukou cancellation. The subsequent entries are arranged by cost-effectiveness. Arrangements the elderly should make before death — such as voluntary guardianship, wills, and accounts — see Section 17. The three benefits for work-related death, see Section 19.

The funeral and interment portion is based on the new Funeral and Interment Administration Regulation, effective March 30, 2026. How much the pension's funeral subsidy and survivor pension amount to is separately regulated by the human resources and social security department. We were unable to obtain a verbatim original text this time, so the body does not state specific amounts.

### 1. If the person passes at home or elsewhere, first determine whether it is a natural death or an unnatural death: accident, unknown cause, or discovered long after living alone — call the police immediately; the body and scene must not be moved
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. One phone call suffices
- Plain-language summary: For accidental death, unknown cause, or discovered long after living alone, only the police may issue the death certificate. If the scene has been moved, the situation cannot be clarified and the certificate is hard to issue. Without this paper, subsequent cremation, hukou cancellation, and insurance claims all stall. If you cannot tell which kind, call 110 first. The police will issue the certificate after they arrive and investigate
- Benefit: Household Registration Regulation, Article 8, paragraph 2: "Where a citizen dies due to an accident or the cause is unknown, the head of household or the discoverer shall immediately report to the local police station or the township or town people's committee." Meaning: for an accident, or where it is not known how the person died, the head of household and the discoverer must immediately report to the police station or the township/town government. Funeral and Interment Administration Regulation, Article 23: "Where a natural person dies a natural death, the medical health institution shall promptly issue the death certificate. For the deceased in unnatural-death cases (incidents) that involve handling by the public security organ in accordance with the law and have not received treatment by a medical health institution, the public security organ issues the death certificate." Meaning: for a natural death from illness, the medical institution that treated the deceased issues the death certificate. For those not treated by a hospital and handled by public security, the police issue it. The death certificate for an unnatural death can only be issued by the police. If the scene has been moved, the situation cannot be clarified and the certificate is hard to issue. Subsequent cremation, hukou cancellation, and insurance claims all stall on this single piece of paper
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (1958). Household Registration Regulation of the People's Republic of China (Article 8). <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html> (published on the Guangdong Provincial People's Government portal); State Council (2026). Funeral and Interment Administration Regulation (State Council Order No. 824, effective March 30, 2026, Article 23). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Note: For someone bedridden for a long time or in their final days at home, this counts as a natural death. You can obtain the certificate from the institution that treated them during life, or from the community health service center or township health center of the area where they lived. The death certificate is issued by whoever treated them; see Entry 2 (death certificate). If you cannot tell which kind, treat it as unnatural — call 110 first; the police will issue the certificate after on-site investigation. If the scene is tidied before calling the police, no one can help you. Do not move the body yourself, do not bathe or change clothes, and do not remove medicine bottles or notes

### 2. The death certificate is the key to everything that follows: issued by whoever treated them; for natural death at home, go to the community health service institution; issued within one day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money
- Plain-language summary: For a death in a hospital, the institution that treated them issues the death certificate. For a natural death at home, the community health service institution or township health center of the area where they lived issues it. Where the cause of death is clear, it is issued within one day. Hukou cancellation, cremation, withdrawal of housing fund, claiming social insurance benefits, insurance claims, and inheritance — every one of these requires it. Make several copies upon receipt
- Benefit: The Specification for the Registration and Administration of Population Death Information (Trial) states: where death occurs in a medical health institution, it is "issued by the medical health institution responsible for treatment"; for deaths in a hospital or health center, the institution responsible for treatment issues it. For natural death at home or in another setting, "the community health service institution or township (subdistrict) health center of the local area issues it" — go to the community health service center or the township or subdistrict health center of the area where they lived. For deaths determined by public security or judicial authorities to be unnatural, the public security or judicial department handles it in accordance with current provisions and procedures. A 2026 notice by five ministries states: the Death Certificate's "paper version and electronic license have the same legal effect" — the electronic version is as valid as the paper one. For natural death where the cause has been determined, issuance is within one day of death. If the certificate is lost, one reissue may be applied for from the original issuing institution. If basic information is incorrect, one re-issuance may be applied for with supporting materials
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: General Office of the National Health and Family Planning Commission (2014). Specification for the Registration and Administration of Population Death Information (Trial) (Guo Wei Ban Gui Hua Fa [2014] No. 68, Article 9). <https://mzj.sz.gov.cn/szmz/pc/zwgk/jcxxgk/zcfg/byfw/content/post_2952215.html> (reproduced by the Shenzhen Municipal Civil Affairs Bureau); National Health Commission, Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of Civil Affairs, National Administration of Traditional Chinese Medicine, and National Disease Control Bureau (2026). Notice on Strengthening the Registration and Electronic License Management of Resident Death Medical Certificate Information (Guo Wei Gui Hua Fa [2026] No. 5). <https://wjw.fujian.gov.cn/xxgk/fgwj/gjwj/202602/t20260228_7102810.htm> (reproduced by the Fujian Provincial Health Commission)
- Note: Hukou cancellation, cremation, withdrawal of housing fund, claiming social insurance benefits, insurance claims, and inheritance — every one of these requires it. Make several copies upon receipt, and apply for the electronic license where available. Reissue is a one-time opportunity; do not lose the original. If the name or ID number is wrong, have it corrected on the spot — discovering this at inheritance time means running every window again

### 3. Body transport, storage, and cremation must go through a funeral home; hospital morgues do not handle funerals; storage is generally no more than 3 days
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money
- Plain-language summary: Transport, storage, preservation, restoration, and cremation can only be done by a funeral home. Hospital morgues do not handle funeral affairs; at most they hold the body temporarily. Funeral homes generally store bodies for no more than 3 days; longer storage requires an extension. Cremation requires the death certificate and the cremation confirmation. The cremation certificate issued after cremation is also needed for inheritance and insurance claims
- Benefit: Funeral and Interment Administration Regulation, Article 25: "Body transport, storage, preservation, restoration, and cremation services are exclusively provided by funeral homes." These tasks cannot be done by others. Article 24: "The morgue of a medical health institution may not be contracted out and may not conduct funeral services; it may provide convenience services for the temporary parking of bodies." Hospital morgues may only temporarily hold bodies, not handle funeral affairs. Article 27: "Funeral homes generally store bodies for no more than 3 days; where extension is needed, the bereaved or the body transfer party shall go through the extension storage procedures at the funeral home." For longer storage, go to the funeral home for an extension. Article 28: "Funeral homes shall cremate bodies on the basis of the death certificate and the cremation confirmation, and shall issue a cremation certificate." Both materials are required; after cremation, you receive a cremation certificate. Article 29: "In areas where cremation is practiced, bodies shall be cremated locally and nearby at a funeral home" — bodies are to be cremated at a funeral home near the place of death
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2026). Funeral and Interment Administration Regulation (State Council Order No. 824, passed by the 72nd executive meeting of the State Council on November 14, 2025, "effective March 30, 2026," Articles 8, 9, 24, 25, 27, 28, and 29). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm>
- Note: The "all-in-one" services that approach you after a hospital death are mostly not from a funeral home. Transport itself is a basic item with a set price; see Entry 6 (basic items list). Keep the cremation certificate safe — it is needed for inheritance, insurance, and transferring ashes. Whether the locality practices cremation or burial is delineated by the provincial government. Minority funeral customs are protected by Article 9 of the regulation. Where cremation should be practiced but burial is done, or where burial or grave construction is done outside public cemeteries and ecological burial areas, the civil affairs department orders correction within a time limit (i.e., rectified back)

### 4. If you disagree with the cause of death, request an autopsy within 48 hours, and simultaneously seal the medical record
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is remembering to ask at that moment
- Plain-language summary: If you have doubts about the cause of death, request an autopsy within 48 hours of death. Where the hospital has frozen storage, this may extend to 7 days. The autopsy also requires the consent and signature of close relatives. Without signature, or if the time passes, responsibility for the inability to determine the cause falls on the delaying party. When requesting the autopsy, also request that the medical record be sealed. Once the body is cremated, an autopsy is no longer possible
- Benefit: The time limit for autopsy is in Article 26 of the Regulation on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes. The original text: "Where the patient dies and the medical staff and patient sides disagree on the cause of death, an autopsy shall be conducted within 48 hours of the patient's death; where frozen storage of the body is available, this may be extended to 7 days." So to determine the cause of death, an autopsy must be conducted within 48 hours of death; where the hospital has frozen storage, it may be extended to up to 7 days. The same article also states: "An autopsy shall be subject to the consent and signature of the deceased's close relatives; refusal to sign shall be regarded as the deceased's close relatives not consenting to an autopsy." If the family does not sign, this is regarded as not consenting. It also states: "Where disagreement or delay in autopsy exceeds the prescribed time and affects the determination of the cause of death, the party that disagreed or delayed bears responsibility." Whoever disagreed or delayed past the time bears responsibility for the inability to determine the cause of death. Article 24: Sealing and unsealing of medical record materials "shall be conducted in the presence of both medical staff and patient." Both you and the hospital must be present when the medical record is sealed and unsealed
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2018). Regulation on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes (State Council Order No. 701, Articles 24 and 26). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm>
- Note: Raising "I had doubts back then" past this time limit shifts responsibility back onto the delaying party. Once the body is cremated, autopsy is no longer possible, so this matter must be settled before contacting the funeral home. How to seal the medical record, see Section 24

### 5. Hukou cancellation has a statutory deadline: urban before interment, rural within one month
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- Cost: No money. Bring the death certificate and hukou booklet, and make one trip to the police station
- Plain-language summary: Urban areas must cancel hukou before interment; rural areas within one month. Bring the death certificate and hukou booklet to the police station. Delay blocks inheritance transfer, claiming benefits, and insurance surrender — all of these depend on hukou status
- Benefit: Household Registration Regulation, Article 8, paragraph 1: "Where a citizen dies, urban areas before interment, rural areas within one month, the head of household, relatives, supporter, or neighbor shall report the death registration to the household registration authority and cancel the hukou. Where a citizen dies in a temporary residence, the household registration authority of the temporary residence notifies the household registration authority of the permanent residence to cancel the hukou." Meaning: in urban areas, before interment; in rural areas, within one month. The head of household, relatives, supporter, or neighbor may report the death and cancel the hukou at the household registration authority (police station). Where the person died while in a temporary residence, the police station of the temporary residence notifies the police station of the permanent residence to cancel the hukou
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (1958). Household Registration Regulation of the People's Republic of China (Article 8). <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html> (published on the Guangdong Provincial People's Government portal)
- Note: If the hukou remains outstanding, inheritance transfer, claiming benefits, and insurance surrender all stall — these windows all depend on hukou status. If pension continues to be paid, that is fraudulent collection, and sooner or later must be returned. When you visit the police station, also ask clearly how social insurance and medical insurance benefits are stopped, and how the funeral subsidy is claimed. Those sums, see Entry 9 (housing fund balance, social insurance benefits)

### 6. Funeral services are divided into basic items and non-basic items; basic items have a list, and prices are set according to law
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Spend a few minutes looking at the list before departure
- Plain-language summary: Body transport, storage, farewell, cremation, ashes storage, and ecological burial are all on the national basic items list. Ashes compartment burial provided by government-run institutions is also on the list. The prices of these items are set according to regulation. Outside the list, no other item may be established for charging. Before departure, ask clearly which items are basic and what the local standard is, then discuss anything else
- Benefit: Body transport, body storage, body farewell, body cremation, ashes storage, and ecological burial are all on the national basic items list. Ashes compartment burial provided by government-run funeral service institutions is also on the list. The fee standards for these items are set according to law — prices are set by regulation, not by the funeral home itself. The non-basic items list is determined by the civil affairs department at or above the prefecture level (the civil affairs department of a prefecture-level city or higher)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2026). Funeral and Interment Administration Regulation (State Council Order No. 824, passed by the 72nd executive meeting of the State Council on November 14, 2025, "effective March 30, 2026"). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> Article 5: "Funeral services are divided into basic items and non-basic items; the fee policy is formulated by the development and reform department, finance department of the State Council together with the civil affairs and other departments of the State Council." "The state formulates a list of basic items for funeral services, including body transport, body storage, body farewell, body cremation, ashes storage, ecological burial, and ashes compartment burial provided by government-run funeral service institutions, within the list scope"; Article 50: "Funeral services are subject to list-based management and are dynamically adjusted; the establishment of items outside the list and the charging of fees are prohibited."
- Note: Provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the central government may add items to the national list. First ask clearly which items are basic and what the local fee standard is, then discuss the rest. Items on the list have prices set by regulation; items off the list are optional — distinguishing the two gives you grounds to dispute any overcharge. Wanting to arrange a relative's affairs with dignity is not wrong; we do not urge simplicity — where the money goes is your own decision

### 7. If you encounter forced bundling, charges above the marked price, or splitting one item into several fees, record it and complain
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. The hard part is being willing to insist at that moment
- Plain-language summary: These are all price violations named in the regulation: not posting a price list, charging above the marked price, forcing the sale of funeral goods by bundling, and forcing you to accept a third party's paid service. Splitting one item into several fees is also disguised price inflation. On the spot, first request a price list, and keep charge receipts and chat records. Complaining afterward is still in time. Price violations are handled by market regulation; funeral services are handled by civil affairs
- Benefit: The regulation lists five categories of price violations; four are described below. Not posting a price list, or charging more than the marked price, constitutes failure to mark prices openly or charging above the marked price. Refusing to handle one thing unless another is bought constitutes bundling or attaching unreasonable conditions for forced sale. Forcing you to accept another party's paid service constitutes forced or disguised forced acceptance of a third party's paid service. Charging more under a different name constitutes disguised raising of the fee standard. Also prohibited is "splitting items or expanding the scope of charges in disguised forms of raising the fee standard." Splitting one service into several fees, or expanding the scope of charges outward, is prohibited
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2026). Funeral and Interment Administration Regulation (State Council Order No. 824, passed by the 72nd executive meeting of the State Council on November 14, 2025, "effective March 30, 2026"). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> Article 42: "Funeral service institutions may not, in violation of relevant provisions, arbitrarily establish charge items, or raise fee standards in disguised form by splitting items, expanding the scope of charges, or the like." Article 43 lists price violations including "(1) failing to mark prices openly in accordance with regulations, or charging above the marked price or charging fees not indicated" "(3) using bundling, attaching unreasonable conditions or unreasonable restrictions, or the like to force or disguised-force the sale of funeral goods, the provision of funeral-related services, and the charging of fees" "(4) forcing or disguised-forcing the bereaved to accept third-party paid services, or using relevant service outlets to repeatedly charge fees"; Article 51 requires the civil affairs department to establish an information-sharing supervision mechanism with the government service convenience hotline, "issues related to funeral services raised by the bereaved shall be handled in a timely manner together with the relevant departments, and the handling results shall be communicated to the bereaved"
- Note: Investigation of price violations falls to the market regulation department; funeral services themselves fall to the civil affairs department. On the spot, first request a price list, keep charge receipts and chat records, and complain afterward — still in time

### 8. Funeral agents, goods purchasing agents, planning and hosting — such intermediaries must register at the county-level civil affairs department
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money. Ask one question about whether they are registered
- Plain-language summary: Funeral agents, goods purchasing agents, planning and hosting, and information consulting intermediaries must register at the county-level civil affairs department. Those not registered are not in the civil affairs department's funeral service information system. Whether you can check this yourself varies by locality; the most reliable approach is to call the county-level civil affairs department to ask. Those who use deception to charge irregular fees or sell the deceased's information will be handled in accordance with the law
- Benefit: Unregistered intermediaries are not in the funeral service information system managed by the civil affairs department. Those who use deception to charge irregular fees, induce excessive funeral arrangements, force acceptance of services, or sell the deceased's information will be handled in accordance with the law, and will also be marked in the system and made public, so anyone can see
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2026). Funeral and Interment Administration Regulation (State Council Order No. 824, passed by the 72nd executive meeting of the State Council on November 14, 2025, "effective March 30, 2026"). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> Article 41: "Other than funeral service institutions, organizations and individuals engaged in funeral-related service activities such as funeral service agency, goods purchasing agency, planning and hosting, and information consulting shall register with the civil affairs department of the county-level people's government." "Those who harm the rights and interests of the bereaved through deception to charge irregular fees, induce excessive funeral arrangements, force acceptance of services, sell the deceased's information, or the like shall be handled in accordance with the law, and shall be marked in the funeral service information system and made public to society."
- Note: "Selling the deceased's information" is the most common pitfall in this trade. Receiving a pile of marketing calls right after completing procedures usually means the information has been resold. Online memorial platforms are likewise regulated. Article 45 of the regulation prohibits them from inducing consumption and from unlawfully processing the deceased's information

### 9. Money scattered across various places must be claimed one by one: housing fund balance, social insurance benefits, workplace injury benefits
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: No money, but several windows must be visited. Before going, prepare the death certificate and proof of kinship
- Plain-language summary: The remaining balance in their housing fund account may be withdrawn by heirs and legatees. This money is not in a bank account and does not automatically enter estate distribution — it is the most easily missed. For work-related death, there are three additional sums: funeral subsidy, dependent relative pension, and one-time work-death benefit. The balance of the pension individual account and survivor benefits — ask at the social insurance handling agency at the place of participation
- Benefit: The housing fund account balance (the remaining money in their housing fund account) may be withdrawn by heirs and legatees. A legatee is the person named in the will to receive property. For work-related death, there are three additional sums: funeral subsidy, dependent relative pension, and one-time work-death benefit
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2026). Housing Fund Administration Regulation (State Council Order No. 844 as amended, effective September 20, 2026) Article 24, last paragraph. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2026/issue_12946/202608/content_7079363.html>: "Where an employee dies or is declared dead, the employee's heirs or legatees may withdraw the storage balance in the employee's housing fund account; where there are no heirs and no legatees, the storage balance in the employee's housing fund account is included in the appreciation income of the housing fund."; the three benefits under Article 39 of the Workplace Injury Insurance Regulation, see Section 19. <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1778064.htm>; TODO (to verify: the inheritance rules for the individual account balance of basic pension insurance, and the specific standards of the funeral subsidy and survivor pension — the relevant MOHRSS documents could not be obtained this time)
- Note: The housing fund sum is the most often forgotten, because it is not in a bank account and does not automatically enter estate distribution. The balance remaining in the pension individual account and the survivor benefits paid to the family — ask at the social insurance handling window at the place of participation. The materials required vary by locality. For the form requirements of wills and inheritance, see Section 17

### 10. A deceased's accounts and personal information may be reviewed, copied, corrected, or deleted by close relatives
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: No money. Only where the platform refuses do you need to complain or sue
- Plain-language summary: After someone passes away, close relatives may, for their own lawful and legitimate interests, request that the platform review, copy, correct, or delete the deceased's personal information. If the platform refuses, it must state the reason. You may also sue in court. One exception: where the deceased made other arrangements during life — for example, setting an account heir or leaving clear wishes — those arrangements prevail
- Benefit: The law states plainly: where a natural person dies, close relatives may, for their own lawful and legitimate interests, exercise rights to review, copy, correct, or delete the deceased's relevant personal information. Close relatives may request that the platform produce the deceased's information for review, copy it, correct errors, or delete it
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2021). Personal Information Protection Law. China NPC Network. <http://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202108/t20210820_313088.html> Article 49: "Where a natural person dies, their close relatives may, for their own lawful and legitimate interests, exercise the rights to review, copy, correct, delete, and the like, as provided in this Chapter, in relation to the deceased's relevant personal information; where the deceased made other arrangements during life, those arrangements prevail."; Article 50: "Personal information handlers shall establish a convenient application acceptance and handling mechanism for individuals to exercise their rights. Where a request by an individual to exercise rights is refused, the reason shall be stated" "An individual may bring a lawsuit in a people's court in accordance with the law"
- Note: The statute includes "where the deceased made other arrangements during life, those arrangements prevail" — where an account heir was set during life, or clear wishes were left, follow those arrangements. So while the person is still lucid, it is worth writing down the disposition of accounts and passwords; see Section 17. Canceling an account and deleting personal information are two different things; see Section 14
