# 8. Don't Get Yourself In: Legal and Property Safety

This section counts money and personal freedom, not converted to lifespan. It covers three things: what responsibilities the law imposes on you, how much less you pay if you follow the rules, and what procedural rights you have when something happens. Article numbers have all been verified against official sources. The ordering: matters where a single incident causes the largest loss and that are easiest to do come first; matters requiring advance arrangement come in the middle; those backed only by experience and without official documentation come last.

### 1. In a traffic accident, first stop, save people, call police — don't flee
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Stop, turn on hazard lights, save people, call police; waiting for traffic police takes at most one or two hours. For minor accidents with no injuries and no dispute over the facts, take photos first. Then record the other party's name, phone, license plate, driver's license number, insurance certificate number, and the parts that were hit. After both sides sign, you can leave the scene first and negotiate afterward.
- Plain-language summary: Flee, and this accident is by default your full liability. Driver's license revoked, and you can never test for one again for the rest of your life. There's also a more practical layer: vehicle damage insurance and third-party liability insurance don't pay for hit-and-run, so hundreds of thousands in compensation all come out of your own pocket. Staying at the scene, saving people, and calling police takes at most one or two hours.
- Benefit: The party that flees bears full liability for this accident. Only if you can prove the other party was also at fault can liability possibly be reduced. Fleeing also revokes your driver's license, and you can never test for one again for life. If the accident constitutes the crime of traffic casualty, fleeing gets 3 to 7 years. If fleeing leads to death, 7 years or more. Insurance doesn't cover it either: in commercial auto insurance, vehicle damage insurance and third-party liability insurance, hit-and-run, intentional destruction or falsification of the scene, or destruction of evidence are not paid out across the board — hundreds of thousands in compensation is entirely on you (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A

- Note: If someone is injured, save them first, then call police. If you must move the scene to save them, first mark the original positions of vehicles and people. "Bumped a bit, didn't feel anything, drove off" can also be counted as hit-and-run afterward; if unsure, call police. ### 2. If you discover you've been defrauded, immediately call 110 or 96110 to request a stop-payment; don't investigate yourself first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Make one phone call, stating clearly the time, amount, and recipient account of the transfer. Keep transfer screenshots and chat logs.
- Plain-language summary: The brief window right after money is transferred out is the only one. During this window, police can do emergency stop-payment and rapid freezing; banks must cooperate. Once the money is layered through transfers and dispersed, recovery is essentially impossible. So call 110 or 96110 first; don't investigate or ask around first. When 96110 calls in, it's the police trying to dissuade you — pick up.
- Benefit: Public security organs have the authority to make real-time inquiries, emergency stop-payments, and rapid freezes on funds involved in a case. Banks and payment institutions must cooperate. Frozen funds are returned to victims per procedure. Once money is transferred and dispersed, the probability of recovery drops sharply, so the earlier the stop-payment, the more effective. 96110 is the national unified anti-fraud warning hotline; the incoming call is police dissuading you — be sure to pick up (nationwide, since December 2022)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2022). Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law (Articles 20, 34). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>; Fujian Provincial Public Security Department (2023). 96110 is calling, please be sure to answer. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm> (provincial-level public security page)
- Note: Anyone claiming to be police, procuratorate, or court and asking you to transfer money to a "safe account" is running a scam. The law only says "establish a stop-payment and freeze system," meaning the bank is to hold the money. How much can be recovered depends on whether the money was still in the account when you called police. The "National Anti-Fraud Center" App and 96110 introduction are quoted from the Fujian provincial public security page; the Ministry of Public Security's original page does not open

### 3. Remember the anti-fraud hard rules: don't trust callers, don't disclose information, don't click links, verify before transferring; the seven most common scams all share this shape
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Memorize the "three no's and one more": no trust, no disclosure, no clicking, more verification. Read through the seven categories below, install the "National Anti-Fraud Center" App, and answer 96110 calls without fail. Anyone asking you to transfer money, share your screen, download an unfamiliar App, click links, or give out verification codes — hang up first, then verify by calling back the official number. The hard part is the caller often pushes urgently; you must withstand this push and hang up.
- Plain-language summary: Anyone asking you to transfer money, share your screen, download an unfamiliar App, click links, or give out verification codes — hang up first, then call back via the official number to verify. Task-rebate scams, fake investment schemes, online loans, impersonating customer service, impersonating police/procuratorate/court, pig-butchering scams, and sex-lure task scams are the seven most common types — all require you to send money first. Recognizing them beforehand is far cheaper than chasing money after.
- Benefit: The law requires the government and relevant departments to explain various telecom and online fraud methods to the public. Public security, together with finance, telecom, and cyberspace authorities, has established a warning-and-dissuasion system that identifies people about to be defrauded and dissuades them in time. 96110 is this national unified warning hotline; pick up when it rings. The Fujian Provincial Public Security Department's per-category breakdown of recognition features follows. Task-rebate: "anyone inviting you to make money together probably wants to make money off you" — task lists never end and the principal isn't returned. Fake investment: shills in groups post returns, give small payouts first to draw you in, then ask you to scale up; withdrawals require "paying taxes." Online loans: "loans don't require margin or bank flow statements — if you encounter them, they're definitely fake." Impersonating customer service: says you opened a membership that will auto-deduct and affect your credit, pressuring you to transfer money to cancel. Impersonating police/procuratorate/court: "there is no such thing as a 'safe account' in police, procuratorate, or courts; any 'police/procuratorate/court' doing case work or taking statements by phone, QQ, WeChat, or online is a scam." Pig-butchering: "for 'lovers' you've never met, only seen photos or video of, don't trust easily" — they never video chat or meet but constantly have emergencies requiring money. Sex-lure task scams: arrange to meet, but first "do tasks," with money paid escalating. Recognizing them beforehand is far cheaper than stop-payment after. How much stop-payment can recover depends on whether the money was still in the account when you called police; see Entry 2 for how to do stop-payment (nationwide, since December 2022)
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2022). Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law (Articles 8, 20, 34). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>; Fujian Provincial Public Security Department (2023). 96110 is calling, please be sure to answer. <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202303/t20230306_6126156.htm> (provincial public security page, containing the mnemonic "don't trust callers, don't disclose information, don't click links, verify transfers more" and the warning "be alert to scam calls starting with 192"); Fujian Provincial Public Security Department (2023 to 2024). - Note: Anyone claiming to be police/procuratorate/court, customer service, a leader, or platform risk-control, asking you to "cooperate with investigation," "cancel membership," "do task rebates," or share "investment tips" is running the same playbook. Real loans don't require margin, and police/procuratorate/courts have no "safe accounts" — these two sentences alone block over half. ### 4. Seeing a face on video or hearing a voice on the phone doesn't count as verification; for anything involving money transfer, hang up first and call back using an old number saved in your contacts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Make one extra phone call, wait a few extra minutes. Post fewer front-face videos and long voice messages on public accounts. The hard part is the caller often pushes urgently; you must resist transferring on the spot.
- Plain-language summary: Seeing a face or hearing a voice doesn't count as verification. As long as money transfer is involved, hang up first, then call back using an old number saved in your contacts, or ask a mutual acquaintance. If the other party says "bad signal" or "in a meeting" and refuses to video chat in person, treat it as a scam.
- Benefit: The Ministry of Public Security's Cybersecurity Bureau gives three methods. The first is action verification: ask the other party to make a continuous head-turn, or to fully cover the face with a palm and then quickly remove it. The original text says "current AI technology, when simulating these complex facial occlusions and lighting changes, tends to stutter, blur, or distort." The second is listening to voice details: "AI-generated speech sometimes lacks natural breathing pauses, emotional fluctuations, or carries a slight mechanical quality, with abnormal background noise." The third is multi-channel verification. The original text calls this "the most core move": "after hanging up, directly call the other party's regular phone number that you're familiar with (don't redial the suspicious incoming number), or verify sideways through a third person you both know." The mnemonic is "don't trust video or voice, verify by action, verify more." Legally, face-swapping and voice-cloning have long been prohibited for fraud. Face generation, face replacement, synthesized voice, and voice imitation services must carry prominent labels on platforms. No organization or individual may maliciously delete, tamper with, forge, or conceal this label. But the rule-following party is the platform; scammers won't add the label, so "not seeing an AI label" can't be used as evidence the other party is a real person. The Ministry of Public Security's Criminal Investigation Bureau once published a case. A 14-person gang was found to have synthesized over 50,000 dynamic face-swap videos and over 500,000 pieces of citizens' personal information. Police also seized 60 cloud servers (nationwide; labeling requirement effective September 1, 2025)
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Ministry of Public Security Cybersecurity Bureau (2025). What you see ≠ what's real: be alert to new AI fraud! <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202509/t20250922_7013008.htm> (reproduced by Fujian Provincial Public Security Department); Cyberspace Administration of China, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, Ministry of Public Security (2022). Provisions on the Administration of In-Depth Synthesis of Internet Information Services (Articles 6, 16, 17; effective January 10, 2023). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-12/12/content_5731431.htm>; Cyberspace Administration of China, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, Ministry of Public Security, National Radio and Television Administration (2025). Measures for Labeling AI-Generated Synthetic Content (Article 10; effective September 1, 2025). <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2025-03/14/c_1743654684782215.htm>; Ministry of Public Security Criminal Investigation Bureau, CCTV News (2026). Police seized over 50,000 dynamic face-swap videos — who is "copying" your face? <http://gat.fujian.gov.cn/ztzl/fjjffpzxrx/spjq/202603/t20260330_7117146.htm> (reproduced by Fujian Provincial Public Security Department)
- Note: For callbacks, use old numbers saved in your contacts. Don't redial the incoming caller ID number, and don't use any new number the other party sends in chat. If the other party refuses to video chat in person or perform a specified action on grounds of "bad signal" or "in a meeting," treat it as a scam. The action verification method is only auxiliary; AI keeps improving, and the only stable method is the third — verification. If you've already transferred money, see Entry 2 of this section (immediately call 110 to request stop-payment). For preventing your own face and voice from being collected, see Section 14. Graded B because the official sources give recognition methods and cases, without "how much fraud fell after verifying by this method." ### 5. If accused or summoned, get a lawyer first; don't settle privately, don't delete records
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Lawyer fees start at several thousand yuan. Those in financial difficulty can apply for a free lawyer through legal aid. When questioned, answer only questions related to this case; leave the rest to the lawyer. The hard part is steadying yourself on the spot, not rushing to explain yourself.
- Plain-language summary: From the first questioning, you can hire a lawyer. When a lawyer requests to meet you, the detention center must arrange it within 48 hours at the latest, and the conversation won't be monitored. You may refuse to answer questions unrelated to this case.
- Benefit: From the first interrogation, or from when coercive measures are taken (when personal freedom is restricted), you have the right to retain a lawyer. During the period the case is still under investigation by public security, only a lawyer can be retained; you can't have a non-lawyer serve as defense counsel. When a lawyer requests a meeting, the detention center must arrange it within 48 hours at the latest, and the meeting is not monitored. No one may be forced to prove their own guilt. You have the right to refuse to answer questions unrelated to this case. A single summons or compulsory summons lasts up to 12 hours; for especially major and complex cases, up to 24 hours. A summons is a notice to come in for questioning; a compulsory summons is being forcibly taken in for questioning. Successive summons may not be used to effectively detain someone. Fabricating facts to falsely accuse another, intending them to face criminal prosecution, is punishable by up to 3 years; serious consequences: 3 to 10 years. Those detained or arrested and then had the case dropped, were not prosecuted, or were acquitted may apply for state compensation, calculated at the previous year's national average daily wage (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2018). Criminal Procedure Law (2018 amendment, Articles 34, 35, 39, 52, 119, 120). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/201810/t20181027_396818.shtml>; National People's Congress (1997). Criminal Law (Article 243). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2012). State Compensation Law (2012 amendment, Articles 17, 33). <https://www.stats.gov.cn/gk/tjfg/xgfxfg/202503/t20250306_1958899.html> (reproduced by the National Bureau of Statistics)
- Note: Don't privately seek out the other party, and don't seek out their family. Any "private settlement" can be construed as your admission. Chat logs, call logs, and itinerary records are your evidence of innocence; deleting them is deleting your own evidence. A genuine mistake, or a report that on later investigation doesn't hold up, doesn't count as the crime of false accusation. This entry applies to the accused regardless of sex. The three expansions are in Entry 33 (pursuing the fabricating party), Entry 34 (insufficient evidence should yield acquittal and appeal for retrial), and Entry 35 (state compensation after detention, calculated by the day) of this section

### 6. When something happens, take the initiative to come clean: surrender (自首) can cut up to 40% off the sentence; "outlast the prosecution limitation period" is a dead end
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Turn yourself in to public security, or stay where you are and call police, then truthfully recount what happened. The hard part is resisting the thought "wait a bit longer, maybe no one's investigating."
- Plain-language summary: Surrender (自首) can cut up to 40% off a sentence; for relatively minor crimes, punishment can be waived outright. Truthfully confessing after being sought out can also reduce up to 20%. Running away and refusing to admit yield none of this. Hoping to wait out the prosecution limitation period doesn't work either: for cases already filed, the time you spend on the run doesn't count toward the limitation period.
- Benefit: Criminal Law Article 67: voluntarily turning oneself in after committing a crime and truthfully confessing one's own crime constitutes surrender (自首). For those who surrender, the punishment may be lightened or reduced; for relatively minor crimes, punishment may be waived. Truthfully confessing one's own crime without having turned oneself in (called "坦白," honest confession) can lighten punishment; if the truthful confession prevents especially serious consequences, punishment can be reduced. The Supreme People's Court and Supreme People's Procuratorate sentencing guidance gives explicit ranges. The baseline sentence is the sentence computed from the case facts; the percentages below are reductions on top of it. Surrender can reduce the baseline by up to 40%; for relatively minor crimes, by 40% or more, or punishment may be waived per law. For honest confession: truthfully confessing one's own crime reduces by up to 20%; truthfully confessing a more serious crime of the same type that the judicial organs did not yet know reduces by 10% to 30%; truthful confession preventing especially serious consequences reduces by 30% to 50%. Guilty plea and punishment acceptance reduces by up to 30%; with additional circumstances such as surrender, major honest confession, returning stolen goods or paying compensation, and obtaining victim forgiveness, reduction is up to 60%; for relatively minor crimes, 60% or more, or punishment may be waived per law. The prosecution limitation period is divided into four tiers by statutory maximum sentence: 5, 10, 15, and 20 years (Criminal Law Article 87). But Criminal Law Article 88 states that after the procuratorate, public security organs, or state security organs have filed a case for investigation, or after a court has accepted a case, evading investigation or trial is not subject to the prosecution limitation period (nationwide; sentencing ranges effective July 1, 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: National People's Congress. Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China (consolidated text as amended by Criminal Law Amendment XI), Articles 67, 87, 88. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181796a636a0179822a19640c92>; Supreme People's Court, Supreme People's Procuratorate (2021). Sentencing Guidance for Common Crimes (Trial) (Fa Fa [2021] No. 21, effective July 1, 2021), III (6)(7)(14). Full text reproduced by the People's Procuratorate of Zhangye City, Gansu. <http://www.zhangye.jcy.gov.cn/info/1039/2086.htm>; Supreme People's Court (2010). Opinions on Several Specific Issues Concerning the Handling of Surrender and Meritorious Service (Fa Fa [2010] No. 60), I. Supreme People's Court Gazette. <http://gongbao.court.gov.cn/Details/f3089058d37e11cd16d801530934ae.html>
- Note: The beneficiary of this entry is yourself. Surrender is only a mitigating circumstance; the crime is still established; how much reduction is decided by the court; the sentencing guidance requires strict treatment of the leniency for serious violent crimes and drug crimes. Traffic accidents have a separate calculation: staying at the scene, saving people, and reporting to public security counts as voluntary surrender (自首), but because these are already statutory obligations, the leniency is to be applied strictly; surrendering after fleeing still counts as surrender (自首), only the heavier tier of statutory sentence is used as the baseline before deciding whether to reduce and by how much — see Entry 1 of this section (in a traffic accident, stop, save people, call police). Hiring a lawyer first and truthfully confessing don't conflict, see Entry 5 of this section (get a lawyer first when accused): what the lawyer checks for you is whether the record is written correctly, not help you fabricate a story

### 7. If you've been drinking, don't touch the steering wheel — including electric vehicles and "just moving the car a bit"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Designated driver or taxi, tens of yuan. Before the gathering, agree on who won't drink today. The hard part is pushing back when pressed at the table.
- Plain-language summary: Driving after drinking: license suspended for 6 months, fined 1,000 to 2,000 yuan. Reoffending: detention (拘留) plus license revocation. Drunk driving: license revoked outright, can't retest for 5 years, and criminal liability for dangerous driving — leaving a criminal record that affects political vetting and certain professions. Insurance also doesn't cover it: commercial auto insurance doesn't pay for any drunk-driving incident. "Just moving the car a bit" also counts as driving.
- Benefit: Driving after drinking: driver's license suspended for 6 months, fined 1,000 to 2,000 yuan. Reoffenders: detained up to 10 days with license revoked. Drunk driving: license revoked outright, cannot retest within 5 years, and criminal liability pursued for the crime of dangerous driving, sentenced to criminal detention (拘役) and a fine — 拘役 means short-term imprisonment. Commercial-vehicle drivers face heavier penalties: driving after drinking — detained 15 days, fined 5,000 yuan, 5-year driving ban; drunk driving — 10-year ban, and can never drive commercial vehicles again for life. Serious accidents caused by driving after drinking or drunk driving that constitute crimes: lifetime driving ban. Commercial auto insurance does not pay for any driving after drinking or drug use (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A

### 8. Don't lend bank cards, phone cards, or payment accounts to anyone; "running points" is not a side gig
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Someone says "lend your card to pass through transactions, here are a few hundred yuan" — refuse.
- Plain-language summary: Renting, lending, or selling phone cards, bank accounts, payment accounts, or internet accounts to others is itself illegal. Penalty: 1 to 10 times the illegal income; with no illegal income, up to 200,000 yuan; serious cases: detention (拘留) up to 15 days, plus credit-record entry and account-function restrictions. If you knowingly help someone collect and transfer money for crimes committed online, that's the crime of aiding information network criminal activities, with up to 3 years' imprisonment.
- Benefit: Renting, lending, buying, or selling phone cards, bank accounts, payment accounts, or internet accounts is itself illegal. Illegal income is confiscated, with an additional fine of 1 to 10 times the illegal income. With no illegal income: up to 200,000 yuan fine. Serious cases: detention (拘留) up to 15 days. May also be entered into credit records, have account functions restricted, or have non-counter services suspended (must transact at the counter). Knowingly providing payment-and-settlement type assistance to someone using the network to commit crimes, in serious cases, constitutes the crime of aiding information network criminal activities (abbreviated as the 帮信 crime), punishable by up to 3 years and a fine (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2022). Anti-Telecom and Online Fraud Law (Articles 31, 44). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202209/t20220902_575631.shtml>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2015). Criminal Law Amendment (IX) (Article 29, i.e., Criminal Law Article 287-2). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180205_364562.shtml>
- Note: Saying "I didn't know what they were doing with the card" doesn't hold. During investigation, your knowledge will be inferred from external circumstances: receiving kickbacks, obviously abnormal transaction flows — all may be counted as you knew. If your card is used to receive stolen money, even if you ultimately don't constitute a crime, your account will be frozen and you will be sanctioned

### 9. Check your own credit report free twice a year for loans and cards you didn't open
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- Cost: Free. Check online; takes ten minutes to finish.
- Plain-language summary: You can check your own credit report free online twice a year, finished in ten minutes. Use the People's Bank of China Credit Reference Center website or mobile banking; don't use third-party "credit-check" Apps — those Apps are collecting your information. Adverse records are kept for 5 years from the day the matter ends; after that they must be deleted. Disputes can be raised, with a written reply required within 20 days.
- Benefit: Administrative regulations state "personal information subjects have the right to obtain their own credit report free twice a year." Adverse information retention: "5 years from the date the adverse behavior or event ends; beyond 5 years, it must be deleted." If you dispute the report, you can raise it; the credit reference agency or the agency that provided the information must reply in writing within 20 days of receiving the dispute
- Evidence grade: A

### 10. In a conflict, call police first, don't raise a hand; the one who strikes first almost always loses
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Retreat to a crowded place and call police; let the other party's actions be captured on surveillance. The hard part is holding back from answering back or shoving.
- Plain-language summary: Striking someone: detention (拘留) 5 to 10 days, fine 500 to 1,000 yuan. Group fights, hitting the elderly, pregnant women, children, or disabled, repeat offenders: detention 10 to 15 days, fine 1,000 to 2,000 yuan. Causing minor injury is a criminal case, punishable by up to 3 years; serious injury, 3 to 10 years. Mutual fighting doesn't count as justifiable defense; the one who strikes first almost always loses. Retreating to a crowded place and calling police is more effective than fighting back.

### 34. Insufficient evidence should have yielded acquittal; coerced confessions should be excluded; after a verdict, there are appeal and retrial
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Going through the procedure itself costs nothing, but appeal and retrial take a lot of time. A lawyer is usually needed; fees start at several thousand yuan. - Plain-language summary: The threshold for conviction is written into the Criminal Procedure Law: evidence for the verdict must be verified through legal procedures, and combined evidence must exclude reasonable doubt. After a verdict takes effect, you, your legal representative, and close relatives can still appeal.
- Benefit: Criminal Procedure Law Article 55: evidence that is conclusive and sufficient must meet these conditions — the evidence for the verdict has been verified through legal procedures, and combined evidence has excluded reasonable doubt regarding the established facts. Excluding reasonable doubt means: looking at all the evidence together, the established facts cannot have another plausible possibility. Article 56: confessions of criminal suspects and defendants collected through torture and other unlawful methods, and witness testimony and victim statements collected through violence, threats, and other unlawful methods, must be excluded. Where evidence that should be excluded is found during investigation, review for prosecution, or trial, it must be excluded per law and may not serve as the basis for prosecution opinions, prosecution decisions, or verdicts. Article 200 (3): where evidence is insufficient and the defendant's guilt cannot be established, an acquittal on grounds of insufficient evidence and failure of the charged crime must be rendered. Article 252 provides the appeal route. The parties, their legal representatives, and close relatives may appeal to a people's court or people's procuratorate against a legally effective judgment or ruling. But appeal does not stop execution of the judgment or ruling. Article 253 lists the situations where a people's court must retry (nationwide)

### 35. If after detention the case is dropped, not prosecuted, or you're acquitted, apply for state compensation; money is calculated by the day

- Plain-language summary: Those detained or arrested and then had the case dropped, were not prosecuted, or were acquitted can apply for state compensation. Non-prosecution due to minor circumstances doesn't pay. Past the prosecution limitation period, below age, and self-fabricated confessions also don't pay.
- Benefit: The State Compensation Law Article 17 lists several situations where victims have the right to compensation. The first is detention: detaining a citizen in violation of the Criminal Procedure Law, or lawfully detaining but exceeding the statutory time limit. Thereafter, the case is dropped, not prosecuted, or a verdict of acquittal terminates criminal pursuit. The second is arrest: after arresting a citizen, the case is dropped, not prosecuted, or a verdict of acquittal terminates criminal pursuit. The third is retrial: a case is retried under the trial supervision procedure and the verdict is changed to acquittal, with the original sentence already executed. The trial supervision procedure is the procedure for re-examining a verdict that has already taken effect. Article 33: where personal liberty is infringed, daily compensation is calculated by the national average daily wage of employees for the previous year. Article 35: where mental harm is caused, the impact of the infringing act should be eliminated within its scope, reputation restored, apologies made; where serious consequences occur, the corresponding mental distress compensation must be paid. Article 19 lists six situations where the state does not compensate. First, the person intentionally fabricated their own confession or forged other inculpatory evidence, and was therefore detained or convicted. Second, those not criminally responsible under Criminal Law Articles 17 and 18 were detained — e.g., below the age of criminal responsibility, or mentally ill persons causing harm while unable to recognize or control their behavior. Third, those not subject to criminal pursuit under the Criminal Procedure Law were detained. This includes circumstances where the act is obviously minor and not significant enough to constitute a crime, where the prosecution limitation period has passed, and where the suspect has died. It also includes cases where the crime is minor and the procuratorate decides not to prosecute — what's commonly called relative non-prosecution (also called discretionary non-prosecution). Minors whose conditional non-prosecution probation period expires and are not prosecuted, and those not prosecuted after reaching a settlement with the victim, are also included. Fourth, personal actions of case-handling personnel unrelated to the exercise of their authority. Fifth, harm caused by the person's own self-injury, self-mutilation, or other intentional acts. Sixth, other situations stipulated by law. ### 38. "Buy insurance on someone first, then act" is blocked from the start by law: not a cent of money, and the crime is punished as concurrent counts of intentional homicide and insurance fraud
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free.
- Plain-language summary: All four doors are closed. Buying a death-benefit insurance policy on a family member behind their back makes the contract void. Where the policyholder intentionally causes the death of the insured, the insurance company does not pay. Intentionally killing an heir forfeits inheritance rights. The Criminal Law separately lists this kind of insurance fraud, and punishes it as concurrent counts with intentional homicide, up to the death penalty. Not a cent of money.
- Benefit: Insurance Law Article 34: a contract conditioned on death as the basis for paying insurance benefits is void without the insured's consent and approval of the insurance amount. The insured is the person covered. Parents buying insurance on minor children are exempt from this restriction. Article 39: when the policyholder designates a beneficiary, the insured's consent must be obtained. The policyholder is the person paying for the insurance; the beneficiary is the person who receives the money after an incident. Article 43 covers intentional harm. Where the policyholder intentionally causes the death, injury, or illness of the insured, the insurer does not bear the responsibility for paying insurance benefits. Where the policyholder has paid premiums for two years or more, the cash value is refunded to other right-holders. Where the beneficiary intentionally causes the death, injury, or illness of the insured, or intentionally kills the insured but the attempt fails, that beneficiary forfeits beneficiary rights. Article 27 paragraph 2: where the policyholder or insured intentionally creates an insured event, the insurer has the right to dissolve the contract and does not bear the responsibility for compensation or payment, and aside from Article 43 does not refund premiums. Civil Code Article 1125: intentionally killing the decedent forfeits inheritance rights. Criminal Law Article 198 paragraph 1 item 5 lists "the policyholder or beneficiary intentionally causing the death, injury, or illness of the insured to defraud insurance money" as insurance fraud. Relatively large amounts: up to 5 years and a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 yuan. Large amounts: 5 to 10 years. Especially large amounts: 10 years or more. Paragraph 2: where the same act also constitutes another crime, punishment is per the provisions on concurrent counts — concurrent counts meaning combining the sentences of multiple crimes. Article 232: intentional homicide — death penalty, life imprisonment, or fixed-term imprisonment of ten years or more. The case is the Zhang Moumeng case in Xuancheng, Anhui. In the early hours of July 18, 2016, he drove his car into a pond, and his wife drowned. Less than half a month before the incident, he had bought four insurance policies on his wife, each for 1,000,000 yuan. On July 25 he was apprehended. The court sentenced him, for concurrent counts of intentional homicide and insurance fraud, to death with reprieve and a fine of 50,000 yuan

### 39. When reporting to police, get a receipt on the spot; for non-filing, demand written notice: apply for reconsideration within 7 days, then review within another 7 days; the procuratorate can notify police to file

- Cost: Free. Ask one more question on the spot to get the case receipt. Then follow up on progress by the dates above. The hard part is opening your mouth to ask again and again.
- Plain-language summary: After reporting, get the case receipt on the spot — it's the only proof you reported. If public security decides not to file, they should give you a written notice of non-filing. If you disagree, you can apply for reconsideration within 7 days; if you still disagree, apply for review within another 7 days. ### 40. Don't give money or gift cards to case-handling, enforcement, or judicial personnel: bribery lands you a sentence too, and bribing supervisory, enforcement, or judicial personnel is also explicitly a heavier-treatment category
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Just don't do it.
- Plain-language summary: "Spend a little money to make it go away" is an independent crime under the Criminal Law; those caught aren't just the recipients. Bribery: up to 3 years. Serious circumstances: 3 to 10 years; especially serious: 10 years or more, or life. Bribing supervisory, administrative-enforcement, or judicial personnel is explicitly the heavier-treatment category. Being extorted and paying without gaining illegitimate benefits doesn't count as bribery.
- Benefit: Criminal Law Article 389 defines bribery: "to obtain illegitimate benefits, giving property to state functionaries is the crime of bribery (行贿罪)." The same article also states: "in economic transactions, in violation of national regulations, giving property to state functionaries in amounts that are relatively large, or in violation of national regulations giving kickbacks or handling fees of various names to state functionaries, is treated as bribery." It also leaves an exception: "giving property to state functionaries under extortion, without obtaining illegitimate benefits, is not bribery." Sentencing is in Criminal Law Article 390 (amended by Criminal Law Amendment XII, effective March 1, 2024). Original text: "for the crime of bribery, up to three years of fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention, plus a fine; where bribery is for illegitimate benefits with serious circumstances, or causes major losses to state interests, three to ten years of fixed-term imprisonment, plus a fine; where circumstances are especially serious, or where especially major losses are caused to state interests, ten years or more of fixed-term imprisonment or life imprisonment, plus a fine or confiscation of property." Seven categories warrant heavier sentencing: "(1) repeated bribery or bribing multiple people; (2) state functionaries engaging in bribery; (3) bribery in national key projects or major programs; (4) bribery for position, rank promotion, or adjustment; (5) bribing supervisory, administrative-enforcement, or judicial personnel; (6) bribery in fields like ecological environment, fiscal finance, production safety, food and drugs, disaster prevention and relief, social security, education, or healthcare, while committing illegal or criminal acts; (7) using illegal gains for bribery." The final paragraph leaves an opening for voluntary disclosure: "where the briber voluntarily discloses the bribery before prosecution, punishment may be lightened or reduced. Where the crime is relatively minor and the disclosure plays a key role in investigating or solving a major case, or where there is major meritorious performance, punishment may be reduced or waived" (nationwide)

### 43. If subjected to domestic violence: first call police and leave a response record, then apply to the court for a personal safety protection order; you don't have to divorce first, and it's free
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- Cost: Free. Applying for a personal safety protection order requires no litigation fee and no security deposit. What it costs is the time to report, get examined, and write the application. The hardest part is opening this mouth to family.
- Plain-language summary: Among women murdered worldwide, more than a third are killed by partners — often the endpoint of long-term domestic violence. Among women similarly beaten, those who had been strangled had over 7 times the chance of later being murdered as those who hadn't. So if beaten, call police, then apply to the court for a protection order. The court generally must respond within 72 hours.
- Benefit: First, the risks. Data from 66 countries pooled (systematic review). In female homicide cases, 38.6% were committed by intimate partners, versus 6.3% for males. The authors say such murders are often the final step of long-term abuse. Another study compared 506 homicides and attempted homicides with 427 women who were beaten but didn't suffer such outcomes. Women previously strangled by a partner had 6.70 times the chance of attempted homicide (OR 6.70, 95% CI 3.91–11.49) and 7.48 times the chance of homicide (OR 7.48, 95% CI 4.53–12.35) compared with those not strangled. Now the law. Victims and close relatives can call police. Public security must respond promptly, stop the violence, investigate and collect evidence, and assist with medical treatment and injury examination. For relatively minor circumstances, public security can issue a warning letter to the other party. Where subjected to domestic violence or facing a real danger of it, you can apply to the court for a personal safety protection order. The court must issue a ruling within 72 hours of accepting the application; in emergencies, within 24 hours. A protection order can prohibit the other party from committing violence, prohibit harassment, tracking, or contact with you and your close relatives, and can require the other party to move out of your residence. A protection order lasts up to 6 months and can be extended before expiration. Application does not require divorce as a prerequisite. The court can issue one when it considers domestic violence "has a relatively high likelihood." Where the other party violates the protection order, the court can fine up to 1,000 yuan and detain up to 15 days. Where it constitutes a crime, it's handled as the crime of refusing to execute judgments or rulings. Frequent insults, threats, stalking, harassment, and being left to freeze or go hungry also count as domestic violence
