# 15. Renting and Buying Housing

Metric: money. Where renting loses money most easily is the deposit and the intermediary. Where buying costs the most is mortgage interest, plus the things the seller knows that you don't.

### 1. The deposit amount, return time, and deduction conditions must be written into the contract
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Spend ten more minutes when signing. The hard part is raising it in front of the landlord.
- Plain-language summary: How much the deposit is, when it is returned, and under what conditions it can be deducted — these must be written into the contract before signing. Deduction reasons not written into the contract cannot be used by the landlord to take your deposit at move-out.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation is clear: "Where a lessor collects a deposit, the amount of the deposit, the time of return, and the situations in which the deposit may be deducted shall be specified in the housing lease contract. Except for the situations stipulated in the housing lease contract, the lessor may not deduct from the deposit without justified reason." Three things must be written into the contract: how much deposit, when it is returned, and under what conditions it can be deducted. Reasons not written into the contract cannot be used by the landlord to deduct your money
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2025). Housing Lease Regulations (State Council Order No. 812, in force September 15, 2025) (Article 10). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Note: On move-out day, take photos and video together with the landlord. Photograph the readings of the water meter, electricity meter, and gas meter, as well as walls and floors. If the deposit is deducted without cause, first file a complaint with the housing lease management department. For small amounts, use small-claims litigation; see Section 8.

### 2. If water or power is cut, locks are changed, or someone comes to threaten eviction, first call the police and preserve evidence: regulations prohibit these methods to force you out
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=none benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Make a phone call to the police; doable on the spot.
- Plain-language summary: A landlord cannot force you to terminate the lease or move out by cutting water or power, changing locks, or coming to threaten you; the regulation explicitly prohibits it. If it happens, call the police first. The dispatch record is your evidence for later claiming damages. Don't fight the other party with your own hands.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation is clear: "A lessor shall not use violence, threats, or other illegal means to force a lessee to terminate the housing lease contract or vacate the leased housing." "Vacate" means making you move out. When encountering such situations, call the police first and preserve the evidence. Don't fight back yourself
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2025). Housing Lease Regulations (State Council Order No. 812, in force September 15, 2025) (Article 12). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Note: Calling the police is to leave a dispatch record, which becomes evidence when you later claim damages. Also notify the housing lease management department.

### 3. Intermediaries may not collect or pay rent and deposits on behalf of others; money goes directly to the landlord
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Verify the recipient before paying; a few minutes. The hard part is resisting the intermediary's pitch to transfer the money to them.
- Plain-language summary: By regulation, rent and deposits cannot be collected or paid by the intermediary; money is paid directly to the landlord. Money that passes through the intermediary's hands is one more chance for them to abscond with it. If they really run, your rent is wasted and the landlord will also evict you. Before paying, verify that the recipient is the name on the property certificate.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation lists "collecting and paying housing rent and deposits" as a prohibited act for real estate brokerage agencies; the intermediary cannot collect your rent and deposit on the landlord's behalf. Money that passes through the intermediary's hands is one more chance for them to abscond with it
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2025). Housing Lease Regulations (State Council Order No. 812, in force September 15, 2025) (Article 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Note: Before paying, verify that the recipient is the name on the property certificate. If it is not the person themselves, ask clearly and keep a written authorization. The intermediary may only charge a referral service fee for bringing the parties together.

### 4. Before renting from a long-term rental apartment company, check its fund supervision account; don't pay a year up front just for a discount
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. A check of a dozen or so minutes.
- Plain-language summary: Paying one year up front gets only a small discount. If the company collapses, your deposit and the remaining dozen months of rent are gone together. Bundling in a rent loan is worse: if the apartment company runs, you still repay the loan monthly. Before signing, check whether it has a publicly disclosed fund supervision account.
- Benefit: The administrative regulation requires housing lease enterprises that engage in sublease operations to "establish a housing lease fund supervision account and disclose it to the public". Sublease operation means the company leases from the landlord as a whole and then subleases to you. The small discount from paying one year up front cannot offset the risk of losing deposit and remaining rent when the company runs. Those who also sign rent loans face greater risk
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2025). Housing Lease Regulations (State Council Order No. 812, in force September 15, 2025) (Article 19). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202507/content_7032956.htm>
- Note: When signing, check whether a loan is bundled. If an installment app pays the apartment company the full year up front while you repay monthly, then if the apartment company runs, you still have to keep repaying. Do not sign such a contract.

### 5. If the house is sold during the lease term, the lease remains valid; you don't have to move
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=none benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free.
- Plain-language summary: If the house is sold during the lease term, your lease remains valid. The new landlord must let you stay to the end of the term per the original contract. You don't have to move, and you don't have to renegotiate the price. The premise is that you are already lawfully living there; keep the contract, transfer records, and evidence of move-in time.
- Benefit: The Civil Code contains a provision called "sale does not break the lease". The statute's original text reads: a change in ownership of the leased property during the lease term does not affect the validity of the lease contract. That means if the house changes owners during the lease, the signed lease remains valid. The new landlord must let you live there until the end of the term per the original contract
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Article 725). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Note: The premise is that you have already lawfully moved in — the statute's phrasing is lawful possession and use. Keep the contract, transfer records, and evidence of move-in time.

### 6. Before signing, verify the property certificate and mortgage status; route all payments through bank transfer with a memo of purpose
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. A check of half an hour.
- Plain-language summary: The two most common ways to lose everything are: rent paid to someone who is not the property owner, and the house having long been mortgaged to a bank and later seized. Before signing, check the property rights and mortgage status once at the real estate registration center. Route all money through bank transfer with the memo "rent for X house, month Y"; this is direct evidence in any dispute.
- Benefit: The two most common ways to lose everything are paying rent to someone who is not the property owner, and the house having long been mortgaged to a bank and later seized. When transferring, memo "rent for X house, month Y"; this is direct evidence in any dispute
- Evidence grade: C
- Source: Author experience, no direct literature; the same logic of preserving evidence and transfer memos is in the two entries in Section 8 on bride price and IOUs
- Note: Property information can be checked at the local real estate registration center. At signing, have the landlord appear in person; if they cannot, get a power of attorney. For a sublease from a "second landlord", you must have written consent from the original landlord.

### 7. If a second-hand home intermediary collects the purchase price, it must go through the intermediary's dedicated bank transaction-fund deposit account
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The hard part is insisting on the dedicated account; you may have to argue a few more rounds with the intermediary.
- Plain-language summary: The purchase price is hundreds of thousands to several million yuan — the single largest amount in this book. When an intermediary collects the purchase price, by regulation it must go through its dedicated account at a bank; you cannot WeChat-transfer it to an individual agent. Also, when two intermediaries collaborate on one deal, only one commission may be charged; loan-processing and title-transfer services must be under separately signed contracts with fees disclosed in advance.
- Benefit: The regulation is clear: where the parties agree that a real estate brokerage agency will collect and pay transaction funds, the funds shall be transferred through the agency's dedicated bank deposit account for customer transaction settlement funds. This means the purchase price goes into a supervised dedicated account, not the intermediary's own account and not an individual agent's personal account
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development, National Development and Reform Commission, Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security (2011). Real Estate Brokerage Management Measures (Order No. 8, modified by Order No. 29 of 2016) Article 24: "Where the parties to a real estate transaction agree that a real estate brokerage agency will collect and pay transaction funds on their behalf, the transaction funds shall be transferred through the dedicated deposit account for customer transaction settlement funds that the real estate brokerage agency has opened at a bank." Article 18 "Real estate brokerage services implement a clearly priced system... prominently display in the business premises the real estate brokerage service items, service content, and fee standards"; Article 19 "Where two or more real estate brokerage agencies cooperate on the same real estate brokerage transaction, commission may be charged only as one transaction, and additional charges to the client are not permitted"; Article 17 Other services such as loan processing and real estate registration processing "shall describe the service content, fee standards, and other circumstances to the client, and after the client agrees, a separate contract shall be signed". <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1918920.htm>
- Note: The purchase price is hundreds of thousands to several million yuan — the single largest amount in this book; don't WeChat-transfer it to an agent. There are also two common ways to overcharge. First, two intermediaries jointly working on the same deal can charge only one commission. Second, loan processing and title transfer services are additional services requiring a separate contract with fees disclosed in advance. Route all payments through bank transfer with a memo of purpose; see Entry 6 (verify property certificate and mortgage status before signing).

### 8. Don't rent partitioned rooms: the minimum rental unit is the originally designed room; kitchens, bathrooms, and balconies may not be lived in
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=none benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The cost is that with the same budget, you either live farther away or in a smaller place.
- Plain-language summary: By regulation, the minimum rental unit is the originally designed room. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and underground storage rooms cannot be lived in. When such a place is investigated and punished, you may have to move. Also, the landlord cannot unilaterally raise the rent during the lease term.
- Benefit: The regulation is clear: the minimum rental unit is the originally designed room; one room cannot be partitioned into several and rented separately. The per-capita rental building area shall not fall below the local minimum standard. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and underground storage rooms may not be rented for people to live in. The lessor may not unilaterally raise the rent during the lease term
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (2010). Commercial Housing Lease Management Measures (Order No. 6) Article 8: "When leasing housing, the originally designed room shall be the minimum rental unit, and the per-capita rental building area shall not be lower than the minimum standard set by the local people's government. Kitchens, bathrooms, balconies, and underground storage rooms may not be rented for people to live in." Article 9: "The lessor shall fulfill the housing's repair obligations per the contract and ensure the safety of the housing and indoor facilities... during the housing lease contract term, the lessor may not unilaterally raise the rent level at will." <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2011/content_1845070.htm>
- Note: When illegal partitions are investigated and punished, it's the tenant who moves, and the deposit and paid rent are often unrecoverable. Safety is also worse: partition walls often block escape routes, and many people in one room share a single electrical circuit. Three things to check when viewing: whether the wall was built later, whether the room has a window, and whether the electrical meter can handle so many people.

### 9. Urban hukou holders wanting to move to the countryside should only rent rural housing; do not buy homesteads or houses on homesteads
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. The cost is that the house does not belong to you; one lease term is up to 20 years, and at expiration you must renegotiate with the landlord.
- Plain-language summary: Urban hukou (household registration) holders cannot buy rural homesteads, farmers' houses, or "small-property-rights housing" in the countryside; state documents explicitly prohibit it. Village residents saying "just write up a contract" doesn't work either; once money is paid, the house still cannot become yours. To live in the countryside long-term, sign a lease contract, for up to 20 years at a time, renewable at expiration.
- Benefit: A State Council General Office document is clear: "Urban residents shall not go to the countryside to purchase homesteads, farmers' dwellings, or 'small-property-rights housing'." The same document also provides that farmers who have sold or leased their housing will not be approved if they apply for a homestead again. A 2019 document from the Central Rural Work Leading Group Office and the Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs reiterated: "It is strictly prohibited for urban residents to go to the countryside to purchase homesteads." The same document provides the lawful path: urban residents may rent rural housing for living or business, with lease contracts not exceeding 20 years, renewable by separate agreement upon expiration
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council General Office (2007). Notice on Strictly Implementing Laws and Policies Concerning Rural Collective Construction Land (Guo Ban Fa [2007] No. 71). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_2395.htm>; Central Rural Work Leading Group Office, Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Affairs (2019). Notice on Further Strengthening Rural Homestead Management (Zhong Nong Fa [2019] No. 11), Parts 5, 6. <https://www.moa.gov.cn/govpublic/NCJJTZ/201909/t20190920_6328397.htm>
- Note: A homestead (宅基地) is the land allocated by the village to its members for building houses; it can only be allocated to village members. When renting rural housing, sign a written contract with the farmer-lessor specifying the lease term, rent, whether renovation or remodeling is allowed, and how renovation costs are settled at expiration. The document caps lease terms at 20 years; do not sign "lease for 50 years" or "lease for 70 years" contracts, and even less should you pay multiple years' rent in one lump sum. The document also prohibits going to the countryside to build villas or private clubs on homesteads; do not pay villagers to "jointly build".
