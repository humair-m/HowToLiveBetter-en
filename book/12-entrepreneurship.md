# 12. Entrepreneurship and Doing Business: Don't Lose the Family Savings

This section tallies only money and legal liability, not converted into life expectancy. Opening a shop, starting a company — at which step would your own money, your family's money, your spouse's money all be lost together? What does the law say, and can it be walled off in advance? The entries that most easily sink the family savings and are easiest to avoid are placed at the front. So signature-type entries come first; do-it-yourself method entries come later. Entries based only on experience with no literature support are graded C.

### 1. Use only money you can afford to lose to start a business; don't touch the family savings, don't borrow to open
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Before acting, set a number for yourself: even if this money is entirely lost, you can still pay the mortgage, raise the kids, see the doctor. Beyond that number, do not invest. Family members' money and borrowed money do not count toward this number. The hard part is resisting the urge to add more when the business seems to be picking up.
- Plain-language summary: Debts of an individual business (个体户) must be repaid with money and assets in your own name. If it cannot be distinguished whether the whole family ran it, the whole family's property is used to repay. Setting up a company is not safe either: when the company cannot repay its debts, the capital subscription (认缴出资) you originally promised but had not yet come due can be demanded early. So decide how much to invest by "even if all is lost, you can still pay the mortgage and see the doctor".
- Benefit: A loss is not just money gone. Debts of an individually-owned business, if you ran it alone, must be repaid with money and assets in your own name (borne by personal property). If the whole family ran it, or it cannot be distinguished who ran it, the whole family's money and assets are used (borne by family property). Debts that both spouses signed are joint debts repaid by both. One party's subsequent ratification also counts. Money borrowed by one party in their own name, as long as the creditor can prove it was used in a business the couple ran together, is also repaid by both. There is also a rule on the company side: when the company cannot repay its due debts, the creditor may demand that the shareholder pay in advance the capital subscription (认缴出资) originally promised in the registered capital but not yet due (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Articles 56, 1064). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2023). Company Law (2023 revision, Articles 47, 54). <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Note: "Affordable to lose" means: even if this money is entirely lost, you do not have to sell your home or borrow from relatives to fill the hole. How many businesses survive and how long they last on average — this kind of official statistics was not found in verifiable original form on the National Bureau of Statistics or State Administration for Market Regulation websites this round. So the body gives no numbers, only legal consequences, and the grade is B.

### 2. Don't sign a personal guarantee for a company loan; the spouse even less so
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. When the bank, supplier, or landlord asks you to sign "shareholder personal joint guarantee" or "spouse signature", pause first. Signing that page is the same as you personally giving up the protection of "company debts do not come after individuals" (giving up limited liability). Don't sign if you can avoid it. If you must sign, sign only a "general guarantee" and write in the maximum amount you will guarantee. The hard part is the frequent "no signature, no loan"; you must withstand it.
- Plain-language summary: If what you sign is a "joint liability guarantee", when the company cannot pay, the creditor can bypass the company and come directly to you. A "general guarantee" means suing the company first, selling the company's assets to cover what can be covered, and only then does your turn come. A spouse who signs puts the family assets of both on the line; the "limited" in limited liability company no longer applies to you personally.
- Benefit: That sentence on the guarantee means "if the debtor doesn't repay, you do". Written as "joint liability guarantee", the creditor can skip the company and come directly to you. Written as "general guarantee", the creditor must first sue the company or go to arbitration, then ask the court to forcibly sell the company's assets to satisfy the debt. After the sale, however much is still short, only then do you fill in. Debts that both spouses signed are joint debts repaid by both. One party's subsequent ratification also counts. A spouse who signs puts the family assets of both on the line (nationwide, from 2021)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Articles 681, 687, 688, 1064). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>
- Note: Banks making loans to small and micro enterprises almost always require shareholders to sign personal joint guarantees. The loan can still be taken; just be clear before signing what that page signs away, and keep the guaranteed amount within the "affordable to lose" number from Entry 1 (use only money you can afford to lose to start a business). General rules on IOUs and guarantees are in Section 8, Entry 18.

### 3. Choose the right entity before opening: sole proprietorships and general partners pay to the end; only limited companies are "limited"
<!-- cost-tag: money=little time=medium willpower=none benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Registration is done on the local government services website; a few hours per attempt. Once a limited company is opened, monthly bookkeeping and tax filing are required — this is a long-term cost: doing it yourself takes time, hiring an agency costs money, prices vary by region.
- Plain-language summary: Sole proprietorships and general partners pay to the end; however much is owed, however much is repaid. Shareholders of limited liability companies only pay up to the amount they promised at registration. But whatever was promised must be paid in within 5 years: filling in 1 million means a potential debt of 1 million. Mixing company accounts with personal accounts also invalidates "limited".
- Benefit: Debts of an individually-owned business must be repaid with personal money and assets, or with the whole family's money and assets. Partners in a general partnership bear unlimited joint liability for the partnership's debts — however much is owed is repaid; "not finished" is not an option. The creditor can also single out any one partner and demand full repayment. Limited partnerships are different: the limited partners in them only pay up to the capital they promised to contribute (limited to their capital subscription). Shareholders of limited liability companies also only pay up to the capital they promised (limited to their capital subscription). But "limited" fails in three situations. First, the promised capital must be paid in full within 5 years of the company's establishment; when the company cannot repay due debts, the creditor may demand early payment. Second, money already invested and then withdrawn (capital withdrawal) must be returned. Third, using the company as a shell to evade debt. A single-shareholder company that cannot prove the company's money is separate from its own money also falls into the third kind. Under these two practices, the company's debts are also borne by you personally (joint liability) (nationwide, from July 2024)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: National People's Congress (2020). Civil Code (Article 56). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2006). Partnership Enterprise Law (2006 revision, Article 2). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2006/content_413955.htm>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2023). Company Law (2023 revision, Articles 4, 23, 47, 53, 54). <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Note: The premise of "limited" is a clear separation between public and private: company accounts and personal accounts are not mixed, company money is not used for household expenses, and accounting records are complete. Don't fill in a large registered capital to look impressive. Whatever is subscribed must be paid in within 5 years. Filling in 1 million means 1 million of potential liability.

### 4. Don't be a nominal shareholder; don't hold equity for someone else
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=none benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. If someone comes to you saying "let me borrow your ID to hang you as a shareholder" or "let's put the shares in your name first", refuse outright.
- Plain-language summary: Once your name is on the register, you are legally a shareholder. The capital you promised at registration must be paid in full on time; failure to pay requires making up the shortfall and compensating the company for losses. If other shareholders did not actually contribute their capital at company formation, you also pay along with them for the shortfall. The business was run by others, but the debt is recorded in your name.
- Benefit: A shareholder registered on the register is a shareholder in law. You must pay in the promised capital in full by the deadline set in the company's articles of association (full payment of subscribed capital). Those who fail to pay on time must make up the shortfall and compensate the company for losses. At company formation, if any shareholder did not actually contribute capital, the other founding shareholders bear joint liability for the shortfall. When the company cannot repay due debts, the creditor may demand early payment of capital not yet due. The person actually running the business is not on the register, but the debt is recorded in your name (nationwide, from July 2024)
- Evidence grade: B
- Source: Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2023). Company Law (2023 revision, Articles 47, 49, 50, 54). <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm>
- Note: Whether the proxy-holding agreement between a nominal shareholder and the actual capital contributor is valid, and who bears liability, is set out in the Supreme People's Court's Provisions (III) on several issues concerning the application of the Company Law. ### 5. Before joining a franchise, check the Ministry of Commerce filing; get written disclosure; write the "cooling-off period" into the contract
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=none benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Free. Go to the Ministry of Commerce's "Commercial Franchise Information Management System" website and check whether the brand owner has filed. Then ask the other party to write out the disclosure information as written materials before signing, along with the contract text. A few hours will do.
- Plain-language summary: By regulation, the brand owner must first run 2 directly-operated stores for at least 1 year, and file with the commerce authority within 15 days of signing. Before signing, it must give you written materials on 12 items, including the franchise fee, deposit refund terms, and whether existing stores are profitable. If you cannot find a filing, the other party refuses to provide materials, or requires payment before letting you see the contract, walk away.
- Benefit: Regulations require the brand owner (called the franchisor in the regulation) to have at least 2 directly-operated stores that it itself runs, and that have been operating for more than 1 year. From the date of the first franchise contract, the franchisor must file with the commerce authority within 15 days. Failure to file: fine of 10,000 to 50,000 yuan. Failure to file past the deadline: fine of 50,000 to 100,000 yuan, plus public announcement. Before signing, the franchisor must give you written disclosure materials covering 12 items, including the categories and amounts of the franchise fee, the conditions under which the deposit is refunded, the number of existing franchise stores, and how their business is doing. Also required: a summary of the audited financial statements for the past 2 years, and the lawsuits and arbitrations of the past 5 years. The contract must also specify that the franchisee may unilaterally terminate the contract within a certain period after signing (unilateral termination). If the franchisor conceals information or provides false information, the franchisee may terminate the contract (nationwide, from May 2007)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2007). Commercial Franchise Operation Management Regulations (State Council Order No. 485, Articles 7, 8, 12, 22, 23, 25). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2008-03/28/content_4179.htm>; Ministry of Commerce (2012). Commercial Franchise Information Disclosure Management Measures (Ministry of Commerce Order No. 2 of 2012, Articles 5, 9). <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2012/content_2177025.htm>; Ministry of Commerce. Commercial Franchise Information Management System. <https://txjy.syggs.mofcom.gov.cn/>
- Note: If you cannot find a filing, the other party refuses to give disclosure documents, or requires payment before letting you see the contract, walk away. The "cooling-off period" is the few days after signing during which you can still unilaterally terminate. The number of days is set by the contract; the regulation only says "a certain period", so when signing require that the number of days be written in. For the "average sales, gross profit, net profit of existing franchise stores" disclosed by the franchisor, follow up on where the numbers come from — the measures require the franchisor to state the source.

### 6. Before registering, settle the name, business premises, business scope, and registered capital; with materials in hand, the license is issued on the spot
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=none benefit=medium metric=money -->
- Cost: Registration itself is free. The registration fee for enterprises and individually-owned businesses has been suspended since January 1, 2015. Doing it yourself on the local government services website, or at the government services hall, takes a few hours per attempt. Having an official seal cut, hiring a bookkeeping agency, and renting a registration address (address hosting) are services purchased on the market — not government fees.
- Plain-language summary: Registration itself is free; the registration fee has been suspended since 2015. The required materials are only five items: application form, identity or qualification proof, business premises documents, articles of association or partnership agreement (not required for individually-owned businesses), plus any other items specifically named by regulations. With materials in hand, the license is issued on the spot; if not on the spot, processed within 3 working days. The money collected is mostly an agency's errand fee.
- Benefit: The regulation fixes the required materials at five items. "(1) Application form". "(2) Applicant qualification documents, natural-person identity proof". "(3) Documents relating to the domicile or main business premises". "(4) Articles of association of a company, non-company enterprise legal person, or farmers' specialized cooperative (federation), or partnership agreement of a partnership enterprise". "(5) Other materials required by laws, administrative regulations, and the market supervision and administration department of the State Council". In plain English: an application form; a document proving who you are or that you are qualified to do this; a document proving you have the right to use the shop or office space; articles of association or partnership agreement; plus any other items specifically named by regulation. Companies must also submit appointment documents and identity proofs for the legal representative, directors, supervisors, and senior managers. Individually-owned businesses do not need articles of association. The name "is independently declared by the applicant according to law" — you pick it and report it. Registered capital "implements the subscribed registration system" — write down how much you promise to contribute; no need to pay on the spot. Business scope is selected from the categories published by the registration authority. With complete materials and correct format, "confirmation and on-the-spot registration" applies. If on-the-spot processing is not possible, "within 3 working days", with an additional 3 working days for complex cases. "The date the business license is issued is the date the market entity is established": the day printed on the license is the day the company or shop was established (nationwide, from March 2022)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2021). Market Entity Registration Management Regulations (State Council Order No. 746, Articles 10, 13, 14, 16, 19, 21). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2021/content_5636139.htm> ; State Administration for Market Regulation (2022). Market Entity Registration Management Regulations Implementation Rules (SAMR Order No. 52, Articles 12, 25, 26). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2022-03/02/content_5676403.htm> ; Ministry of Finance, National Development and Reform Commission (2014). Notice on Canceling, Suspending, and Exempting a Batch of Administrative and Institutional Fees (Cai Shui [2014] No. 101, Annex 1). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/2014-12/23/content_5023513.htm>
- Note: Registration itself charges nothing; the money collected is mostly an agency's errand fee. Seal-cutting prices are set by the market. The State Council General Office explicitly states "designating seal-making units is strictly prohibited", so when someone says "you must go to the designated place to have the seal cut", you need not accept. The amount of registered capital does not affect appearances; it only sets your liability cap, and must be paid in within 5 years; see this section, Entry 3 (choose the right entity before opening). For business scope items that require a license, the business may not open before the license is issued; see this section, Entry 7 (trades that require a license).
### 7. Trades that require a license: do not open before the license is issued
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=none benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Before opening, find out clearly whether your business requires a license. Catering and food sales require a food business license. Selling only pre-packaged food made by others does not require a license; it is changed to filing. Only open for business after the license is issued.
- Plain-language summary: Opening without a license, in the lighter case, brings an order to cease operations, confiscation of earnings, and an additional fine up to 10,000 yuan. If you are dealing in items under monopoly or restricted sale, or in securities, futures, insurance, or fund payment settlement, in serious circumstances it constitutes the crime of illegal business operations, with sentences starting at up to 5 years. "Decorate first, license later", "trial operation before the license is issued" is the most common pitfall.
- Benefit: Operating without the required license is investigated by the department designated by laws and regulations. Operating without a license where the law does not specify punishment: ordered to cease, the earnings (illegal gains) confiscated, and an additional fine up to 10,000 yuan. There is a heavier tier. Items designated by laws and regulations as monopoly, exclusive sale, or restricted sale, operated without a license. Securities, futures, insurance, fund payment settlement — operated without approval. In serious circumstances these constitute the crime of illegal business operations: up to 5 years of fixed-term imprisonment or criminal detention (short-term custody), with a concurrent fine of 1 to 5 times the illegal gains. Especially serious circumstances carry 5 years or more (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A
- Source: State Council (2017). Measures for Investigating and Punishing Unlicensed and Unregistered Operation (State Council Order No. 684, Articles 5, 6, 13). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2017-08/23/content_5219861.htm>; State Administration for Market Regulation (2023). Food Business License and Filing Management Measures (SAMR Order No. 78, Article 4). <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/2023/issue_10606/202307/content_6894763.html>; National People's Congress (1997). Criminal Law (Article 225). <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml>
- Note: The license conditions for finance, medical, and education/training each have their own specific laws; this entry verifies only the general investigation rules and the Criminal Law article. Milk-tea shops and snack shops are catering services and require a food business license. "Decorate first, license later", "trial operation before the license is issued" is a common pitfall. Which tier food falls into, and whether selling raw meat and vegetables requires a license, is in Entry 8 (look at which tier of food you fall into first).

### 8. For food, first see which tier you fall into: production and catering require a license; selling only pre-packaged requires filing; selling raw meat and vegetables requires no certificate
<!-- cost-tag: money=little time=medium willpower=none benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: The license itself is free. But the premises and facilities must meet standards; on-site inspection plus license processing takes several weeks. Meat sellers must also keep quarantine certificates and meat quality inspection certificates for each batch.
- Plain-language summary: Making things yourself for sale, like snack shops, braised meats, baked goods, homemade pickled vegetables — all require a license. Selling only pre-packaged food made by others requires no license; just file with the county-level or higher market regulation department. Selling raw meat and vegetables requires no certificate. Doing it without a certificate, even the minimum fine is 50,000 to 100,000 yuan — higher than the capital of most small businesses.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: doing food business requires a license first. Producing food, selling food, and catering all require a license according to law. "However, the sale of edible agricultural products and the sale of pre-packaged food only does not require a license. The sale of pre-packaged food only shall be filed with the food safety supervision and administration department of the local people's government at or above the county level". Edible agricultural products are raw meat, vegetables, and similar unprocessed items. Pre-packaged food is made by others and sold together with its packaging. "Specific management measures for food production workshops and food vendors shall be formulated by provinces, autonomous regions, and municipalities directly under the central government"; these two categories are set by each province. Doing food business without a license: the earnings, the food, the tools, equipment, and raw materials are all confiscated. For batches whose total value at sale price (货值金额) is less than 10,000 yuan, an additional fine of 50,000 to 100,000 yuan. For batches with value of 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of 10 to 20 times the goods value. Pig Slaughter Management Regulations: pigs may only be slaughtered at state-designated slaughterhouses, with centralized official quarantine. "Except in rural areas where individuals slaughter for their own consumption and do not implement designated slaughter, no unit or individual may engage in pig slaughter activities without designation". "Pig products sold, used for production and processing of meat foods by units and individuals, as well as by catering service operators and centralized meal-providing units, must be pig products that have passed quarantine and meat quality inspection at a designated pig slaughterhouse (场)". So the pork you sell or use must come from a designated slaughterhouse and pass both quarantine and meat quality inspection. Slaughtering pigs privately at a non-designated slaughterhouse: ordered to cease, pigs, slaughtering tools and equipment, and earnings are all confiscated, and the fine is the same as the no-license food tier above. Animal Epidemic Prevention Law: animal products sold and transported must carry the quarantine certificate and quarantine mark with the goods. Missing: ordered to correct, plus a fine of up to one time the goods value of comparable qualified goods. Judicial interpretation: foods produced and sold by small workshops and food vendors that do not meet food safety standards — when buyers demand tenfold compensation (punitive damages, the extra amount on top of the refund), the court shall support it (nationwide)
- Evidence grade: A

Food Safety Law (Articles 35, 36, 122). <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=7b5a76d0461745a08d3f964916b87ef3>; State Council (2021). Pig Slaughter Management Regulations (State Council Order No. 742, Articles 2, 23, 31). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-07/22/content_5626534.htm>; Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (2021 revision). - Note: The thresholds for small workshops, small catering, and food vendors are set by each province; this entry gives no specific numbers. Go to your provincial market regulation bureau's website and search for "food small workshop small catering small vendor management regulations" to read the local version. Selling homemade food online does not get an exemption from a license just because it is "online"; what requires a license still requires a license. ### 9. Selling in packaging makes it pre-packaged food: production date, shelf life, ingredient list on the label — none may be missing
<!-- cost-tag: money=little time=medium willpower=none benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Printing a label costs a few cents to a few tenths of a yuan each. Each batch requires the date and ingredients to be filled in correctly, which takes some time.
- Plain-language summary: Once food is packaged for sale, it is legally pre-packaged food; the packaging must have a label, and nine items must be marked — production date and shelf life included. Bulk food must also be marked with the name, date, and the seller's name and address. Failure to mark what should be marked: goods value under 10,000 yuan brings a fine of 5000 to 50,000 yuan; 10,000 yuan or above brings a fine of 5 to 10 times the goods value.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: pre-packaged food (food packaged for sale) must have a label on the packaging. The label must state the name, specifications, net content, production date, ingredient or formula list, the producer's name, address, and contact information, and shelf life. Also the product standard code, storage conditions, the general name in the national standard of any food additives used, and the production license number. Items additionally required by laws, regulations, or food safety standards must also be marked. Bulk food is unpackaged, sold by weight as needed. For bulk food sold, the containers and outer packaging must also state: the food's name, production date or production batch number, shelf life, and the name, address, and contact information of the producer or seller. Pre-packaged food produced or sold without a label, or with a label or instruction manual not in compliance with this law: earnings and food are confiscated. For batches whose total value at sale price (货值金额) is less than 10,000 yuan, an additional fine of 5000 to 50,000 yuan. For batches with value of 10,000 yuan or more, a fine of 5 to 10 times the goods value. Serious cases: ordered to cease production and business, up to revocation of the license. Labels and instruction manuals with only minor defects (瑕疵) that do not affect food safety and do not mislead consumers: first ordered to correct, with a fine up to 2000 yuan only if refused. The new "General Standard for the Labeling of Pre-packaged Foods" is numbered GB 7718-2025, issued on March 16, 2025, effective from March 16, 2027, with a two-year transition period in between. It requires a separate area on the packaging to simultaneously display the production date and the shelf-life expiration date. Dates must use high-contrast, easily legible fonts and colors, and be arranged in year-month-day order (nationwide)

### 10. Ordinary food may not claim to cure disease: labels, instruction manuals, ads, and livestream scripts all count
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Run through the marketing copy, product detail pages, and livestream scripts. The hard part is holding back from saying things like "lowers the three highs" and "treats constipation".
- Plain-language summary: The labels, instruction manuals, and ads of ordinary food may not claim to prevent or treat disease. Except for ads of medical, pharmaceutical, and medical-device categories, no other ads may claim to treat disease, nor use terms that sound like drugs. The fine is 1 to 3 times the ad fee. If the ad fee cannot be computed, the fine is 100,000 to 200,000 yuan. Serious cases can also bring revocation of the business license.
- Benefit: Food Safety Law: "Labels and instruction manuals of food and food additives shall not contain false content and shall not involve disease prevention or treatment functions. Producers and operators are responsible for the content of the labels and instruction manuals they provide". "The content of food advertisements shall be truthful and lawful, shall not contain false content, and shall not involve disease prevention or treatment functions. Food producers and operators are responsible for the truthfulness and lawfulness of food advertisement content". That means packaging, instruction manuals, and ads may not claim to prevent or treat disease; the seller is responsible for whatever is written. Exaggerating or deceiving consumers in ads is punished under the Advertising Law. The Advertising Law also has a ban: "Except for medical, pharmaceutical, and medical-device advertisements, no other advertisements may involve disease treatment functions, nor use medical terminology or terminology that may easily confuse the marketed goods with drugs or medical devices". Except for ads in the three categories of medical, pharmaceutical, and medical devices, no other ads may claim to treat disease, nor use terms that sound like drugs or medical devices. ### 20. For each batch of incoming goods, keep a batch of receipts and supplier information; do not buy goods whose purchase price is clearly below market: for counterfeit goods bought by employees, the boss is the one sentenced
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. For each batch, keep these: purchase contract, invoice, payment record, the supplier's business license and authorization documents. Set one internal rule: whoever buys the goods signs, and any price clearly below market is not bought. The hard part is letting go of those low-price goods that look very profitable.
- Plain-language summary: Selling counterfeit goods, with earnings of 30,000 yuan or sales of 50,000 yuan, is enough for a criminal sentence of up to 3 years. Goods held in inventory that have not yet been sold also count. "I didn't know it was fake" is not up to you: a purchase price clearly below market with no explanation will be deemed to mean you knew. Keeping purchase contracts, invoices, payment records, and supplier information is the only path to exemption.
- Benefit: Selling goods knowingly counterfeit registered-trademark goods, with earnings (illegal gains) of 30,000 yuan or sales amount of 50,000 yuan, reaches the threshold for a criminal sentence. Up to 3 years, with a concurrent fine or sole fine. At ten times this standard: 3 to 10 years and a fine. Goods still in inventory that have not been sold also count: goods value reaching three times the sales amount standard also constitutes a crime. "Knowing" does not require your admission. The Supreme People's Court and Supreme People's Procuratorate's 2025 judicial interpretation lists five situations that can directly establish your knowledge, unless you can produce evidence proving you truly did not know. First, knowing that the registered trademark on the goods has been altered, swapped, or covered. Second, forging or altering the trademark owner's authorization documents, or knowing that these documents have been forged or altered. Third, having received criminal or administrative punishment for selling counterfeit goods, and selling the same kind again. Fourth, without justified reason, purchasing or selling at prices clearly below market. Fifth, after discovery by administrative enforcement or judicial authorities, transferring or destroying infringing goods and accounting vouchers and other evidence, or providing false proof. Where a company commits this, "the unit is fined, and the person directly in charge and other persons directly responsible" are punished under the same article. That is, the company is fined, and the decision-maker and the person handling it are sentenced under the same article. So if an employee bought the goods, the decision-maker is the one fined and sentenced. Conversely, the Trademark Law leaves a way out: if you truly did not know when selling that the goods infringed another's registered trademark, "and can prove the goods were lawfully obtained and identify the supplier, no compensation liability is borne". Whether you can be exempt from compensation depends entirely on whether you have receipts and supplier information; without keeping them, this article is unusable (nationwide; the judicial interpretation took effect April 26, 2025)

Beyond criminal penalties, there is an administrative penalty: where infringement is established, the infringing goods and tools are confiscated, and where the illegal business amount is 50,000 yuan or more, a concurrent fine of up to 5 times may be imposed. Do not continue selling, and do not destroy the goods or the books. Destroying evidence falls squarely into the fifth situation of "presumed to know it is counterfeit" above. Using others' patterns is in Entry 21 (patterns must be your own or licensed). There is a tactic that specifically exploits this entry: someone comes to your door, says a batch of pens or other small items is given to you for free, to be sold in your shop and the money split later. After some days, another person comes to buy them, takes evidence on the spot, and then claims infringement. For goods given for free, all three conditions for exemption from compensation happen to be missing: no purchase contract or payment record, no real supplier identified, and a clearly abnormal price. If you really want to do consignment sales, sign a written consignment contract, keep a photocopy of the other party's ID and business license, and write in who owns the goods and who is liable for infringement.

### 21. Patterns on goods, packaging, hangtags, and promotional images must be your own or licensed; changing a color or adding an icon does not count as "altered"
<!-- cost-tag: money=little time=little willpower=some benefit=large metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Licensed stock-image fees start at a few tens of yuan per image. Original commissioned design runs from a few hundred to a few thousand yuan. For each image used on a product, keep an authorization certificate and purchase record. The hard part is resisting the temptation to use images casually searched online.
- Plain-language summary: Changing the color, swapping the background, or adding your own icon on someone else's image still constitutes using their work without permission. Copying someone else's art work for profit, with earnings of 30,000 yuan, sales of 50,000 yuan, or 500 copies printed, constitutes the crime of copyright infringement, up to 3 years. On the trademark side, "similar enough to cause confusion" itself constitutes infringement.
- Benefit: The Copyright Law lists "using a work without the copyright holder's permission by means such as adaptation, translation, or annotation" alongside direct copying as infringement. So changing the color, swapping the background, or adding your own icon on an original image is still using someone else's work without permission. The threshold for a criminal sentence is here: for profit, without the work's copyright holder's permission, printing and selling (reproducing and distributing) a work of art. Three numbers trigger the crime. Earnings (illegal gains) of 30,000 yuan. Or sales of this business reaching 50,000 yuan (illegal business amount). Or the cumulative number of copies printed reaching 500 (pieces). Reaching any one of these constitutes the crime of copyright infringement. Up to 3 years, with a concurrent fine or sole fine. Ten times the standard: 3 to 10 years. Merely selling infringing copies made by others, with earnings of 50,000 yuan or sales of 100,000 yuan, is convicted under the crime of selling infringing reproductions, up to 5 years. Trademarks likewise: using a trademark similar to a registered trademark on the same kind of goods, or using an identical or similar trademark on similar goods, "easily causing confusion" itself falls within infringement. "Similar" is explicitly written in the statute as an infringing situation; minor alterations still count as infringement (nationwide; the judicial interpretation took effect April 26, 2025)

### 22. On the job, follow the procedures; for favors outside the procedures, first think about who bears responsibility
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=little willpower=some benefit=medium metric=freedom -->
- Cost: Free. Do things at work according to the system, the doctor's orders, and the procedures. Do not make decisions for others beyond your authority. - Plain-language summary: Taking nurses as an example: the regulations specify that upon discovering a critically ill patient, the doctor must be notified immediately; upon discovering that a doctor's order violates regulations, the doctor issuing the order must be raised. The law also does not require standing by and letting someone die: voluntary emergency rescue that injures the person does not require compensation.

### 23. If you lose, exit by procedure: use simplified cancellation if possible, file for bankruptcy if insolvent; do not leave it untended
<!-- cost-tag: money=0 time=medium willpower=some benefit=large metric=money -->
- Cost: Simplified cancellation can be done yourself: publish for 20 days on the National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System. If there are outstanding debts, first settle the accounts, collect what is owed, and pay what is owed — this step is called liquidation. Bankruptcy requires applying to the court and takes several months. The hard part is not wanting to face it after a loss, and dragging it out.
- Plain-language summary: If there are no debts, no unpaid wages, taxes, or social insurance, the investors write a written commitment, publish for 20 days without objection, and simplified cancellation goes through. Individually-owned businesses do not need to publish; cancellation goes through directly if there is no objection within 10 days. If due debts cannot be repaid, apply to the court for bankruptcy. Leaving it untended: the legal representative may not serve as legal representative of another enterprise for 3 years.
- Benefit: No money owed to others and no money owed by others, or all already repaid. No unpaid wages, social insurance, or taxes. All investors have written a written commitment. Satisfying these, publishing for 20 days without objection allows simplified cancellation. Individually-owned businesses do not need to publish; if no objection is raised by the relevant departments within 10 days, cancellation goes through directly. Where a company cannot repay due debts and owes more than it has (insolvent), it may apply to the court for reorganization, reconciliation, or bankruptcy liquidation. Where it has already been dissolved but not liquidated, and owes more than it has, the person responsible for liquidation should apply for bankruptcy liquidation. The consequences of leaving it untended: failure to publish the annual report on time results in being listed in the operating-abnormal directory. Failure to publish for 3 years results in being listed in the serious-violation enterprise list. The legal representative or person in charge may not serve as legal representative or person in charge of another enterprise for 3 years. Those already in the operating-abnormal directory cannot even use simplified cancellation (nationwide)
