# Section 12 Three Addendum Entries: Registration, Tax Filing, Tax Fraud · Verification Record (2026-09-08)

Task origin: A reader asked about the process, materials, fees, and tax filing for opening a company, and reported that after registering an individual business, they received a call impersonating the government and were directed to the other party's official-account mini-program for per-incident "tax filing on your behalf" fees. Verification showed that the existing 14 entries in Section 12 had no registration process, no filing obligations, and no such scam; three entries were added, and one stale tax citation was fixed along the way.

Ordering adjustment: New entries were inserted by start-up sequence; old Entries 6 to 14 shifted down; the section now has 17 entries. The order is entity choice (3) → registration (6) → licenses (7) → filing obligations (8) → invoices (9) → tax fraud (10). There were no cross-references of the form "Section 12, Entry N" within the section; the shift does not affect other entries.

Tool notes: This round first had two search subtasks find the official original text separately, then the main task verified key quotes one by one. The "Verified" column in the table below indicates which the main task personally opened and verified. npc.gov.cn reported SSL handshake failure via WebFetch; legal provisions were switched to the State Taxation Administration's regulation database (fgk.chinatax.gov.cn) and the SPP portal; mps.gov.cn is 521 site-wide; the Ministry of Public Security original page remains unretrievable.

## A stale citation fixed along the way

Old Entry 7 (now Entry 9) "invoices only for actual transactions" cited Ministry of Finance/STA Announcement 2023 No. 19's "monthly sales of 100,000 yuan or less exempt from VAT". Verification showed that the Value-Added Tax Law took effect 2026-01-01, with the threshold now set by the State Council: Ministry of Finance/STA Announcement 2026 No. 10 Article 1 "from January 1, 2026 to December 31, 2027... (1) for a one-month tax period, the threshold is monthly sales of 100,000 yuan. For a one-quarter tax period, the threshold is quarterly sales of 300,000 yuan. (2) For per-transaction tax payment, the threshold is per-transaction (daily) sales of 1,000 yuan"; Article 6 "domestic-stage VAT preferential policies specified in documents issued before December 31, 2025 shall cease to apply simultaneously"; the supporting STA Announcement 2026 No. 4 has explicitly repealed Announcement 2023 No. 1. The amount threshold is unchanged; the basis is fully replaced; the entry has been rewritten per Value-Added Tax Law Article 23 and 2026 No. 10; pages opened and verified by the main task.
