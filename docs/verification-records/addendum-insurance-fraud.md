# Addendum: Two Entries on Insurance Fraud · Verification Record (2026-09-18)

Task origin: A user asked "is there anything on insurance fraud? I see online that many people harbor the illusion of luck, thinking they won't be caught, who buy insurance for a relative and then brutally murder that relative to defraud the insurance — and they were still caught afterward; if there isn't, then add it."

Existing coverage: Whole-book search for "insurance fraud," "insurance scam," "insurance swindle," "defrauding insurance" only turns up one sentence in Section 5, Entry 13's (medical insurance family pooling) Note: "using it to buy health supplements at a pharmacy to cash out is insurance fraud." The crime of insurance fraud, murder for insurance fraud, fabricating accidents to defraud claims — zero coverage.

Landing point: Section 8 gets Entry 36 (the tier involving loss of life: four legal gates + concurrent punishment for multiple crimes + official case), and Section 9 gets Entry 21 (the tier ordinary people are more likely to encounter: fabricating accidents, exaggerating losses, finding people to testify, and how anti-fraud investigations work). The two entries point to each other, with the README and CLAUDE.md descriptions for Sections 8 and 9 synchronized.

## Case (Zhang Mou-song, Xuancheng, Anhui)

| URL | Verified | Original quotation |
|---|---|---|
| <http://www.ah.jcy.gov.cn/jczt/jjslhyqkjc/mtjj/202001/t20200113_2758841.shtml> | Yes (Anhui Provincial People's Procuratorate website reproducing Legal Daily 2020-01-13, fetched word for word; page is UTF-8, decoding as GBK produces all-garbled text) | "Zhang Mou-song was sentenced to death with reprieve and a fine of 50,000 yuan for the crime of intentional homicide and the crime of insurance fraud under concurrent punishment for multiple crimes, while Wang Mou-xin and 8 others were sentenced to fixed-term imprisonment ranging from four years to two years for the crime of insurance fraud, each also fined 10,000 yuan"; "At around 3 a.m. on July 18, 2016, at a gas station on Xuanjiu Section, Feicai Subdistrict, Xuanzhou District, Xuancheng City"; "After on-scene investigation, police found no brake marks before the vehicle entered the water, and the tire tracks on the roadside grass were straight, judging that the vehicle drove straight into the water without taking braking measures before entering, inconsistent with normal evasive action"; "Less than half a month before the incident, Zhang Mou-song purchased 4 insurance policies for Guo, each with an insured amount of 1,000,000 yuan. And two days before the incident, Zhang repeatedly searched online for 'vehicle falling into water video' 'vehicle falling into water self-rescue methods' 'how long does drowning take to cause death' and similar information"; "On the day of the incident, Zhang Mou-song reported the case to the insurance company by phone, and the next day searched online for 'statutory beneficiary allocation ratio'"; "On July 25 of that year, the Xuancheng Municipal Public Security Bureau Development Zone Branch apprehended Zhang Mou-song. Faced with the evidence, Zhang Mou-song admitted that he intended to manufacture a traffic accident to murder his wife and defraud the insurance to pay off debts"; "On identification by the Xuancheng Municipal Public Security Bureau Forensic Center, Guo died by drowning"; on the false litigation side, "the insurance company later assessed damages at 20,000 yuan ... the court awarded compensation of over 70,000 yuan," and the Xuanzhou District Court "on September 26, 2019 ruled to revoke the effective civil judgment in question"; the article notes that this "case within a case" came from "the typical civil false-litigation supervision case published by the Anhui Provincial People's Procuratorate" |

Graded A: Three laws plus one departmental normative document are all verifiable word for word; the case is from a typical case published by a provincial-level procuratorate (reported by Legal Daily and reproduced on the procuratorate's official site). Benefit tier "large" mechanically applies the freedom-metric threshold (what is avoided is the death penalty and fixed-term imprisonment of ten years or more), cost all zero; both entries land in the very high cost-effectiveness tier.

Two deliberate omissions: ① **Did not cite the Phuket, Thailand wife-murder-for-insurance case.** On verification, that case was sentenced by the Phuket Provincial Court in Thailand on 2019-12-24 to life imprisonment, not a Chinese court; using it to discuss concurrent punishment for multiple crimes under Chinese criminal law does not fit, so it was replaced with the domestic Xuancheng case. ② **The body only writes the case details that have been made public in the case's original text** (no brake marks, straight grass tracks, timing and number of policies, pre-incident searches, beneficiary allocation ratio searched the day after reporting), without any extrapolation — this entry's main line is "legally, not a single yuan will be paid out"; the investigation details only serve as corroboration that "thinking you won't be caught" is an illusion.
