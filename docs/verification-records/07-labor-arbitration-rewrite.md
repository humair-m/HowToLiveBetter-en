## 5. "Labor Dispute Mediation and Arbitration Law" (主席令第八十号 / Presidential Order No. 80)
- URL: <https://chinajob.mohrss.gov.cn/h5/c/2022-07-15/356212.shtml> (China Employment Network, MOHRSS)
- Status: opened successfully.
- Article 27: "The limitation period for applying for arbitration of a labor dispute is one year. … During the continuance of the labor relationship, a dispute arising from defaulted labor compensation is not subject to the limitation period in paragraph 1 for the worker's application for arbitration; however, where the labor relationship has terminated, the application shall be filed within one year from the date of termination of the labor relationship."
- Article 43: "An arbitral tribunal shall conclude a labor dispute case within 45 days from the date the labor dispute arbitration committee accepts the arbitration application. … The extension shall not exceed 15 days."
- Article 44: "For cases seeking labor compensation, work-related injury medical expenses, economic compensation, or damages, the arbitral tribunal may, upon application by a party, order advance enforcement and transfer the case to the people's court for enforcement."
- Article 47: Disputes "seeking labor compensation, work-related injury medical expenses, economic compensation, or damages, with the amount in dispute not exceeding the equivalent of 12 months of the local monthly minimum wage" are final arbitration awards.
- Articles 48, 49: A worker dissatisfied with a final award may bring a lawsuit within 15 days; an employer may only apply to the intermediate court for annulment within 30 days.
- Article 50: "A party dissatisfied with the arbitral award in a labor dispute case other than those specified in Article 47 of this Law may bring a lawsuit in a people's court within 15 days from the date of receipt of the arbitral award."
- Article 53: "Labor dispute arbitration is free of charge. The funding of the labor dispute arbitration committee is guaranteed by the fiscal authority."

## 10. "Regulations on Ensuring the Payment of Migrant Workers' Wages" (国务院令第 724 号 / State Council Order No. 724)
- URL: <https://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2020/content_5469641.htm>
- Status: opened successfully. Effective 2020-05-01.
- Article 10: "Migrant workers whose wages are in arrears have the right to file complaints in accordance with the law, or apply for labor dispute mediation and arbitration and file lawsuits. Any unit or individual has the right to report wage arrears against migrant workers to the human resources and social security administrative department or other relevant departments."
- Article 24: "… The disbursement cycle for labor costs shall not exceed one month."
- Article 26: "The general construction contractor shall, in accordance with relevant regulations, open a dedicated migrant-worker wage account, to be used exclusively for paying the wages of migrant workers on the construction project."
- Article 30: "Where a subcontractor defaults on migrant workers' wages, the general construction contractor shall pay them first and then seek recourse in accordance with the law."
- Article 31: "In the field of engineering construction, a system is promoted under which the subcontractor's migrant-worker wages are paid by the general construction contractor on a delegated basis … through the dedicated migrant-worker wage account, wages are paid directly into the migrant worker's personal bank account."
- Article 32: "The general construction contractor shall, in accordance with relevant regulations, deposit a wage guarantee … "
- Article 36: Where the contract is awarded to an individual or unqualified unit causing wage arrears, "the construction project owner or the general construction contractor shall pay the wages".
- Article 41: Where the crime of refusing to pay labor compensation is suspected, the case "shall be transferred to the public security organ for review and a decision in accordance with relevant regulations".
