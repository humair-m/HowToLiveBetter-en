# issue #37: Professional Titles — How to Apply, How to Test, What Pitfalls to Avoid (2026-09-27)

Task source: GitHub issue #37 (superman-death), asking whether information on professional titles could be improved: how to obtain, what materials to prepare, what institution to go to.

## How the Scope Was Set

The book previously had no entry on professional-technical titles; only Sections 24, 31, 32, and 33 touched on them in passing. Section 23, Entries 8 and 11 are about professional qualifications and skill levels, not titles.

No new section opened; 4 entries appended at the end of Section 23 (Entries 20 to 23), not inserted in the middle, to keep Entries 14 to 19 from being shifted (those are referenced more than a dozen times elsewhere).

"What materials to prepare" was not written as a checklist. Titles fall into 27 series; the materials list, application timeline, and performance conditions are set by each series and each province's annual application notice, with no national unified version. Writing item by item would turn into an unmaintainable local-procedure guide. The body text only points the way: first find the current year's application notice from your province's Human Resources and Social Security Department.

## What Was Not Written

- Specific "professional qualification to title" mapping table: not found in the policy document repository by title; only the principle-level provisions in the 2016 Opinions were cited.
- Points-based hukou (household registration) and talent subsidies keyed to titles: vary by locality, no national original text found, not written in Entry 23's Note.
- Whether each series has a unified exam: only the accounting series was verified; other series direct readers to that series' "Guiding Opinions on Deepening Reform of the ×× Professional Title System."

## How Benefit Magnitude Was Determined

- Entries 20 and 21: metric set as Time; effect is one-time avoiding wasted trips; thresholded as "Small."
- Entry 22: metric set as Freedom; consequence is revocation of title, 3-year dishonesty record, 3-year application ban; nature close to administrative action; thresholded as "Medium" by "avoid administrative penalty."
- Entry 23: metric set as Money, but the documents contain no number for "how much salary rises after the title"; judgment sets it "Small"; evidence grade B.
