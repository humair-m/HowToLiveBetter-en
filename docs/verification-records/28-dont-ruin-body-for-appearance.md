## Handling of the "MTF" Half-Sentence (Writing Decision, Not Fact-Checking)

The user's original words included "follow online MTF, don't be defined by gender". This section **does not** write this as "don't transition"; instead it is rewritten as Entry 7 "Sex-hormone drugs are only to be used under a doctor's prescription and with regular follow-up; do not buy online, do not self-increase the dose", for three reasons:

1. What the evidence supports is the mode of medication use, not the motivation. Getahun 2018 measures the difference in vascular events between transgender women and cisgender controls; what it supports is "those who use estrogen need long-term vascular monitoring", not "should not use". Writing it as discouragement would be using this data to say what it does not say, violating this project's rule that "numbers must be findable in the original".
2. A regulated medical pathway exists in China (endocrinology or the appropriate specialist's evaluation, prescription, follow-up); lumping it together with black-market channels and discouraging both would, in effect, push people toward the latter — which is precisely the highest-risk path.
3. The same rule naturally covers the other two items the user enumerated: self-increasing doses of contraceptives / menopausal hormones / testosterone supplementation, and the anabolic steroids of Entry 5. Writing it as "prescription + regular follow-up" is more accurate and shorter than splitting it by population.

The section's introduction states "no judgment on what anyone wants to look like", confining the scope to the difference in risk between two paths: "with prescription, with qualification, with follow-up" versus "online purchase, unlicensed institution, self-increased dose". The "feeling ugly" the user raised is separately placed as Entry 8 (body dysmorphic disorder — assess first), which is the only quantified-literature-supported landing point among these concerns.

## What Was Not Done

- The association between social media use and body dissatisfaction was not written. Meta-analyses in this area are mostly cross-sectional correlational (r values), with small effect sizes and unclear causal direction; including them would only land at Grade C, mismatched with the evidence strength of the other entries in this section. The user's "don't follow trends" was instead expressed in the section introduction's "decisions mostly come from comparing yourself with others", without a separate entry.
- GLP-1 class weight-loss drugs (semaglutide, etc.) indications and misuse were not written. They change fast, the domestic indications and reimbursement metrics are still being adjusted, and Entry 6's "prescription + doctor's judgment" already covers the behavioral advice; a separate entry would require checking the current package insert and indication approval, deferred.
- Official typical cases of illegal medical aesthetics were not included. CLAUDE.md only requires cases for Section 9; this section does not require them; moreover, the Ministry of Public Security site is consistently 521 on this machine, so first-hand bulletins cannot be obtained.
