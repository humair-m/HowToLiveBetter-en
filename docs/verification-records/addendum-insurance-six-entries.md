# Addendum: Section 5, Entries 40 to 45 — Insurance

2026-09-29. User asked "do I really need to buy insurance." The book already had five entries on specific insurance types: Section 5, Entries 25 and 26; Section 7, Entry 20; Section 21, Entry 4; Section 17, Entry 6. What was missing: an umbrella entry stating what to buy and what not to buy; plus a few pre-purchase and post-incident actions. Six entries appended at the end of Section 5, not inserted in the middle, so subsequent entry numbers are not shifted.

## 1. Insurance Law (2015 amendment)
- URL: <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7c4060811>. Per the National Laws and Regulations Database API, sxx=3 (effective); both the publication and effective dates are 2015-04-24; full docx verified verbatim.
- Article 15: After an insurance contract is established, the policyholder may terminate the contract.
- Article 16: Truthful disclosure. Paragraph 3 reads "where more than two years have elapsed since the contract was established, the insurer may not terminate the contract; if an insured event occurs, the insurer shall bear the liability for compensation or payment of insurance benefits." Paragraph 4 reads that for intentional non-disclosure, insured events before the contract is terminated are not covered and premiums are not refunded. This is placed in the Note of Section 7, Entry 20.
- Article 26: For insurance other than life insurance, the limitation period is two years; for life insurance, five years. Both "calculated from the date on which it knew or should have known that the insured event occurred."
- Articles 33 and 34: Restrictions on insuring death for persons with no capacity for civil conduct, with the exception of minor children, but the total payout may not exceed the limit. Contracts with death as a payout condition require the insured's consent and approval of the coverage amount.
- Articles 39, 41, 42: Designation and change of beneficiaries, and the three scenarios in which the insurance benefit is treated as estate, and the presumption that the beneficiary died first when both die in the same event.
- Article 47: When the policyholder terminates the contract, the insurer refunds the cash value within 30 days.

## 2. Interpretation III of the Insurance Law Judicial Interpretations (2020 amendment)
- URL: <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=ff808181799df4000179ac03d7f21115>, sxx=3, effective 2021-01-01.
- Article 9, paragraph 2, item 3: "Where the agreed beneficiary includes both a name and a relationship, and the relationship changes at the time of the insured event, it shall be deemed that no beneficiary was designated."
- Article 10, paragraph 2: Where a beneficiary change is not notified to the insurer and the insurer claims the change has no effect on it, the court supports the insurer.

## 3. Measures for the Administration of Commercial Banks' Insurance Agency Business (Yinbaojianbanfa [2019] No. 179)
- URL: <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-12/03/content_5457853.htm>, effective 2019-10-01.
- Article 30: Documents and promotional materials may not contain the words "deposit," "savings," or "jointly launched with the bank."
- Article 31: Cover page must mark "insurance contract" in font no smaller than 72-point, and the insurance company name in no smaller than size-2 font.
- Article 32: Annual income below the local per-capita disposable income and age over 65, or the policyholder of a periodic-payment product over 60 — in principle sell products with definite policy benefits; no system-automated underwriting with on-site issuance.
- Article 36: For insurance periods exceeding one year, agree a 15-day hesitation period, calculated from the date the policyholder receives the policy and signs for it in writing.
- Article 42: May not allow insurance-company personnel to sell in bank business premises.
- Article 49: May not sell mixed with savings deposits, may not draw simple analogies, may not promise uncertain returns as guaranteed returns.
- Articles 67 and 68: Original wording of the hesitation-period reminder; "15 days" means calendar days.

## 4. Other regulations
- Measures for the Administration of Health Insurance (CBIRC Order 2019 No. 3) Article 15: Long-term health-insurance hesitation period no less than 15 days. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-12/04/content_5458542.htm>
- Provisions on Basic Services for Personal Insurance Business (CIRC Order 2010 No. 4) Article 15: During the hesitation period, callback for new policies of more than one year; callback content includes surrender loss and hesitation-period rights. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2010/content_1702219.htm>
- Information Disclosure Rules for Personal Insurance Products of One Year or Longer (Yinbaojiangu [2022] No. 24): The product specification must list the hesitation-period start time, days, and rights. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2023-01/04/content_5735014.htm>
- Notice on Regulating Short-Term Health Insurance Business (Yinbaojianbanfa [2021] No. 7): For individual short-term health insurance, the comprehensive loss ratio is disclosed on the company's official website every six months, with a calculation formula given. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-01/12/content_5579193.htm>
- Measures for the Administration of Insurance Clauses and Insurance Rates of Personal Insurance Companies (CIRC Order 2011 No. 3) Article 8 defines term life insurance and whole life insurance; Article 12 defines accident insurance. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2012/content_2163594.htm>. This measure was amended in 2015; this round only verified the 2011 original, and whether the definition articles were amended was not checked.

## 5. Risk warnings on surrogate policy surrender
- Five-department joint risk warning (2026-02-06): retrieved verbatim from the Cyberspace Administration of China repost page, <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-02/06/c_1772110875635198.htm>. Original text includes "'full refund'… and the like are all false information," "not only may you pay high service fees, but if you wish to exit midway, you may even face litigation for 'breach of contract'," and the collection of SIM cards, bank cards, policies, home addresses, and the schools your children attend.
- Ningxia Financial Regulatory Bureau (2026-06-16): nfra.gov.cn bureau pages are front-end rendered; body text retrieved via headless Chrome. Original text includes "agree to collect a high agency fee of 30% to 50% of the surrender amount after successful surrender," "may call 12378 — the banking and insurance consumer-complaint rights-protection hotline… the channel is free throughout," along with cash-value-reduced paid-up, policy loans, suspension of premium payment, and other preservation services, and possible violation of the Public Security Administration Punishment Law, false accusation, or joint crime of extortion.
- Hunan Financial Regulatory Bureau (2025-07-23): also retrieved via headless Chrome. Contains the case of Ms. Zhang, who was diagnosed with breast cancer three months after surrendering. The agency fee it states is "20% to 50% or even more," differing from the Ningxia metric; the entry used Ningxia's 30% to 50%.

## What Was Not Written
- Specific death-coverage limits for minors: the widely circulated "under 10 years old, 200,000 yuan; 10 to 18 years old, 500,000 yuan" comes from a 2015 CIRC notice. This round only saw it on insurance-company repost pages, with no government-site original found; the entry only states "there is a limit."
- Premium-to-income ratios such as "20% of household annual income via bank channel, 4× annual premium for single payment": search results only point to reposts by commercial institutions; no regulatory original was verified, so it was not written.
- Specific prices for various insurance types: no official statistics. Entry 41's Cost field states "author rough estimate, no official figures."
