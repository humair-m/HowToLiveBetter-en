Graded B: The official source gives script breakdowns and individual cases, with no incidence rates and no statistics on "how much the fraud rate drops after this lesson is taught." Benefit tier "large" — money metric; the case losses in the materials range from 7,000 yuan to 73,000 yuan, reaching the 10,000-yuan-class; cost money=0 time=few willpower=some (genuinely practicing "if scammed, say so first, don't scold"), synthesized as "high."

The entry's landing point consolidates the six categories into one actionable judgment: **the last step of all six categories is to have the child operate the parent's phone or report a verification code**, so the body's title writes this sentence, with the specific categories placed in the Benefit field as a recognition checklist. Another recurring script is "you violated the rules, your parents will go to jail"; the body notes that this sentence alone is a fraud marker.

Not adopted: "Many children have already fallen for it! Beware of the 'Impersonating Public Security' scam during summer vacation" (2026-08-26) and "Lottery for signed memorabilia, add the idol's private account? Scam syndicate targets student celebrity-chasers" (2026-05-13) from the same column duplicate this entry's category 5 and are not cited again. National-level statistics on minor fraud victimization have no official source (Ministry of Public Security site mps.gov.cn constantly 521), so the body does not write proportions.
