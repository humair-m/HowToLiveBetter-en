## Grading and selection

- Graded A: The primary evidence is a SAFE normative document, with nationally uniform numbers and no regional variation; the cap, suspension, penalty, and query rights are all verifiable word-for-word against the original text; the gov.cn Q&A is the same authority's official explanation of the same document, used to supplement "does not count against the $50,000 forex-purchase quota", "delayed control", and "in 2016, 81% were below 30,000 RMB".
- **Graded A, not marked "disputed"**: The UnionPay International page restates the SAFE numbers consistently with the previous two documents, not constituting opposing evidence.
- **Metric and magnitude**: money metric. Benefit magnitude set to "medium", **not mechanically applied from the threshold**—per CLAUDE.md's money-metric threshold (10,000-yuan class = large), 100,000 yuan would normally fall as "large", but what this entry buys back is not "money saved/recovered" but "a qualification not getting suspended" and "not being unable to withdraw cash overseas"; the amount threshold does not fit, so judgment is used to set "medium", with the basis stated here. Cost: money 0, time few, willpower some (changing a habit: pay by card when you can); per the threshold, lands in the average cost-effectiveness tier.
- **Not written**: specific rates for issuing-bank fees and interest on overseas cash withdrawals (vary by bank; the UnionPay International page explicitly states "that fee is unrelated to UnionPay" and there is no unified rate, so no specific number is given); the default state of overseas-transaction switches at each bank (only the ABC page is verifiable, and that entry was per the author's decision not written; material is preserved in the table above); cross-border cash carriage limits (a different topic, not verified).
- References: the body's note points to Section 14, Entry 5 (card stolen—report and freeze first, then call the police), with an anchor, not repeating the SPC regulation; no "see Entry N of this section" relative pointers were written.
