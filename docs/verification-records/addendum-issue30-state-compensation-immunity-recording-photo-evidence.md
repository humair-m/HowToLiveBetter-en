# issue #30: Completing State Compensation Immunity Scenarios, Adding Two Entries on Recording and Photographing Evidence (2026-09-25)

Task source: GitHub issue #30 (ceiminya). First, pointed out that Section 8, Entry 35 (State Compensation) only writes Article 19 item 1 of the State Compensation Law, omitting scenarios like relative non-prosecution that are not compensated; second, suggested adding two evidence-preservation entries on "recording" and "photographing the scene," with drafts attached. The drafts and quotations in the issue were used only as leads; every entry in the table below was verified against the original text fetched this round, word by word.

## Handling

- Entry 35: Benefit field writes out all six items of Article 19, adds Fashi [2015] No. 24 Articles 7 and 8; Plain-language summary replaces "there's a situation where the state doesn't pay"; Note adds "first read which legal basis the non-prosecution decision is based on" and the 7-day appeal; Source field adds two entries and notes the 2012/2018 Criminal Procedure Law article-number correspondence. Title unchanged (titles may only have words added; the current title's "non-prosecution" lets the reader know there are exceptions through the Note and Plain-language summary).
- Entry 41 (recording): A. Beneficiary is self and family. Added two points the issue's draft lacked: don't post the recording online (privacy and reputation disputes, pointing to Entry 16), and this entry's basis is the civil-litigation rules.
- Entry 42 (photographing the scene): C. The law only governs whether photos/videos can serve as evidence and that originals are required; the "panorama — position — detail" sequence is experience, and the issue itself noted it could be downgraded to C.
- Both entries appended at the end of Section 8, not inserted in the middle, to avoid renumbering. Reference check table grew from 548 to 551, +3, matching.
