Method consistent with previous rounds: English-language literature was retrieved via Europe PMC REST (`search?query=TITLE:"…"&resultType=core`) for bibliographic records and abstract verbatim text; psychology journals not indexed in PubMed were retrieved via Crossref (`api.crossref.org/works?query.bibliographic=`) for records and Semantic Scholar (`api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/DOI:…`) for abstracts. Chinese regulations were located via the State Council policy document repository JSON interface (`sousuo.www.gov.cn/search-gov/data?t=zhengcelibrary`), then the original-text page was fetched and stripped of tags, and articles were verified one by one; laws enacted by the NPC are not in that repository, and were instead verified via the Supreme People's Procuratorate official repost.
