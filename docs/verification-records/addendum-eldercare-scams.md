# Addendum: Investment-Style Eldercare and "House-for-Pension" Scams · Verification Record (2026-09-08)

Task source: A follow-on from the "should add anti-fraud" inventory, doing the second real gap. Section 17 previously only had Entry 3 (keep the elderly's money in a separate account, require two-person confirmation for large withdrawals) and Entry 4 (a scripted excuse line) touching this topic, both C-grade with no sources, only stating "build trust over a long campaign and harvest all at once" — without specifying the kinds of pretexts or the legal consequences. The previous round's "Addendum — chronic-disease long-term-care insurance and personal-information rights" explicitly recorded: "eldercare scams (house-for-pension, health-product sales meetings) — official cases: no representative SPP cases available for citation; this entry in Section 17 not written" — this round retrieved them.

Landing point: Section 17 adds 2 entries (Entries 5 and 6); original Entry 5 (long-term care insurance) shifts to Entry 7. Entries 3 and 4 are kept as-is, with no grade or source changes. No other section references Section 17 by number, so no cascade changes needed.

Reason for splitting into two rather than one: the former is paying money for a promise (membership, bed, apartment, sojourn, senior products) — the action to prevent is "don't pay," and the loss ceiling is the cash the elder holds; the latter is mortgaging the house for cash to then buy wealth-management products — the action to prevent is "don't mortgage, don't sign," and what's lost is the house plus a remaining loan. The magnitude and the handling path differ.

## Section 17, Entry 6 (house-for-pension)

| URL | Verified | Original quotation |
|---|---|---|
| <https://dfjrjgj.hunan.gov.cn/dfjrjgj/yhlj/202106/t20210608_19450856.html> (Hunan Provincial Local Financial Regulatory Bureau repost, source noted "China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission," 2021-06-08) | Yes | CBIRC Consumer Rights Protection Bureau 2021 Issue 3 risk warning. Legitimate product: "Legitimate house-for-pension refers to reverse-mortgage pension insurance for the elderly, an innovative commercial pension insurance combining housing mortgage with lifetime pension annuity insurance. Elderly people with full lawful property rights to their home mortgage the home to an insurance company, continue to hold possession, use, income, and disposal rights (with the mortgagee's — the insurance company's — consent), and receive pension payments on agreed conditions until death" "currently still in pilot stage in our country and rather niche"; scams: "What scammers call 'house-for-pension' has nothing to do with the state's piloted reverse-mortgage pension insurance — it's just a means of borrowing national policy to hype illegal fundraising activities" "Often a 'rob-Peter-to-pay-Paul' Ponzi scheme. Some participants don't even know their home was mortgaged, and ultimately lose the house and remain saddled with the loan"; thresholds: "If a wealth-management product promises a return above 6%, question it; above 8% is very dangerous; above 10%, prepare to lose all principal — 'capital-guaranteed high return' is financial fraud"; also "be careful with contract signing; do not sign blank contracts" |
