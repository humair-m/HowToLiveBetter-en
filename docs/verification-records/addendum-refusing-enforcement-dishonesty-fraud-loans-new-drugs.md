# Addendum: Enforcement resistance and dishonesty list, forged-material loan fraud, and sweets/drinks from strangers · Verification Record (2026-09-08)

Task origin: A reader raised four things—what to do after being listed as a dishonest person, whether the short-video practice of "transferring all money and the house to relatives and registering the villa in a company name as an employee dorm" works to evade enforcement, the gang that forged materials to take out loans for "wool-pulling" and then fled overseas with the money, and protecting against new-type drugs (not eating sweets from strangers, not drinking drinks given by others in public).

Existing coverage: Section 7, Entry 20 only covers the duration and removal of the dishonesty list (the path back); it does not cover what to do or not do during the enforcement period; Section 8, Entry 12 only covers IOUs and guarantees; Section 9, Entry 4 covers cash withdrawal and transfer for others (two cards), not loan fraud; Section 22, Entry 3 covers leaving when someone hands you something in an entertainment venue, but not the "being drugged" side. All four are non-duplicates; the landing points are 2 new entries in Section 8 (Entries 12 and 13), 1 new entry in Section 9 (Entry 5), and 1 new entry in Section 22 (Entry 4). Old Section 8 Entry 13 onward, Section 9 Entry 5 onward, and Section 22 Entry 4 onward are renumbered.
