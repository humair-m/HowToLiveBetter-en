## Several Writing Decisions

- **Entry 2's cost is recorded as "early or late", not as the treatment itself**. The action in this entry is "don't push a treatment that has a window past an exam"; the money and time the treatment itself requires do not change with timing, so the cost label is recorded as `money=0, time=few, willpower=some`, while the cost field notes that the brace itself has separate costs and requires at least 18 hours of daily wear, so readers don't think it's free.
- **Scoliosis is used as the example, rather than a vague "don't delay"**. It satisfies three conditions simultaneously: it has a randomized trial, it has a window tied to bone age, and it happens to be one of the key items in student physicals, so it can connect to Entry 5. Strabismus, amblyopia, dental caries, and elective surgery for congenital heart disease are only named in the Notes, without writing unverified windows.
- **Entry 6 given B**. USPSTF is a recommendation grade rather than a convertible effect size, and is a US recommendation; there is no corresponding official screening recommendation in China; the body explains this.
- **"One psychological assessment per school year" was not written as a screening tool**. The original phrasing of the ten measures is "each student generally receives no more than 1 psychological assessment per school year"; this is a rule controlling assessment frequency, not a diagnostic basis; the body accordingly writes "assessment is not diagnosis".
- **Entry 11 did not write the coverage scope of free programs**. Policy library searches with `searchfield=title` for "children's oral health" and "healthy oral cavity" returned zero hits; no verbatim verifiable national document was obtained, so it only writes "many regions have free programs; ask the local CDC or community health service center".
- **The only emergency quantified was testicular torsion**. It is one of the few conditions with a systematic review of survival by hour segment; the perforation-and-delay time curve for appendicitis could not be obtained this round (Europe PMC title searches returned no results), so it is only named in the Notes, without numbers.
- **Section 6 was also incidentally modified**: the line "Childhood myopia prevention and control is another topic; this book has not verified the evidence in this area and gives no advice" was changed to point to Section 30, Entries 4, 10, and 7 — this section has now verified the evidence in that area; the original statement is void.
