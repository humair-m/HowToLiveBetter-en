### 1. Road Traffic Safety Law (2021 revision)
- URL: <https://jtgl.beijing.gov.cn/jgj/jgxx/flfg/fl/205308/index.html> (Beijing Municipal Public Security Traffic Management Bureau repost page, official local site)
- Page title "Road Traffic Safety Law of the People's Republic of China [2021 revision]" (中华人民共和国道路交通安全法【2021年修订】), with version line: "Revised at the 28th meeting of the Standing Committee of the 13th National People's Congress on April 29, 2021." Confirmed.
- Article 70, paragraph 1: "Where a traffic accident occurs on a road, the vehicle driver shall immediately stop, protect the scene; where personal injury or death is caused, the vehicle driver shall immediately rescue the injured and promptly report to the on-duty traffic police or the public security traffic management department. Where the scene is altered due to the rescue of the injured, the position shall be marked." Paragraph 2: "Where no personal injury or death is caused and the parties have no dispute over the facts and causes, the scene may be immediately vacated and traffic restored, and the parties may negotiate damages themselves"; paragraph 3: "Where only minor property damage is caused and the basic facts are clear, the parties shall first vacate the scene before negotiating."
- Article 91, all five paragraphs located: "Driving a motor vehicle after drinking alcohol: the motor vehicle driver's license shall be temporarily confiscated for six months, and a fine of 1,000 to 2,000 yuan shall be imposed. Where a person is punished for driving a motor vehicle after drinking alcohol and again drives a motor vehicle after drinking alcohol: detention of up to ten days, a fine of 1,000 to 2,000 yuan, and revocation of the motor vehicle driver's license." "Driving a motor vehicle while intoxicated … the motor vehicle driver's license shall be revoked and criminal liability pursued in accordance with the law; re-issuance of the motor vehicle driver's license within five years is not permitted." "Driving an operating motor vehicle after drinking alcohol: detention for fifteen days, a fine of 5,000 yuan, revocation of the motor vehicle driver's license, and no re-issuance within five years" "Driving an operating motor vehicle while intoxicated … no re-issuance of the motor vehicle driver's license within ten years, and after re-issuance, the person may not drive an operating motor vehicle." "Driving a motor vehicle after drinking alcohol or while intoxicated and causing a major traffic accident that constitutes a crime … no re-issuance of the motor vehicle driver's license for life."
- Article 101, paragraph 2: "Where a person flees after causing a traffic accident, the public security traffic management department shall revoke the motor vehicle driver's license, and no re-issuance for life."
- A directly openable full-text link to the 2021 version on the NPC / Chinese government website was not found (the gov.cn Presidential Order No. 81 page only has the amending decision); the Beijing Traffic Management Bureau repost page is cited, with a note in the source column.

### 2. Implementing Regulations for the Road Traffic Safety Law (2017 revision)
- URL: <http://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=75> (Ministry of Justice National Administrative Regulations Database)
- Version line: "Promulgated by State Council Order No. 405 on April 30, 2004, revised according to the 'Decision of the State Council on Amending Certain Administrative Regulations' of October 7, 2017." Confirmed.
- Article 92: "Where a party flees after a traffic accident, the fleeing party bears full responsibility. However, where there is evidence that the other party was also at fault, liability may be reduced. Where a party intentionally damages or forges the scene or destroys evidence, that party bears full responsibility."
- Article 86: "Where a traffic accident causing no personal injury or death occurs between motor vehicles, or between a motor vehicle and a non-motor vehicle, on a road, and the parties have no dispute over the facts and causes, after recording the time and place of the traffic accident, the name and contact information of the other party, the motor vehicle plate number, driver's license number, insurance certificate number, and point of collision, and signing jointly, the parties shall vacate the scene and negotiate damages themselves."

### 3. Criminal Law (1997 revised text)
- URL: <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/201802/t20180206_364975.shtml> (Supreme People's Procuratorate law and regulations database)
- Page title "Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China (1997 revision)". Confirmed. Articles 20, 133, 234, 243, 246, 258, 266 cited in this section have not been modified by any subsequent amendment since 1997 (verified against amendment pages below); Article 246 had a third paragraph added by Amendment (Nine).
- Article 20: "… constitutes legitimate self-defense, with no criminal liability. Where legitimate self-defense manifestly exceeds the necessary limits and causes major harm, criminal liability shall apply, but the punishment shall be reduced or exempted."
- Article 133: "… imprisonment of up to three years or criminal detention; where the person flees after a traffic accident or has other particularly serious circumstances, imprisonment of three to seven years; where the flight causes the death of the person, imprisonment of seven years or more."
- Article 234: "Whoever intentionally injures another person's body shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to three years, criminal detention, or control. Whoever commits the crime in the preceding paragraph and causes serious injury to another person shall be sentenced to imprisonment of three to ten years"
- Article 243: "Whoever fabricates facts to falsely accuse and frame another person, with the intent of subjecting that person to criminal prosecution, where the circumstances are serious, shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to three years, criminal detention, or control; where serious consequences are caused, imprisonment of three to ten years … Where the accusation is not intentionally false but is a mistaken accusation, or the report is not in accordance with the facts, the preceding two paragraphs shall not apply."
- Article 246: "Whoever by violence or other means publicly insults another person or fabricates facts to defame another person, where the circumstances are serious, shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to three years, criminal detention, control, or deprivation of political rights. The crime in the preceding paragraph is handled only upon complaint, except where it seriously endangers social order and state interests."
- Article 258: "Whoever has a spouse and commits bigamy, or who knowingly marries a person who has a spouse, shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to two years or criminal detention."
- Article 266: "Whoever defrauds public or private property, where the amount is relatively large, shall be sentenced to imprisonment of up to three years, criminal detention, or control, and shall also or only be fined; where the amount is huge or there are other serious circumstances, imprisonment of three to ten years and a fine; where the amount is particularly huge or there are other particularly serious circumstances, imprisonment of ten years or more or life imprisonment, and a fine or confiscation of property."

### 13. Supreme People's Court Bridewealth Dispute Provisions (法释〔2024〕1 号)
- URL: <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/423442.html>
- Title, document number 法释〔2024〕1 号, adopted 2023-11-13, effective 2024-02-01 all confirmed.
- Article 2: "Extortion of property in connection with marriage is prohibited. Where one party, under the name of bridewealth (cai li, 彩礼), extorts property in connection with marriage, and the other party requests its return, the people's court shall support the request."
- Article 3: "Property delivered under the following circumstances does not constitute bridewealth: (1) gifts and gift money of little value given on holidays, birthdays, or other occasions of special commemorative significance; (2) daily consumption expenditure for the purpose of expressing or enhancing affection; (3) other property of little value."
- Article 5: "Where both parties have completed marriage registration and cohabit, and at divorce one party requests the return of bridewealth given according to custom, the people's court generally shall not support the request. However, where the period of cohabitation is short and the bridewealth amount is excessively high, the people's court may, considering the actual use of the bridewealth and the dowry circumstances, and taking into account factors such as the amount of bridewealth, the period of cohabitation, pregnancy, and the fault of both parties, in conjunction with local customs, determine whether to return it and the specific proportion. In determining whether the bridewealth amount is excessively high, the people's court shall consider factors such as the per-capita disposable income of residents in the place of residence of the party giving the bridewealth, the family's financial circumstances, and local customs."
- Article 6: "Where the parties have not completed marriage registration but have cohabited, and one party requests the return of bridewealth given according to custom, the people's court shall, considering the actual use of the bridewealth and the dowry circumstances … determine whether to return it and the specific proportion."

### 14. Civil Code Marriage and Family Section Interpretation (I) (法释〔2020〕22 号)
- URL: <https://www.court.gov.cn/fabu/xiangqing/282071.html>
- Title, document number, effective 2021-01-01 confirmed.
- Article 5: "Where a party requests the return of bridewealth given according to custom, and the following circumstances are verified, the people's court shall support the request: (1) the parties have not completed marriage registration; (2) the parties have completed marriage registration but in fact have not cohabited; (3) the bridewealth was paid before marriage and caused the payer financial difficulty. Application of items 2 and 3 of the preceding paragraph shall be conditional on divorce."
- Article 29: "Before the parties marry, where parents fund the purchase of housing for both parties, the funding shall be deemed a gift to their own child, unless the parents have expressly stated it is a gift to both parties. After the parties marry, where parents fund the purchase of housing for both parties, it shall be handled according to agreement; in the absence of an agreement or where the agreement is unclear, it shall be handled according to the principles in item 4, paragraph 1, Article 1062 of the Civil Code."
- Article 31: "Property specified as personal property of one spouse under Article 1063 of the Civil Code does not, by virtue of the continuation of the marriage, become marital joint property. However, where the parties have agreed otherwise, that agreement prevails."
- Article 32: "Before marriage or during the marriage, where the parties agree to gift real property owned by one party to the other party or to make it jointly owned, and the gifting party revokes the gift before the property's change-of-registration, and the other party requests a judgment of continued performance, the people's court may handle the matter according to Article 658 of the Civil Code."
