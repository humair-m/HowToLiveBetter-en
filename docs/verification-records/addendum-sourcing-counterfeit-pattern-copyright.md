# Addendum: Sourcing Counterfeits and Pattern Copyright · Verification Record (2026-09-08)

Task origin: A reader raised two real incidents — "many people selling things, with employees under them sourcing counterfeit goods and getting sentenced, selling cosmetics"; "and people selling clothing, printing others' copyrighted images, also getting caught," and believed "they didn't change anything at all; I think at minimum they should change it, add an icon, change the color or something."

Existing coverage: Section 11's copyright material only covers software, open-source licenses, and works made for hire (Entries 11 and 12), aimed at employed technical workers; Section 12, Entry 19's Note contains only one sentence "check the China National Intellectual Property Administration trademark search system for similar registered marks before applying before investment; this section has not verified the trademark law provisions, just a reminder." The two red lines for people doing business — "is the sourced goods counterfeit" and "is the image used someone else's" — have no place in the book. Landing point: Section 12 gets two new entries (Entries 16 and 17), with the original Entries 16 and 17 renumbered to 18 and 19; the intra-section "see Entry 17 of this section" (the exit entry) is changed to Entry 19; the reminder sentence in Entry 15's Note is changed to point to the new entries. No external references by entry number to Section 12 exist.

**The reader's idea needs to be explicitly corrected in the body**: changing the color, adding an icon does not constitute a defense. Two statutory reasons: Copyright Law Article 52 item (6) places "using a work by adaptation, translation, annotation, or other means" alongside direct copying as infringing acts; Trademark Law directly lists "using on the same kind of goods a trademark similar to its registered trademark, or using on similar goods a trademark identical or similar to its registered trademark, likely to cause confusion" as an infringing circumstance — "similar" is itself within range. So Entry 17's title is written as "changing a color, adding an icon does not count as 'having changed it.'"
