# New: Section 31 — What Paths Are There After Eighteen

2026-09-10. The reader's question was "should I join the military, what are the benefits, how many life choices are there — is it only wage work and studying?"

**This section was reworked once; the reason is recorded up front**: the first version wrote 10 of 11 entries all on joining the military. The reader immediately pointed out: "How come you wrote everything on joining the military — I was just giving an example." The problem wasn't whether the content was correct, but in misreading the question — what was asked was "what paths are there," and joining the military was only an example. The revised version repositioned as a path map: Entry 1 lists the statutory thresholds for eight paths, military service compressed to 5 entries (by merging; content largely preserved), plus 6 additional entries on other paths, 12 entries in total. **Lesson: the example a reader gives is not the question itself; confirm which question to answer before drafting.**

## Two corrections that must be remembered

- **The Regulations on the Placement of Retired Soldiers (State Council and Central Military Commission Order No. 608, 2011) was repealed on 2024-09-01.** Currently in force is the Regulations on the Placement of Retired Military Personnel (Order No. 787), Article 93 of which states: "These Regulations shall take effect on September 1, 2024. The Regulations on the Placement of Retired Soldiers shall be repealed simultaneously." The conditions for placement in employment changed in wording: the old version required "non-commissioned officers who have served 12 years" and "those who received a second-class merit award or higher in peacetime, or a third-class merit award or higher in wartime"; the new version requires "military sergeants who have served 12 years," "individuals who have received a medal or honorary title," "individuals who have received a third-class wartime merit, second-class merit, or higher," "individuals who have received a first-class commendation," plus those disabled at grades 5–8 in combat and children of martyrs. Online guides generally still cite the old regulation.
- **The current standard for tuition compensation for military service is 20,000 yuan per year for undergraduate/junior-college students and 25,000 yuan for graduate students** (from the fall 2024 semester). History: 8,000/12,000 (Caikejiao [2019] No. 19) → 16,000/20,000 (Jiaocai [2023] No. 4) → 20,000/25,000 (Caijiao [2024] No. 188). The pair 12,000/16,000 never appears in search results — anyone citing it is making an error. The Ministry of Veterans Affairs' 2022 policy summary still states 8,000/12,000.

One further point: **The Provisions on Civil Servant Recruitment does not contain a "targeted recruitment" clause.** After reading the full text word by word and searching, the four words "grassroots," "targeted," "service-grassroots programs," and "veteran" appeared 0 times; the Civil Servant Law only says "higher-level organs should focus on openly selecting civil servants from grassroots organs." The real basis for targeted recruitment is the 2017 General Office of the Party Central Committee and General Office of the State Council document "Opinions on Further Guiding and Encouraging College Graduates to Work at the Grassroots" and the 10% hard target in Renlishebu [2021] No. 32. Entry 7 hangs its source on this.

## Tools and Site Tests Added This Round

- **The backend API of `flk.npc.gov.cn` (National Laws and Regulations Database) was this round's biggest gain** — laws enacted by the National People's Congress can finally be retrieved verbatim in full: `POST /law-search/search/list` (Content-Type application/json; the body's keys must all be present, missing one returns code 500), returns `rows[].bbbs/sxx`; **`sxx` is the validity status: 3 effective, 2 amended, 1 repealed, 4 not yet effective** — that's how Order No. 608's repeal was discovered; then `GET /law-search/download/mobile?format=docx&bbbs=<id>&fileId=` downloads the docx, unzip and extract `word/document.xml`, strip tags, and you get the verbatim full text.
