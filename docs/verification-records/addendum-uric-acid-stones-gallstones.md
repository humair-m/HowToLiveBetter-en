## III. Why a new section was not opened

The issue suggested a "modern common-case medical management" chapter. Not adopted, three reasons:

1. **Genre mismatch.** Each entry in this book answers "what you pay and what you get back", as a decision guide; a diagnostic handbook answers "how to diagnose, which drug, what dose", which the reader cannot decide anyway and should not act on after reading. It cannot be mechanically graded by the existing benefit-magnitude rules, and the risk is real.
2. **The three highs are already covered; a new section would massively duplicate.** Blood pressure measurement and medication target-attainment is in Section 1, Entry 7; checking fasting glucose after age 35 if overweight is in Section 1, Entry 8; adherence to antihypertensives and statins is in Section 2, Entry 8 (with grade-A effect size); low-sodium salt, sugary drinks, BMI, exercise are across multiple entries in Section 2; chronic-disease adherence, 12-week long-term prescriptions, cross-region chronic/special disease settlement, and diabetic complication screening occupy the entirety of Section 16; tiered diagnosis and treatment is in Section 24.
3. **The only real gaps are uric acid, kidney stones, and gallstones**—a repository-wide search found these three previously mentioned only in passing in the notes of other entries. This round fills exactly those three.
