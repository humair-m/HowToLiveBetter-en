## Trade-off Notes

- **Did not write conclusions like "luxury goods don't hold value"**: China has no citable official secondhand-transaction statistics; secondhand-platform listing prices are not transaction prices. So Section 5, Entry 33 only writes the hard numbers verifiable on both sides — the consumption-tax rate embedded in the purchase price, and the rates and forfeit rules of pawn (the only realization channel with a statutory price cap) — and notes in the Note that "one year, 50%" is a rough estimate of monthly fee × 12, used to see the order of magnitude.
- **Does not comment on categories, does not judge motives**: If you like it, wear it as a consumption good; the book only blocks the "for value preservation / to sell higher later" purchase rationale. Section 6, Entry 21 likewise states it does not judge belief or custom, only negating "verifiable efficacy and predictive validity."
- **Section 6, Entry 22 marked disputed**: The included studies are mostly cross-sectional correlation, and the authors themselves say more experimental, longitudinal, and developmental studies are needed; r = -0.19 explains only a few percentage points; both studies are foreign samples, with no citable Chinese-population counterpart — all written into the Note, so set B not A.
- **Section 31 expanded as a "path map," not adding more on joining the military** (per the 2026-09-10 user criticism): the three new entries are all not military. Entry 1's threshold table expanded from eight paths to twelve (adding government-funded normal-school students, targeted medical students, overseas labor, platform-based work and flexible employment), and the section opening adds a paragraph stating the inclusion boundary — entering an SOE, going into a factory, becoming a seafarer, and doing freelance work have no national-level public documents to consult, and their conditions are not written from impression.
- **Did not write about selected civil servants (选调生)**: The Organization Department of the CPC Central Committee has no verifiable national-level public regulation, and provincial announcements differ; per project rules, better not to write.
- **Entrepreneurship guarantee loan placed in Section 31, not Section 12**: Section 12, Entry 1's stance is "only use money you can afford to lose; don't borrow to start"; this entry is "if you've already decided to borrow, which tier has the lowest cost," an available resource on the path; the Note explicitly states it does not overturn Section 12, Entry 1. The 2020 pandemic-era "individuals up to 200,000" finance-and-tax notice was a temporary document; the current effective document is Caijin [2023] No. 75, noted in the Note.
- **Link reachability**: All 11 new Chinese official links tested 200 this round; the three doi.org links return 403 (APA paywall, consistent with Section 6's existing entries, not treated as dead).
