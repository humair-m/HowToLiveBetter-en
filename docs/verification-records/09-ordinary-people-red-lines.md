## Candidates not included and reasons
- Drunk cycling on electric bicycles: the distinction between electric bicycles and electric motorcycles and the relevant case law are only on China Court Network (could not be opened); Guiding Case 271, when opened, is a drunk-driving assisted-driving case unrelated to electric bicycles; not included per the "write only what can be verified" requirement; Section 8 Entry 4 title already covers electric vehicles.
- Fireworks and firecrackers, open-air fires: the 2025 Public Security Administration Punishment Law has no general provision on discharge (only Article 28 on discharge at sports and cultural venues, and Article 43 on releasing fire-carrying objects into the air); the original text of the Fireworks and Firecrackers Safety Management Regulations and the Forest Fire Prevention Regulations was not verified; not included.
- Lending "two cards" / helping-information-network-crime, online defamation: already covered in Section 8; this section's Entry 4 only addresses the angle of concealing or disguising criminal proceeds and cross-references Section 8.
