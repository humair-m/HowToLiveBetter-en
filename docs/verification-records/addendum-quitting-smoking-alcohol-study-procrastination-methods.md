The user-selected approach is **to distribute across the four existing sections, not to open a new one** (the alternative candidate was a new Section 34 "How to actually follow through").

## II. What was not written in, and why

- **Pomodoro, time-boxing**: still no directly tested randomized trials. Section 4, Entry 8's note already states this, and the Section 6 verification record also noted "multitasking/Pomodoro: no direct evidence, not included as required". The few trials found this round that use Pomodoro as a CBT behavioral module to compare are OSF preprints, not journal articles; not adopted under the repository's citation rules.
- **"It takes an average of 66 days to form a habit"**: this number is in the body of Lally 2010, but the abstract only gives the range of 18 to 254 days. By the rule "numbers must be findable in the original text", the body only states the range.
- **Effect sizes of spaced learning**: The abstract of Latimier, Peyre, Ramus (2020, Educational Psychology Review, doi:10.1007/s10648-020-09572-8) could not be retrieved on Springer, Semantic Scholar, or Europe PMC (Semantic Scholar explicitly states the abstract is hidden by the publisher). So Section 23, Entry 15 does not state a g value; it uses only the study scale and the rule "the longer the retention interval, the longer the optimal spacing" verifiable in the Cepeda 2006 abstract, plus Dunlosky's rating.
- **National quit-smoking hotline 400-888-5531**: no verifiable official page found (nhc.gov.cn persistent 412). So the body only states 12320 and the "China Smoking Cessation Platform" mini-program, both verifiable on the Chinese CDC official site.
- **Domestic indications of acamprosate and naltrexone**: nmpa.gov.cn persistent 412; no verifiable approval information retrieved. So Section 2, Entry 22 only states "defer to the physician and drug label", without stating whether the indication is approved domestically.
- **Case-fatality rate of delirium tremens**: the secondhand "1% to 4%" was not adopted. The annual mortality rate (8.0% / 5.0% / 3.6%) and standardized mortality ratio of 9.8 verifiable in the Norwegian national registry cohort were used instead.
