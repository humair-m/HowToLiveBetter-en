# Addendum: Revenge-Style Extreme Violence, Four Entries · Verification Record (2026-09-08)

Task origin: A reader raised—"recent domestic violent incidents are numerous and reckless: someone owed back pay who went straight to the boss's home and killed him; someone driving randomly into pedestrians; a pilot who took the whole plane down with him in suicide. These are all impulsive, harming others and self, zero benefit, purely to vent."

Existing coverage: Section 8, Entry 9 only writes "call the police first, don't strike in a conflict" and stops at administrative penalties and minor injuries, with nothing above that; Section 9, Entry 18 calculates the cost of fighting, also only up to intentional injury. Whole-book search for "revenge (报复)", "venting anger (泄愤)", "endangering public safety by dangerous means (以危险方法危害公共安全)" found only one crime-name mention in the Section 9 high-altitude object-throwing entry; no entry addresses "don't kill" on this side. On the psychological-crisis side, the book covers only self-harm: Section 1, Entry 25 (suicidal ideation, restricting lethal means, 12356) and Section 3, Entry 19 (what to do first when mood is low); no entry covers "wanting to harm others" or "what to do when someone close to you shows such thoughts".

Landing: Section 8 adds 4 new entries (Entries 10 to 13, inserted after the conflict entry, progressing from a specific grievance target, to indiscriminate, to one's own impulse, to the impulse of those around you—four progressive tiers); old Entries 10 to 24 are renumbered 14 to 28. Cross-section references synchronized: Section 9 (two places: 12→16, 22→26), Section 10 (one place: 12→16), Section 12 (two places: 12→16, 22→26), Section 17 (one place: 11→15), Section 19 (one place: 13→17), Section 22 (one place: 23→27); Section 9, Entry 18's note had a sentence added pointing to Section 8, Entries 11 to 14.

On "a pilot taking the whole plane down with him in suicide": the actionable landing for Chinese readers on extended-suicide cases is not in aviation-industry regulation (two-in-cockpit and similar are company and regulator business), but in "people around knew but did not say anything", so it is written as evidence in Entry 13, not as a separate entry.

Benefit magnitude "large" (metric free; what is avoided is the death penalty); cost recorded as money=0 time=medium willpower=yes—time is the months of running labor inspection and arbitration; willpower is the endurance of not striking during those months.

The body deliberately writes two judgments without numbers: First, "his grievance was with the school and that pay, but he stabbed his classmates; not a yuan of that money was recovered"—this follows directly from the notification's facts. Graded A (the statute is verifiable word-for-word; the BEA report is the final report of an official accident-investigation body). The 150 figure is the sum of crew and passengers in the report; the report itself does not separately state this total, so the body lists the composition.

The thing this entry most needs to prevent is misreading, so the note locks in three boundaries: only for genuine danger signals, not angry words; cannot be used to commit a disobedient family member to hospital; and the law also states that re-diagnosis cannot confirm serious mental disorder and so hospitalization is not permitted. If unsure how to act, two exits are given: call 12356 first to ask, or call 110 to have police respond on site.

Cost recorded as money=few (registration and consultation fee) time=medium (accompanying to the hospital) willpower=some; cost-effectiveness tier accordingly "average"—this entry is not something that can be done casually.
