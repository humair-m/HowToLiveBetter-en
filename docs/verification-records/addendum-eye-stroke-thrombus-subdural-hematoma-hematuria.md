Graded B: ICOPER is observational registry data; WRIGHT is an official project bulletin; neither has an effect size for "early recognition changes outcome". Benefit magnitude "large". Literature not adopted: Barritt DW, Jordan SC (1960). Anticoagulant drugs in the treatment of pulmonary embolism. A controlled trial. Lancet, 1(7138), 1309-1312 (DOI 10.1016/s0140-6736(60)92299-6). This is the classic controlled trial of anticoagulation for pulmonary embolism; the DOI and source were verified, but Europe PMC has no abstract and the death counts verifiable word-for-word could not be retrieved this round, so its numbers are not cited.
