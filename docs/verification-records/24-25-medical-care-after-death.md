# Verification Record: New Sections 24 and 25

Verification date: 2026-09-07. Added Section 24 "Medical Care: How to Spend Less Money and Avoid Detours" with 6 entries, and Section 25 "What to Do After Someone Passes Away" with 5 entries. The book went from 333 to 344 entries, and from 23 to 25 sections.

Both sections are adjacent to existing chapters; boundaries were drawn before writing: benefit eligibility and assistance are in Section 7, chronic-disease long-term management in Section 16, acute-event handling in Section 13, pre-death arrangements for the elderly in Section 17, and workplace-death benefits in Section 19. These two sections cover only what those chapters do not; overlapping points are written as mutual cross-references.

## IV. Metrics and Benefit Magnitude

Of the six entries in Section 24, four have a money metric and two a time metric. The benefit magnitude is set per the money thresholds: deductible and reimbursement rate differences are in the hundreds-to-thousands of yuan range, set to "medium"; none reach the ten-thousand-yuan level, so this section has no "large". The first three entries of Section 25 are about avoiding improper charges of hundreds to thousands of yuan, set to "medium"; Entry 4 involves provident fund balances and social insurance benefits that can reach the ten-thousand-yuan level, set to "large"; Entry 5 has a freedom metric (including personal information), and like the two entries in Section 14 falls into a category for which the thresholds do not apply; per judgment it is set to "medium".

The costs of both sections are almost zero, but multiple entries record "willpower=some": the difficulty is not knowing the rules but being willing, at the moment, to first go to the community clinic, and being willing to scrutinize a single price list during funeral arrangements.
