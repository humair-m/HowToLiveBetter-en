## Several Writing Decisions

- **Did not write that ICD-11 / DSM-5-TR includes prolonged grief disorder**. The original phrasing of Lundorff 2017 is "expected to be included in the forthcoming ICD-11", and Prigerson 2009 is "proposed for DSM-V and ICD-11"; both only reach "proposed/expected". A verbatim verifiable ICD-11 or DSM-5-TR original could not be obtained on this machine, so the body is written as "called complicated grief or prolonged grief disorder in different literature; different criteria require different durations (6 or 12 months); don't get stuck on the month count yourself — go by whether it affects eating, sleeping, working, or childcare, and seek registration", avoiding this unverified claim.
- **Entry 6 given B rather than A**. Li 2014 is a three-country whole-population cohort, and the numbers themselves are A-grade; the downgrade to B is because there is no direct trial evidence from "mortality 50% higher" to "tell the child truthfully, let them participate in the farewell, don't send them away" — this is extrapolation.
- **Entry 9 benefit magnitude set to "medium" rather than "large"**. By the project's threshold the relative reduction should be examined, but the original gives no applicable pooled figure, only direction and group comparisons; per the rule "where numbers are insufficient for determination, use judgment and state what it's based on", here it's set to medium based on "only direction".
- **Entry 8 metric set to money**. Its actual role is "don't spend this consultation fee first"; the benefit falls on the money-saving side; the health endpoint of grief itself is borne by Entry 7.
- **Directions not included**: Bereavement leave days (Ministry of Labor General Salary Office Document [1980] No. 29). State Council policy document library searches with `searchfield=title` for "bereavement leave", "wedding and bereavement leave", "request for wedding and bereavement leave", "employee family visit benefits" all returned zero hits; that library only contains current State Council system documents, and the 1980 Ministry of Labor notice is not among them; a verbatim verifiable official full text could not be found on this machine either; per project rules it is not written.
