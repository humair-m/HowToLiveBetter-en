# Addendum: When Someone Dies, What to Do Right Then (First Five Entries of Section 25) · Verification Record (2026-09-08)

Task origin: The author read Section 25 and noted "what's written is all about problems, with no concrete procedure for what to do when a body is discovered."

Existing coverage: Section 25 originally had 5 entries, all falling on the aftermath — funeral home fee lists, price violations, intermediary registration, housing fund and social security benefits, and the deceased's personal-information rights. The stretch from "someone died" to "obtaining the death certificate, the body entering the funeral home, and canceling the household registration" was completely blank: searching the whole book for "death certificate" only turned up one hit inside Section 25, Entry 4's document list, and "funeral home," "cremation," "morgue," "autopsy," "cancel household registration" returned zero hits.

Landing point: Add 5 new entries at the front of Section 25, ordered by the sequence in which things happen — Entry 1 the scene and calling the police, Entry 2 the death certificate, Entry 3 body transport and cremation, Entry 4 dispute over cause of death and autopsy, Entry 5 canceling the household registration; the original Entries 1–5 become 6–10. The opening paragraph of the section gains one sentence noting that the first five entries are ordered chronologically, while everything after is ordered by cost-effectiveness. Section 25 grows from 5 to 10 entries; the whole book goes from 408 to 413 entries (A 255 → 260, B and C unchanged; high cost-effectiveness 206 → 210, ordinary 130 → 131, very high 72 unchanged, the ratio shifting from 18/50/32 to 17/51/32; the 42 disputed entries and 36 TODOs are unchanged). The Section 25 entry list in README and CLAUDE.md, and the one row in the README reading-guide table, get matching wording added. No other file in the book refers to Section 25 by entry number, so no cross-references need back-editing.

## Entry 1 (Scene and calling the police)

| Source | Verified | Original quotation |
|---|---|---|
| Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (1958). Regulation on Household Registration of the People's Republic of China (Article 8). <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html> (reproduced on the Guangdong Provincial People's Government portal) | Yes | Article 8, paragraph 2: "Where a citizen dies in an accident or the cause of death is unknown, the head of household or the discoverer shall immediately report to the local public security police station or the people's committee of the township or town" |
| State Council (2026). Regulation on Funeral and Burial Management (State Council Order No. 824, effective 2026-03-30). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> | Yes | Article 23: "Where a natural person dies a natural death, a medical and health institution shall issue a death certificate in a timely manner. For the deceased involved in non-natural death cases (incidents) handled by public security organs according to law and not treated by a medical and health institution, the public security organ shall issue the death certificate." |

Graded A: Both are statutory articles that can be verified word for word. Benefit tier is "medium," and the metric is recorded as "freedom" — this entry guards against the investigatory and procedural headaches that come when the scene is disturbed and the cause cannot be stated clearly or the death certificate cannot be issued; it is not about money.

The body-prose instructions "do not move the body, do not wash or redress, do not take away medicine bottles or notes" are operating practice derived from the article "non-natural deaths are certified by the public security organ." They are the author's wording, with no separate statute, so they are not listed as a separate quotation in the Benefit field.

## Entry 2 (Death certificate)

| Source | Verified | Original quotation |
|---|---|---|
| General Office of the former National Health and Family Planning Commission (2014). Specification for Registration Management of Population Death Information (Trial) (Guo Wei Ban Gui Hua Fa [2014] No. 68). <https://mzj.sz.gov.cn/szmz/pc/zwgk/jcxxgk/zcfg/byfw/content/post_2952215.html> (forwarded by the Shenzhen Municipal Civil Affairs Bureau) | Yes | Article 9: For deaths inside a medical and health institution, the certificate is "signed and issued by the medical and health institution responsible for treatment"; for natural deaths at home or other locations, it is "signed and issued by the community health service institution or township (subdistrict) health center of the relevant jurisdiction"; for those judged to be non-natural deaths by public security or judicial organs, the public security or judicial department handles it according to existing regulations and procedures |
| National Health Commission, Ministry of Public Security, Ministry of Civil Affairs, State Administration of Traditional Chinese Medicine, National Administration of Disease Prevention and Control (2026). Notice on Strengthening the Registration and Electronic License Management of Resident Death Medical Certificate Information (Guo Wei Gui Hua Fa [2026] No. 5, 2026-02-27). <https://wjw.fujian.gov.cn/xxgk/fgwj/gjwj/202602/t20260228_7102810.htm> (forwarded by the Fujian Provincial Health Commission) | Yes | "The paper version and the electronic license of the Death Certificate have the same legal effect"; for natural deaths with the cause already established, it shall be issued within one day of death; if lost, one reissue may be applied for from the original issuing institution; if the basic information is incorrect, a re-issuance may be applied for with supporting materials from the original issuing entity (limited to once) |

Graded A: Both are ministry documents that can be verified. Metric recorded as "time," benefit tier "medium" — there is no monetary figure and no mortality rate here, so no mechanical threshold applies, and the call is made by judgment: the death certificate is a shared prerequisite for canceling the household registration, cremation, the housing fund, social security, insurance, and inheritance; if it is missing or its information is wrong, the family ends up shuttling between multiple windows repeatedly, which puts it above "one-off."

Content not written into the body: which of the four copies of the Death Certificate goes to whom. The specification only writes the format requirement of "four copies (the latter three are consistent)" and does not specify a receiving entity copy by copy; practice varies by locality, so the body only states the use and not the copy number.

## Entry 3 (Body transport, storage, and cremation)

| Source | Verified | Original quotation |
|---|---|---|
| State Council (2026). Regulation on Funeral and Burial Management (State Council Order No. 824). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202601/content_7054169.htm> | Yes | Article 24: "The morgue of a medical and health institution shall not be contracted out, shall not provide funeral services, and may provide convenience services for temporary parking of remains"; Article 25: "Body transport, storage, preservation, restoration, and cremation services shall be provided exclusively by funeral homes"; Article 27: "A funeral home shall generally store remains for no more than 3 days; if extended storage is required, the bereaved or the party transferring the remains shall go through the extended storage procedures at the funeral home"; Article 28: "A funeral home shall cremate remains on the basis of the death certificate and the cremation confirmation, and shall issue a cremation certificate"; Article 29: "In areas where cremation is practiced, remains shall be cremated locally or at the nearest funeral home"; Article 8: Provincial governments "steadily expand the scope of areas practicing cremation" on the basis of already-delineated cremation and burial zones; Article 9: "The funeral and burial customs of ethnic minorities shall be respected; those who voluntarily reform their funeral and burial customs shall not be interfered with by others"; Article 60: Anyone who buries remains that should have been cremated, or buries remains or builds graves outside of public cemeteries, rural public-welfare cemeteries, or lawfully designated ecological burial areas, shall be ordered by the civil affairs department at or above the county level to make correction within a time limit |

Graded A. Benefit tier "medium," metric money: this guards against morgue outsourcing and the "one-stop" intermediaries' markup on the transport leg, at the same tier as Entries 6 and 7.

## Entry 4 (Dispute over cause of death and autopsy)

| Source | Verified | Original quotation |
|---|---|---|
| State Council (2018). Regulation on the Prevention and Handling of Medical Disputes (State Council Order No. 701). <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2018-08/31/content_5318057.htm> | Yes | Article 26: "Where a patient dies and the medical and the patient sides dispute the cause of death, an autopsy shall be conducted within 48 hours after the patient's death; where conditions for frozen preservation of the body exist, this may be extended to 7 days"; "An autopsy shall be consented to and signed by the close relatives of the deceased; refusal to sign shall be deemed as the close relatives of the deceased not consenting to the autopsy"; "Where a party disagrees with or delays the autopsy and exceeds the stipulated time, thereby affecting the determination of the cause of death, the party that disagreed or delayed shall bear responsibility"; Article 24: Sealing and unsealing of medical records "shall be conducted in the presence of both the medical and the patient sides" |

Graded A. Benefit tier "large": the determination of the cause of death directly decides whether a claim for medical malpractice compensation can be pursued, with amounts reaching the tens of thousands of yuan or more; willpower is recorded as "some," because the difficulty lies in the family remembering to raise it at that moment. How the autopsy fee is paid in advance and borne could not be obtained in a verifiable provision this round; the body does not state it.

## Entry 5 (Canceling the household registration)

| Source | Verified | Original quotation |
|---|---|---|
| Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (1958). Regulation on Household Registration of the People's Republic of China (Article 8). <http://www.gd.gov.cn/zwgk/wjk/zcfgk/content/mpost_2531969.html> (reproduced on the Guangdong Provincial People's Government portal) | Yes | Article 8, paragraph 1: "When a citizen dies, in cities before burial, and in rural areas within one month, the head of household, relative, supporter, or neighbor shall report the death to the household registration authority and cancel the household registration. If a citizen dies at a temporary residence, the household registration authority at the temporary residence shall notify the household registration authority at the permanent residence to cancel the household registration." |

Graded A. Metric "time," benefit tier "small": only a one-off saving of legwork, so the cost-effectiveness tier lands at "ordinary." The point that fraudulently drawn pensions must be returned is a common-sense consequence with no separate quotation, and is written into the Note rather than as a benefit.
