# Section 12 Source Verification Record (2026-09-07)

Verification method: This session's WebSearch quota was exhausted. For regulation lookup, the China Government Network policy document library search interface (sousuo.www.gov.cn/search-gov/data) was used (only to find URLs, not as a source). Each URL was first opened with WebFetch to confirm the title, document number, and articles; full-text legal pages were additionally downloaded with curl to a scratchpad (s12/page_*.html), then tags were stripped and the original text was located verbatim by "Article X." All quotations below come from local positioning. The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security pages have anti-scraping scripts; WebFetch returned blank, so curl was used with cookies computed by the script to open and obtain the full text (page title and version line verified). The Ministry of Commerce franchise system certificate did not match the domain, causing WebFetch to error; curl -k was used to open and verify the title. The DOI was redirected via doi.org to pubsonline.informs.org, which returned 403; the Crossref API and Semantic Scholar API were used instead to verify the bibliographic record and abstract.

## Confirmed Sources

### 1. Civil Code
- URL: <https://www.spp.gov.cn/spp/fl/202006/t20200602_463888.shtml> (Supreme People's Procuratorate laws and regulations database)
- Page title "Civil Code of the People's Republic of China", version line "Adopted at the third session of the 13th National People's Congress on May 28, 2020." Confirmed by both WebFetch and local positioning.
- Article 56: "The debts of an individual industrial and commercial household (getihu) shall, if the household is operated by an individual, be borne by the individual with personal property; if operated by a family, be borne by the family with family property; where the two cannot be distinguished, be borne with family property."
- Article 184: "Where a rescuer voluntarily performs emergency rescue and thereby causes harm to the rescued person, the rescuer bears no civil liability."
- Article 469: "The parties may conclude a contract in writing, orally, or in another form. A written form is one that can tangibly express the content contained therein, such as a contract document, letter, telegram, telex, or fax."
- Article 585: "The parties may agree that when one party breaches the contract it shall pay a certain amount of liquidated damages to the other party according to the circumstances of the breach... Where the agreed liquidated damages are lower than the loss caused, the people's court or arbitration institution may, upon request of the party, increase them; where the agreed liquidated damages are excessively higher than the loss caused, the people's court or arbitration institution may, upon request of the party, reduce them appropriately."
- Article 586: "The parties may agree that one party pay a deposit (dingjin) to the other as security for the claim. The deposit contract is formed when the deposit is actually paid. The amount of the deposit shall be agreed by the parties; however, it shall not exceed 20 percent of the amount of the principal contract, and any excess has no effect as a deposit."
- Article 587: "Where the party paying the deposit fails to perform the debt... it has no right to claim return of the deposit; where the party receiving the deposit fails to perform the debt... it shall return the deposit in double."
- Article 588: "Where the parties have agreed on both liquidated damages and a deposit, when one party breaches the contract, the other party may choose to apply the liquidated damages clause or the deposit clause."
- Article 668: "A loan contract shall be in writing, except where a loan between natural persons is otherwise agreed. The contents of a loan contract generally include clauses on the type of loan, currency, purpose, amount, interest rate, term, and method of repayment."
- Article 681: "A guarantee contract is a contract by which, to secure the realization of the claim, the guarantor and the creditor agree that when the debtor fails to perform the due debt or a situation agreed by the parties occurs, the guarantor performs the debt or assumes liability."
- Article 687: "Where the parties agree in the guarantee contract that the guarantor assumes guarantee liability when the debtor is unable to perform the debt, it is a general guarantee. The guarantor of a general guarantee has the right to refuse to assume guarantee liability to the creditor before the principal contract dispute is tried or arbitrated and the debtor's property is enforced according to law and the debt still cannot be performed."
- Article 688: "Where the parties agree in the guarantee contract that the guarantor and the debtor bear joint liability for the debt, it is a joint liability guarantee. Where the debtor of a joint liability guarantee fails to perform the due debt... the creditor may request the debtor to perform the debt or request the guarantor to assume guarantee liability within the scope of the guarantee."
- Article 1064: "Debts incurred under joint signatures of both spouses or under a joint expression of intent subsequently ratified by one spouse... are joint debts of the spouses. A debt incurred by one spouse in his or her own name during the marriage that exceeds the needs of the family's daily life is not a joint debt of the spouses; however, this does not apply where the creditor can prove that the debt was used for the joint life of the spouses, for joint production or business operations, or was based on a joint expression of intent of both spouses."

### 2. Company Law (2023 Revision)
- URL: <https://www.gov.cn/yaowen/liebiao/202312/content_6923395.htm> (China Government Network)
- Page title "Company Law of the People's Republic of China", version line includes "Second revision adopted at the seventh session of the Standing Committee of the 14th National People's Congress on December 29, 2023." Confirmed by both WebFetch and local positioning; article numbers were checked against the 2023 revised text.
- Article 4: "A shareholder of a limited liability company is liable to the company to the extent of its subscribed capital contribution; a shareholder of a joint stock limited company is liable to the company to the extent of the shares it has subscribed."
- Article 23: "Where a shareholder of a company abuses the independent legal person status of the company and the limited liability of shareholders to evade debts and seriously harms the interests of the company's creditors, it shall bear joint liability for the company's debts... Where a company has only one shareholder and the shareholder is unable to prove that the company's property is independent of the shareholder's own property, it shall bear joint liability for the company's debts."
- Article 47: "The registered capital of a limited liability company is the total amount of capital contributions subscribed by all shareholders registered with the company registration authority. The total amount of subscribed capital contributions of all shareholders shall be paid in full by the shareholders within five years from the date of establishment of the company in accordance with the company's articles of association."
- Article 49: "Shareholders shall pay in full and on time the subscribed capital contributions specified in the company's articles of association... Where a shareholder fails to pay the capital contribution in full and on time, in addition to paying the full amount to the company, it shall also be liable for compensation for losses caused to the company."
- Article 50: "Where, at the time of establishment of a limited liability company, a shareholder fails to actually pay the capital contribution in accordance with the company's articles of association... the other shareholders at the time of establishment shall bear joint liability with that shareholder to the extent of the underpaid contribution."
- Article 53: "After a company is established, shareholders shall not withdraw their capital contributions. Where the preceding paragraph is violated, the shareholders shall return the withdrawn capital contributions."
- Article 54: "Where a company is unable to pay its due debts, the company or a creditor of a due claim has the right to require shareholders who have subscribed capital contributions but whose capital contribution period has not yet expired to pay the capital contributions in advance."
