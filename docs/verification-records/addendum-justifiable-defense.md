## What the body did not write

- Defensive devices (self-installed electric fences, animal traps, etc.) are not written: not covered by the Guidance Opinions, judicial practice is divided, and no verbatim citable official position found.
- The term "unlimited defense right" is not used: the statute and Guidance Opinions both use "special defense"; the former is a secondhand-reader term.
- The actual sentencing range for excessive defense is not written: only individual cases (Case No. 46 Zhu Fengshan, second instance reduced to 7 years); no official statistics; giving a number would be taken as a universal value.
- Home invasion is not listed separately: Article 5 of the Guidance Opinions states "unlawful intrusion into another's dwelling... defense may be exercised", already covered by the general "unlawful infringement" wording in this entry; listing it separately would crowd out the three boundary sentences.
- The daily amount of state compensation is not repeated here; points to Section 8, Entry 34 (state compensation after detention).
