## III. Metric and benefit magnitude

- Wild mushrooms and gas appliances set as "mortality, large": the intervention is complete avoidance of a lethal exposure, not a percentage reduction.
- Compulsory traffic insurance set as "money, large", and one of the few "money=many" entries in the book (commercial third-party liability insurance runs to a thousand-plus yuan in annual premiums): what it buys back is tail risk; compensation beyond the compulsory insurance limit has no backstop.
- Prepayment, live-stream commerce, personal-account mutual aid, and minor top-ups all set as "money, medium": amounts in the hundreds to tens of thousands.
- Dog-keeping liability set as "money, large": medical costs plus lost income plus vaccines and immunoglobulin—tens of thousands per incident is common, and the magnitude is higher when children are involved.
- The minor top-up entry records time cost as "medium": the real cost is evidence-gathering and repeated back-and-forth with the platform, not filing the claim itself.
