Content not written into the body and reasons:

- **The specific number of days for "Newborns enrolled within 90 days of birth can enjoy benefits from the date of birth" is not written**. This is widely circulated, but this round no verbatim verifiable unified national statement was obtained from the China Government Network or the National Healthcare Security Administration's public documents; only the 2024 briefing statement "enrollment online with the birth medical certificate, direct reimbursement on discharge" was obtained. Therefore, the body writes only the verifiable portion and notes in the body that the day-count rule differs by locality and should follow the local healthcare security authority.
- **The specific grading of the five-color management of maternal pregnancy risk assessment**. The "Work Specifications for Maternal Pregnancy Risk Assessment and Management" is not in the State Council policy document library (re-checked with `searchfield=title` still no hit); verifiable original text could not be obtained, so the body only writes the qualitative statement "the earlier the card is established, the earlier the pregnancy risk grading is determined" without specifying colors and corresponding measures.
- **National popularity rate of painless childbirth and Chinese cesarean-section rate**. No verbatim verifiable official annual statistics were obtained; not written.
- **Specific methods of fetal movement counting (counts per hour, counts per 12 hours)**. Formal fetal movement counting protocols have not shown a mortality-reduction effect in large randomized trials, which is not the same thing as "seek care if fetal movement markedly decreases"; the body retains only the latter (merged into Entry 7's CDC list) and does not provide a counting metric.
- Entry 8 is graded B rather than A: The Beijing Municipal Health Commission's popular-science page gives the management steps but does not explain the cord prolapse mechanism; the mechanism sentence comes from Beijing Shunyi Maternal and Child Health Hospital (an official public medical institution's website), and neither material is a guideline or meta-analysis.
- Entry 10's benefit magnitude is set to "medium" rather than "large": The WHO statement gives the negative conclusion "no further improvement above 10%", not an effect size of "how much mortality is reduced by not performing non-medically-indicated cesarean section".

Section 13, Entry 37 is graded B rather than A: the time limits and treatment course come from official popular-science and technical guidelines (trial), not randomized trials; PEP itself has, and can have, no placebo-controlled trial. The follow-up time points (at 4 to 6 weeks, 3 months) are written in the body's Notes with the explanation "per physician's orders", and are not marked as verified official figures.

Section 1, Entry 28 records "willpower=some" for cost: the difficulty is consistent use, not spending money. Entry 29 records "money=0, willpower=some" for cost: the regulation explicitly states free, and the willpower is "willingness to get tested". Section 13, Entry 37 records "money=much" for cost: a single course of medication costs thousands of yuan and is self-paid in most regions; no unified national fee or reimbursement rule was obtained this round, so the body writes "self-paid in most regions" without giving a specific amount range beyond the qualitative statement.
