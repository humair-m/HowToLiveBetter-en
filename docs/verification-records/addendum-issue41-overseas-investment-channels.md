# Addendum: Section 5, Entry 39 — Overseas Investment Through Legal Channels (issue #41)

2026-09-28. issue #41's original text was "stay away from A-shares, open a US stock account early, enjoy a winning life," with no source provided.

## What Was Not Adopted Verbatim

- "Stay away from A-shares" and "US stocks win lying down": these are judgments about market direction with no citable original literature. They run opposite to Entry 17 (broad index funds) and Entry 19 (don't bet everything on one stock); those two entries are about not betting on a single market and not chasing past performance. This book does not give judgments on which market will rise.
- "Open a US stock account early": domestic residents opening accounts with overseas internet brokers has, since end-2022, been ruled by the China Securities Regulatory Commission (CSRC) as illegal cross-border business activity. Since May 2026 this enters a two-year concentrated cleanup, with new account openings prohibited. Writing this path as-is would steer readers toward a violation.

## What Was Adopted

The real problem the issue touches: for people who want to allocate overseas assets, where is the legal path. Section 5 previously had no such entry; added at the end of the section, not inserted in the middle, to avoid renumbering.

## 1. Eight-department cleanup plan
- URL: <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7634324/content.shtml>
- Confirmed: publication date 2026-05-22; issuing departments: CSRC, Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, Ministry of Public Security, People's Bank of China, State Administration for Market Regulation, National Financial Regulatory Administration, Cyberspace Administration of China, State Administration of Foreign Exchange.
- Original text: "Through 2 years of concentrated cleanup, comprehensively ban overseas securities, futures, and fund institutions' illegal cross-border business activities"; "prohibit overseas institutions from providing existing investors in the mainland with illegal buy-side transactions, fund transfers, etc., and only allow one-way sell-side transactions and fund transfers out"; "prohibit overseas institutions from conducting securities, futures, and fund business-related marketing and solicitation in the mainland, providing related account-opening, processing trading instructions, fund transfer, etc."

## 2. Q&A
- URL: <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7634328/content.shtml>
- Confirmed: publication date 2026-05-22.
- Original text: "Set a 2-year concentrated cleanup period to clear illegal existing business"; after the cleanup period expires, "comprehensively shut down mainland websites, trading software, and supporting servers, and prohibit the provision of trading and other services to existing investors in the mainland"; "guide mainland investors to conduct overseas investment through legal channels such as Stock Connect, Qualified Domestic Institutional Investors (QDII), and Cross-boundary Wealth Management Connect."
- Not found: penalties for the investor personally, whether accounts would be forcibly closed. The entry's Note only states "the document does not specify penalties for the investor personally," and does not state "accounts will not be closed" (the latter was only seen in media reports from Xinhua and others, not verified against the CSRC original).

## 3. Penalty notices for Tiger Brokers, Futu, and Longbridge
- URL: <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c7634330/content.shtml>
- Confirmed: publication date 2026-05-22.
- Original text: "The Commission proposes to confiscate all illegal income of Tiger Brokers, Futu, and Longbridge's related domestic and overseas entities, and to impose severe penalties in accordance with law."

## 4. Personal foreign-exchange purchase application form
- URL: <https://www.safe.gov.cn/safe/file/file/20210402/30fab7a9646f417ca9d2f67ff4c1e5f0.pdf> (State Administration of Foreign Exchange official site, attachment 1; the same file on m.safe.gov.cn has a certificate mismatch, so the www domain was used)
- Original text: "May not be used for overseas property purchase, securities investment, purchase of life insurance, or investment-type returning-dividend insurance, or other capital-account items not yet opened"; "Individuals placed on the 'Watch List' will not enjoy personal convenience-quota in the year they are placed on the Watch List and the two consecutive years thereafter"; "The foreign-exchange authority will impose administrative penalties in accordance with the Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Foreign Exchange Administration, and may also refer the case for anti-money-laundering investigation; relevant information will be incorporated into the personal credit record in accordance with law."

## Benefit Magnitude and Evidence Grade
- Evidence grade A: all four are official original texts, with explicit deadlines and penalty consequences.
- Benefit set as "Medium," metric "Money": no amount to threshold against. It recovers two things: money not stuck in a sell-only account, and not losing the forex-purchase quota or facing administrative penalties. Under the money metric, it cannot be called a 10,000-yuan tier; under the freedom metric, it is "avoid administrative penalty," also falling in "Medium." Set as "Medium," metric follows Section 5 in using Money.
