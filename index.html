How To Live Better

Every entry answers two questions: what it costs, and what it gets back.

What it gets back is also tiered by who receives it: yourself is the highest, then spouse and immediate family, then friends and colleagues, with strangers at the lowest — lowest doesn't mean zero, just a smaller expected return and risks that must be considered alongside it.

You don't have to do all of them: this is a cost-effectiveness-ranked list of options, not a to-do list. Picking one or two counts; come back for the rest when needed. To pick the easy ones, set "Money spent" to "Costs nothing" and "Willpower required" to "None" on the left, and what's left is the easy set. The author himself hasn't done most of them either. The dashed "Entry X" markers in the body text can be clicked to see what that entry says inline, without flipping back yourself.
